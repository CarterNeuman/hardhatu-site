overview: >
  This plan assumes you've already read the Consultants & Advisory Get
  Hired guide: this is the one category on the site where the job
  search barely resembles a job search, these roles are reached through
  a track record and a professional network, not a resume dropped into
  an applicant tracking system. The market itself is genuinely heating
  up, with new specialist consultancies launching and demand for
  experienced quantum, delay, and technical specialists rising since
  late 2025. From here it's about proof and visibility: leading with
  one real engagement and number, confirming the credential your
  specialty requires, and checking the rare junior-analyst doors that
  do exist at a handful of firms. The basic stuff (naming your
  engagement, confirming your credential track) always lands near the
  start no matter how long your timeline is. Staying visible in your
  network and working toward another credential repeat for as long as
  your plan runs: fewer hours a week or a shorter timeline means fewer
  rounds of each, more hours or a longer timeline means more, which
  matters more here than almost anywhere else on this site, since this
  category hires on relationships built over time, not a single
  application.
dayByDay:
  - dayStart: 1
    dayEnd: 1
    title: "Identify one past engagement with a real number attached to it"
    detail: "Dispute value resolved, value engineering savings delivered, claims defended. Write the one sentence that leads with it, this is what separates a credible advisory background from a generalized one."
    appliesWhen: both
  - dayStart: 1
    dayEnd: 2
    title: "Confirm the credential your specialty actually requires"
    detail: "Bar admission for construction attorneys and claims consultants, a PE for technical experts, CCM or CCP for construction consultants, LEED credentials for sustainability consulting. This signals the track record before anyone reads your resume. Use the Consultants & Advisory Resume Guide to see how a firm principal actually wants that written up."
    appliesWhen: both
  - dayStart: 2
    dayEnd: 2
    title: "Read through two or three Consultants & Advisory career pages"
    detail: "Compare claims consulting, scheduling and delay consulting, expert witness work, sustainability consulting, and commissioning, these draw on very different years-in-the-field backgrounds before anyone gets hired into them."
    appliesWhen: browsing
  - dayStart: 3
    dayEnd: 4
    title: "Check whether a rare junior-analyst or associate-consultant seat is open"
    detail: "A small number of the larger claims and forensic consulting firms, names like HKA, Secretariat, Ankura, FTI Consulting, Kroll, and J.S. Held, hire engineering, quantity surveying, or construction management graduates directly. These seats are scarce and almost never posted on a general job board, so check each firm's own careers page."
    appliesWhen: both
  - dayStart: 4
    dayEnd: 4
    title: "Get known inside your specialty's network before you need the job"
    detail: "AACE International for cost and schedule specialists, USGBC for sustainability consulting, and a published case study or article are how this category's hiring actually happens, far more than job boards."
    appliesWhen: browsing
  - dayStart: 5
    dayEnd: 5
    title: "Keep building your track record inside a GC, owner, or specialty firm"
    detail: "This is the standard route in for almost everyone in this category, not a detour. The credibility these roles require comes from years of hands-on experience somewhere else in the industry first."
    appliesWhen: both
  - dayStart: 6
    dayEnd: 6
    title: "Reach out to one firm actively expanding right now"
    detail: "The dispute consulting market has been in a genuine growth phase since late 2025, with new specialist consultancies launching and rising demand for experienced quantum, delay, and technical specialists specifically."
    appliesWhen: applying
  - dayStart: 7
    dayEnd: 8
    title: "Reach out to your next contact, and follow up on anyone who's gone quiet"
    detail: "Referrals from relationships built earlier in your career drive more hiring here than any job board, so this round is as much about staying visible in your network, a conference contact, an association connection, a former colleague, as it is about a specific opening. Follow up using the template below if it's been a reasonable window with no response."
    appliesWhen: applying
    recurring: true
    recurringKind: outreach
  - dayStart: 9
    dayEnd: 9
    title: "Decide if you're ready to move from browsing to applying"
    detail: "If a specific specialty still feels like the right fit, this is the point to lead with your strongest engagement and start reaching out for real."
    appliesWhen: browsing
  - dayStart: 8
    dayEnd: 9
    title: "Work toward the credential your specialty rewards"
    detail: "Bar admission, a PE license, CCM or CCP, or LEED credentials, whichever track you're building toward. Each one signals the track record before anyone reads further."
    appliesWhen: both
    recurring: true
    recurringKind: certification
  - dayStart: 12
    dayEnd: 12
    title: "Prepare to discuss one engagement in real depth, not in summary"
    detail: "These are relationship-driven hires, usually with a firm principal rather than an HR screener, and the interview typically goes deep into how you actually handled one real situation."
    appliesWhen: applying
  - dayStart: 13
    dayEnd: 14
    title: "Review your responses and decide your next move"
    detail: "By now you should know which contacts are worth staying on and which have gone quiet. Keep the relationship warm either way, this category rewards the long game more than almost any other on this site."
    appliesWhen: applying
    anchorEnd: true
