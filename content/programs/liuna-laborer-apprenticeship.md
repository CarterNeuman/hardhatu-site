whatToExpect: >
  Apprentices work full-time, paid, under experienced laborers while
  attending related classroom instruction at a local LIUNA training
  center. The core Construction Craft Laborer program is commonly
  built around 4,000 hours of on-the-job training plus roughly
  300-400 hours of classroom instruction, which some locals structure
  as a two-year program and others stretch to four years depending on
  local standards. Training covers a wide range of skills — from
  concrete and demolition to hazardous materials handling and
  highway construction — reflecting how broad the laborer trade is.
  Pay rises on a set schedule, training carries no tuition cost, and
  LIUNA reports apprentices can earn well into six figures combined
  over a four-year program depending on the local market. Exact
  length and entry process are set by the local training fund.
