  Apprentices work full-time, paid, under a journeyman millwright
  while attending related classroom and shop instruction covering
  precision alignment, rigging, hydraulics, and the installation and
  repair of industrial equipment. Millwright apprenticeships are
  structured in levels combining on-the-job hours with blocks of
  in-school training, and most apprentices need around four years to
  complete the full program, though length can range more widely
  depending on the apprentice's pace and prior experience. Pay rises
  on a set schedule as hours and levels are completed, and training
  carries no tuition cost. Exact structure, level length, and
  recruitment are set by the local training center.
