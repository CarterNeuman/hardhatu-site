whatToExpect: >
  ABC is a national trade association for "merit shop" (open-shop,
  non-union) contractors, and its chapters sponsor U.S. Department of
  Labor-registered apprenticeship programs using NCCER's standardized
  craft curriculum, across occupations from carpentry and electrical
  to pipefitting and equipment operation. Program length and
  structure follow the Department of Labor's registered apprenticeship
  requirements and vary by trade and chapter — typically several
  years of on-the-job training paired with related classroom
  instruction, with apprentices employed and paid by an ABC member
  contractor throughout. Unlike a union apprenticeship, there's no
  union membership or hiring hall involved; you apply directly to a
  participating contractor or your local ABC chapter. Exact trades
  offered, length, and recruitment process vary significantly by
  chapter, so check your local chapter's offerings directly.
