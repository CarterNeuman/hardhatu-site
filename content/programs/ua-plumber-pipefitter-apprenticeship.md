tagline: A five-year paid apprenticeship into plumbing and pipefitting, run through local UA union halls and their Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committees.
whatToExpect: >
  Apprentices work full-time, paid, under a journeyman plumber or
  pipefitter while attending related classroom instruction, usually a
  couple of evenings a week at the local UA training center. Programs
  are five years, combining roughly 8,000-10,000 hours of on-the-job
  training with several hundred to a thousand classroom hours
  depending on the local — some locals' broader apprenticeships also
  cover welding or HVACR service alongside plumbing and pipefitting.
  Pay increases in steps throughout, and graduates earn United
  Association journeyman status. Admission requirements, application
  windows, and waitlists are set by the local UA chapter, not
  nationally, so timing varies a lot by region.
