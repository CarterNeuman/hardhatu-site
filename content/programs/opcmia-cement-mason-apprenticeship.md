whatToExpect: >
  Apprentices work full-time, paid, under a journeyman cement mason or
  plasterer while attending related classroom instruction at a local
  training center. Most OPCMIA programs run three years, combining
  around 3,600-6,000 hours of on-the-job training with 400-480 hours
  of related classroom instruction, and starting pay is typically
  around 70% of journeyman scale with scheduled raises. Training is
  free to the apprentice, and some locals allow apprentices to earn
  college credit alongside their hours. OPCMIA also partners with Job
  Corps on a pre-apprenticeship track for applicants with no prior
  trade experience, including recent high school graduates. Exact
  length and classroom hours are set by the local training fund.
