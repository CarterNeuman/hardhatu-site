whatToExpect: >
  Apprentices work full-time, paid, alongside a journeyman mason while
  attending related classroom instruction at a local BAC or
  International Masonry Institute training center. Apprenticeships for
  bricklaying and the allied trowel trades (stonemasonry, tile,
  terrazzo, marble) run two to four years depending on the specific
  craft and local requirements, with pay rising on a set schedule as
  apprentices complete hours. Training is free to the apprentice, and
  graduates earn journeyman status recognized across BAC's network.
  The union also runs an annual international apprentice contest where
  top performers from local programs compete. Exact program length,
  recruitment windows, and local requirements vary by BAC local.
