  Candidates qualify with 8 years of industry-related experience, or 4 years
  of experience plus a 4-year related college degree. There is no required
  training course; AACE never requires purchasing or taking a prep class to
  sit for the exam, though most candidates study independently or through a
  review course from a local AACE section. The exam includes a written
  memo/technical component in addition to multiple-choice questions, and
  candidates agree to AACE's Canon of Ethics. Both credentials require
  recertification every three years through continuing education or
  volunteer service.
