whatToExpect: >
  Apprentices work full-time, paid, under a journeyman painter while
  attending related classroom instruction through the Finishing
  Trades Institute's hybrid online-and-in-person curriculum. A typical
  commercial or industrial painting apprenticeship runs about three
  years, though it can be shorter for applicants with prior trade
  experience; some district-specific programs run longer, up to five
  years in certain locals. Curriculum covers union orientation, health
  and safety, surface prep, coatings, and leadership development, with
  on-the-job learning tracked alongside classroom hours. Pay rises on
  a set schedule and training is free to the apprentice. IUPAT's
  Finishing Trades Institute also runs separate apprenticeship tracks
  for industrial painting, glazing, and drywall finishing. Exact
  length and schedule are set by the local district council.
