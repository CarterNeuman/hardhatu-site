whatToExpect: >
  A self-paced online course covering the same supervisor-level safety
  material as the [[exam-osha-30|OSHA 30 exam]] on this site — the Focus
  Four hazards plus the judgment calls a supervisor is expected to make
  on site. Because OSHA caps instructional time at 7.5 hours a day, a
  legitimate course runs over a minimum of four sittings even online; a
  "30-hour" course claiming to finish in one sitting isn't run to
  program requirements. The completion card is issued by the
  OSHA-authorized training provider, not by OSHA itself, and doesn't
  expire. Several different companies are authorized to sell this
  course online — OSHA's own outreach page (linked above) lists current
  authorized providers, since the roster changes over time.
