whatToExpect: >
  A self-paced or short instructor-led course, offered through various
  training providers, covering PV system components, sizing, basic
  electrical concepts, and safety — ending in the NABCEP PV Associate
  exam. Unlike NABCEP's advanced installer certifications, which
  require documented field experience and a separate exam fee, the
  Associate credential has no experience requirement and many
  training providers bundle the exam fee into the course price. It's
  positioned as a starting point for people transitioning into solar
  without a professional certification yet, not a stand-alone career
  credential — most solar installer jobs still expect on-the-job
  training or a trade background alongside it. Course price and
  length vary by provider; some run under a week, others longer.
