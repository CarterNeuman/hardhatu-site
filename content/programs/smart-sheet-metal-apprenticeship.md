whatToExpect: >
  Apprentices work full-time, paid, under a journeyman sheet metal
  worker while attending related classroom instruction at a local
  training center, usually overseen by a joint committee of union and
  signatory contractor representatives. Programs most commonly run
  four to five years, combining roughly 8,000 hours of on-the-job
  training with 850-930+ classroom hours covering HVAC installation,
  shop fabrication, welding, and architectural sheet metal work. Pay
  rises in steps as apprentices complete hours, and training carries
  no tuition cost. SMART Heroes, a shortened track for military
  veterans, grants advanced placement as a second-year apprentice.
  Exact length, schedule, and classroom hours vary by local training
  center.
