tagline: A paid four-plus-year apprenticeship into elevator and escalator installation, maintenance, and repair, administered nationally by NEIEP for the IUEC.
whatToExpect: >
  Entry starts with the Elevator Industry Aptitude Test and an
  interview; accepted applicants begin as a probationary apprentice,
  paired with an experienced mechanic in the field and in the
  classroom. The registered apprenticeship runs a little over four
  years, with roughly 160 classroom hours and 2,000 on-the-job hours
  per year, covering installation, maintenance, and repair across
  the full range of elevator, escalator, and other conveyance
  equipment. Pay rises on a set schedule as hours are completed, and
  training is free to the apprentice. Because NEIEP is a single
  national training trust rather than dozens of independent locals,
  curriculum and standards are more consistent across regions than in
  some other building trades, though recruitment is still handled
  locally by IUEC locals and signatory employers.
