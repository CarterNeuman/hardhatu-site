whatToExpect: >
  Apprentices work full-time under a journeyman carpenter while
  attending related classroom and shop instruction at a local UBC
  training center. Most programs run four years and combine about
  8,000 hours of on-the-job training with roughly 576 hours of
  related classroom instruction, though some locals run shorter
  tracks for specialties like trade show or floor-covering work.
  Apprentices are paid from day one on a rising wage scale tied to
  completed hours, with raises roughly every six months, and training
  itself carries no tuition cost. Some UBC training centers also
  award college credit or an associate degree through a partner
  community college. Admission requirements, recruitment windows, and
  exact wage scales are set by the local training center, not
  nationally, so specifics vary by region.
