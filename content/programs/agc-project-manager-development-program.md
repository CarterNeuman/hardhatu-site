  planning, documentation, cost and schedule control, risk, and closeout.
  Modules can be taken individually or as a full sequence, and are offered
  through local AGC chapters, member companies, or virtually through AGC
  EDGE. It's designed to run alongside on-the-job experience rather than
  replace it, so most candidates are already working as a project engineer
