whatToExpect: >
  Apprentices work full-time, paid, under a journeyman roofer while
  attending related classroom instruction covering built-up, modified
  bitumen, single-ply (PVC/TPO), and waterproofing systems used on
  plaza decks and building foundations. Many local programs run
  around 42 months, combining roughly 4,000-6,000 hours of on-the-job
  training with 400-450+ hours of classroom instruction, though exact
  length varies by local. Pay and benefits are negotiated through
  each local's collective bargaining agreement, and training carries
  no tuition cost. Locals periodically run open recruitment windows
  for a set number of apprentice slots rather than accepting
  applications year-round, so timing to apply varies by region.
