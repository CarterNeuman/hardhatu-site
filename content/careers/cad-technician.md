title: CAD Technician
category: "Technology & Design"
tagline: Turns a designer's or engineer's sketch and markup into a precise, buildable drawing, often the fastest way into the design side of construction without an architecture or engineering degree.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A CAD (Computer-Aided Design) Technician produces and revises technical
  drawings for architects, engineers, or contractors, translating design
  intent and markups into precise, coordinated drawings. It's frequently a
  first step into design-adjacent construction work, and a common path
  into more specialized roles like BIM/VDC Specialist as 3D modeling
  increasingly supplements traditional 2D drafting.
whatTheyDo:
  - Produce and revise 2D drawings from architect or engineer markups and sketches
  - Maintain drawing standards and consistency across a project's drawing set
  - Coordinate revisions across disciplines to keep drawing sets aligned
  - Prepare shop drawings for subcontractor submittal and review
  - Support quantity takeoffs by producing accurate, to-scale drawings
  - Organize and maintain drawing files and revision histories
typicalDay:
  - time: "8:00 AM"
    activity: Update a floor plan with markups received from the project architect
  - time: "9:30 AM"
    activity: Prepare a shop drawing for subcontractor submittal
  - time: "11:00 AM"
    activity: Coordinate a drawing revision across the structural and architectural sets
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Check completed drawings against the office's drafting standards
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Organize and distribute the week's drawing revisions to the project team
whereTheyWork:
  - Office
skills:
  - CAD software proficiency
  - Reading and interpreting design markups
  - Precision and attention to detail
  - Basic understanding of construction methods and materials
  - Organization (drawing sets and revision control)
software:
  - Computer-aided design software (e.g. AutoCAD)
education:
  required: High school diploma or equivalent. No certification or license is legally required to work as a CAD Technician
  preferred: Associate's degree in drafting, CAD technology, or a related field
  helpful: Autodesk Certified Professional credential
  notNecessary: A four-year design or engineering degree; this is a common entry point that doesn't require one
progression:
  - CAD Technician
  - Senior CAD Technician
  - BIM/VDC Specialist or Design Coordinator
  - CAD/BIM Manager

  - Specialty (MEP) Subcontractors
