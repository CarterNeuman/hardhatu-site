title: Project Manager
category: "Project & Operations"
tagline: Runs the business side of a construction project so the people building it can focus on building it.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Project Manager (PM) owns a construction project's budget, schedule, and contracts.
  They're the main point of contact between the owner, the architect, and the people
  actually building the project, making sure everyone has what they need, on time and
  on budget.
whatTheyDo:
  - Track budget and approve payments to subcontractors
  - Review and respond to RFIs from the field
  - Negotiate and process change orders
  - Coordinate schedules between the owner, architect, and subcontractors
  - Manage risk and resolve disputes before they become claims
typicalDay:
  - time: "7:30 AM"
    activity: Review overnight RFIs and flag anything urgent for the site team
  - time: "9:00 AM"
    activity: Owner/architect coordination call
  - time: "11:00 AM"
    activity: Walk the site with the superintendent to check progress against schedule
  - time: "1:00 PM"
    activity: Review and approve subcontractor pay applications
  - time: "3:00 PM"
    activity: Draft a change order for scope the owner just added
whereTheyWork:
  - Office
  - Jobsite
  - Hybrid
skills:
  - Budgeting
  - Scheduling
  - Negotiation
  - Contract reading
  - Communication
software:
  - Procore
  - Microsoft Project
  - Bluebeam
education:
  required: High school diploma or equivalent
  preferred: Bachelor's degree in construction management, engineering, or related field
  helpful: PMP or CCM certification
  notNecessary: A four-year degree is common but not the only path; many PMs move up from estimator or superintendent roles
progression:
  - Project Engineer
  - Assistant Project Manager
  - Project Manager
  - Senior Project Manager
  - Director of Operations
hirerTypes:
  - General Contractors
  - Construction Management Firms
  - Owners with in-house construction teams
