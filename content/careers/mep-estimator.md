whatIs: >
  An MEP Estimator prices the mechanical (HVAC), electrical, and plumbing
  systems within a building, often together with fire protection and
  low-voltage/technology systems, reading engineering drawings that a
  general Estimator usually isn't trained to price in the same depth. It's
  a distinct specialty from a general Estimator the same way Heavy Civil
  Estimator is: MEP systems routinely make up a third or more of a
  building's total cost on complex projects (hospitals, labs, data
  centers), and pricing them accurately depends on understanding equipment
  selection, system capacity, and code-driven design the way an MEP
  Engineer does, not just the material-and-labor unit pricing that covers
  most of a general building estimate. Some MEP Estimators work for a
  general contractor pricing MEP scope as part of a full building bid;
  others work directly for a mechanical, electrical, or plumbing
  subcontractor pricing only their own trade's work.
whatTheyDo:
  - Perform quantity takeoffs for mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems from engineering drawings
  - Solicit and evaluate pricing from MEP subcontractors, equipment vendors, and material suppliers
  - Analyze mechanical, electrical, and plumbing drawings and specifications to catch coordination gaps or missing scope before bidding
  - Price design-build or design-assist MEP scope from a conceptual design, before drawings are complete
  - Track equipment lead times and pricing volatility, since MEP equipment pricing swings more than most building materials
  - Present MEP pricing to the broader estimating team as part of a full building bid
