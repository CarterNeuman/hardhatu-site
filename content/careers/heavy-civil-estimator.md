title: Heavy Civil Estimator
category: "Preconstruction & Estimating"
tagline: Estimates earthwork, utilities, and infrastructure work using an entirely different cost basis than pricing a building, opening the door to a whole side of the industry this site hadn't represented yet.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Heavy Civil Estimator prices infrastructure and site work, roads,
  bridges, utilities, earthwork, and similar heavy civil projects, using
  a cost basis built around production rates for equipment and crews
  moving earth or laying pipe, rather than the material-and-labor
  unit-cost approach that dominates vertical building estimating. It's a
  distinct specialty from a general Estimator: heavy civil bids often
  come from public agencies with strict unit-price bid formats, and
  pricing accuracy depends heavily on understanding soil conditions,
  hauling distances, and equipment productivity.
whatTheyDo:
  - Perform quantity takeoffs for earthwork, utilities, paving, and other heavy civil scope from civil drawings
  - Estimate costs based on equipment and crew production rates rather than purely unit material costs
  - Analyze soil and geotechnical data to price earthwork, dewatering, and shoring requirements
  - Prepare unit-price bids for public infrastructure projects
  - Solicit and evaluate quotes from subcontractors and material/aggregate suppliers
  - Assemble and submit competitive bids on public infrastructure and site work projects
