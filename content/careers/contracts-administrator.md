title: Contracts Administrator
category: "Business"
tagline: Manages the paperwork that makes every relationship on a project legally enforceable, the prime contract, every subcontract, and every change to either.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Contracts Administrator drafts, reviews, and manages the contracts that
  govern a construction project, the prime contract between the owner and
  general contractor, and every subcontract between the GC and its
  subcontractors. They make sure contract terms are consistent, insurance
  and bonding requirements are met, and changes to scope or price are
  documented in a way that holds up if a dispute ever ends up in front of a
  lawyer.
whatTheyDo:
  - Draft and issue subcontracts based on the terms of the prime contract
  - Track subcontractor insurance certificates, bonds, and lien waivers for compliance
  - Review change orders for proper contractual documentation before they're executed
  - Flag contract clauses that create outsized risk (liquidated damages, indemnification terms) for leadership review
  - Maintain the contract files that get pulled if a dispute or claim arises
  - Coordinate with legal counsel on contract language for complex or high-risk projects

  - time: "11:00 AM"

  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Flag a liquidated damages clause in a new prime contract for the operations team to review
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Update the contract tracking log ahead of a weekly compliance review
