title: Senior Estimator
category: "Preconstruction & Estimating"
tagline: Leads the bid on the biggest, most complex jobs and mentors the estimators doing the takeoffs underneath them.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Senior Estimator leads the pricing effort on larger or more complex
  projects, often managing a team of estimators who handle individual
  scopes of a bid. Where an Estimator focuses on producing an accurate
  takeoff and bid, a Senior Estimator is also responsible for the
  bid strategy: how aggressively to price the job, which risks to carry a
  contingency for, and ultimately whether the bid gets submitted at all.
whatTheyDo:
  - Oversee and review takeoffs and pricing produced by junior estimators on a bid team
  - Set overall bid strategy, margin, contingency, and which risks to price in versus qualify out
  - Present final bids to company leadership or ownership for approval before submission
  - Build and maintain relationships with key subcontractors and suppliers for competitive pricing
  - Review contract terms for risk before a bid goes out, often alongside a contracts administrator or legal
  - Mentor estimators earlier in their career and help standardize the team's estimating practices
typicalDay:
  - time: "8:00 AM"
    activity: Review a junior estimator's takeoff on a large bid due at the end of the week
  - time: "9:30 AM"
    activity: Call a strategic subcontractor partner to negotiate pricing ahead of the deadline
  - time: "11:00 AM"
    activity: Meet with the preconstruction team to review bid strategy and target margin
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Review contract terms and flag two clauses that need qualification before the bid goes out
  - time: "4:00 PM"
    activity: Present the completed bid to the VP of Operations for sign-off
whereTheyWork:

education:

  helpful: Certified Professional Estimator (CPE) credential
  notNecessary: A specific estimating degree; this role is almost always reached by being promoted up from Estimator rather than hired in directly
