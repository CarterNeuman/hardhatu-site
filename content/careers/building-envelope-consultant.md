whatIs: >
  A Building Envelope Consultant specializes in the exterior enclosure systems
  that separate a building's interior from the outdoors: roofing,
  waterproofing, curtain walls, windows, and cladding. On new construction, a
  consultant reviews an Architect's envelope design for performance and
  constructability, writes technical specifications, and oversees mockup
  testing before a curtain wall or facade system is released for fabrication.
  On existing buildings, the work shifts to forensic investigation: diagnosing
  water infiltration, condensation, or facade failures and recommending
  repairs. Because a failed envelope detail can show up years after
  construction as a hidden, expensive leak, this role often also serves as an
  expert witness when an envelope failure becomes a construction defect
  dispute.
whatTheyDo:
  - Review and advise on envelope design details for water, air, and thermal performance
  - Write technical specifications for roofing, waterproofing, and curtain wall systems
  - Oversee full-scale mockup testing of curtain wall and facade systems before fabrication
  - Conduct forensic investigations of water infiltration, condensation, or cladding failures on existing buildings
  - Perform building envelope commissioning, verifying installed systems meet design performance
  - Prepare technical reports and, when a failure becomes a dispute, serve as an expert witness
