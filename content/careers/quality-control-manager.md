title: Quality Control Manager
category: "Project & Operations"
tagline: Makes sure the finished work actually matches the contract specifications, the counterpart to a Safety Manager, protecting the project instead of the people.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Quality Control (QC) Manager verifies that construction work complies
  with contract specifications, drawings, and applicable codes, inspecting
  work, tracking deficiencies, and managing the submittal and testing
  process that documents compliance. On federal government construction
  contracts (U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, NAVFAC, and other federal
  agencies), serving as the Contractor Quality Control (CQC) Manager
  legally requires completing the Construction Quality Management for
  Contractors (CQM-C) course and holding a current certificate, a real
  contract requirement on that specific type of work, not a general
  industry mandate for every QC role.
whatTheyDo:
  - Develop and manage the project's quality control plan and inspection schedule
  - Inspect completed work for compliance with drawings, specifications, and codes before it's covered or turned over
  - Track deficiencies through to correction and maintain quality documentation
  - Coordinate material testing and manage the submittal review process for quality compliance
  - On federal contracts, perform the specific reporting and three-phase inspection process (preparatory, initial, follow-up) the contract requires
  - Report quality status to project leadership and, on federal work, to the government's own quality assurance representative
typicalDay:
  - time: "7:00 AM"
    activity: Review the day's scheduled inspections and preparatory meeting requirements
  - time: "8:00 AM"
    activity: Hold a preparatory phase inspection meeting before a new activity starts
  - time: "10:00 AM"
    activity: Inspect completed work against the specifications before it's covered by the next trade
  - time: "1:00 PM"
    activity: Update the quality control log with deficiencies found and their correction status
  - time: "3:00 PM"
    activity: Review material test results against specification requirements
whereTheyWork:
  - Jobsite
  - Office (for documentation and reporting)
skills:
  - Reading specifications and drawings in detail
  - Inspection and testing knowledge
  - Documentation and recordkeeping
  - Communication with trades, inspectors, and (on federal work) government representatives
  - Attention to detail
software:

education:
  required: >
    High school diploma or equivalent; the Construction Quality Management
    for Contractors (CQM-C) certification becomes legally mandatory the
    moment this role is performed as the Contractor Quality Control
    Manager on a U.S. federal government construction contract; private
    commercial projects don't carry that specific mandate, but many
    employers still prefer it as a sign of formal QC training
  preferred: Bachelor's degree in construction management, engineering, or a related field
  notNecessary: A four-year degree, since many QC managers move into the role from the trades or from a superintendent track
progression:
  - Quality Control Inspector
  - Quality Control Manager
  - Senior QC Manager or Director of Quality
