whatIs: >
  A Sprinkler Fitter installs, inspects, and maintains automatic fire
  sprinkler systems and related fire protection piping. It's a distinct
  piping trade from plumbing and mechanical pipefitting, represented by
  its own union structure (the UA's Road Sprinkler Fitters, commonly
  Local 669, and local sprinkler fitter locals in major markets) and its
  own certification path through NICET (National Institute for
  Certification in Engineering Technologies). BLS does not break fire
  sprinkler fitters out as their own occupation; it groups them within
  the broader Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters category. No
  personal license is legally required in most states to work as an
  employee sprinkler fitter, though some states and local jurisdictions
  require individual fire protection licensing or certification,
  particularly for anyone signing off on system design or inspections.
whatTheyDo:
  - Read fire protection blueprints and layout sprinkler piping, heads, and valve assemblies
  - Thread, cut, and join piping for sprinkler systems, using methods specific to fire protection piping
  - Install sprinkler heads, alarm check valves, and fire department connections per NFPA code requirements
  - Perform hydrostatic and flow testing on completed systems before they're approved for use
  - Inspect and maintain existing sprinkler systems, including periodic code-required testing
  - Coordinate with the general contractor and fire marshal's office around inspection scheduling and code compliance
