title: Construction Data Analyst
category: "Technology & Design"
tagline: Turns the mountain of data construction generates, costs, schedules, safety incidents, productivity, into answers that change how the next project gets planned.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Construction Data Analyst collects, organizes, and analyzes data across
  a construction company's projects, cost trends, schedule performance,
  safety incidents, labor productivity, to help leadership make better
  decisions and to improve estimating accuracy on future work. It's a
  newer role in construction than most on this site, reflecting the
  industry's growing use of project management software, BIM, and IoT
  jobsite sensors that now generate far more data than in the past. No
  license is legally required for this role.
whatTheyDo:
  - Collect and clean data from project management, accounting, and scheduling software
  - Build dashboards and reports tracking cost, schedule, and safety performance across projects
  - Analyze historical project data to improve the accuracy of future estimates and schedules
  - Identify trends or outliers that flag a project at risk before it becomes an obvious problem
  - Support leadership with data-driven answers to specific business questions
  - Work with IT and software vendors to improve how project data is captured in the first place
typicalDay:
  - time: "8:00 AM"
    activity: Pull updated project data from Procore and the accounting system into the reporting dashboard
  - time: "9:30 AM"
    activity: Analyze cost trends across a project type to identify where estimates have been running high or low
  - time: "11:00 AM"
    activity: Build a report answering a specific question from leadership about labor productivity
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Meet with operations to review a dashboard flagging a project trending over budget
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Clean and validate a new data source being added to the company's reporting system
whereTheyWork:

skills:
  - Data analysis and visualization
  - Spreadsheet and database proficiency
  - Understanding of construction cost, schedule, and safety metrics
  - Communicating data findings clearly to non-technical audiences
  - Attention to detail and data accuracy
software:

  - Business intelligence and data visualization software
education:
  required: High school diploma or equivalent. No license is legally required for this role
  preferred: Bachelor's degree in data analytics, business, construction management, or a related field
  helpful: Certifications in specific BI tools, a background in construction operations that helps interpret what the data actually means
  notNecessary: A construction-specific degree; many people in this role come from a general data/analytics background and learn the industry context on the job
