title: Bid Coordinator
category: "Preconstruction & Estimating"
tagline: Manages the logistics of getting a bid out the door on time, a realistic first job for someone interested in estimating who isn't ready to price a project yet.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Bid Coordinator manages the administrative and logistical side of the
  bidding process, tracking invitations to bid, distributing drawings and
  addenda to subcontractors, organizing incoming subcontractor quotes,
  and making sure a complete, compliant bid package gets submitted before
  the deadline. It's a genuinely useful entry point into preconstruction:
  the role doesn't require pricing a job yet, but it puts someone in the
  middle of the estimating process early enough to learn how bids
  actually come together before moving into estimating itself.
whatTheyDo:
  - Track incoming invitations to bid and maintain the bid calendar
  - Distribute drawings, specifications, and addenda to subcontractors and suppliers
  - Organize and log incoming subcontractor quotes for the estimating team
  - Assist with bid leveling, comparing subcontractor quotes on an apples-to-apples basis
  - Assemble and submit the final bid package before the deadline
  - Maintain a database of subcontractor and supplier contacts and pricing history
typicalDay:
  - time: "7:30 AM"
    activity: Review the bid calendar and confirm which submissions are due this week
  - time: "9:00 AM"
    activity: Distribute a bid addendum to the subcontractor list and confirm receipt
  - time: "11:00 AM"
    activity: Log incoming subcontractor quotes and flag any scope gaps for the estimator
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Assist the estimator with bid leveling ahead of an afternoon deadline
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Assemble and submit the final bid package before the cutoff
whereTheyWork:
  - Company office
skills:
  - Organization and deadline management
  - Basic understanding of drawings and specifications
  - Communication with subcontractors and suppliers
  - Spreadsheet and bid management software proficiency
  - Attention to detail (a missed addendum or scope gap can sink a bid)
software:

education:
  required: High school diploma or equivalent; no license is legally required for this role
  preferred: Associate's or bachelor's degree in construction management or business
  helpful: An internship or prior office experience in a construction or estimating environment
  notNecessary: A construction-specific degree; this is a realistic entry point for someone without prior construction experience who's organized and detail-oriented
