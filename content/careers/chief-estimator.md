whatIs: >
  A Chief Estimator leads a construction company's entire estimating
  department, setting the department's bid-win strategy, standardizing how
  estimators across the company build and present a bid, and personally
  reviewing or signing off on the company's largest and highest-risk
  pursuits before they go out the door. Where a Senior Estimator leads the
  pricing effort on one bid at a time, a Chief Estimator operates one level
  up, managing the whole team of estimators and senior estimators, deciding
  which opportunities the company pursues in the first place, and answering
  directly to company ownership or executive leadership for the department's
  win rate and the accuracy of its numbers. It's a career destination this
  site's own Estimator and Senior Estimator pages have pointed toward from
  the start, naming it as the next step without this site ever building the
  page for it.
whatTheyDo:
  - Set department-wide bid-win strategy and decide which opportunities the company pursues
  - Review and personally sign off on the company's largest or highest-risk bids before submission
  - Manage, hire, and develop a team of estimators and senior estimators
  - Standardize estimating processes, templates, and software use across the department
  - Report win rates, pipeline, and estimating accuracy directly to ownership or executive leadership
  - Negotiate directly with ownership on strategic pursuits, joint ventures, or major subcontractor partnerships
