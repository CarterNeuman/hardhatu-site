id: career-document-control-specialist
title: Document Control Specialist
category: "Project & Operations"
tagline: Owns the submittal logs, RFI logs, and drawing revisions that keep everyone on a project working from the same current information, a real entry point into the office side of construction.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Document Control Specialist manages the flow of project
  documentation, submittals, RFIs, drawing revisions, contracts, and
  correspondence, making sure everyone on a project is working from the
  current version of every document and that a complete, organized record
  exists if a dispute or claim ever needs one. On a large project, dozens
  of drawing revisions and hundreds of submittals move through the office
  at once; a document control specialist's job is to make sure nothing
  gets lost, nothing gets built off an outdated drawing, and every
  approval has a clear paper trail.
whatTheyDo:
  - Maintain submittal, RFI, and transmittal logs, tracking status and due dates
  - Distribute current drawing revisions and flag outdated versions before they cause a mistake
  - Organize and archive project correspondence, contracts, and closeout documentation
  - Set up and administer document control within cloud-based project management software
  - Support the project team by tracking document turnaround times and flagging bottlenecks
  - Assemble the final project record set at closeout
typicalDay:
  - time: "7:30 AM"
    activity: Review overnight submittals and RFIs, logging each one and assigning it to the right reviewer
  - time: "9:00 AM"
    activity: Distribute a new drawing revision to the field team and update the master drawing log
  - time: "11:00 AM"
    activity: Follow up on submittals approaching their review deadline
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Organize contract correspondence for an upcoming change order review
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Audit the project's document control software for missing or misfiled records
whereTheyWork:
  - Project office (jobsite trailer or company office)
skills:
  - Extreme attention to detail and organization
  - Document management and version control
  - Cloud-based project management software proficiency
  - Clear written communication
  - Understanding of the submittal and RFI process
software:
  - Procore
  - Bluebeam
education:
  required: High school diploma or equivalent; no license is legally required for this role
  preferred: Associate's or bachelor's degree in construction management, business administration, or a related field
  helpful: The Construction Specifications Institute's Construction Documents Technologist (CDT) certification, which covers construction documentation standards and is a recognized credential for this kind of role
  notNecessary: A construction-specific degree; many document control specialists move into the role from general administrative or office-management backgrounds and learn the construction-specific process on the job
progression:

  - Senior Document Control Specialist
  - Project Engineer or Project Coordinator
  - Contracts Administrator or Project Manager
