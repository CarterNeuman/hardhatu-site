whatIs: >
  A Marketing & Proposals Manager runs the content, design, and production of
  a construction firm's proposals, statements of qualifications (SOQs), and
  responses to requests for proposals (RFPs), along with the firm's broader
  brand presence, website, case studies, and awards submittals. This is a
  distinct specialty from Business Development or Sales: a BD professional
  builds the relationship that gets a firm invited to compete, but it's the
  Marketing & Proposals Manager who turns the firm's qualifications, project
  history, and staff resumes into a compelling, deadline-driven written
  submission, often managing input from multiple project teams under a tight
  RFP response window. The Society for Marketing Professional Services (SMPS)
  is the recognized professional association for this specialty across the
  architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) industry, offering its own
  Certified Professional Services Marketer (CPSM) credential.
whatTheyDo:
  - Write and design statements of qualifications (SOQs) and RFP responses under tight deadlines
  - Coordinate input from project managers, estimators, and leadership into a single cohesive proposal
  - Maintain the firm's library of project profiles, staff resumes, and past-performance narratives
  - Manage the firm's brand, website, case studies, and awards or industry-recognition submittals
  - Coordinate interview/presentation preparation when a shortlisted firm is invited to present
  - Track proposal win rates and debrief on losses to improve future submissions
