title: Renewable Energy Project Manager
category: "Specialized Construction"
tagline: Manages solar, battery-storage, and microgrid builds from permitting through commissioning, the project-management path into the fastest-growing segment of the construction industry.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Renewable Energy Project Manager runs solar, battery storage, and
  other renewable energy construction projects the same way a general
  Project Manager runs a building project, managing budget, schedule,
  permitting, and subcontractors, but specialized around the specific
  permitting, utility interconnection, and equipment commissioning
  process renewable projects require. It's a natural next step for a
  Solar PV Installer who wants to move off the tools and into running
  projects, or for a general Project Manager who wants to specialize in
  the renewable energy side of the industry.
whatTheyDo:
  - Manage the budget, schedule, and subcontractors for solar, battery storage, or microgrid projects
  - Navigate permitting and utility interconnection approval processes specific to renewable energy projects
  - Coordinate equipment procurement and delivery for solar panels, inverters, and battery systems
  - Oversee installation crews and specialty subcontractors through construction and commissioning
  - Track system performance data during commissioning to confirm the installation meets design output
  - Manage relationships with utility companies, permitting agencies, and incentive program administrators
typicalDay:
  - time: "7:30 AM"
    activity: Review the week's installation schedule across active solar and storage projects
  - time: "9:00 AM"
    activity: Follow up with the utility company on an interconnection application
  - time: "11:00 AM"
    activity: Walk an active site with the installation crew to review progress
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Coordinate equipment delivery timing with a battery storage supplier
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Review commissioning data confirming a completed system's output matches the design
whereTheyWork:
  - Company office
  - Jobsite visits across active projects
skills:
  - Project scheduling and budget management
  - Understanding of solar and battery storage permitting and interconnection processes
  - Subcontractor and vendor coordination
  - Reading solar and electrical system design drawings
  - Client and utility company communication
software:
  - Solar design and monitoring software (varies by installer/manufacturer)
  - Procore
education:
  required: Bachelor's degree in construction management, engineering, or a related field, plus project management experience, ideally including some renewable energy or electrical project exposure; no license is legally required for this role
  preferred: >
    Project Management Professional (PMP) certification, the same
    credential used broadly across construction project management, and
    NABCEP's PV Installation Professional certification for hands-on
    credibility with installation crews
  notNecessary: A background specifically in solar installation; many renewable energy project managers come from general construction project management and learn the renewable-specific permitting and equipment on the job
progression:
  - Assistant Project Manager (Renewable Energy)
  - Renewable Energy Project Manager
  - Senior Project Manager or Director of Construction
  - VP of Construction or Operations (renewable energy developer)
hirerTypes:
  - Solar and Renewable Energy Developers
  - Electrical Contractors (with a renewable energy division)
  - Utility Companies (for utility-scale renewable projects)
