title: "Construction Sales / Business Development"
category: "Business"
tagline: Wins the work that everyone else on the project team eventually gets to build, relationship-driven sales in an industry where the next job usually comes from the last client.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Construction Sales or Business Development professional identifies and
  pursues new project opportunities for a contractor, developing
  relationships with owners, developers, and architects to win work before
  it goes out to bid, or to be the contractor a repeat client calls
  directly without a competitive bid at all. Unlike estimating, which prices
  a specific project once it's identified, business development is
  responsible for finding and cultivating the relationships that produce
  those opportunities in the first place. No license is legally required
  for this role.
whatTheyDo:
  - Identify and qualify new project opportunities in the market
  - Build and maintain relationships with owners, developers, architects, and repeat clients
  - Lead or support the proposal and interview process for pursued projects
  - Track a pipeline of prospective work and report on business development activity
  - Represent the company at industry events, trade associations, and networking functions
  - Coordinate with preconstruction and estimating once a pursuit moves toward a bid
typicalDay:
  - time: "8:00 AM"
    activity: Review the pipeline and prioritize follow-ups on active pursuits
  - time: "9:30 AM"
    activity: Meet with a developer to discuss an upcoming project before it's formally bid
  - time: "11:30 AM"
    activity: Coordinate with the preconstruction team on a proposal due the following week
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Attend an industry association lunch to build relationships with potential clients
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Update the CRM and report pipeline status to company leadership
whereTheyWork:
  - Office
  - Client meetings and industry events
skills:
  - Relationship building and networking
  - Persuasive communication and presentation
  - Market awareness (tracking upcoming projects and client plans)
  - Basic understanding of construction costs and schedules
  - Organization (managing a pipeline of pursuits over long sales cycles)
software:
  - Customer relationship management (CRM) software
education:
  required: High school diploma or equivalent. No license is legally required for this role
  preferred: Bachelor's degree in business, construction management, or a related field
  helpful: Prior experience in estimating, project management, or a related construction role, which builds credibility with clients
  notNecessary: A sales-specific degree; many people in this role move over from estimating or project management rather than a dedicated sales background
progression:
  - Business Development Coordinator
  - Business Development Manager
  - Director of Business Development
  - VP of Business Development or Chief Revenue Officer
