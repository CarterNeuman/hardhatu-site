whatIs: >
  A Demolition Supervisor plans and oversees the safe demolition of
  structures, sequencing the work, managing hazardous material concerns
  alongside an Environmental/Remediation Specialist, and directing the
  crews and equipment that bring a building down without endangering
  workers, neighboring structures, or the public. Demolition carries
  enough distinct risk, structural collapse sequencing, utility
  disconnection, hazardous material exposure, that the industry built its
  own credential for it: the National Demolition Association's Certified
  Demolition Supervisor (CDS), a newer, rigorous exam-based certification
  that only launched in the past few years.
whatTheyDo:
  - Plan the sequence and method for safely demolishing a structure
  - Coordinate utility disconnection before demolition begins
  - Supervise demolition crews and heavy equipment operators during the work
  - Coordinate with environmental specialists on hazardous material abatement ahead of demolition
  - Monitor for structural instability and adjust the demolition plan as conditions change
  - Ensure demolition debris is properly sorted, recycled, and disposed of
