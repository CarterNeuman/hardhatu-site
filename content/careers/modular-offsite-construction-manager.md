whatIs: >
  A Modular / Offsite Construction Manager runs a construction project built
  primarily from factory-manufactured modules or prefabricated components
  rather than assembled piece by piece on site. The job looks like general
  project management on the surface, budget, schedule, permitting,
  subcontractors, but the critical skill is coordinating two schedules that
  rarely move in sync: the factory's production line and the site's readiness
  to receive, set, and connect each module. A module that arrives before its
  foundation or utility connections are ready creates an expensive bottleneck,
  while a factory that falls behind can leave an idle site crew. This role has
  grown alongside the construction industry's broader shift toward offsite
  manufacturing for multifamily housing, hospitality, and data center
  projects, where speed and labor-cost predictability matter more than custom
  design.
whatTheyDo:
  - Coordinate the factory production schedule against the site's foundation and utility readiness
  - Manage transportation logistics for oversized modules from factory to site
  - Oversee crane selection and rigging plans for setting modules in sequence
  - Manage the connections between modules (structural, MEP, waterproofing) once set
  - Track budget and schedule across both the factory and field portions of the project
  - Coordinate with the manufacturer's engineering team on design changes that affect fabrication
