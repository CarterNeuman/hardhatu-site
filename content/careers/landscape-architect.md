whatIs: >
  A Landscape Architect designs the outdoor environment around and between
  buildings: grading and planting plans, hardscape (walkways, plazas,
  retaining walls), site furnishings, and often stormwater features like
  bioswales and detention ponds. On a typical project, landscape architecture
  work runs alongside the Architect's building design and the Civil Engineer's
  utility and drainage design, and the Landscape Architect's plans become part
  of the permit set the jurisdiction reviews. Licensure requirements vary by
  state, but most states require a license to practice and stamp site design
  drawings independently.
whatTheyDo:
  - Design grading, planting, and hardscape plans for the outdoor areas of a site
  - Select plant materials and site furnishings suited to the climate, soil, and maintenance budget
  - Coordinate with civil engineers on site grading and stormwater management features
  - Prepare construction documents and specifications for landscape contractors to bid and build from
  - Navigate local landscape ordinances, tree preservation rules, and zoning requirements
  - Visit the site during construction to confirm plantings and hardscape match the design
