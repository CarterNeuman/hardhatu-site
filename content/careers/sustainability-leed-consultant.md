tagline: Guides a project through green building certification, most often LEED, managing the credits, documentation, and coordination that turn a design intent into a certified building.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Sustainability or LEED Consultant advises project teams on green
  building strategy and manages the certification process for rating
  systems like LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design), run
  by USGBC and its credentialing body, GBCI. No government license is
  legally required for this work. The relevant credentials, LEED Green
  Associate and LEED AP with a specialty, come from GBCI and aren't a
  legal requirement either, but they function as a real-world requirement
  on many projects anyway: a project pursuing LEED certification generally
  needs a LEED-credentialed professional involved to earn certain credits
  and to navigate the submission process, so it's a certification-program
  and contractual expectation rather than a government mandate.
whatTheyDo:
  - Advise project teams on which green building rating system and certification level to pursue
  - Track and manage the specific credits required for a target certification level throughout design and construction
  - Coordinate documentation from architects, engineers, and contractors needed to support credit submissions
  - Prepare and submit the certification application to the rating system's administering body
  - Advise on sustainable material selection, energy modeling coordination, and construction waste management
  - Train project teams on the sustainability requirements specific to their scope of work
typicalDay:
  - time: "8:00 AM"
    activity: Review the project's LEED credit tracker and flag credits at risk of not being met
  - time: "9:30 AM"
    activity: Meet with the architect to confirm a design decision supports a targeted energy credit
  - time: "11:30 AM"
    activity: Collect and review documentation from a subcontractor supporting a materials credit
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Prepare a credit submission package for the certifying body
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Train the site team on construction waste management requirements tied to a pursued credit
whereTheyWork:
  - Office
  - Occasional site visits to verify sustainable construction practices
skills:
  - Deep knowledge of a green building rating system (most often LEED)
  - Project coordination across architects, engineers, and contractors
  - Technical writing and documentation for credit submissions
  - Understanding of building energy and materials concepts
  - Organization (tracking dozens of credits across a long project timeline)
software:
  - Energy modeling and green building tracking software
education:
  required: >
    High school diploma or equivalent; no government license is legally
    required for this role. LEED Green Associate or LEED AP credentialing
    from GBCI isn't a legal requirement either, but many projects pursuing
    LEED certification contractually expect a credentialed professional on
    the team to earn certain credits and manage the submission
  preferred: Bachelor's degree in architecture, engineering, construction management, or environmental studies
  helpful: LEED Green Associate or LEED AP with a specialty (e.g. Building Design + Construction)
  notNecessary: An environmental science degree; many sustainability consultants come from architecture, engineering, or construction management backgrounds
