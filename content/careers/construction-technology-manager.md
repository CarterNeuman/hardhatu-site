id: career-construction-technology-manager
title: Construction Technology Manager
category: "Technology & Design"
tagline: Oversees BIM, drone, and software rollout across a whole company, the director-level counterpart to the hands-on specialists who execute technology on a single project.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Construction Technology Manager oversees how a construction company
  adopts and standardizes technology across every project, not just one:
  choosing which BIM, project management, and field software the company
  standardizes on, rolling it out and training staff, and evaluating new
  tools like drones, reality capture, or AI-driven analytics before the
  company invests in them. It's distinct from a BIM/VDC Specialist or
  Drone/UAV Specialist, who execute their specialty on individual
  projects; a Construction Technology Manager sets company-wide strategy
  and standards for all of it.
whatTheyDo:
  - Evaluate and select construction technology platforms (BIM, project management, field software) for company-wide use
  - Standardize technology workflows and templates across projects
  - Train project teams on new software and technology adoption
  - Pilot emerging technology (drones, reality capture, AI-driven analytics) before company-wide rollout
  - Manage relationships with software vendors and negotiate licensing
  - Track technology ROI and identify where new tools would solve a real operational problem
typicalDay:
  - time: "8:00 AM"
    activity: Review adoption metrics for a recently rolled-out project management platform
  - time: "9:30 AM"
    activity: Meet with a software vendor evaluating a new field data collection tool
  - time: "11:30 AM"
    activity: Train a project team on a new BIM coordination workflow
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Pilot a drone mapping workflow to evaluate for company-wide rollout
  - time: "3:00 PM"
    activity: Present a technology investment proposal to company leadership
whereTheyWork:
  - Company office
  - Occasional project site visits to support rollout and training
skills:
  - Broad knowledge of construction technology platforms
  - Change management and training
  - Vendor evaluation and negotiation
  - Data analysis (adoption metrics, ROI)
  - Communication across field and office teams
software:
  - Procore
  - Autodesk Build
  - Building information modeling software (e.g. Revit, Navisworks)
education:
  required: Bachelor's degree in construction management, engineering, or a related field, plus several years of hands-on experience with construction technology (BIM, project management software, or similar); no license is legally required for this role
  helpful: >
    Autodesk Certified Professional credentials and Procore's
    certification tracks, both already used on individual projects by
    BIM/VDC Specialists and project teams, are common ways a technology
    manager demonstrates platform-specific expertise
  notNecessary: A software development background; this role is about construction workflows and adoption, not building the software itself
progression:
  - BIM/VDC Specialist or Construction Data Analyst
  - Construction Technology Manager
  - Director of Construction Technology or Innovation
  - Chief Information Officer or VP of Technology
