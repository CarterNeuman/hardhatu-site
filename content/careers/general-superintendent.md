whatIs: >
  A General Superintendent oversees the field execution of every active
  project a construction company has running at once, managing the
  individual Superintendents on each jobsite rather than running one
  project's day-to-day field operations personally. Where a Superintendent
  is the senior field presence on a single project, deciding sequencing
  and quality on the ground every day, a General Superintendent operates
  one level up: standardizing field practices across projects, stepping
  in personally on the company's largest or most troubled jobs, and
  developing the next generation of Superintendents and Assistant
  Superintendents coming up through the field. It's a career destination
  this site's own Superintendent and Assistant Superintendent pages have
  pointed toward from the start, naming it as the next step without this
  site ever building the page for it.
whatTheyDo:
  - Oversee and support the Superintendents running each of the company's active projects
  - Standardize field practices, safety expectations, and quality standards across all projects
  - Personally step in on the company's largest, most complex, or most troubled jobs
  - Allocate field staff, equipment, and labor resources across competing project needs
  - Mentor and develop Superintendents and Assistant Superintendents
  - Report field performance, safety metrics, and schedule risk to company leadership
