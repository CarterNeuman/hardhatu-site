whatIs: >
  A Certified Payroll Specialist manages payroll for construction
  companies working on public and prevailing-wage projects, where
  federal, state, or local law requires detailed weekly certified payroll
  reports proving every worker was paid at least the required prevailing
  wage for their trade and classification. It's a more specialized role
  than general construction payroll: certified payroll reporting has its
  own forms, deadlines, and audit exposure, and getting it wrong can hold
  up payment or trigger penalties on an entire project.
whatTheyDo:
  - Process weekly payroll for field and office employees, tracking hours by project and job classification
  - Prepare and submit certified payroll reports (such as federal Form WH-347) for prevailing-wage projects
  - Verify worker classifications and pay rates match the applicable prevailing wage determination
  - Respond to certified payroll audits and requests for documentation
  - Track and reconcile fringe benefit contributions on union and prevailing-wage projects
  - Coordinate with project managers and HR on new-hire classifications and rate changes
