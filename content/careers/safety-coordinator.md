whatIs: >
  A Safety Coordinator handles the day-to-day, hands-on side of a
  construction company's safety program: running daily safety meetings
  and toolbox talks, conducting jobsite safety walks and inspections,
  maintaining safety documentation and training records, and helping
  new hires complete required orientation. It's a genuinely realistic
  entry point into a safety career, since it doesn't require the years
  of experience or advanced certifications a Safety Manager role
  typically expects, while still putting someone in the middle of real
  jobsite safety work, learning OSHA standards and field hazards
  firsthand before stepping into Safety Manager themselves.
whatTheyDo:
  - Run daily toolbox talks and jobsite safety meetings
  - Conduct routine jobsite safety walks and inspections, flagging hazards for correction
  - Maintain safety training records, orientation documentation, and certifications tracking
  - Help new hires complete required safety orientation before starting work
  - Assist with incident documentation and near-miss reporting
  - Support the Safety Manager in preparing for client, insurance, or regulatory safety audits
