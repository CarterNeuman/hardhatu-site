whatIs: >
  A GIS (Geographic Information Systems) Specialist manages and analyzes
  spatial data, turning survey data, drone-captured photogrammetry, and
  utility records into usable maps, site analyses, and asset databases. A
  Surveyor collects precise field measurements and a Drone/UAV Specialist
  captures aerial data; a GIS Specialist is often the person who takes
  that raw data and layers it with other spatial information (zoning,
  utilities, floodplains, existing infrastructure) to support planning,
  permitting, and asset management decisions.
whatTheyDo:
  - Build and maintain GIS databases of site, utility, and infrastructure data
  - Process survey and drone-captured spatial data into usable maps and models
  - Perform spatial analysis to support site selection, permitting, or asset management decisions
  - Create maps and visualizations for project teams, clients, and public agencies
  - Integrate GIS data with BIM and other project management systems
  - Maintain data accuracy and update GIS layers as a project or facility changes

education:

  notNecessary: A civil engineering or surveying license; GIS work itself isn't a licensed profession the way surveying or engineering is
