whatIs: >
  A Senior Project Manager leads a construction company's largest, most
  complex, or highest-risk projects, the ones with the tightest margins,
  the most demanding owners, or the most moving pieces. Where a Project
  Manager owns a project's budget, schedule, and paperwork day to day, a
  Senior Project Manager operates one level up: carrying the ultimate
  accountability for a project's bottom-line profitability, mentoring
  less experienced Project Managers, and often managing a portfolio of
  several projects or one very large one at the same time. It's a
  career destination this site's own Project Manager, Project Engineer,
  and Assistant Project Manager pages have all pointed toward from the
  start, naming it as the next step without this site ever building the
  page for it.
whatTheyDo:
  - Carry ultimate accountability for a project's or portfolio's budget, schedule, and profitability
  - Mentor and develop Project Managers and Project Engineers earlier in their careers
  - Manage the most demanding owner and design-team relationships on the company's largest pursuits
  - Step in to resolve the most serious disputes, delays, or scope conflicts a Project Manager can't settle alone
  - Review and approve major change orders, subcontractor buyouts, and cost forecasts across several projects
  - Represent the project team directly to company executives and ownership
