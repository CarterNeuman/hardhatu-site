id: career-owners-representative
title: "Owner's Representative"
category: "Specialized Construction"
tagline: Works for the owner and only the owner, brought in specifically because everyone else on a project (the GC, the architect, the CM) has their own interests to balance too.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  An Owner's Representative is hired directly by a project owner to protect
  the owner's interests throughout a project, often starting before design
  even begins and continuing through construction and closeout. The role
  exists because most owners aren't in the construction business full time
  and don't have the in-house expertise (or the time) to manage architects,
  contractors, and consultants effectively on their own, and because a
  general contractor or construction manager, however good, is ultimately
  managing their own contract and scope, not acting purely on the owner's
  behalf the way an Owner's Rep does.
whatTheyDo:
  - Help the owner select and negotiate contracts with architects, contractors, and consultants
  - Represent the owner in design and construction meetings, translating technical issues into decisions the owner can act on
  - Review and advise on change orders and pay applications from the owner's side of the table
  - Monitor budget and schedule against the owner's goals, independent of the contractor's own reporting
  - Coordinate move-in, occupancy, and closeout on the owner's behalf
  - Flag risks and conflicts of interest the owner might not otherwise catch
typicalDay:
  - time: "8:00 AM"
    activity: Review the contractor's latest schedule update against the owner's occupancy deadline
  - time: "9:30 AM"
    activity: Attend the weekly owner-architect-contractor (OAC) meeting on the owner's behalf
  - time: "11:30 AM"
    activity: Review a proposed change order for reasonableness before advising the owner on approval
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Walk the site independently to verify progress claims in the contractor's pay application
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Brief the owner directly on project status, risks, and upcoming decisions they need to make
whereTheyWork:
  - Jobsite
  - Office (owner's office and/or their own)
skills:
  - Contract negotiation and administration
  - Budget and schedule oversight
  - Clear communication with non-technical owners
  - Broad construction knowledge across design, cost, and field operations
  - Independent judgment (representing the owner's interest, not the contractor's)
software:
  - Procore
education:
  required: High school diploma or equivalent
  preferred: Bachelor's degree in construction management, architecture, or engineering, plus significant experience in project management or construction management
  helpful: Certified Construction Manager (CCM) credential, or a Project Management Professional (PMP) background
  notNecessary: A specific "owner's rep" credential, since this role is reached through years of PM, CM, or design experience rather than a dedicated entry path
progression:
  - Project Manager or Construction Manager
  - Owner's Representative
  - Senior Owner's Representative or Director of Real Estate/Facilities (for an owner organization)
hirerTypes:
  - Property Owners and Developers
  - Institutions (hospitals, universities, corporations) building or renovating their own facilities
  - Owner's Representative consulting firms
