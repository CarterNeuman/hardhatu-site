whatIs: >
  A Scheduling & Delay Consultant provides specialized schedule analysis
  for construction disputes, determining the cause, responsibility, and
  quantified impact of project delays. It overlaps with the broader
  Construction Claims Consultant role but focuses specifically on
  schedule and delay analysis rather than cost or defect claims generally,
  often the most technically demanding piece of a claim, since it
  requires reconstructing what actually happened on a project's critical
  path from potentially incomplete records.
whatTheyDo:
  - Analyze project schedules using recognized delay analysis methods (e.g. time impact analysis, as-planned vs. as-built)
  - Determine which party's actions or inactions actually caused a schedule delay
  - Quantify the time and cost impact of delays for use in claims or litigation
  - Prepare detailed schedule analysis reports and exhibits
  - Serve as an expert witness on delay-specific questions when a case goes to litigation
  - Advise clients on schedule risk and delay mitigation before disputes arise
