title: Value Engineering Consultant
category: "Preconstruction & Estimating"
tagline: Leads the structured process of finding ways to cut cost without cutting function, a specialized advisory role built around a concept most newcomers walk right past without realizing it's a distinct career.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Value Engineering Consultant leads formal value engineering studies,
  a structured process for finding ways to deliver a project's required
  function at lower cost, brought in independently of the design and
  construction team specifically so the analysis isn't biased by whoever
  already made the original decisions. It's a distinct skill from a Cost
  Engineer or Estimator's day-to-day cost management: value engineering
  is a facilitated, team-based methodology with its own certification
  track, typically used at key decision points on larger projects rather
  than as an ongoing part of running the job.
whatTheyDo:
  - Facilitate structured value engineering workshops with owners, designers, and contractors
  - Analyze a project's cost and function relationship to identify high-cost, low-value items
  - Develop and present alternative design or construction approaches that preserve required function at lower cost
  - Document value engineering proposals and their projected cost impact for owner decision-making
  - Track which proposals get implemented and their actual cost savings
  - Train project teams on the value methodology for use on future projects
typicalDay:
  - time: "8:00 AM"
    activity: Review project cost data and drawings to prepare for an upcoming value engineering workshop
  - time: "9:30 AM"
    activity: Facilitate a value engineering workshop with the owner's design and construction team
  - time: "1:00 PM"
    activity: Develop a cost comparison for an alternative structural system proposed in the workshop
  - time: "2:30 PM"
    activity: Present value engineering proposals and their projected savings to the project owner
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Document approved proposals for the design team to incorporate
whereTheyWork:
  - Office
  - On-site or virtual workshops with project teams
skills:
  - Facilitation and workshop leadership
  - Cost estimating and function analysis
  - Creative problem-solving under constraints
  - Clear, persuasive presentation to owners and design teams
  - Understanding of construction means, methods, and materials broadly across disciplines
software:
  - Bluebeam
  - Cost estimating software
education:
  required: Bachelor's degree in engineering, architecture, or construction management, plus several years of design or construction experience; no license is legally required for this role
  preferred: >
    SAVE International's Certified Value Specialist (CVS) credential,
    the organization's highest certification level, which requires
    completing SAVE's value methodology training and meeting the
    organization's experience and application requirements
  notNecessary: A cost estimating background specifically; value engineering draws on broader design and construction experience across many project types
progression:
  - Value Engineering Team Member
  - Value Engineering Consultant
  - Senior Value Engineering Consultant
  - Principal Consultant or Value Management Practice Lead
hirerTypes:

  - Construction Management Firms (as an in-house service)
  - Government Agencies (many public projects require formal VE studies)
