title: Preconstruction Manager
category: "Preconstruction & Estimating"
tagline: Runs everything that has to happen before a shovel hits the ground, estimating, scheduling, and design coordination pulled into one team's responsibility.
tier: free
whatIs: >
  A Preconstruction Manager leads a project's preconstruction phase,
  coordinating estimating, scheduling, constructability review, and early
  design input so a project is well-planned and biddable before
  construction starts. Rather than owning one piece of preconstruction
  directly, the role manages the team members (estimators, schedulers)
  who do, and represents preconstruction's interests in conversations with
  designers and owners.
whatTheyDo:
  - Lead the preconstruction team through conceptual, schematic, and final estimating phases
  - Coordinate constructability reviews, flagging design issues before they become expensive change orders
  - Develop the project's preliminary schedule alongside the scheduling team
  - Present preconstruction budgets, schedules, and risk assessments to ownership
  - Manage the transition from preconstruction to the construction team once a project is awarded
  - Build and maintain relationships with architects and owners during the pursuit and design phases

  - time: "9:30 AM"

  - time: "11:30 AM"
    activity: Present a preliminary budget and schedule to a prospective owner
  - time: "1:30 PM"
    activity: Coordinate with the scheduling team on sequencing options for a complex phased project
  - time: "3:30 PM"
    activity: Meet with the operations team to plan the handoff of an awarded project

  notNecessary: A specific preconstruction degree, since this role is almost always reached by being promoted up from Estimator or Senior Estimator
