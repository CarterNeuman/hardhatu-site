whatIs: >
  A Cost Engineer applies cost estimating, cost control, and forecasting
  methods across a project's full lifecycle, not just the initial bid an
  Estimator produces, but ongoing tracking of actual costs against budget,
  forecasting cost at completion, and analyzing the cost impact of changes
  as the project proceeds. The discipline is formalized by AACE
  International, which offers the field's dedicated Certified Cost
  Professional (CCP) credential.
whatTheyDo:
  - Develop and maintain detailed cost estimates and budgets across a project's phases
  - Track actual costs against budget and forecast cost at completion using methods like earned value management
  - Analyze the cost impact of design changes, schedule delays, and scope changes
  - Support risk analysis by quantifying the cost exposure of identified project risks
  - Benchmark project costs against historical data to improve future estimates
  - Report cost status and variance to project leadership and, on owner-side roles, to the owner directly
