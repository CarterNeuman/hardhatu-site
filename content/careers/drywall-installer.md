whatIs: >
  A Drywall Installer (hanger) and Finisher (taper) install gypsum board on
  walls and ceilings, then tape, mud, and sand the joints to create a
  smooth, paint-ready surface. Hanging and finishing are sometimes done by
  the same person and sometimes split into two specialties on larger crews.
  No government license is legally required to do this work.
whatTheyDo:
  - Measure, cut, and hang drywall panels on walls and ceilings per the framing layout
  - Install corner bead and other trim components at edges and openings
  - Apply joint tape and multiple coats of joint compound ("mud") over seams and fasteners
  - Sand finished joints smooth and check the wall under raking light for imperfections
  - Coordinate with framers, electricians, and plumbers to make sure rough-in work is complete before hanging
  - Repair damaged drywall on renovation and remodel projects
