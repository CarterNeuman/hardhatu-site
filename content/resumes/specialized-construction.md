tagline: "These roles are hired almost entirely on the specific license or certification tied to the specialty, so naming it clearly matters more than general trade-resume language."
sections:
  - heading: "What recruiters actually look for"
    content: >
      The exact credential the specialty requires: OSHA's 40-hour
      HAZWOPER for hazardous waste or remediation work, an NCCCO
      operator certification for pile driving, an elevator mechanic
      license, whatever the specific role legally or practically
      requires. Because these specialties are narrow, a resume that
      names the credential precisely screens in immediately, while one
      that only says "specialized construction experience" screens out
      just as fast. Hands-on experience with the specific systems,
      hazards, or equipment the specialty involves.
  - heading: "How to structure it"
    content: >
      Put the specialty credential in the first line, not the last. If
      you hold more than one relevant certification, list all of them,
      since these roles often stack requirements (HAZWOPER plus OSHA 10,
      for example). Describe your experience in terms of the specific
      hazard, system, or equipment involved rather than generic
      construction language. One page is usually enough; the credential
      is doing most of the work.
  - heading: "What to leave off"
    content: >
      Generic trade-resume phrasing that never actually names the
      specialty credential, forcing the reviewer to guess whether you
      have it. Padding with unrelated general-construction experience
      when the specialty itself is what's being hired for. And never
      imply a credential is current when it's lapsed, these specialties
      often involve real hazard exposure, and a lapsed certification is
      a real liability, not a technicality.
exampleBullets:
  - "OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER certified, 3 years of lead and asbestos abatement experience"
  - "NCCCO Dedicated Pile Driver Operator certified"
  - "Managed post-loss restoration for 40+ residential and commercial water damage claims"
  - "Coordinated historic preservation compliance on 2 state-registered landmark renovations"
