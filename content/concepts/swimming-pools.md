---
id: concept-swimming-pools
title: Swimming Pools
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "13, Special Construction"
definition: >
  Constructed water recreation structures, gunite/shotcrete, poured concrete,
  or vinyl-liner, requiring specialized waterproofing, structural design for
  soil and water pressure, and dedicated filtration and chemical treatment
  systems that fall outside standard plumbing scope.
whyItMatters: >
  A pool shell that isn't waterproofed and structurally designed for the
  specific soil and groundwater conditions on site can crack or heave, and
  pool filtration and chemical systems have their own code requirements
  (barriers, drain covers, circulation rates) separate from a building's
  general plumbing code.
example: >
  A contractor building a commercial pool coordinates the gunite shell's
  reinforcement and waterproofing with a geotechnical report on the site's
  soil and groundwater conditions, rather than using a standard shell design
  regardless of site conditions.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-plumber|Plumber]] specializing in pool systems handles
  circulation and chemical feed, while the
  [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates the shell construction
  with the structural engineer's design for site-specific conditions.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A pool shell is built to a standard design without accounting for the site's
  high water table, and hydrostatic pressure beneath the empty shell causes it
  to shift before the pool is ever filled.
relatedIds:
  - career-plumber
  - career-superintendent
