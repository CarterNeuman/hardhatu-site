id: concept-asbestos-om-plan
title: Asbestos Operations & Maintenance (O&M) Plan
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  A written program for managing asbestos-containing material left in place in
  an existing building rather than removed, covering periodic condition
  inspections, worker notification and training, and specific work practices
  for any maintenance activity that might disturb the material.
whyItMatters: >
  Not every building with asbestos needs full abatement right away; an O&M plan
  is often the more practical and far less disruptive path for material that's
  currently intact and undisturbed, as long as the building owner commits to
  the ongoing monitoring and controlled work practices the plan requires.
example: >
  A building owner chooses to manage intact asbestos floor tile in place under a
  documented O&M plan rather than abating it immediately, scheduling periodic
  condition inspections and training maintenance staff on safe work practices
  anytime they need to drill or cut near that material.
careerAngle: >
  Facility managers and renovation contractors should check whether a building
  has an existing O&M plan before any work begins, since drilling or cutting
  into managed-in-place asbestos material without following the plan's
  specific work practices can turn a routine repair into an abatement-level
  incident.
whatGoesWrong: >
  People sometimes assume any asbestos found in a building has to be abated
  immediately, when managing undisturbed material in place under a properly
  maintained O&M plan is an accepted, far less disruptive regulatory path as
  long as the material stays intact and undisturbed.
