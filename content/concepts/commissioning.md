---
id: concept-commissioning
title: Commissioning
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The process of testing a building's systems, such as HVAC, electrical, fire alarm, and
  plumbing, after installation to confirm they actually work, and work together, the way they were
  designed to before the owner takes over.
whyItMatters: >
  A building can look completely finished and still have systems that don't actually
  perform right together, an HVAC system that was never tested under real load, for
  instance. Commissioning is what catches that before the owner is the one discovering
  it.
example: >
  A commissioning agent runs a new building's HVAC system through a full test cycle,
  checking that each zone actually reaches its set temperature and that the fire alarm
  properly shuts down the air handlers during a test alarm.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]] is often closely involved
  in coordinating commissioning, since it's the last real quality check before an owner
  takes over. A [[career-hvac-technician|HVAC Technician]] usually does the hands-on
  testing and adjusting during the process itself.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building passes inspection and opens on schedule, but a step gets skipped to save a
  few days: the fire alarm system is never actually tested to confirm it shuts down the
  HVAC system during an alarm. Months later, during a real fire drill, the HVAC keeps
  running and pushes smoke through the ductwork instead of containing it, turning a
  documentation shortcut into a life-safety failure.
