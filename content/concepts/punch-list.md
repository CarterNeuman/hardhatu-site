id: concept-punch-list
title: Punch List
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The list of small, mostly cosmetic items, such as a scuff on a wall, a door that doesn't latch
  right, or a light fixture with the wrong trim, identified near the end of a project that
  still need to be fixed before the owner considers the work fully complete.
whyItMatters: >
  It's the last real checkpoint before everyone walks away. An owner who's still finding
  half-finished details months later has little leverage left to get them fixed: the
  punch list is what turns "basically done" into something both sides actually sign off
  on.
example: >
  A superintendent walks a finished office space with the owner's representative, and
  together they note fourteen items, mostly paint touch-ups and a sticking door, each
  one assigned to a specific subcontractor with a deadline to fix it.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] runs the actual punch walk and chases
  subcontractors to close out their items. A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]]
  cares because [[concept-closeout|closeout]] and final payment usually don't happen
  until the punch list is signed off as complete.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor tells the owner the punch list is "basically done" and requests final
  payment, but three items, including a fire door that doesn't latch properly, are
  still open. The owner withholds a chunk of the final payment until every item is
  actually verified closed, souring the relationship right at the moment it should be
  ending on good terms.
