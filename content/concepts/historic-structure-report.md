---
id: concept-historic-structure-report
title: Historic Structure Report (HSR)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "02, Existing Conditions"
definition: >
  A documented study of a historic building's construction history, existing
  physical condition, and significance, used to guide preservation,
  restoration, or adaptive-reuse decisions before any work begins.
whyItMatters: >
  Working on a historic building without first understanding what's original,
  what's been altered, and what materials and methods were used risks
  destroying the very features that make the building historically
  significant, and many preservation tax credits and grants require an HSR as
  a condition of funding.
example: >
  Before restoring a 1890s courthouse, a preservation team produces an HSR
  documenting the building's original materials, later alterations, and
  condition issues, which then guides which elements get restored to their
  original appearance versus repaired in place.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-historic-preservation-specialist|Historic Preservation
  Specialist]] typically leads or commissions the HSR, and an
  [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] uses its findings to
  scope the restoration budget realistically.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A renovation proceeds without an HSR, and a later-stage discovery that
  original plaster and trim were more extensive than assumed forces a
  mid-project scope change, blowing past the budget that was set without that
  information.
relatedIds:
  - career-historic-preservation-specialist
  - career-owners-representative
