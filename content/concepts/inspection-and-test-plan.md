id: concept-inspection-and-test-plan
title: Inspection and Test Plan (ITP)
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A document, prepared before work begins on a specific scope item, listing
  every inspection and test required for that work, who performs each one,
  what standard it's checked against, and which checks require sign-off before
  the work can proceed to the next step.
whyItMatters: >
  Without an ITP spelling out exactly which checks are required and in what
  order, a crew can cover up work, like backfilling a trench, before a
  required inspection actually happens, and uncovering already-completed work
  to inspect it after the fact is far more disruptive and costly than
  inspecting it before it's covered.
example: >
  A structural concrete pour has an ITP specifying that rebar placement must be
  inspected and signed off before any concrete is placed, and that cylinder
  samples must be taken and tested at specific ages afterward. The crew knows
  not to pour until the rebar inspection sign-off is in hand, since the ITP
  makes that sequence a hard requirement rather than a suggestion.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] performs the inspections and
  tests the ITP specifies and documents each sign-off, and the
  [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]] develops the ITP
  itself for each major scope item before work begins.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A crew proceeds with the next step of work without confirming the ITP's
  required inspection sign-off actually happened first, and the project
  discovers the gap only after work that should have been inspected is already
  covered up and far more difficult to verify.
