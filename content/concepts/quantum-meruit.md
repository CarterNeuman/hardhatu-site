id: concept-quantum-meruit
title: Quantum Meruit
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A legal doctrine letting a contractor recover the reasonable value of work
  performed when no enforceable contract covers it, or when the contract
  itself is found invalid, rather than leaving the contractor with nothing.
whyItMatters: >
  It becomes the fallback remedy when a written contract is missing, unsigned,
  or unenforceable for some defect, but work was still performed and the other
  party benefited from it, since the law will not let someone keep that
  benefit for free.
example: >
  A subcontractor begins mobilizing and ordering material off a verbal agreement
  and a few emails while the formal subcontract is still being negotiated. The
  deal falls apart before anyone signs, and the subcontractor sues in quantum
  meruit to recover the value of the work already put in place.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-construction-attorney|Construction Attorney]] evaluates whether a
  quantum meruit claim is available when a [[concept-contract|contract]]
  dispute leaves no enforceable agreement in place, and the
  [[career-construction-claims-consultant|Construction Claims Consultant]]
  helps document and value the work performed to support the claim.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A party assumes that without a signed contract there's no way to get paid for
  work already done, and either walks away from legitimate value owed or
  underprices a quantum meruit claim by failing to document the work.
