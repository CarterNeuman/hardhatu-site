id: concept-architectural-woodwork
title: Architectural Woodwork
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "06, Wood, Plastics & Composites"
definition: >
  High-end custom millwork and finish carpentry, paneling, wood ceilings,
  custom trim, and feature woodwork, fabricated to a specific grade and quality
  standard, most commonly the Architectural Woodwork Institute's AWI standards,
  distinct from standard [[concept-casework|casework]] since architectural
  woodwork covers finish and trim work rather than cabinetry.
whyItMatters: >
  The AWI grade specified, economy, custom, or premium, sets real, enforceable
  tolerances for things like grain matching and joint gaps, so a contract that
  doesn't clearly specify the grade leaves real room for a dispute over whether
  delivered woodwork actually meets what the owner expected.
example: >
  A lobby's custom wood paneling is fabricated to AWI premium grade, with the
  specification explicitly calling for book-matched grain patterns across
  adjacent panels, a level of detail economy grade wouldn't guarantee.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-carpenter|Carpenter]] installs architectural woodwork fabricated
  off site by a specialty millwork shop, working from approved shop drawings.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contract specifies architectural woodwork without calling out an AWI grade
  at all. The installed panels have visible, unmatched grain seams the owner
  considers unacceptable, but without a specified grade in the contract, the
  woodwork shop has grounds to argue the work meets a lower standard than the
  owner assumed.
