---
id: concept-spcc-plan
title: Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) Plan
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  A written EPA-required plan, triggered once a facility's aggregate aboveground
  oil storage exceeds 1,320 gallons, detailing how the site prevents oil
  discharges into navigable waters, including secondary containment,
  inspection schedules, and spill response procedures, separate from the
  stormwater-focused SWPPP.
whyItMatters: >
  A jobsite running fuel tanks for generators and equipment can cross the
  1,320-gallon threshold without anyone really noticing, since the trigger
  counts aggregate storage capacity across every container on site, not just
  one big obvious tank.
example: >
  A jobsite running several portable fuel tanks for generators and heavy
  equipment totals over 1,320 gallons of aggregate aboveground storage
  capacity, triggering the requirement for a written SPCC Plan even though no
  single tank on site looks large enough to draw attention on its own.
careerAngle: >
  Project managers running fuel-heavy jobsites should total up every container's
  capacity across the whole site, not just the obviously large ones, since the
  SPCC threshold is based on the aggregate, not any single tank.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Contractors sometimes confuse this plan with a project's stormwater pollution
  prevention plan, when the SPCC Plan specifically addresses oil discharge
  prevention under a separate EPA regulation, with its own distinct trigger
  threshold and its own separate written plan.
