---
id: concept-audiovisual-systems
title: Audiovisual Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "27, Communications"
definition: >
  Built-in projection, display, and conference-room video/audio equipment,
  along with the structured cabling and power that feed it, planned early
  enough that the right conduit and blocking get built into walls and ceilings
  before they're closed up.
whyItMatters: >
  Retrofitting a projector mount, a recessed screen, or AV cabling into a
  finished ceiling or wall is far more disruptive than roughing it in during
  construction, so AV design has to be locked in before drywall rather than
  treated as a furniture-style addition after occupancy.
example: >
  A contractor building a corporate conference room installs blocking in the
  ceiling for a motorized projector screen and runs the AV cabling conduit
  before the ceiling grid goes in, coordinating exact locations with the AV
  integrator's layout.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] runs AV power and low-voltage cabling,
  and the [[career-construction-technology-manager|Construction Technology
  Manager]] coordinates the AV integrator's rough-in requirements with the
  rest of the MEP trades.
whatGoesWrong: >
  AV blocking and conduit are skipped because the AV integrator hasn't been
  selected yet when the ceiling closes in, and the eventual projector mount
  has to be surface-run with exposed conduit instead of the clean, concealed
  installation that was planned.
relatedIds:
  - career-electrician
  - career-construction-technology-manager
