id: concept-monument-site-signage
title: Monument & Site Signage
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "32, Exterior Improvements"
definition: >
  Freestanding exterior signs, typically a low masonry or metal monument
  structure displaying a building or development's name, distinct from
  building-mounted signage and interior wayfinding, requiring its own
  foundation and often its own permit separate from the building permit.
whyItMatters: >
  Monument signs usually require a separate sign permit from the local
  jurisdiction, with its own setback and size restrictions, so the sign design
  can't simply follow the architect's building design without checking the
  jurisdiction's specific sign code.
example: >
  A contractor building a new retail center's monument sign confirms the
  sign's height and setback from the street against the jurisdiction's sign
  code before the foundation is poured, since sign code limits are often
  stricter than the building's own zoning setbacks.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] tracks the separate sign
  permit on the project schedule, apart from the main building permit.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A monument sign's design is finalized based on the architect's rendering
  alone, and the permit application is rejected because the sign's height
  exceeds the jurisdiction's sign code limit, forcing a redesign after the
  foundation was already scheduled to be poured.
relatedIds:
