id: concept-crane-lift-plan
title: Crane Lift Plan
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  A documented plan for a crane lift specifying the load weight, [[concept-rigging|rigging]]
  configuration, the crane's rated capacity at the lift's actual radius, ground
  conditions, and the exclusion zone around the lift, required for critical or complex
  lifts where the margin for error is small.
whyItMatters: >
  A crane's lifting capacity isn't a single number; it changes with boom angle, radius,
  and ground conditions, and a lift that looks routine can exceed the crane's actual
  rated capacity at that specific configuration. A documented lift plan forces those
  numbers to be checked in advance rather than estimated in the moment.
example: >
  Before lifting a heavy precast panel, the crew calculates the crane's rated capacity at
  the specific radius the lift requires, confirms it exceeds the load weight with an
  adequate margin, and marks an exclusion zone so no one is working underneath the load
  path.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-equipment-operator|Equipment Operator]] operating the crane follows the
  lift plan's specified configuration exactly, since deviating from it, a different
  angle, a different radius, can put the lift outside the crane's verified capacity. A
  [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] reviews and approves lift plans for
  higher-risk or critical lifts before they happen.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A crew proceeds with a lift at a slightly wider radius than the approved lift plan
  specified, assuming the difference is negligible. The wider radius reduces the crane's
  actual rated capacity below the load's weight, a mismatch the original lift plan's
  calculations would have caught immediately.
