id: concept-prompt-payment-act
title: Prompt Payment Act
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  Federal and state laws requiring payment to flow down the contracting chain
  within specific deadlines, from owner to general contractor after invoice
  approval, and from the GC to subcontractors after the GC itself gets paid,
  with interest penalties attached for late payment.
whyItMatters: >
  The penalty for missing the deadline is automatic interest, not something a
  subcontractor has to prove damages for, which makes a prompt payment claim one
  of the more straightforward remedies available when a GC sits on payment too
  long.
example: >
  A general contractor receives payment from the owner and, under the
  applicable prompt payment act, has a set number of days to pay its
  subcontractors their share before interest penalties start accruing
  automatically.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-construction-accountant|Construction Accountant]] tracks payment
  deadlines against the prompt payment act's requirements, while a
  [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] manages the
  underlying subcontract terms those deadlines apply to.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A GC receives payment from the owner but sits on it for six weeks before
  paying a subcontractor, well past the prompt payment act's deadline. The
  subcontractor invokes the statute's interest penalty, a cost the GC could have
  avoided entirely just by paying on time.
