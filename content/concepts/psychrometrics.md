---
id: concept-psychrometrics
title: Psychrometrics
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The science of how air holds moisture at different temperatures, the technical
  basis restoration technicians use to actually plan and monitor a structural
  drying job, rather than just running fans and dehumidifiers and hoping for the
  best.
whyItMatters: >
  Advanced restoration credentials like IICRC's Applied Structural Drying (ASD)
  teach this directly, since a drying plan built on real psychrometric
  readings, temperature, relative humidity, and grains per pound of moisture, catches
  hidden moisture that a purely visual inspection misses.
example: >
  A technician tracks relative humidity and grains per pound at set intervals
  throughout a drying job, using the readings to confirm the structure is actually
  approaching a dry standard rather than just assuming it is because the carpet
  feels dry to the touch.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-restoration-project-manager|Restoration Project Manager]] relies on
  psychrometric readings to decide when a job is genuinely finished and walls can be
  safely closed back up, rather than guessing based on how the space looks or feels.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A drying technician runs equipment on a job without tracking psychrometric readings
  at set intervals, so nobody can prove the structure actually reached a dry standard
  before the walls go back up. Hidden moisture left behind grows mold weeks later,
  reopening a claim that should have been closed clean the first time.
