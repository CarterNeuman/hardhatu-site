id: concept-parking-control-equipment
title: Parking Control Equipment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Automated gates, ticket dispensers, payment kiosks, and access-control
  barriers installed at parking garage and lot entries, tied into electrical,
  low-voltage, and often the building's broader access-control system rather
  than installed as a standalone system.
whyItMatters: >
  Parking control equipment sits at the intersection of several trades,
  electrical power, low-voltage data and card-reader wiring, and sometimes the
  fire department's required emergency gate-arm release, so coordination gaps
  here tend to surface as the equipment that doesn't work on day one.
example: >
  A contractor installing gate arms at a new parking garage coordinates the
  required fire department knox-box gate release with the life-safety system
  early, rather than treating the gate as a standalone electrical
  installation.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] runs power and low-voltage wiring to
  each gate and kiosk, and the
  [[career-construction-technology-manager|Construction Technology Manager]]
  integrates the system with the building's access-control platform.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Parking gates are installed and powered on schedule, but the required fire
  department override wasn't coordinated with the life-safety contractor, and
  the gates fail fire marshal inspection days before the garage was set to
  open.
relatedIds:
  - career-electrician
  - career-construction-technology-manager
