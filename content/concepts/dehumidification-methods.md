id: concept-dehumidification-methods
title: Dehumidification Methods (Refrigerant vs. Desiccant)
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The two main categories of equipment used to pull moisture out of the air
  during structural drying: refrigerant dehumidifiers, which cool air to
  condense out water and work best in warmer conditions, and desiccant
  dehumidifiers, which use a moisture-absorbing material and perform better in
  cold or low-humidity environments where refrigerant units lose efficiency.
whyItMatters: >
  Choosing the wrong type for the job's temperature and humidity conditions
  slows drying time significantly, which extends the claim, the equipment
  rental, and the time before the structure can be rebuilt.
example: >
  A water-damage crew working a flooded basement in winter switches from a
  standard refrigerant dehumidifier, which struggles below about 65 degrees,
  to a desiccant unit that keeps pulling moisture efficiently even in the cold
  space.
careerAngle: >
  Drying technicians need to match equipment type to jobsite conditions, since
  using the wrong dehumidifier type can stretch a drying job from days into
  weeks and inflate the equipment-rental line of the claim.
whatGoesWrong: >
  People sometimes think more dehumidifiers always dries faster, when the bigger
  factor is often choosing the right technology for the ambient temperature
  and pairing it with adequate air movement.
