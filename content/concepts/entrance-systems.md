id: concept-entrance-systems
title: Entrance Systems (Automatic Doors)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "08, Openings"
definition: >
  Automatic and manual entrance door systems, sliding, swinging, and revolving
  doors with their operators and safety sensors, engineered and installed to a
  specific performance and safety standard, ANSI/BHMA A156.10 for automatic
  doors, governing sensor coverage and force limits, distinct from standard
  [[concept-door-hardware|door hardware]] since these are complete,
  code-regulated assemblies.
whyItMatters: >
  An automatic entrance door's safety sensors have to be field-tested and
  adjusted after installation to confirm they actually detect a person in the
  door's path and stop or reverse, since a misadjusted sensor turns a
  convenience feature into a genuine pinch or impact hazard.
example: >
  An installer commissions an automatic sliding entrance door by testing its
  presence sensors at multiple points across the opening, confirming the door
  reverses reliably before it can strike someone crossing through at a normal
  walking pace.
careerAngle: >
  A specialty door installer sets and commissions automatic entrance systems.
  A [[career-commissioning-agent|Commissioning Agent]] may independently
  verify sensor performance as part of overall building commissioning.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An installer sets an automatic entrance door's sensor sensitivity too low to
  reduce nuisance triggering from nearby foot traffic outside the doorway. The
  reduced sensitivity means the door doesn't reliably detect someone moving
  through it at a normal pace, and it strikes a visitor before the installer is
  called back to properly recalibrate it.
