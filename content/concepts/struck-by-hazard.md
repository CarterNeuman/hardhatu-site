id: concept-struck-by-hazard
title: Struck-By Hazard
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  Being hit by a moving vehicle, piece of equipment, falling object, or flying
  material, one of OSHA's "Fatal Four" hazard categories that account for most
  construction fatalities, alongside falls, electrocutions, and caught-in or
  caught-between hazards.
whyItMatters: >
  Struck-by incidents happen in ways a new worker doesn't always anticipate: a
  swinging excavator counterweight, material falling from a floor above, a
  reversing dump truck. Recognizing the specific situations where struck-by
  risk is highest is a core part of basic jobsite awareness, which is exactly
  why it anchors entry-level safety orientation.
example: >
  A laborer working near an active excavation stays clear of the excavator's
  swing radius and the path of trucks backing up to load, rather than assuming
  a hard hat alone is enough protection from moving equipment.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] designs site traffic patterns and
  spotter procedures specifically to reduce struck-by risk around moving
  equipment.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A worker walks through an active equipment zone without making eye contact
  with the operator first, assuming the operator has already seen them. The
  operator, focused on a different task, doesn't notice the worker step into
  the machine's path until it's too late to stop.
