id: concept-telecommunications-room-infrastructure
title: Telecommunications Room (TR) Infrastructure
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "27, Communications"
definition: >
  Dedicated rooms housing a building's network switches, patch panels, and
  cabling terminations, sometimes called an IDF or telecom closet, sized and
  climate-controlled according to the number of cable drops and equipment they
  serve, distinct from a full data center.
whyItMatters: >
  A telecom room's size, power, and cooling needs scale directly with the
  number of data drops in the building, so sizing it based on the current
  floor plan rather than anticipated future growth is a common reason
  buildings run out of room in these spaces within a few years.
example: >
  A contractor building a new office floor sizes the telecom room's cooling
  and electrical capacity for twice the current cable count, anticipating
  future tenant buildouts that will add more data drops without expanding the
  room itself.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] sizes the room's dedicated power and
  cooling, and the [[career-electrician|Electrician]] installs it alongside
  the main electrical rough-in.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A telecom room is sized exactly to the current floor plan's cable count with
  no growth margin, and a tenant buildout two years later can't fit its
  additional network equipment into the room without expensive expansion.
relatedIds:
  - career-mep-engineer
  - career-electrician
