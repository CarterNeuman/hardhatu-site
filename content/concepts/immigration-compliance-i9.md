id: concept-immigration-compliance-i9
title: Immigration Compliance / I-9 Verification
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  The requirement that every U.S. employer complete and retain a Form I-9 for
  each new hire, documenting the employee's identity and authorization to
  work, generally within three business days of the employee's start date,
  distinct from E-Verify, the separate electronic system only some contractors
  are additionally required to use.
whyItMatters: >
  Every employer in the country has to do I-9 verification regardless of whether
  E-Verify applies to them, so a company that assumes it's covered because
  it's not an E-Verify employer can still be badly out of compliance on the
  universal I-9 requirement itself.
example: >
  A construction company completes Form I-9 for every new hire within the
  required window and retains the forms in a separate, secure file, even
  though the company isn't currently an E-Verify employer, since the I-9
  requirement itself applies regardless of E-Verify status.
careerAngle: >
  HR staff on construction projects should treat I-9 completion as a strict,
  universal deadline-driven task separate from any E-Verify obligation the
  company might also have on a given contract.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Employers sometimes think E-Verify replaces or satisfies the I-9 requirement,
  when I-9 completion is the universal baseline for every employer and
  E-Verify is only an additional electronic check that some, not all,
  employers are also required to run.
