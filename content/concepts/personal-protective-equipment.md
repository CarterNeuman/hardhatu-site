id: concept-personal-protective-equipment
title: Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  Gear worn to reduce a worker's exposure to a jobsite hazard, such as hard hats, safety
  glasses, gloves, hearing protection, respirators, and fall-arrest harnesses among the
  most common, selected based on the specific hazards a task actually presents rather
  than a single standard kit for every job.
whyItMatters: >
  PPE is deliberately the last line of defense in OSHA's hierarchy of controls, behind
  eliminating or engineering out the hazard entirely, but for a lot of construction work,
  it's also the most immediate, practical protection a worker has on any given day, which
  is why getting the right PPE for the actual task matters as much as wearing PPE at all.
example: >
  A crew grinding concrete switches from a standard dust mask to a proper respirator
  rated for [[concept-silica-exposure-standard|respirable silica]] once the task involves
  extended cutting, since the hazard changes meaningfully with the specific work being
  done.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-laborer|Construction Laborer]] wears PPE across a wide range of tasks and
  has to know which gear actually matches the hazard in front of them at any given
  moment. A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] sets the jobsite's PPE requirements
  by task and verifies compliance during site walks.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A worker wears standard safety glasses for a grinding task that actually calls for a
  full face shield, since the eye protection they have on hand is close enough in their
  own judgment. A metal fragment causes an injury a properly matched face shield would
  have prevented.
