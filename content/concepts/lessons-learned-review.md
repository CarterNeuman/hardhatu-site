---
id: concept-lessons-learned-review
title: Lessons Learned Review
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A structured project-closeout discussion capturing what went well, what
  didn't, and what the team would do differently next time, documented for
  use on future projects rather than left as informal institutional memory
  that leaves with whoever happened to be on the job.
whyItMatters: >
  Institutional memory that only lives in people's heads walks out the door
  the moment those people move to a different project or a different company,
  documenting it is what actually lets the next team benefit from what this
  one learned.
example: >
  A project team holds a lessons learned session at closeout and documents
  that a specific MEP coordination conflict recurred throughout the job, a
  finding the next project's team can reference before their own coordination
  process starts.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] facilitates the lessons
  learned review at closeout, turning the team's real experience into a
  written record the company can actually reuse.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project team spends weeks untangling a recurring MEP coordination
  conflict but never holds a formal lessons learned review at closeout to
  document how it kept happening. The same avoidable coordination conflict
  recurs on the company's next similar project, costing another few weeks,
  since nothing was ever documented for the next team to learn from.
relatedIds:
