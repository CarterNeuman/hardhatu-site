---
id: concept-med-arb
title: Med-Arb (Mediation-Arbitration)
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A hybrid dispute resolution process that starts as mediation, with a neutral
  helping the parties negotiate toward a voluntary settlement, and then
  automatically converts to binding arbitration on any issue the mediation
  doesn't resolve, often using the same neutral in both roles.
whyItMatters: >
  It's designed to guarantee a final resolution either way, since any issue left
  unsettled after the mediation phase simply moves into arbitration rather
  than remaining unresolved, but having the same person serve as both mediator
  and arbitrator raises its own concerns about what one side shared privately
  during mediation influencing the later arbitration decision.
example: >
  Two parties in a payment dispute agree to med-arb. Mediation resolves most of
  the disputed change orders, but the parties remain at an impasse on one
  large item. Rather than starting an entirely new arbitration process from
  scratch, that single remaining item automatically converts to binding
  arbitration under the same agreement.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-construction-attorney|Construction Attorney]] advises clients on
  whether to agree to a combined neutral for med-arb or require separate
  mediators and arbitrators, and the [[career-construction-claims-consultant|
  Construction Claims Consultant]] prepares the technical position for
  whichever phase the dispute ultimately reaches.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Parties agree to med-arb without considering that candid settlement positions
  shared during the mediation phase could color the same neutral's judgment if
  the dispute converts to binding arbitration, a concern a separate-neutral
  structure would have avoided entirely.
