id: concept-material-freight-lifts
title: Material & Freight Lifts
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "14, Conveying Equipment"
definition: >
  A lifting platform or car designed to move materials, equipment, or goods
  between floors, sized and rated for cargo rather than passengers, regulated
  under a different set of standards than passenger elevators and typically
  installed in warehouses, loading areas, and industrial facilities.
whyItMatters: >
  A material lift's rated capacity and the way it's operated, often requiring
  an attendant or specific controls, differ meaningfully from a passenger
  elevator, and using one interchangeably with the other, letting people ride a
  freight-only lift not rated or configured for passenger use, is both a code
  violation and a real safety hazard.
example: >
  A distribution center installs a freight elevator rated for a specific heavy
  load and posts clear signage restricting it to material handling, distinct
  from the building's separate passenger elevators.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-elevator-technician|Elevator & Escalator Technician]] installs
  and maintains material and freight lifts, a related but distinct scope from
  passenger elevator work.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Warehouse staff begin riding a freight-only lift instead of walking the
  stairs, since it's more convenient, even though the lift isn't configured or
  rated for regular passenger use. A safety audit catches the practice and
  requires the facility to formally restrict access and post proper signage
  before the lift can continue operating that way.
