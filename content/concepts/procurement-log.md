---
id: concept-procurement-log
title: Procurement Log
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A tracking document listing every material and equipment item a project
  needs to buy, its lead time, order date, expected delivery, and current
  status, used to keep [[concept-long-lead-item|long-lead items]] from
  slipping through the cracks against the schedule.
whyItMatters: >
  A procurement log's real value is catching a missed order early, not just
  recording that one happened, once a long-lead item's delivery date arrives
  with nothing actually ordered, there's no longer enough lead time left to fix
  it.
example: >
  A project's procurement log flags that a long-lead switchgear order hasn't
  been placed yet, two weeks before it needed to be, giving the team time to
  push it through before that delay could reach the schedule.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-document-control-specialist|Document Control Specialist]]
  maintains the procurement log's day-to-day accuracy, while a
  [[career-procurement-manager|Procurement Manager]] acts on what it flags.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A critical piece of long-lead equipment falls off the procurement log's
  active tracking during a personnel transition, and its order never actually
  gets placed on schedule. The gap isn't discovered until the equipment's
  delivery date, months later, arrives with nothing actually ordered.
