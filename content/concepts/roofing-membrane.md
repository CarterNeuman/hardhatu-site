id: concept-roofing-membrane
title: Roofing Membrane
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "07, Thermal & Moisture Protection"
definition: >
  The waterproof material, such as a single-ply membrane (TPO, EPDM), built-up
  roofing, or modified bitumen, installed across a roof's surface to keep water out
  of the building, typically installed over insulation and a cover board on a
  low-slope commercial roof.
whyItMatters: >
  A roofing membrane is one of the most consequential water barriers on a building,
  since a failure anywhere in it can allow water to enter and damage the structure,
  insulation, and interior finishes below, often silently, since roof leaks aren't
  always immediately visible from inside.
example: >
  A commercial building's low-slope roof is covered with a fully adhered TPO
  membrane, with all seams heat-welded and every roof penetration, such as vents,
  drains, and equipment curbs, carefully flashed and sealed according to the
  manufacturer's installation requirements.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-roofer|Roofer]] installs the membrane and every seam and penetration
  detail, since the membrane's water-tightness depends entirely on those details being
  done correctly, not just the field area. A
  [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]] often requires a roofing
  manufacturer's own representative to inspect the installation before issuing a
  warranty, since manufacturers won't warrant work they haven't verified.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A roofing crew heat-welds the membrane seams too quickly to keep up with a tight
  schedule, and a section doesn't achieve a full, continuous weld. The gap isn't
  visible during a casual walk-through, and water finds its way through that weak seam
  during the first significant storm after the roof is otherwise considered complete.
