id: concept-rugs-floor-mats
title: Rugs & Floor Mats
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Area rugs and entrance floor mats, including recessed walk-off mat wells
  built into the entry flooring to trap dirt and moisture before it tracks
  further into the building, specified for both appearance and slip resistance
  at building entrances.
whyItMatters: >
  A recessed mat well has to be coordinated with the floor slab and finish
  flooring during construction, since adding one after the floor is poured
  means cutting into finished work rather than forming it in from the start.
example: >
  An architect specifies a recessed entrance mat well at a building's main
  entry, and the concrete contractor forms the recess into the slab before the
  pour rather than trying to retrofit it into finished flooring later.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] selects ongoing mat
  maintenance and replacement schedules, while the
  [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates the recessed well's
  formwork with the concrete schedule.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building's entrance is finished without a planned mat well, and the owner
  later has to add a surface-mounted mat that creates a trip hazard at the
  threshold rather than the flush, recessed solution that should have been
  built in.
relatedIds:
  - career-facilities-manager
