id: concept-lien-waiver
title: Lien Waiver
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A document a contractor, subcontractor, or supplier signs giving up their right to
  file a [[concept-mechanics-lien|mechanic's lien]] against a property for a specific
  amount of work, usually exchanged for a payment, and often required before that
  payment is released at all.
whyItMatters: >
  An owner paying for work wants proof that the money won't be followed by a lien
  anyway, whether from the party they paid directly or from someone further down the
  chain who never got their share. Lien waivers are what turn a payment into a clean,
  final transaction instead of an open risk.
example: >
  Before releasing a subcontractor's monthly payment, a general contractor requires a
  signed conditional waiver, valid only once the check actually clears, along with
  waivers from that subcontractor's own material suppliers, so nobody down the chain can
  still file a lien for the same work.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] collects and tracks lien
  waivers as a routine part of processing every payment, since a missing waiver can hold
  up funding on some projects. A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] relies on a
  complete set of final waivers at [[concept-closeout|closeout]] to confirm the project
  is actually free of lien risk before releasing the last payment.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor accepts an unconditional lien waiver from a subcontractor before
  the subcontractor's payment check has actually cleared. The check bounces, but the
  subcontractor has already legally waived their lien rights for that amount, leaving
  them without their strongest tool to recover the unpaid money, a mistake a conditional
  waiver would have prevented.
