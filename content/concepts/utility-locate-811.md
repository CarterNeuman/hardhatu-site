id: concept-utility-locate-811
title: Utility Locate (811 / One-Call)
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The legally required process of contacting the national 811 call-before-
  you-dig system before any excavation, triggering utility owners to mark the
  approximate location of their buried lines with color-coded paint or flags
  within a required notice period that varies by state, typically a few
  business days.
whyItMatters: >
  This is the notification and marking step itself, required by law before
  excavation begins; it's a separate step from
  [[concept-utility-locating-potholing|potholing]], which physically confirms
  a marked line's exact depth once the general area has already been located
  and marked.
example: >
  A contractor planning to trench for a new utility line submits an 811 request
  several business days before the scheduled work, as required by state law.
  Utility owners respond by marking the approximate location of their buried
  lines with color-coded paint, red for electric, yellow for gas, blue for
  water, giving the crew a general picture of what's underground before any
  digging begins.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] submits the 811 request far
  enough ahead of planned excavation to meet the state's required notice
  period, and the [[career-permit-expediter|Permit Expediter]] sometimes
  tracks 811 requests alongside other pre-construction regulatory
  requirements.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A crew begins excavation without submitting an 811 request first, or without
  waiting out the required notice period, and strikes a utility line that
  would have been marked and avoided if the legally required notification
  process had actually been followed.
