---
id: concept-radon-mitigation-system
title: Radon Mitigation System
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "02, Existing Conditions"
definition: >
  A system, most often active sub-slab depressurization (a continuously
  running fan drawing soil gas from beneath the foundation through a sealed
  pipe and venting it above the roofline), installed to reduce indoor radon
  gas below the EPA's 4 pCi/L action level.
whyItMatters: >
  Radon is an invisible, odorless radioactive gas that seeps up from soil and
  is a leading cause of lung cancer in non-smokers; a home or building can
  only be shown to need mitigation through testing, since there's no way to
  detect it without equipment.
example: >
  After a pre-sale radon test comes back at 6 pCi/L, a contractor installs a
  sub-slab depressurization system, sealing foundation cracks and running a
  vent pipe with an inline fan from beneath the slab to above the roof, then
  retests to confirm the level has dropped below 2 pCi/L.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-hvac-technician|HVAC Technician]] or specialized radon contractor
  installs and tests the system, often working alongside the
  [[career-environmental-remediation-specialist|Environmental Remediation
  Specialist]] handling the broader indoor-air assessment.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A mitigation system is installed but the fan is undersized for the house's
  foundation layout, and a post-installation retest still shows radon above
  the action level, meaning the homeowner paid for a system that doesn't
  actually solve the problem.
relatedIds:
  - career-hvac-technician
  - career-environmental-remediation-specialist
