id: concept-chiller
title: Chiller
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "23, HVAC"
definition: >
  Central plant equipment that cools water (or another refrigerant-based fluid), then
  circulates that chilled water to air handling units throughout a building, the
  standard cooling source for large commercial, institutional, and high-rise buildings
  where individual rooftop units aren't practical.
whyItMatters: >
  A chiller is one of a project's largest and longest-lead mechanical purchases, so
  ordering it late, or ordering the wrong capacity, doesn't just risk a schedule delay:
  it can leave an entire building without cooling capacity for months while a
  replacement unit is fabricated and shipped.
example: >
  A hospital's central plant is designed around a chiller sized for the building's
  full cooling load, ordered many months ahead of when it's actually needed on site
  because the manufacturer's fabrication and delivery lead time is longer than nearly
  any other piece of equipment on the project.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-hvac-technician|HVAC Technician]] commissions and maintains the chiller
  plant, work that requires specialized refrigerant handling training beyond typical
  rooftop unit service. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] relies on
  the chiller as critical equipment worth a preventive maintenance contract, since an
  unplanned chiller failure can mean losing cooling for an entire building at once.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project team delays finalizing the chiller's exact capacity until late in design,
  not realizing the manufacturer's lead time was already the tightest item on the
  entire procurement schedule. The late order pushes the chiller's delivery past when
  the building needs cooling for testing and occupancy, forcing a costly temporary
  cooling rental to bridge the gap.
