id: concept-toilet-partitions
title: Toilet Partitions
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  The stall dividers and doors in a public or commercial restroom, manufactured in
  several material types, plastic laminate, solid plastic, powder-coated metal, or
  stainless steel, each with different cost, moisture resistance, and durability
  tradeoffs, and mounted in one of several configurations, floor-mounted,
  ceiling-hung, or floor-to-ceiling, that each affect installation sequencing.
whyItMatters: >
  Material and mounting choice affects both long-term maintenance cost and
  installation sequence: floor-to-ceiling mounting in particular requires precise
  floor layout coordination with the finish flooring installer before anchors go
  in.
example: >
  A school district specifies solid plastic partitions, floor-mounted, for a new
  building's restrooms, chosen for moisture resistance and lower long-term
  maintenance over the powder-coated metal typically used in the district's office
  buildings.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-carpenter|Carpenter]] or specialty installer sets and anchors the
  partitions, coordinating layout with whoever installs the finish flooring.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An installer sets floor-mounted partition anchors before the finish flooring
  installer lays out the tile pattern. The anchor bolts end up landing mid-tile
  instead of on a grout line, forcing the tile installer to cut awkwardly around
  each one.
