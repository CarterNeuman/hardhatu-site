id: concept-preconstruction-conference
title: Preconstruction Conference
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A formal kickoff meeting held after [[concept-notice-to-proceed|notice to proceed]]
  but before work begins, bringing together the owner, contractor, and key
  subcontractors to review the schedule, safety plan, communication procedures, and site
  logistics before the first crew shows up.
whyItMatters: >
  Every project has dozens of procedural details, such as who submits what to whom, how
  RFIs get routed, and what the safety expectations are, that are much cheaper to agree on
  before work starts than to sort out in the middle of a disagreement on-site.
example: >
  Before mobilizing on a school renovation, the general contractor holds a
  preconstruction conference with the owner, architect, and major subcontractors,
  walking through the phasing plan for keeping the school partially occupied during
  construction and confirming everyone's emergency contact procedures.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] typically organizes and runs the
  preconstruction conference, using it to set expectations before the first day of
  work. A [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] uses it to walk the field-level
  logistics, such as site access, staging, and safety rules, directly with the subcontractors
  who'll be executing them.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project skips a formal preconstruction conference to save a week and jumps straight
  into mobilization. Three weeks in, a subcontractor is still submitting RFIs the wrong
  way and nobody agreed in advance on which gate deliveries should use, creating
  friction and confusion that a single upfront meeting would have prevented.
