---
id: concept-design-assist
title: Design-Assist
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  An arrangement where a specialty subcontractor, such as a mechanical or
  curtain-wall sub, is brought onto the team during the design phase to
  provide constructability input and pricing feedback before the design is
  finalized, without yet being formally contracted to perform the work.
whyItMatters: >
  It captures trade expertise early enough to actually influence the design,
  unlike a traditional bid process where a subcontractor only sees finished
  drawings, but because the sub isn't yet contractually bound, an owner
  typically still competitively bids or negotiates the actual construction
  contract once design is complete.
example: >
  A curtain-wall subcontractor is brought in during design development to advise
  on panel sizing and anchorage details before the architect finalizes the
  facade design, helping avoid a design that would be difficult or expensive
  to build. The actual installation contract is negotiated separately once the
  design-assist input has been incorporated.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-preconstruction-manager|Preconstruction Manager]] coordinates
  which specialty trades are brought in under design-assist arrangements, and
  the [[career-value-engineering-consultant|Value Engineering Consultant]]
  works alongside design-assist subcontractors to identify cost and
  constructability improvements while the design is still flexible.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner assumes a design-assist subcontractor is automatically awarded the
  construction contract once design is finished, when design-assist input and
  the eventual construction award are often two separate steps unless the
  agreement specifically ties them together.
