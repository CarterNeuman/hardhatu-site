id: concept-irrigation-system
title: Irrigation System
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "32, Exterior Improvements"
definition: >
  The piping, valves, controllers, and sprinkler or drip emitter heads
  installed to automatically water a site's landscaping, designed around
  the specific plant material's water needs and typically zoned so
  different areas, turf, planting beds, trees, can be watered on different
  schedules.
whyItMatters: >
  An irrigation system installed without coordinating its water source and
  backflow prevention with the site's domestic water design can create a
  cross-connection hazard, letting untreated irrigation water potentially
  flow back into the building's potable water supply, which is exactly why
  code requires a dedicated backflow preventer on the irrigation line.
example: >
  A landscaping contractor installs a dedicated backflow preventer on the
  irrigation system's connection to the domestic water supply, isolating
  the irrigation piping from the building's potable water in case of a
  pressure drop that could otherwise pull water backward.
careerAngle: >
  A specialty irrigation contractor installs and zones the system. A
  [[career-plumber|Plumber]] typically installs the required backflow
  preventer where the irrigation system connects to the domestic water
  supply.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A landscaping contractor connects a new irrigation system directly to the
  domestic water supply without installing the required backflow preventer,
  unaware it fell outside their scope and assuming the plumber had already
  handled it. A code inspector catches the missing device during a final
  inspection, and the connection has to be redone before the project can
  close out.
