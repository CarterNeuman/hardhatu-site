---
id: concept-mortgage-company-endorsement-process
title: Mortgage Company Endorsement Process
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The administrative process a mortgage lender's loss-draft or
  insurance-tracking department follows to review and sign off on an insurance
  claim check before releasing disbursements, typically involving inspections
  at key repair milestones.
whyItMatters: >
  Contractors who don't plan around this process often get caught off guard by
  payment delays, since the lender's own inspection and sign-off requirements
  run on a separate timeline from the insurer's claim approval.
example: >
  A general contractor rebuilding a fire-damaged home submits a mid-project
  progress photo package to the mortgage company's insurance-tracking
  department, which must review and endorse the next disbursement before the
  contractor can draw additional funds from the loss-draft account.
careerAngle: >
  Contractors on lender-involved claims should map out the lender's inspection
  and endorsement milestones early in the schedule, since draw requests
  submitted without meeting the lender's documentation requirements commonly
  stall.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Contractors sometimes assume once the insurance company approves a claim the
  money flows freely, when a mortgaged property adds the lender's own separate
  endorsement and inspection process on top of the insurer's approval.
