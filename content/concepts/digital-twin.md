---
id: concept-digital-twin
title: Digital Twin
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  A digital model of a building that continues to be maintained and updated after
  construction ends, reflecting the building's actual as-built condition and, often,
  live data from its own building systems, distinct from a construction-phase
  [[concept-bim|BIM]] model, which typically stops being updated once the building is
  turned over.
whyItMatters: >
  A construction-phase model that's frozen at turnover quickly drifts out of sync with
  a building that gets renovated, has equipment replaced, or has systems reconfigured
  over years of operation. A digital twin exists specifically to keep that model current
  and useful long after the construction team has left the project.
example: >
  A hospital's facilities team uses a digital twin that reflects the building's actual
  as-built mechanical systems, letting them locate a specific valve behind a wall without
  guessing or opening up finishes to find it.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] relies on an accurate digital twin
  to plan maintenance and locate building systems without opening up finished spaces to
  look. A [[career-bim-vdc-specialist|BIM/VDC Specialist]] is often responsible for
  ensuring the construction-phase model is accurate enough at turnover to actually serve
  as the starting point for a useful digital twin.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project hands over its construction-phase model to the owner without reconciling it
  against the building's actual as-built conditions first. The facilities team later
  discovers the "digital twin" doesn't match what's actually behind the walls, making it
  less useful than simply not having one and relying on field verification instead.
