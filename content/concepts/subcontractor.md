id: concept-subcontractor
title: Subcontractor
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A company hired by the general contractor, not the owner directly, to perform a
  specific portion of the work, such as electrical, plumbing, or drywall, under a
  subcontract that flows down many of the same terms the GC agreed to in the
  [[concept-prime-contract|prime contract]].
whyItMatters: >
  Most of the actual physical work on a project gets performed by subcontractors, not
  the GC's own employees; the GC's real job is coordinating and holding them
  accountable, which only works if the subcontract properly passes down the
  obligations the GC itself owes the owner.
example: >
  A general contractor holds one prime contract with the owner, then signs separate
  subcontracts with an electrical subcontractor, a plumbing subcontractor, and a
  drywall subcontractor to actually perform each piece of the work.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] manages subcontractor relationships and
  payments day to day. A [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]]
  drafts the [[concept-subcontract-agreement|subcontract agreement]] itself, making
  sure it properly flows down the prime contract's key terms.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor signs a subcontract that never actually references or
  incorporates the terms of the prime contract. When a dispute arises over a schedule
  delay, the subcontractor argues they're not bound by the prime contract's liquidated
  damages clause at all, since their own subcontract never mentioned it.
