---
id: concept-total-float-vs-free-float
title: Total Float vs. Free Float
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  Two different ways of measuring schedule cushion: total float is how long an
  activity can slip without delaying the whole project's finish date, while
  free float is the smaller amount it can slip without delaying the very next
  activity that depends on it.
whyItMatters: >
  An activity can have plenty of total float but zero free float, which means
  delaying it won't push back the project finish date, but it will still
  immediately push back whoever's scheduled to start right after it, a
  distinction that matters a lot to whichever trade is next in line.
example: >
  A rough-in electrical activity has ten days of total float before it threatens
  the project's finish date, but zero free float, since the drywall crew
  scheduled to start the moment electrical finishes has no float of its own.
  Delaying electrical by even one day pushes the drywall start, even though
  the overall project schedule absorbs it fine.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-scheduler|Scheduler]] tracks both total and free float on every
  activity, since a trade foreman who only hears "you have ten days of float"
  from the overall schedule might not realize the very next crew in line has
  none.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A foreman assumes an activity with plenty of total float can safely slip a few
  days with no consequence, without realizing the next trade queued up behind
  it has zero free float and gets pushed back immediately as a result.
relatedIds:
  - career-scheduler
  - career-superintendent
---
