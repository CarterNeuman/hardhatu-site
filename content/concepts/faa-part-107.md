id: concept-faa-part-107
title: FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  The FAA license required to legally fly a drone for commercial purposes in
  the United States, earned by passing an aeronautical knowledge test and
  maintained through a recurrent training requirement every twenty-four
  months.
whyItMatters: >
  Flying a drone for a business purpose, including construction site mapping,
  is federally regulated differently from flying one recreationally, and Part
  107 is the specific license that makes commercial drone work legal. It
  covers real aeronautical knowledge, airspace rules, weather, and operating
  limitations, not just how to fly the aircraft itself.
example: >
  A construction company hiring a drone operator for site mapping confirms the
  operator holds a current FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate before
  scheduling any commercial flight over the jobsite.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-drone-uav-specialist|Drone/UAV Specialist]] has to hold a current
  Part 107 certificate to legally perform commercial drone work, and has to
  complete recurrent training every twenty-four months to keep it valid.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A company has an employee without a Part 107 certificate fly a drone over a
  jobsite for a progress photo, treating it as a casual task rather than a
  regulated commercial flight. The flight is a real FAA violation regardless
  of how harmless the intent was.
