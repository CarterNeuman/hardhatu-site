---
id: concept-pre-task-plan
title: Pre-Task Plan
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  A brief, informal hazard check a crew runs through immediately before starting
  a specific task that day, distinct from the more formal, often pre-written
  Job Hazard Analysis it typically references, and distinct from a Toolbox
  Talk, which covers a general safety topic rather than that day's specific
  task.
whyItMatters: >
  Site conditions change day to day even for a task that's been done the same
  way many times before, so a quick pre-task check catches the hazard that's
  different today, like a new obstruction or a change in weather, that a
  pre-written JHA from weeks ago wouldn't capture.
example: >
  Before starting the day's excavation work, a crew runs a quick pre-task plan
  huddle, noting that overnight rain has made the spoil pile unstable in a way
  the task's original written JHA never anticipated, and adjusts their
  approach before anyone steps into the trench.
careerAngle: >
  Foremen should run this quick check every day a hazardous task starts, even
  one the crew has done dozens of times before, since it's specifically meant
  to catch what's different about today, not to repeat what a written JHA
  already covers.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Crews sometimes treat a once-written JHA as covering a task permanently, when
  conditions that change day to day are exactly what a quick pre-task plan is
  meant to catch before work actually starts each time.
relatedIds:
