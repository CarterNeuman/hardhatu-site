---
id: concept-phasing-plan
title: Phasing Plan
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A plan dividing a construction project into distinct sequential or overlapping
  phases, each defining what work happens where and in what order, used
  specifically on projects where parts of a facility stay occupied or
  operational while other parts are under construction.
whyItMatters: >
  Building in an occupied hospital or a school that stays in session is
  fundamentally different from building on an empty site, since the phasing
  plan has to account for maintaining life safety, access, and operations in
  occupied areas while construction proceeds in adjacent phases, not just
  sequencing the construction work itself.
example: >
  A hospital renovation project's phasing plan divides the work into four
  sequential phases, each one renovating a different wing while keeping the
  other three wings fully operational, with temporary partitions, dedicated
  construction access routes, and infection-control measures specific to each
  phase's boundary with occupied space.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] executes the phasing plan in the
  field, maintaining the required separation between construction and occupied
  areas, and the [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]]
  coordinates the phasing plan's schedule against the owner's own operational
  needs in each phase.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A renovation project's phasing plan sequences the construction work logically
  but doesn't adequately account for the occupied building's life-safety and
  access requirements in each phase, creating a code or operational conflict
  that isn't discovered until a specific phase is already underway.
relatedIds:
