---
id: concept-professional-liability-insurance
title: Professional Liability Insurance / E&O
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  Errors and omissions coverage for design professionals and other
  consultants, protecting against claims of negligence in the professional
  services they provided, distinct from general liability coverage, which
  covers bodily injury and property damage rather than a professional mistake in
  judgment.
whyItMatters: >
  A design error rarely causes bodily injury or property damage on its own, it
  causes cost and delay, which is exactly the kind of loss general liability
  coverage excludes and E&O coverage exists specifically to address.
example: >
  A structural engineer's calculation error requires costly redesign and rework
  well after construction has started, and the resulting claim is covered under
  the engineer's professional liability policy, not their general liability
  policy, since no bodily injury or property damage actually occurred.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-construction-attorney|Construction Attorney]] identifies which
  policy, general liability or professional liability, actually applies to a
  given claim, since design-related claims and physical-damage claims usually
  belong under two entirely different policies.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A claim arises from a design error, and the owner's attorney initially tries
  to pursue it under the designer's general liability policy, only to find that
  policy excludes professional negligence entirely. That claim belongs under the
  designer's separate E&O policy instead, and the misdirected initial claim
  costs time the owner didn't need to lose.
