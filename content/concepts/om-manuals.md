id: concept-om-manuals
title: Operations & Maintenance (O&M) Manuals
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  Manufacturer manuals, maintenance schedules, warranty information, and equipment
  data sheets compiled by the contractor and handed to the owner at closeout,
  covering every piece of major equipment installed, so facilities staff can
  operate and maintain it correctly once the design and construction teams are
  gone.
whyItMatters: >
  Without accurate O&M manuals, facilities staff are left guessing at maintenance
  intervals and part numbers for equipment they didn't select and weren't around to
  see installed, which is exactly the knowledge gap the manuals exist to close
  before that expertise walks off the jobsite for good.
example: >
  A general contractor compiles O&M manuals organized by CSI division into a
  searchable PDF set, covering every major piece of mechanical and electrical
  equipment installed, and submits it for owner review before requesting final
  payment.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-document-control-specialist|Document Control Specialist]] tracks
  which subcontractors have submitted their required O&M data and chases down what's
  missing. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] on the owner's side
  relies on the finished manuals as a working reference for years after the
  contractor demobilizes.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor submits generic manufacturer catalog sheets for a rooftop unit
  instead of the documentation for the specific model actually installed. Facilities
  staff don't discover the mismatch until the unit fails and the maintenance
  procedures in hand don't match the equipment in front of them, delaying the
  repair while the correct manual gets tracked down.
