id: concept-key-asset-tracking-systems
title: Key & Asset Tracking Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "28, Electronic Safety & Security"
definition: >
  Electronic key cabinets and RFID-based asset tracking installed in
  facilities that need to control and audit who has access to physical keys or
  high-value equipment, tied into the building's network for logging and
  alerts.
whyItMatters: >
  An electronic key cabinet is only as useful as its integration with the
  facility's access-control and reporting systems, since the audit trail it's
  installed to provide depends on that data actually reaching whoever reviews
  key accountability.
example: >
  A contractor installing an electronic key cabinet at a vehicle fleet
  facility coordinates its network connection and reporting integration with
  the facilities team's existing access-control software before final
  commissioning.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] defines the key-control
  and reporting requirements the system has to meet.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An electronic key cabinet is installed but never networked into the
  facility's reporting system, leaving the audit trail stuck on a local screen
  that no one checks, defeating the accountability it was installed to
  provide.
