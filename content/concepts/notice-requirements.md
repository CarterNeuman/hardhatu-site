id: concept-notice-requirements
title: Notice Requirements (Contract Claims)
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  Contract provisions specifying how and when a party must formally notify the
  other of a claim, delay, or changed condition, often with a strict deadline,
  commonly ten to twenty days from the triggering event. Failing to give timely
  notice can waive an otherwise valid claim entirely, regardless of its merits.
whyItMatters: >
  Notice requirements get enforced strictly precisely because they're easy to
  overlook in the middle of an actual jobsite problem, a contractor focused on
  solving a delay in the field can lose the right to be paid for it just by not
  also sending the required letter.
example: >
  A contractor discovers a differing site condition and immediately starts
  working around it in the field, but doesn't submit the written notice the
  contract requires within fifteen days. Even though the condition itself was
  real, the missed notice deadline waives the claim.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] tracks every
  contract's specific notice deadlines and makes sure the field team knows to
  flag triggering events immediately, since a missed deadline can void a claim
  no matter how strong the underlying facts are.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A subcontractor experiences a legitimate owner-caused delay but doesn't submit
  written notice within the contract's strict notice deadline, focused instead on
  managing the delay in the field. By the time a claim is formally raised, the
  subcontractor has lost the right to claim it, even though the delay itself was
  clearly the owner's fault.
