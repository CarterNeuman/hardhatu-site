---
id: concept-ilsm
title: Interim Life Safety Measures (ILSM)
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  Temporary safeguards (extra fire watches, additional portable extinguishers,
  alternate egress routes, staff notification and drills) put in place whenever
  construction temporarily knocks out part of a healthcare facility's normal
  fire or life safety protection, such as a disabled fire alarm zone or a
  blocked exit corridor, required by the Joint Commission for accreditation.
whyItMatters: >
  A hospital can't simply accept reduced fire protection while a project is
  underway, patients may not be able to evacuate quickly on their own. ILSM is
  the formal, documented substitute for whatever protection construction
  temporarily removes, so the facility stays genuinely safe, not just
  technically under renovation.
example: >
  A renovation requires temporarily shutting down a corridor's sprinkler zone
  for a tie-in. The ILSM plan adds continuous fire watch patrols with portable
  extinguishers in that corridor for the entire shutdown, documented and
  reviewed daily, until the sprinkler zone is restored and tested.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-healthcare-construction-manager|Healthcare Construction Manager]]
  manages ILSM compliance whenever construction affects a facility's egress or
  fire protection, coordinating directly with the hospital's life safety and
  facilities staff. The fire marshal reviews and signs off on the ILSM plan
  before work affecting fire protection is allowed to proceed.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor disables a fire alarm zone for a tie-in and assumes the posted
  fire watch covers it, but the fire watch patrol gets pulled onto an unrelated
  task for an hour without a documented handoff. If an actual fire started
  during that gap, nobody would have caught it early, exactly the scenario ILSM
  documentation exists to prevent through a continuous, accountable chain of
  coverage.
