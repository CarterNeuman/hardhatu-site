id: concept-skylights
title: Skylights
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "08, Openings"
definition: >
  Glazed roof openings that bring daylight into a space below, ranging from
  small unit skylights to large structural skylight systems, requiring careful
  coordination of roof penetration flashing and, on a walkable or accessible
  roof, fall-protection guarding since an unguarded skylight opening is a
  well-documented fall hazard.
whyItMatters: >
  OSHA treats skylights as a fall hazard requiring guarding or a screen rated
  to support anticipated loads, since workers on a roof can mistake a skylight
  for a solid walking surface, a documented cause of serious falls, this isn't
  an incidental detail but a specific, enforced safety requirement.
example: >
  A roofer installs a skylight with a code-required guardrail or screen around
  the opening capable of supporting a worker's weight, in addition to properly
  flashing the roof penetration to prevent leaks.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-roofer|Roofer]] installs the skylight and its flashing. A
  [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] verifies skylight guarding is in
  place before crews are allowed to work on the roof around it.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A roofing crew installs a skylight but the required guardrail isn't
  installed until later in the project, leaving the opening unguarded during
  the weeks other trades are actively working on the roof. A worker steps back
  near the opening without realizing it's unguarded, and only a last-second
  catch by a coworker prevents a serious fall.
