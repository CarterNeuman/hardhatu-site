---
id: concept-as-built-drawings
title: As-Built Drawings
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A final set of drawings updated to show what was actually constructed, including
  every field change, substitution, and adjustment made along the way, rather than just
  the original design intent.
whyItMatters: >
  What actually gets built rarely matches the original plans exactly: a wall shifts a
  few inches, a pipe gets rerouted around an obstruction. As-built drawings are the
  record of reality, and they're often the only way anyone can safely renovate or repair
  the building years later without guessing what's actually behind a wall.
example: >
  During construction, a plumbing line gets rerouted around a structural beam that
  wasn't shown correctly on the original drawings. The as-built drawings mark exactly
  where that line actually runs, so nobody drills into it during a future renovation.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-engineer|Project Engineer]] is usually responsible for tracking
  every field change throughout the project and rolling them into the final as-built
  set. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] relies on those drawings for
  years afterward: they're often the only accurate map of what's actually behind the
  walls.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor treats as-built drawings as a low-priority afterthought and hands over a
  set that's barely been updated from the original design. Years later, a maintenance
  crew drills into a wall exactly where the plans show nothing, hitting a rerouted
  electrical line that was never actually documented, causing an outage and a repair
  bill that accurate records would have prevented entirely.
