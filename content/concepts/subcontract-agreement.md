id: concept-subcontract-agreement
title: Subcontract Agreement
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  The contract between a general contractor and a subcontractor defining that
  subcontractor's specific scope, price, and schedule, typically written to "flow
  down" many of the same obligations the GC agreed to in the
  [[concept-prime-contract|prime contract]] with the owner.
whyItMatters: >
  A subcontractor's actual legal rights and obligations depend entirely on what
  their specific subcontract agreement says, not on what the prime contract between
  the owner and GC says on its own: if a key prime contract term never gets written
  into the subcontract, it usually doesn't bind the subcontractor at all.
example: >
  A general contractor's subcontract agreement with an electrical subcontractor
  spells out that subcontractor's specific scope of work, price, schedule milestones,
  and a flow-down clause incorporating the relevant terms of the prime contract.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] drafts subcontract
  agreements for every trade on a project, tailoring the flow-down language and scope
  to each one. A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] manages subcontractor
  performance and payment against the specific terms each subcontract agreement
  actually sets.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor uses a generic subcontract template for a specialty trade
  without customizing the scope language to that trade's actual work. A dispute
  arises over whether a specific task was included, and the vague, generic scope
  language in the subcontract agreement doesn't clearly answer the question either
  way.
