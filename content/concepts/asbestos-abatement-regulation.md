id: concept-asbestos-abatement-regulation
title: Asbestos Abatement Regulation / NESHAP
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  The EPA's asbestos NESHAP rule, requiring a survey for asbestos-containing
  material before most demolition or renovation work. Above specific thresholds,
  roughly 260 linear feet, 160 square feet, or 35 cubic feet of regulated material,
  it requires advance written notification to the regulating agency and proper
  abatement and disposal before that material can be disturbed.
whyItMatters: >
  The survey and any required notification have to happen before demolition
  starts, not during it, so a schedule that doesn't build in time for the survey
  results is setting the project up to either wait mid-demolition or, worse,
  disturb regulated material without the required notice.
example: >
  A demolition contractor commissions an asbestos survey of a 1960s office
  building weeks before the scheduled start date, so any required abatement and
  the agency's advance notification period are both handled before demolition
  equipment ever shows up.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-demolition-supervisor|Demolition Supervisor]] can't start tearing down
  a building until the abatement survey comes back and any required abatement is
  complete, while an
  [[career-environmental-remediation-specialist|Environmental/Remediation Specialist]]
  handles the actual survey and abatement work.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A demolition supervisor starts tearing down an old building under schedule
  pressure before the asbestos survey results come back, disturbing material that
  later tests positive. The job halts immediately, and the unauthorized disturbance
  itself triggers a separate violation on top of the delay.
