id: concept-door-hardware
title: Door Hardware
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "08, Openings"
definition: >
  The hinges, locksets, closers, panic hardware, and other functional components
  installed on a door, specified in detail through a hardware schedule that
  coordinates each door's specific function (fire-rated, accessible, secured,
  exit-required) with the correct hardware for that function.
whyItMatters: >
  Door hardware selection is tightly governed by both fire and accessibility code: a
  fire-rated door needs a self-closing device and often specific hardware to maintain
  its rating, and an accessible route requires hardware operable without tight
  grasping or twisting, so a hardware substitution that looks equivalent can actually
  violate either requirement.
example: >
  A door on an accessible route is specified with a lever handle rather than a round
  doorknob, since code requires hardware on an accessible route to be operable with a
  closed fist, something a round knob doesn't allow.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-carpenter|Carpenter]] installs hardware to the door manufacturer's prep
  and the project's hardware schedule. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] inherits the building's hardware long after construction and relies on
  the original hardware schedule to source matching replacement parts years later.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor substitutes a similar-looking lockset for the one specified on a
  fire-rated door, without confirming the substitute is actually listed for use on
  that fire rating. The mismatch isn't caught until a fire marshal's inspection, and
  the non-compliant hardware has to be replaced with a properly listed unit before
  the door assembly can be approved.
