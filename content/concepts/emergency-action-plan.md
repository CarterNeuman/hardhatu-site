---
id: concept-emergency-action-plan
title: Emergency Action Plan
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  An OSHA-required written plan covering how a workplace, including a jobsite,
  handles emergencies, evacuation routes and procedures, a designated assembly
  point, and who's specifically responsible for accounting for all workers once
  everyone reaches it.
whyItMatters: >
  Knowing where to evacuate to only solves half the problem, without someone
  specifically assigned to account for every worker at the assembly point, a
  jobsite has no reliable way to know whether everyone actually made it out during
  an actual emergency.
example: >
  A jobsite's emergency action plan names a specific supervisor as responsible for
  checking a daily sign-in sheet against everyone present at the assembly point
  after any evacuation, rather than leaving headcount to informal observation.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] develops the plan and runs regular
  drills so the evacuation routes and accountability procedure are second nature
  before a real emergency ever happens.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A fire breaks out in a temporary jobsite trailer, and no one has actually been
  assigned to account for workers at the designated assembly point. Several minutes
  pass before anyone realizes a worker is unaccounted for, exactly the gap an
  emergency action plan's accountability procedure exists to close.
