id: concept-bim-coordination-meeting
title: BIM Coordination Meeting
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A recurring meeting where the trades contributing to a combined
  [[concept-coordination-drawing|coordination drawing]] model, like
  mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and structural, review detected clashes
  together and agree on how to resolve each one before anything is actually
  fabricated or installed.
whyItMatters: >
  Software can detect a clash automatically, but resolving it, deciding whose
  system moves and by how much, still takes the actual trades talking it
  through together, since the fix that's easiest for one trade is sometimes
  expensive or impossible for another, and someone has to own that trade-off.
example: >
  A weekly BIM coordination meeting reviews a batch of newly detected clashes
  between ductwork and a structural beam. The mechanical and structural trades
  discuss the options together and agree the ductwork will reroute slightly,
  since the beam's structural role makes moving it far more disruptive than
  adjusting the duct run.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-bim-vdc-specialist|BIM/VDC Specialist]] runs the clash detection
  and facilitates the coordination meeting, and the [[career-mep-engineer|MEP
  Engineer]] represents the mechanical, electrical, and plumbing trades'
  interests in resolving each clash.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A clash is detected in the model but never actually discussed and resolved at
  a coordination meeting, and the conflict carries through to fabrication,
  where it becomes a far more expensive field conflict between two
  already-fabricated systems instead of a simple model adjustment.
