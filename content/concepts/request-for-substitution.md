---
id: concept-request-for-substitution
title: Request for Substitution
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A formal request, typically submitted as part of the submittal process, asking
  the design team to approve a different product or material than what's
  specified in the contract documents, usually because the specified item is
  unavailable, discontinued, or the contractor has a cost or schedule reason
  to propose an alternative.
whyItMatters: >
  The design team's approval isn't automatic just because the substitute looks
  equivalent on paper; the request has to show the proposed product genuinely
  meets the same performance requirements as what was specified, and an
  unapproved substitution installed without that approval can be rejected and
  require removal and replacement at the contractor's expense.
example: >
  A specified lighting fixture goes out of production mid-project. The
  contractor submits a request for substitution proposing a comparable fixture
  from a different manufacturer, including technical data showing it meets the
  same lighting performance requirements, and the design team reviews and
  approves the substitution before any fixtures are actually ordered.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-project-engineer|Project Engineer]] prepares the request for
  substitution with the technical documentation needed to support it, and the
  [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] or other design professional reviews
  whether the proposed substitute genuinely performs equivalently to what was
  specified.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor installs a substitute product without first getting a request for
  substitution formally approved, assuming the design team would have approved
  it anyway, and ends up having to remove and replace the unapproved product
  at its own expense once the discrepancy is caught during inspection.
relatedIds:
