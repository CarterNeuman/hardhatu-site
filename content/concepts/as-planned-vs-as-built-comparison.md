id: concept-as-planned-vs-as-built-comparison
title: As-Planned vs. As-Built Schedule Comparison
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A forensic scheduling technique comparing a project's original baseline
  schedule against what actually happened in the field, activity by
  activity, to identify where and how significantly the project diverged
  from its plan. It's one input among several in a full delay analysis
  rather than a complete answer on its own.
whyItMatters: >
  Showing that a project ran late isn't the same as proving why, this
  comparison is a starting point for a delay analysis, not a substitute for
  the deeper [[concept-time-impact-analysis|time impact analysis]] that
  actually attributes specific delays to specific causes.
example: >
  A scheduling consultant lays the project's as-built schedule side by side
  with its original baseline and identifies exactly which activities ran
  longer than planned and by how much, a first step toward figuring out why.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-scheduling-delay-consultant|Scheduling & Delay Consultant]]
  builds the as-planned versus as-built comparison as groundwork, then digs
  deeper to attribute specific causes to specific delays.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An analyst builds an as-planned versus as-built comparison but only
  includes the activities that support the client's preferred narrative,
  leaving out several activities that actually ran ahead of schedule. The
  skewed comparison overstates how far behind the project really fell, and
  the opposing side's own analysis catches the omission.
