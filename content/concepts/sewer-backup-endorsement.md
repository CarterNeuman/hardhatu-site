---
id: concept-sewer-backup-endorsement
title: Sewer Backup / Water Backup Endorsement
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  An optional policy endorsement covering damage from sewage or drain water
  backing up into a structure, a loss that standard property policies
  typically exclude outright without this add-on.
whyItMatters: >
  Restoration contractors responding to a sewer backup need to confirm this
  specific endorsement exists before assuming the loss is covered, since this
  is one of the most commonly misunderstood exclusions in a standard
  homeowner's policy.
example: >
  A basement floods with sewage after a municipal sewer line backs up during a
  heavy storm, and the restoration company confirms the homeowner carries a
  water backup endorsement before beginning the biohazard-level cleanup, since
  without it the loss would not be covered at all.
careerAngle: >
  Restoration contractors should ask about this specific endorsement early in a
  sewer-backup job, since confirming coverage upfront avoids a dispute over
  payment after biohazard cleanup work is already underway.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Homeowners frequently assume sewage backup is covered under general water
  damage protection, when it's one of the most commonly excluded perils unless
  this specific, often low-cost endorsement was added.
