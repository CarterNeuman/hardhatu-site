---
id: concept-substrate
title: Substrate
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The underlying surface a material or system gets installed onto, such as a
  roof deck beneath a roofing membrane or wall sheathing beneath siding, whose
  condition has to be checked and approved before the material above it goes
  on.
whyItMatters: >
  A finish material is only as good as the surface it's installed on. A
  pre-installation meeting exists specifically to walk through substrate
  conditions before work begins, since problems with the substrate, moisture,
  unevenness, damage, become far more expensive to fix once the new material
  is already installed on top of them.
example: >
  Before a roofing crew installs a new membrane, the project team holds a
  pre-installation meeting to walk the roof deck and confirm the substrate is
  dry, structurally sound, and properly prepared, since installing a membrane
  over a damaged substrate would trap the problem underneath the new roof.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] walks substrate conditions with
  each incoming subcontractor before that sub's work begins, catching problems
  while they're still cheap to fix.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A subcontractor installs new flooring over a substrate that wasn't actually
  checked for moisture first. Trapped moisture beneath the new flooring causes
  it to fail within months, a problem that traces directly back to a substrate
  condition nobody verified before installation began.
