---
id: concept-loss-of-rents-coverage
title: Loss of Rents Coverage
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  A property insurance provision, similar to business interruption coverage,
  that reimburses a landlord for rental income lost while a covered property
  is being repaired and is uninhabitable, distinct from loss of use coverage,
  which pays a homeowner's own temporary housing costs.
whyItMatters: >
  Contractors scheduling a repair timeline on a rental property should
  understand this coverage often has its own time limit, which gives the
  landlord a real financial incentive to push for a faster completion date.
example: >
  A duplex owner's insurer pays loss of rents coverage for the six months a
  fire-damaged unit sits vacant during repair, replacing the rental income the
  owner would otherwise have collected from a tenant during that period.
careerAngle: >
  Contractors repairing income-producing property should be aware the owner's
  loss-of-rents coverage usually has a coverage period limit, which can create
  pressure to accelerate the schedule as that limit approaches.
whatGoesWrong: >
  People sometimes confuse this with loss of use coverage, when loss of rents
  specifically replaces a landlord's lost rental income, while loss of use
  pays an owner-occupant's own temporary living expenses.
