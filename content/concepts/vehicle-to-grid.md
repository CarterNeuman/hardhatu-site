id: concept-vehicle-to-grid
title: Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G)
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  Bidirectional EV charging technology that lets a parked electric vehicle's
  battery     discharge stored power back into the building or the utility
  grid during periods of peak     demand, not just draw power in one direction
  the way standard EV charging does, standardized     internationally through
  ISO 15118-20.
whyItMatters: >
  A fleet of electric work trucks parked overnight represents a large pool of
  stored     battery capacity sitting idle; V2G lets that capacity actually
  earn money or offset demand     charges by feeding power back during the
  building's or grid's highest-demand hours, instead     of just sitting there
  charged and unused.
example: >
  A contractor's electric van fleet, parked overnight at the yard,
  automatically discharges     a portion of its stored battery capacity back
  into the facility's electrical system during     the utility's afternoon
  peak-demand window, reducing the facility's demand charge before the
  vans resume charging overnight.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-electrician|Electrician]] installing V2G-capable charging
  infrastructure has to     confirm both the vehicle and the charger support
  the same bidirectional communication     standard, since a charger and
  vehicle that aren't certified to the same V2G protocol won't     actually
  discharge back to the grid.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A fleet operator buys bidirectional-capable chargers assuming any electric
  vehicle can use     them for V2G, when the vehicle itself also has to
  support the same certified bidirectional     standard; mismatched vehicle
  and charger certification is a common reason a V2G installation     doesn't
  actually work as installed.
