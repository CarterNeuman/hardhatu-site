---
id: concept-fire-extinguisher-cabinets
title: Fire Extinguisher Cabinets
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Wall-mounted or recessed enclosures that hold portable fire extinguishers at
  required locations throughout a building, sized and placed according to fire
  code travel-distance and hazard-class requirements rather than wherever is
  visually convenient.
whyItMatters: >
  Fire code sets maximum travel distances to an extinguisher based on the
  hazard class of the occupancy, so cabinet placement isn't a finish decision,
  it's a life-safety requirement that gets checked during inspection.
example: >
  A fire protection contractor lays out extinguisher cabinet locations on the
  architectural plans before drywall goes up, confirming each cabinet falls
  within the code-required 75-foot travel distance for the building's hazard
  classification.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] or fire protection subcontractor
  verifies cabinet placement against code before the inspector's walkthrough,
  and the [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates the rough-in
  with the framing and drywall schedule.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Extinguisher cabinets get installed based on the architect's aesthetic
  layout rather than the fire code's travel-distance rule, and the fire
  marshal flags several areas during final inspection as exceeding the maximum
  distance, forcing rework after walls are already finished.
relatedIds:
  - career-safety-manager
  - career-superintendent
