id: concept-virtual-design-construction
title: Virtual Design & Construction (VDC)
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  The organizational discipline of using integrated digital models, not just a
  BIM file but     the coordinated process of design, scheduling, and cost
  data layered onto it, to plan a     project before building it, distinct
  from BIM itself which is the modeling tool VDC relies     on.
whyItMatters: >
  A team can own a sophisticated BIM model and still not practice VDC if
  nobody integrates     the model with the schedule, the cost estimate, and
  logistics planning. VDC is the     management discipline of actually using
  the model that way, not the software license     itself.
example: >
  A VDC manager pulls the structural BIM model, the project schedule, and site
  logistics     plan into one coordinated 4D sequence, catching that a planned
  crane position would block     the only site access road during week 12, a
  conflict none of the three inputs would have     revealed on its own.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-bim-vdc-specialist|BIM/VDC Specialist]] owns this integration,
  pulling the model     together with the schedule, cost, and logistics data
  rather than treating the model as a     standalone 3D reference nobody
  else's planning actually touches.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A company sets up BIM authoring standards and assumes that alone counts as a
  VDC program,     when VDC requires the model to actually drive scheduling,
  cost, and logistics decisions, not     just exist as a well-organized 3D
  reference.
