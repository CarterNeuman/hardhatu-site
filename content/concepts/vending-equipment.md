---
id: concept-vending-equipment
title: Vending Equipment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Built-in vending machine alcoves and the dedicated power and, for
  refrigerated units, drainage connections they require, planned into break
  rooms and common areas during design rather than treated as a plug-in
  appliance added after occupancy.
whyItMatters: >
  A refrigerated vending machine needs a dedicated circuit and, in some
  installations, a condensate drain, which is far simpler to rough in during
  construction than to add to a finished break room wall later.
example: >
  A contractor building an office break room roughs in a dedicated circuit for
  the planned vending alcove, anticipating the refrigeration load of a cold
  beverage machine rather than treating the alcove as just an open space with
  no particular power requirement.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] plans the vending
  alcove's power needs based on the equipment the building will eventually
  host.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A vending alcove is built with only a standard outlet, and the refrigerated
  machine the facilities team wants to install after occupancy trips the
  shared circuit repeatedly until a dedicated circuit is retrofitted.
relatedIds:
  - career-facilities-manager
