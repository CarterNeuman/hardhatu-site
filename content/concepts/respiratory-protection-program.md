id: concept-respiratory-protection-program
title: Respiratory Protection Program
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  An OSHA-required written program covering any workplace where workers use
  respirators, including medical evaluation to confirm a worker can safely wear one,
  fit testing to confirm a specific model seals properly on that worker's face, and
  training on proper use, not just handing out a respirator and assuming it's
  enough.
whyItMatters: >
  A respirator that isn't fit-tested to the specific worker wearing it can leak
  around the seal without the wearer ever noticing, which means simply providing
  respirators without the program behind them can create a false sense of
  protection instead of real protection.
example: >
  Before a crew starts work involving airborne silica dust, each worker gets
  medically cleared and fit-tested for their specific respirator model, and only
  workers who pass both steps are permitted to use it on that task.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] administers the program company-wide,
  tracking who's medically cleared, which respirator model each worker is
  fit-tested for, and when that fit test expires.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A worker is handed an N95 respirator for a demolition task involving heavy dust
  without medical clearance or fit testing first. An OSHA inspection later cites the
  company for the missing program elements, a violation that exists regardless of
  whether the worker was actually harmed.
