---
id: concept-display-cases
title: Display Cases
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Custom or manufactured glass-front cabinets for displaying merchandise,
  trophies, or exhibits, specified with lighting, security locking, and
  sometimes climate control for sensitive items, distinct from standard
  storage casework.
whyItMatters: >
  Display cases intended for retail security or museum-grade preservation need
  their lighting and lock hardware specified for that purpose, since a
  standard decorative case won't meet the security or UV-light exposure
  requirements those uses actually need.
example: >
  A contractor installing display cases for a school's trophy case specifies
  UV-filtering lighting and a secure locking mechanism, rather than a
  decorative case intended for a lower-security retail display.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] specifies display case
  security and lighting requirements appropriate to what will be displayed.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A decorative display case meant for low-value retail items is installed for
  a museum's valuable exhibit pieces, and the inadequate locking mechanism is
  flagged during a security review after installation.
relatedIds:
  - career-facilities-manager
---
