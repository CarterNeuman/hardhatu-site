---
id: concept-material-delivery-log
title: Material Delivery Log
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A running record tracking what materials arrived on site, when, in what
  quantity, and in what condition, checked against purchase orders and approved
  submittals so the project team can verify a supplier delivered what was actually
  ordered and document any damage or shortage at the moment of delivery.
whyItMatters: >
  Catching damage or a short shipment the moment a truck arrives, and logging it
  immediately, protects the project from a later dispute over who's responsible,
  since a claim made weeks after the fact is much harder to prove than one
  documented on delivery.
example: >
  A receiving crew logs each truckload of precast panels as it arrives, noting one
  panel with a hairline crack, photographs it, and notifies the supplier the same
  day to arrange a replacement before the crack becomes a dispute about when the
  damage actually occurred.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-document-control-specialist|Document Control Specialist]] or
  [[career-project-engineer|Project Engineer]] maintains the log and reconciles it
  against outstanding purchase orders.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A crew accepts a delivery of electrical switchgear without logging or inspecting
  it against the purchase order. Weeks later, during installation, the crew
  discovers shipping damage, but with no delivery-condition record on file, the
  supplier disputes when the damage actually happened and declines to cover it.
