---
id: concept-conveyor-systems
title: Conveyor Systems (Material Handling)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "14, Conveying Equipment"
definition: >
  Fixed mechanical systems moving materials, packages, luggage, or baggage
  along a defined path, common in warehouses, distribution centers, and
  airports, requiring structural support and power coordinated with the
  building's layout before the conveyor's exact path is finalized.
whyItMatters: >
  A conveyor system's support structure and power distribution have to match
  its specific path through the building, so the building's structural and
  electrical design needs the conveyor layout finalized earlier than for most
  other equipment that can be installed into a generic space.
example: >
  A contractor building a distribution center coordinates the conveyor
  system's structural support points with the building's steel erection
  schedule, since the conveyor manufacturer's layout determines exactly where
  additional steel bracing needs to go.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-structural-engineer|Structural Engineer]] designs support for the
  conveyor's specific load path, and the [[career-electrician|Electrician]]
  distributes power along its route.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A conveyor system's final layout isn't locked in until after the building's
  structural steel is erected, and the manufacturer's required support points
  don't align with the steel that's already up, forcing costly supplemental
  bracing.
relatedIds:
  - career-structural-engineer
  - career-electrician
---
