id: concept-submittal-log
title: Submittal Log
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A tracking document listing every required [[concept-submittal|submittal]]
  for a project, shop drawings, product data, samples, its due date, review
  status, and current location in the approval cycle, the working tool behind
  the broader submittal process.
whyItMatters: >
  A submittal sitting unreviewed past its due date only becomes a real
  problem once it's caught, an actively maintained log is what actually
  surfaces that gap before the affected material's lead time makes the delay
  unrecoverable.
example: >
  A submittal log flags that a curtain wall shop drawing has been sitting
  with the architect for review three weeks past its due date, prompting a
  follow-up call before the delay eats into the glazing subcontractor's own
  lead time.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-document-control-specialist|Document Control Specialist]] owns
  the submittal log, flagging overdue items before their delay becomes
  unrecoverable.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A subcontractor starts fabricating custom millwork based on a shop drawing
  it submitted for review, assuming no objection after a few weeks means
  approval. The submittal log would have shown the drawing was still sitting
  unreviewed, not approved, and the architect's markup, once it finally
  comes back, requires a dimension change the subcontractor now has to
  rework into pieces already cut.
