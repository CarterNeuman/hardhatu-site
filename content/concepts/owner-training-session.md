---
id: concept-owner-training-session
title: Owner Training Session (Closeout)
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A hands-on session, held near project closeout, where the contractor or
  equipment vendor walks the owner's facilities staff through how to actually
  operate and maintain newly installed building systems, rather than just
  handing over a binder of manuals.
whyItMatters: >
  The [[concept-om-manuals|O&M manuals]] contain the information, but a
  manual sitting unread on a shelf doesn't actually prepare anyone to operate
  unfamiliar equipment under real conditions, which is why a live training
  session, where staff can ask questions and watch the system actually run, is
  usually what owners actually rely on once the contractor is gone.
example: >
  A new building automation system gets a dedicated owner training session
  before closeout, where the vendor walks the facilities team through actually
  adjusting setpoints and responding to alarms on the live system, rather than
  leaving staff to figure out an unfamiliar interface from the printed manual
  alone once something goes wrong.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-commissioning-agent|Commissioning Agent]] coordinates owner
  training sessions as part of the broader commissioning process, and the
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] and their staff are the
  ones who actually sit through the training and have to retain it.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project turns over a complete set of O&M manuals but skips an actual
  hands-on training session, and facilities staff are left trying to learn an
  unfamiliar system's quirks through trial and error on live equipment instead
  of through guided instruction while the installing contractor was still
  available to answer questions.
