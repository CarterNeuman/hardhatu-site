id: concept-anchor-bolts
title: Anchor Bolts
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "05, Metals"
definition: >
  Threaded steel bolts embedded into a concrete footing before the pour, left
  protruding so that a structural steel column can be bolted directly to the
  foundation once the concrete cures and steel erection begins.
whyItMatters: >
  Anchor bolts are the actual connection between a building's steel frame and
  its foundation, and their exact placement has to match the steel
  fabricator's drawings precisely, since a steel column's base plate is
  drilled for bolts in very specific locations that can't easily shift once
  the concrete has cured.
example: >
  Before a footing is poured, a crew sets a template holding the anchor bolts
  in the exact pattern the steel column's base plate will require, and
  double-checks the layout against the steel drawings before the concrete
  locks the bolts permanently in place.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-ironworker|Ironworker]] bolts a column's base plate down onto the
  anchor bolts as the very first step of steel erection, and relies entirely
  on those bolts having been set correctly weeks earlier during the foundation
  pour.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A foundation crew sets anchor bolts slightly out of position relative to the
  steel drawings, an error that isn't caught until a steel crew shows up weeks
  later to set the first column and finds the base plate's bolt holes don't
  line up, stalling erection until the problem is corrected.
