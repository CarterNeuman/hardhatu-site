---
id: concept-miscellaneous-metals
title: Miscellaneous Metal Fabrications
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "05, Metals"
definition: >
  The custom-fabricated steel and aluminum items on a project that don't fall
  under structural steel as their own scope, stair stringers and handrails,
  ladders, guardrails, embedded steel plates and angles, and similar
  shop-fabricated pieces, each typically built to project-specific shop
  drawings rather than off a standard catalog.
whyItMatters: >
  Because miscellaneous metals items are custom shop-fabricated rather than
  stock catalog products, they carry real lead time and depend on accurate
  field dimensions, a late or wrong measurement can hold up an item a general
  contractor assumed would simply show up when needed.
example: >
  A general contractor submits a custom embedded steel plate's shop drawings to
  the fabricator well ahead of the concrete pour it needs to be cast into,
  giving the fabricator enough lead time to turn the piece around before the
  pour date on the schedule.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-ironworker|Ironworker]] or specialty metals installer sets
  miscellaneous metal items in the field, working from the fabricator's
  approved shop drawings.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor assumes a custom embedded steel plate will simply show
  up when needed and doesn't get shop drawings to the fabricator until the
  concrete pour is already scheduled. The fabricator can't turn around custom
  shop drawings and fabrication in the time remaining, and the pour has to be
  delayed a week waiting on a miscellaneous metals item nobody flagged as a
  long-lead custom fabrication.
