---
id: concept-teaming-agreement
title: Teaming Agreement
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A pre-bid contract between two or more companies agreeing to pursue a specific
  project together, usually with one acting as prime and the other as a
  committed subcontractor, signed before the owner has even made an award
  decision.
whyItMatters: >
  It's distinct from a joint venture because the parties aren't forming a new
  combined entity to jointly hold the contract; instead, one company still
  intends to be the sole prime contractor and is simply locking in a specific
  partner's commitment to perform as a subcontractor if the team wins.
example: >
  A general contractor pursuing a specialized laboratory project signs a teaming
  agreement with a mechanical subcontractor experienced in cleanroom work,
  committing that subcontractor exclusively to the GC's bid in exchange for
  being named as the mechanical sub if the team is awarded the project.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-business-development-manager|Business Development Manager]]
  negotiates teaming agreements to lock in strong subcontractor partners ahead
  of a competitive pursuit, and the
  [[career-construction-attorney|Construction Attorney]] drafts the
  agreement's exclusivity and good-faith negotiation terms.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A team signs a loosely worded teaming agreement assuming it guarantees the
  named subcontractor the actual work if the bid is won, without realizing a
  poorly drafted agreement may only commit the parties to negotiate in good
  faith afterward, not to a final, binding subcontract.
