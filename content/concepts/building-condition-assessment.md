---
id: concept-building-condition-assessment
title: Building Condition Assessment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "02, Existing Conditions"
definition: >
  A documented physical inspection of a building's structural, mechanical,
  electrical, and envelope systems to establish their current condition and
  remaining useful life, used before a renovation, acquisition, or major
  capital planning decision.
whyItMatters: >
  A building condition assessment is about physical condition rather than
  historical significance, the distinction from a Historic Structure Report,
  and it's often the document that determines whether a renovation budget is
  realistic before design even begins.
example: >
  Before acquiring an older office building, a buyer commissions a building
  condition assessment that identifies an aging roof and outdated electrical
  system nearing the end of their useful life, informing the capital budget
  built into the purchase decision.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-property-condition-assessor|Property Condition Assessor]] performs
  the physical inspection and writes the assessment report, while the
  [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] commissions and
  reviews it to inform renovation or acquisition budgeting.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A renovation budget is set without a building condition assessment, and
  mid-project discovery of failing mechanical systems well past their useful
  life forces a scope and budget increase that proper upfront assessment would
  have caught.
