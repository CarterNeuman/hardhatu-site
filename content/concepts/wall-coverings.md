id: concept-wall-coverings
title: Wall Coverings
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "09, Finishes"
definition: >
  Non-paint wall finishes applied as a covering rather than a coating, vinyl
  wallcovering, wallpaper, fabric-wrapped panels, and acoustic wall coverings,
  each rated for a specific level of durability and cleanability and, on many
  commercial projects, required to meet a minimum flame spread rating under the
  building's interior finish requirements.
whyItMatters: >
  Commercial wall coverings, unlike residential wallpaper, have to meet Class A
  flame spread ratings in most occupancies, a requirement that's easy to
  overlook if a design team specifies a covering based purely on appearance
  without checking its fire rating for the specific occupancy it's going into.
example: >
  A hospital corridor's vinyl wall covering is selected specifically because it
  carries a Class A flame spread rating and a documented antimicrobial,
  cleanable surface, requirements a decorative residential-grade wallpaper
  wouldn't meet.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-drywall-installer|Drywall Installer]] or specialty wallcovering
  installer applies commercial wall coverings, working from a substrate that's
  typically finished to a higher gypsum board finish level than paint alone
  would require.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A design team selects a fabric wall covering for a corridor based on color
  and texture without confirming its flame spread rating meets the occupancy's
  requirement. The mistake isn't caught until the fire marshal reviews finish
  material submittals during permitting, forcing a late substitution that
  delays the finish schedule.
