id: concept-price-list-price-database
title: Price List / Price Database (Xactimate)
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The regularly updated regional database of unit costs for labor and materials
  that estimating software like Xactimate draws on to price a repair line
  item, reflecting local market rates that are refreshed on a recurring
  schedule.
whyItMatters: >
  Disputes between contractors and insurers often come down to which price list
  version or regional database was used, since an outdated or mismatched price
  list can underfund a repair relative to current local market costs.
example: >
  A contractor's estimate for drywall repair comes in well above an insurer's
  adjuster estimate, and comparing the two reveals the adjuster's software was
  referencing a price list from an earlier update cycle that hadn't yet caught
  up to a recent regional material cost increase.
careerAngle: >
  Estimators should confirm which price list version and region code an
  insurer's estimate used before assuming a pricing gap reflects padding
  rather than a stale database.
whatGoesWrong: >
  People sometimes assume all estimating software uses identical pricing
  everywhere, when the price list is regionally specific and updated on its
  own schedule, so two estimates built the same week can still differ if one
  draws from an older database snapshot.
