---
id: concept-casework
title: Casework
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "06, Wood, Plastics & Composites"
definition: >
  Built-in cabinetry, such as kitchen and bathroom cabinets, classroom storage,
  and reception desks, either shop-fabricated off site to exact field dimensions
  or, on a tighter budget, assembled from a manufacturer's stock modular units,
  distinct from loose furniture since it's permanently attached to the building.
whyItMatters: >
  Shop-fabricated casework has to be built from accurate field dimensions taken
  after the surrounding walls and floors actually exist, not from the original
  drawings, since real field conditions rarely match the drawings exactly.
example: >
  A millwork shop sends a field measurer to a finished classroom to verify actual
  wall dimensions before fabricating custom casework, rather than building
  strictly to the dimensions shown on the original architectural drawings.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-carpenter|Carpenter]] installs shop-fabricated casework on site,
  working from the shop drawings the millwork shop produced from its own field
  measurements.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A millwork shop fabricates an entire building's worth of classroom casework
  directly from the architectural drawings, skipping a field measurement, and
  several rooms come in slightly out of square. The casework leaves visible gaps
  against the wall that have to be scribed and filled on site.
