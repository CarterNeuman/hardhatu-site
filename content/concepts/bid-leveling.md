---
id: concept-bid-leveling
title: Bid Leveling
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The process of comparing multiple bids or subcontractor quotes on an apples-to-apples
  basis, adjusting for differences in scope, exclusions, and assumptions, so the
  numbers can actually be compared fairly instead of just compared as raw totals.
whyItMatters: >
  Two subcontractor quotes for "electrical" can differ by tens of thousands of dollars
  for reasons that have nothing to do with efficiency: one might exclude permits,
  another might exclude material delivery. Without leveling, the lowest number can just
  be the most incomplete one.
example: >
  An estimator receives three drywall quotes. One excludes fire-taping, another excludes
  material delivery, and the third includes both. Before comparing totals, the estimator
  adds the missing scope's estimated cost back into the two incomplete quotes so all
  three numbers reflect the same actual work.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-estimator|Estimator]] does the hands-on work of leveling subcontractor
  quotes before [[concept-buyout|buyout]], since picking the wrong number without
  leveling can quietly bake a scope gap into the contract. A
  [[career-senior-estimator|Senior Estimator]] reviews the leveled comparison before a
  final subcontractor is selected, especially on large trade packages.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An estimator picks the lowest of three framing quotes without leveling them first,
  missing that the low bidder excluded temporary shoring the other two included. The
  framing subcontractor later submits a change order for the shoring once it turns out
  to be required, and the "savings" from picking the low number disappears, plus a
  delay while the shoring gets sourced.
