id: concept-battery-energy-storage-system
title: Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  A large-scale battery installation that stores electricity, often from an
  on-site solar system or the grid during low-cost periods, for later use
  during peak demand or a utility outage, distinct from a building's
  [[concept-uninterruptible-power-supply|UPS]] since a BESS is typically
  sized to shift or shave a building's overall energy use rather than just
  bridge a brief gap for critical equipment.
whyItMatters: >
  A BESS installation has its own fire and life-safety code requirements,
  since large lithium-ion battery installations carry a real fire risk if
  damaged or improperly installed, requiring specific separation,
  ventilation, and fire suppression considerations that a standard
  electrical room wouldn't otherwise need.
example: >
  A commercial building's BESS is installed in a dedicated room with
  fire-rated separation and ventilation specifically required for its
  battery chemistry, paired with the building's solar array to store
  excess daytime generation for use during the evening peak demand period.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] installs the battery system and
  its electrical connections. A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]]
  designs the system's sizing and the fire and life-safety provisions its
  battery chemistry requires.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor installs a BESS in a standard electrical room without the
  additional fire-rated separation and ventilation its specific battery
  chemistry requires, treating it like conventional electrical equipment. A
  fire marshal's plan review catches the deficiency before installation is
  complete, requiring the room to be reconstructed to the battery-specific
  fire code requirements before the system can be energized.
