id: concept-construction-management-software
title: Construction Management Software
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  The general category of platforms, with Procore the best-known example on this site
  but far from the only one, that centralize a project's budget, schedule, drawings,
  RFIs, submittals, and field documentation in one connected system, rather than
  scattered across spreadsheets, email threads, and paper.
whyItMatters: >
  Before this category of software existed, a project's real status often lived in
  whichever person's inbox had the most recent update, which is a common root cause of
  disputes and delays, someone acting on an outdated drawing, or someone unaware of a
  change someone else already knew about. Centralizing that information doesn't eliminate every
  problem, but it removes "nobody saw the update" as an excuse.
example: >
  A subcontractor uploads a required safety inspection photo directly into the project's
  construction management platform, tagged to its exact location on the floor plan,
  where it's immediately visible to the project manager, superintendent, and owner
  without anyone forwarding an email.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] runs budget, RFI, and change order
  tracking through the platform as the daily backbone of the job. A
  [[career-construction-data-analyst|Construction Data Analyst]] pulls historical data
  out of the platform to analyze cost and schedule trends across many projects at once,
  a use the software wasn't originally built around but has become central to as the
  data has accumulated.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project team keeps using email and spreadsheets alongside the construction management
  platform for a subset of communications, assuming it's faster for quick questions. A
  decision made over email never makes it into the platform's official record, and a
  later dispute over what was actually agreed to has no documented trail to point to.
