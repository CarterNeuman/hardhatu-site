---
id: concept-resource-loading
title: Resource Loading
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  Assigning specific labor, equipment, or crew-size resources to each
  activity in a project schedule, turning a simple sequence of dates into a
  plan that reflects whether the project actually has enough people and
  equipment available to hit those dates.
whyItMatters: >
  A schedule without resource loading can look perfectly achievable on paper
  while quietly assuming the same crew is in two places at once, a conflict
  that only becomes visible once resources are actually assigned to each
  activity.
example: >
  A scheduler resource-loads a project's finish activities and discovers
  three separate tasks are all planned to use the same specialized flooring
  crew during the same week, a conflict invisible in the schedule's dates
  alone.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-scheduler|Scheduler]] resource-loads the schedule to confirm the
  plan is actually achievable with the crews and equipment the project really
  has available.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A schedule shows three activities finishing on time, but none of them
  account for the fact that all three need the same specialized crew during
  the same week, a conflict resource loading would have caught. The schedule
  quietly becomes unachievable the moment all three activities actually
  compete for that one crew.
