id: concept-acoustic-treatment
title: Acoustic Treatment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "09, Finishes"
definition: >
  Materials and assemblies, such as sound-absorbing panels, acoustic ceiling tile, and
  sound-rated wall assemblies with additional insulation and layers of drywall,
  specified to control how sound behaves within and between spaces, addressing both
  unwanted noise transfer between rooms and excessive reverberation within a single
  room.
whyItMatters: >
  Acoustic performance is specified using standardized ratings, such as Sound
  Transmission Class (STC) for sound blocked between spaces and Noise Reduction
  Coefficient (NRC) for sound absorbed within a room, and confusing the two, or substituting a
  similar-looking material with a different rating, can leave a space that looks
  finished but doesn't actually perform the way it was designed to acoustically.
example: >
  A conference room wall is built as a sound-rated assembly with staggered studs,
  additional insulation, and double layers of drywall to achieve a specific STC
  rating, rather than a standard partition wall that would let conversations carry
  through to the adjacent space.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-carpenter|Carpenter]] builds sound-rated wall assemblies to the
  specific detail the acoustic design calls for, since a shortcut in the assembly, such as a
  missed insulation layer or an unsealed penetration, can measurably reduce the wall's
  actual sound isolation. An [[concept-architect|Architect]] specifies the acoustic
  rating requirements based on the space's actual use, and coordinates the wall and
  ceiling assemblies needed to achieve them.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor builds a sound-rated wall between two conference rooms but leaves a
  gap where electrical outlet boxes on opposite sides of the wall are mounted
  back-to-back in the same stud cavity, rather than staggering them. Sound leaks
  directly through that shared cavity, and conversations carry between the rooms
  despite the wall otherwise meeting its acoustic rating everywhere else.
