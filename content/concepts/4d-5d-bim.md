id: concept-4d-5d-bim
title: 4D/5D BIM
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  An extension of a [[concept-bim|BIM]] model that links its 3D elements to additional
  dimensions of project data: 4D ties the model to the project schedule, so a viewer can
  watch the building assemble in sequence over time; 5D ties it to cost, so quantities
  in the model connect directly to a live budget.
whyItMatters: >
  A traditional schedule or budget lives in a separate document from the model, which
  means the sequence or cost implications of a design change aren't automatically visible
  in either one. Linking the model to time and cost data makes the schedule and budget
  impact of a change visible immediately, in the same place the design change was made.
example: >
  A scheduler links the coordinated model to the project schedule and produces a 4D
  simulation showing the building assembling floor by floor over the construction
  timeline, a walkthrough that reveals a sequencing conflict between two crews
  scheduled to occupy the same area in the same week.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-scheduler|Scheduler]] uses 4D BIM to visualize and validate a proposed
  sequence before committing to it, catching space conflicts a written schedule alone
  wouldn't surface. A [[career-cost-engineer|Cost Engineer]] uses the 5D dimension to tie
  model quantities directly to cost data, keeping the budget synced with the design as it
  evolves.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A team builds a 4D sequence simulation early in design but never updates it as the
  schedule changes throughout construction. The simulation drifts further from the actual
  plan with each revision, until relying on it for real sequencing decisions becomes
  riskier than not having one at all.
