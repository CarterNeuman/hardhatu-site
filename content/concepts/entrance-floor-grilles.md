---
id: concept-entrance-floor-grilles
title: Entrance Floor Grilles
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Recessed metal or rubber grating systems set into the entry floor to scrape
  and trap dirt and moisture from shoes before it tracks into the building,
  formed into the slab during construction rather than surface-mounted like a
  removable mat.
whyItMatters: >
  Unlike a removable mat, a recessed floor grille has to be coordinated with
  the structural slab and finish flooring during construction, since the
  recessed frame is cast or set into the floor rather than simply placed on
  top of it.
example: >
  A contractor forms a recessed frame for an entrance floor grille into the
  slab at a building's main entry before the pour, coordinating the exact
  dimensions with the grille manufacturer's specifications.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates the recessed
  frame's formwork with the concrete schedule, since it can't be added after
  the slab is poured.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building's entrance slab is poured without the recessed frame for a
  planned floor grille, and the owner ends up with a surface-mounted mat
  instead of the flush, built-in solution the design called for.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
