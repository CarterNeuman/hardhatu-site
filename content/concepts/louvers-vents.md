id: concept-louvers-vents
title: Louvers & Vents
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Fixed or adjustable openings in walls, doors, or roofs with angled blades
  that allow air to pass through while blocking rain, debris, or direct
  sightlines, used for mechanical equipment intake/exhaust, attic and
  crawlspace ventilation, and smoke venting.
whyItMatters: >
  A louver's free area, the percentage of its opening that actually passes air
  once the blades are accounted for, has to match what the mechanical design
  assumed, or the equipment behind it won't get the airflow it needs.
example: >
  An HVAC contractor installs louvers for a rooftop unit's outside air intake,
  confirming the model's published free-area rating meets the airflow the
  mechanical engineer specified, rather than assuming any louver of the right
  size will do.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-sheet-metal-worker|Sheet Metal Worker]] or
  [[career-hvac-technician|HVAC Technician]] installs and flashes louvers
  correctly to keep them weathertight while meeting the mechanical design's
  airflow requirements.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A substitute louver with a lower free-area rating gets installed in place of
  the specified model, and the rooftop unit behind it can't pull enough
  outside air, leaving the mechanical system undersized for a problem no one
  traces back to the louver for weeks.
relatedIds:
  - career-sheet-metal-worker
  - career-hvac-technician
