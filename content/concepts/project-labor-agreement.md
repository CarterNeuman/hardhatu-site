id: concept-project-labor-agreement
title: Project Labor Agreement (PLA)
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A pre-hire collective bargaining agreement covering all contractors and
  subcontractors working on a specific construction project, setting common
  wage, work-rule, and dispute-resolution terms for the life of that project,
  whether or not individual contractors are otherwise unionized.
whyItMatters: >
  A PLA applies project-wide rather than company-wide, so a normally non-union
  contractor that wins work on a PLA-covered project still has to follow the
  agreement's terms for that job, which is a major factor in how contractors
  price and staff bids on PLA-covered public projects.
example: >
  A state agency requires a PLA on a large public infrastructure project to
  ensure consistent labor terms and reduce the risk of labor disruptions
  across dozens of subcontractors. A non-union mechanical subcontractor that
  wins a piece of the work still has to staff that specific project under the
  PLA's terms, even though its other work elsewhere remains non-union.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-hr-manager-construction|HR Manager]] manages how PLA terms apply
  to project staffing even for an otherwise non-union contractor, and the
  [[career-business-development-manager|Business Development Manager]] weighs
  PLA requirements into the bid-or-no-bid decision on covered public work.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor assumes its normal non-union labor practices and wage structure
  carry over automatically onto a PLA-covered project, without accounting for
  the project-specific terms the PLA actually requires for everyone working on
  that job.
