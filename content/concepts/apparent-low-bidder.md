id: concept-apparent-low-bidder
title: Apparent Low Bidder
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The bidder who submitted the lowest total price at bid opening, before that
  bid has actually been verified as complete, responsive, and from a
  responsible contractor, and before the award is formally made.
whyItMatters: >
  Being the apparent low bidder isn't the same as being awarded the contract;
  the owner still has to confirm the bid is responsive to every solicitation
  requirement and that the bidder is responsible enough to actually perform,
  and either check can disqualify an apparent low bidder before the award is
  finalized.
example: >
  A public bid opening reveals one contractor's price as clearly the lowest,
  making it the apparent low bidder. During the agency's follow-up review,
  that bidder is found to have failed to submit a required bid bond,
  disqualifying it despite having the lowest number, and the award instead
  goes to the next-lowest bidder whose submission was complete.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-bid-coordinator|Bid Coordinator]] identifies the apparent low
  bidder at bid opening, and the [[career-procurement-manager|Procurement
  Manager]] completes the responsiveness and responsibility review before that
  status becomes an actual award.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A bidder celebrates being announced as the apparent low bidder and assumes the
  contract is effectively secured, without realizing the award can still be
  lost if the agency's follow-up review turns up a disqualifying gap in the
  bid's completeness or the bidder's qualifications.
relatedIds:
