---
id: concept-bid-tabulation
title: Bid Tabulation
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A side-by-side summary listing every bidder's price and key terms for a
  project, line by line, used to compare bids consistently and identify the
  apparent low bidder, often required to be made public on government
  projects.
whyItMatters: >
  Comparing bids fairly requires looking at the same line items the same way
  across every submission, and a bid tabulation is what forces that
  consistency, surfacing a bidder who left out a required item or structured
  pricing differently before an owner commits to an award based on an
  apples-to-oranges comparison.
example: >
  A public agency receives six bids on a road project. The bid tabulation lines
  up each bidder's total price, unit prices, and required bid bond alongside
  each other, revealing that one low bidder actually omitted a required
  alternate price, disqualifying that bid from being the true apparent low
  bidder once the omission is caught.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-bid-coordinator|Bid Coordinator]] prepares the bid tabulation
  immediately after bid opening, and the [[career-procurement-manager|
  Procurement Manager]] uses it to verify every bid's completeness before an
  award recommendation goes forward.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner announces an apparent low bidder based on a quick glance at submitted
  totals, without a proper bid tabulation catching that the low number left
  out a required scope item, triggering a dispute once a competing bidder
  points out the comparison wasn't actually apples to apples.
