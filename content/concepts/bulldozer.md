---
id: concept-bulldozer
title: Bulldozer
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "31, Earthwork"
definition: >
  A tracked piece of heavy equipment with a wide, flat blade mounted on the
  front, used to push and rough-grade large volumes of loose material across a
  site rather than to dig precisely in one spot.
whyItMatters: >
  A bulldozer's job is moving a lot of material roughly into place, clearing,
  rough grading, pushing spoil piles, fast, before more precise equipment,
  like a motor grader, comes in afterward to cut the exact final elevations
  the design actually calls for.
example: >
  Before a site's final grading begins, a bulldozer clears vegetation and
  pushes a large volume of loose fill roughly into place across the site,
  getting the ground close enough to final grade that a motor grader can
  efficiently finish the precise cuts afterward.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-equipment-operator|Equipment Operator]] operates a bulldozer
  during the earliest, roughest phase of earthwork, before any of the site's
  more precise grading equipment gets involved.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An operator pushes fill with a bulldozer without checking the site's grading
  plan, over-pushing material into an area the design actually calls for to
  stay lower. The error isn't caught until a surveyor checks elevations later,
  by which point the excess material has to be removed and relocated.
