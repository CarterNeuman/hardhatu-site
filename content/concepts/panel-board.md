id: concept-panel-board
title: Panel Board
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "26, Electrical"
definition: >
  A metal enclosure containing circuit breakers that distribute electrical power from
  a building's main service to individual circuits, the point where a building's
  overall power supply gets divided into the branch circuits that actually feed
  outlets, lighting, and equipment.
whyItMatters: >
  A panel board's labeling has to accurately identify what each breaker controls,
  since anyone who needs to shut off power in an emergency, or safely work on a
  circuit later, depends entirely on that label being correct: a mislabeled panel
  turns a routine shutoff into a guessing game with a live electrical system.
example: >
  An electrician sets and wires a panel board serving a floor's lighting and
  receptacle circuits, then labels every breaker to match the as-built circuit
  directory rather than a generic template, so anyone servicing the panel later knows
  exactly what each breaker controls.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] installs and terminates panel boards to the
  electrical drawings and the National Electrical Code, then verifies and labels every
  circuit before closeout. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] relies
  on accurate panel labeling for years after construction, since it's often the first
  thing consulted during any electrical troubleshooting or emergency shutoff.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An electrical crew wires and labels a panel board quickly near the end of a rushed
  project, mislabeling several breakers that were shuffled during a late circuit
  change. Months after occupancy, a maintenance technician shuts off what the label
  says is a storage room circuit to make a repair, only to accidentally cut power to
  an occupied office instead.
