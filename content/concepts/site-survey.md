---
id: concept-site-survey
title: Site Survey
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "02, Existing Conditions"
definition: >
  A precise measurement of a property's existing conditions, such as boundaries,
  elevations, existing structures, and utility locations, performed before design and
  construction to establish the accurate baseline everything else gets built from.
whyItMatters: >
  Design and construction decisions depend entirely on accurate survey data. A site
  survey with even small errors can cascade into a building placed too close to a
  property line, or a foundation designed for elevations that don't match reality.
example: >
  Before design begins on a new warehouse, a licensed surveyor establishes the property
  boundaries, existing grade elevations, and the location of underground utilities,
  giving the design team an accurate base map to design against.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-surveyor|Surveyor]] performs and certifies the survey data every other
  discipline relies on. A [[career-bim-vdc-specialist|BIM/VDC Specialist]] often
  incorporates survey data directly into the 3D model used for coordination, so an
  error in the survey propagates into every discipline's model built on top of it.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project proceeds using an outdated survey from several years earlier, not realizing
  a neighboring property's recent grading changed the drainage pattern along the shared
  property line. Construction disturbs that altered drainage, sending water onto the
  neighboring property mid-project.
