id: concept-building-energy-benchmarking
title: Building Energy Benchmarking (ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager)
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  Tracking a building's actual, metered energy and water use over time, most
  commonly     through the EPA's free ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager tool,
  which compares a building against     similar buildings nationwide and
  produces a 1-100 score; a building scoring 75 or above     qualifies for
  ENERGY STAR certification.
whyItMatters: >
  Benchmarking measures actual operational performance using real utility
  bills, which makes     it the factual record a carbon performance cap's
  emissions calculation relies on, distinct     from
  [[concept-energy-modeling|energy modeling]], which predicts performance
  before a     building is even built or occupied.
example: >
  A property manager uploads a full calendar year of metered electricity and
  gas bills into     Portfolio Manager, generating an ENERGY STAR score of 68,
  below the certification threshold,     which identifies the building as a
  priority candidate for an efficiency retrofit.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-sustainability-leed-consultant|Sustainability/LEED Consultant]]
  or facilities     manager maintains a building's ongoing benchmarking data,
  since many jurisdictions require     annual filing and a lapse in reporting
  can itself trigger a compliance penalty separate from     the building's
  actual energy performance.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner benchmarks a building once at turnover and assumes that single
  score is     permanent, when ENERGY STAR scores are relative to that year's
  peer-building data and a     building's own actual operational changes, and
  have to be recalculated with fresh metered     data every reporting cycle.
