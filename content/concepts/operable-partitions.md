---
id: concept-operable-partitions
title: Operable Partitions
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Large, movable wall panels, often accordion-folding or panel-track systems, that
  slide open or closed to divide or combine adjacent rooms, such as a hotel
  ballroom or a school cafeteria, typically suspended from an overhead track that
  has to be designed into the structure above rather than added after the fact.
whyItMatters: >
  Operable partitions are heavy, and the overhead structural support their track
  hangs from has to be coordinated with the structural engineer early in design,
  retrofitting adequate support into an already-built structure is far more
  disruptive than designing for it up front.
example: >
  A hotel's ballroom is designed around a structural steel beam sized specifically
  to carry the weight of an overhead operable partition track, coordinated between
  the structural engineer and the partition manufacturer during design.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-structural-engineer|Structural Engineer]] sizes the overhead support
  the track hangs from. A [[career-carpenter|Carpenter]] or specialty installer
  sets the track and panels.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor installs an operable partition track using the structural
  support shown in the original drawings, only to discover during installation
  that the manufacturer's actual selected unit weighs significantly more than what
  the structural engineer designed for, requiring a structural retrofit mid-project.
