---
id: concept-loading-dock-equipment
title: Loading Dock Equipment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  The dock levelers, dock seals and shelters, vehicle restraints, and overhead
  doors installed at a building's loading dock to bridge the gap between a truck
  bed and the warehouse floor and seal the opening against weather, sized to the
  specific truck types and dock heights the building's actual users will need.
whyItMatters: >
  A dock leveler or vehicle restraint sized for the wrong truck type creates a
  real safety hazard: a trailer that shifts or drops away from the dock mid-unload
  can seriously injure a forklift operator crossing the gap.
example: >
  A distribution center's loading dock is designed with hydraulic dock levelers
  and powered vehicle restraints rated for the specific range of trailer heights
  the tenant's fleet actually uses, confirmed with the tenant before the equipment
  is ordered.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] or
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] coordinates equipment
  selection against the tenant's actual fleet
  specs. A [[career-millwright|Millwright]] or specialty installer sets and aligns
  the mechanical dock equipment.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor installs standard dock levelers sized for typical trailer
  heights without confirming the tenant's actual fleet. The tenant's trucks run
  several inches outside the leveler's rated range, forklift operators report the
  plate doesn't seat properly, and the levelers have to be replaced after
  occupancy.
relatedIds:
