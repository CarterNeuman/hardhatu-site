id: concept-ductwork-insulation
title: Ductwork Insulation
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "23, HVAC"
definition: >
  Insulation wrapped around or lining a building's HVAC ductwork, applied for
  two distinct reasons depending on the duct, energy efficiency on supply
  ducts running through unconditioned spaces, and condensation control on
  ducts carrying cold air through warm, humid spaces, where an uninsulated
  duct's cold surface would otherwise sweat.
whyItMatters: >
  Insulating a cold-air duct without also managing the vapor barrier around
  that insulation can make condensation worse rather than better, moisture
  still reaches the duct's cold surface through a gap in the vapor barrier and
  gets trapped against it, so the insulation and its vapor barrier have to be
  installed as one continuous, sealed system.
example: >
  A sheet metal contractor wraps supply ductwork carrying chilled air with
  insulation that includes a continuous, taped vapor barrier at every seam and
  joint, preventing humid ambient air from reaching the duct's cold surface
  anywhere along its run.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-sheet-metal-worker|Sheet Metal Worker]] fabricates and installs
  ductwork, often coordinating with a separate insulation crew who wraps it
  once it's hung.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An insulation crew wraps a cold-air duct but leaves several sections of the
  vapor barrier untaped at the seams to save time. Humid air reaches the
  duct's cold surface through those gaps, and condensation drips from the
  ceiling below within the first humid season, requiring the insulation to be
  removed and reinstalled correctly.
