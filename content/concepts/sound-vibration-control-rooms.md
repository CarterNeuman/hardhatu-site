---
id: concept-sound-vibration-control-rooms
title: Sound & Vibration Control Rooms
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "13, Special Construction"
definition: >
  Rooms built with specialized isolation, floating floors, resilient wall
  channels, and isolated mechanical mounts, to control sound transmission or
  vibration, used for recording studios, hospital imaging suites, and rooms
  housing sensitive equipment near vibration sources.
whyItMatters: >
  Sound and vibration isolation only works if every connection between the
  isolated room and the rest of the building is actually broken; a single
  rigid connection point, a conduit, a shared stud, a mechanical duct, can
  transmit enough sound or vibration to defeat the whole system.
example: >
  A contractor building an isolated recording studio room-within-a-room
  confirms every penetration through the isolated wall, including electrical
  conduit, uses flexible isolation boots rather than rigid connections that
  would bridge the sound isolation.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] designs vibration-isolated mechanical
  mounts for sensitive equipment rooms, and the
  [[career-structural-engineer|Structural Engineer]] reviews floating floor
  assemblies for isolated spaces.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An isolated room's electrical conduit is run rigidly through the isolation
  wall to save time, and acoustic testing after completion reveals the room
  fails its isolation target because sound is bridging through that one rigid
  connection.
relatedIds:
  - career-mep-engineer
  - career-structural-engineer
