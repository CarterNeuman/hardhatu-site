---
id: concept-joint-check-agreement
title: Joint Check Agreement
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  An arrangement where a payment is made out jointly to both a general
  contractor (or subcontractor) and a second party further down the chain,
  like a material supplier, so both must endorse the check before it can be
  cashed.
whyItMatters: >
  It gives a supplier or lower-tier sub added assurance of payment, since funds
  can't be diverted before reaching them, and it's often used when a supplier
  has expressed concern about a contractor's ability or willingness to pass
  payment through promptly.
example: >
  A material supplier threatens to stop delivery to a project over concerns
  about a subcontractor's payment history. The general contractor arranges for
  future payments covering that material to be issued as joint checks payable
  to both the subcontractor and the supplier, reassuring the supplier enough
  to keep deliveries coming.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-procurement-manager|Procurement Manager]] sets up joint check
  arrangements when a supplier's payment concerns threaten to delay material
  delivery, and the [[career-construction-accountant|Construction Accountant]]
  processes the joint payment and tracks that both required endorsements are
  obtained.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A party assumes a joint check protects them from every payment risk on a
  project, when it only addresses the specific payment it covers and does
  nothing to guarantee payment on the rest of the contract.
relatedIds:
