id: concept-safety-orientation
title: Safety Orientation
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  The initial training every worker or visitor completes before starting work on a
  jobsite, covering site-specific hazards, required PPE, emergency procedures, and site
  rules, distinct from the ongoing, day-to-day training a
  [[concept-toolbox-talk|toolbox talk]] provides.
whyItMatters: >
  Every jobsite has hazards specific to that project, such as its layout, its active work
  areas, and its emergency exits, that no amount of general industry experience
  automatically teaches. Orientation exists to close that gap before someone's first day of actual work
  begins, not after.
example: >
  A new hire completes a site orientation covering the location of first-aid stations,
  the site's specific PPE requirements, and which areas are currently active excavation
  zones, information a worker experienced elsewhere on other sites still wouldn't have
  for this particular one.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-laborer|Construction Laborer]] starting a new job, often their entry point
  into the industry, gets their first real exposure to jobsite safety culture through
  orientation. A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] designs and delivers the
  orientation program and makes sure it's actually specific to the current site, not a
  generic slideshow reused across every project.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project reuses the same generic orientation slideshow across multiple sites without
  updating it for a site's actual current hazards. A new worker follows outdated
  information about which area is an active excavation zone, since the orientation never
  reflected that this site's layout is different from the last one.
