id: concept-osha-whistleblower-protection
title: OSHA Whistleblower Protection (Section 11(c))
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  The provision of the OSH Act prohibiting an employer from firing, demoting, or
  otherwise retaliating against an employee for reporting a safety hazard,
  filing an OSHA complaint, or participating in an OSHA inspection.
whyItMatters: >
  Workers who fear retaliation often stay quiet about real hazards, so this
  protection exists specifically to keep the reporting channel open; a worker
  who believes they were punished for raising a safety concern has a narrow
  30-day window to file a complaint under this provision.
example: >
  A carpenter who reports an unsafe scaffold to OSHA is let go two days later;
  because the termination followed so closely after a protected safety
  complaint, the worker files an 11(c) whistleblower complaint, and OSHA
  investigates whether the termination was really retaliation.
careerAngle: >
  Supervisors need to document legitimate, unrelated performance issues
  separately and consistently, since firing a worker soon after a safety
  complaint invites a retaliation claim even when the termination was actually
  justified on other grounds.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Employers sometimes think they're free to discipline a worker for any reason
  as long as the stated reason isn't the safety complaint itself, when OSHA
  investigators look hard at timing and will find retaliation if the stated
  reason looks like a pretext.
