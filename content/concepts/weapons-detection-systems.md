---
id: concept-weapons-detection-systems
title: Weapons Detection Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "28, Electronic Safety & Security"
definition: >
  Walk-through metal detectors or newer AI-based weapons detection systems
  screening people entering a building, increasingly specified for schools,
  courthouses, and large venues, requiring dedicated queuing space and
  power/data infrastructure at each entry point.
whyItMatters: >
  These systems change how an entry vestibule has to be designed, since they
  need queuing space, a bypass lane for staff and deliveries, and power/data
  infrastructure that's much easier to plan into a new entry design than to
  retrofit into existing doors.
example: >
  An architect designs a school's main entry vestibule with enough queuing
  depth for a weapons detection system and a separate staff bypass lane,
  coordinated with the security consultant before the entry's final floor plan
  is set.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] coordinates the security
  consultant's equipment specifications with the architect's entry design
  early, since retrofitting queuing space later is disruptive.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A school's entry vestibule is designed without input from the security
  consultant, and the weapons detection system selected after construction
  doesn't fit the available queuing space, creating a bottleneck at the start
  of every school day.
relatedIds:
  - career-project-manager
