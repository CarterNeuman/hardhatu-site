---
id: concept-progressive-design-build
title: Progressive Design-Build
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A design-build delivery variant where the owner selects a single design-build
  team early, based on qualifications, and then negotiates design, scope, and
  price progressively as the design develops, rather than competitively
  bidding a complete design-build price up front.
whyItMatters: >
  It gives the owner more influence over design decisions and more flexibility
  to adjust scope as the project develops, compared to a traditional lump-sum
  design-build competition where the price is locked in before design has
  really started.
example: >
  A hospital system selects a design-build team for a new wing based on
  qualifications and relevant experience alone, then works with that team
  through schematic design before agreeing on a guaranteed maximum price,
  rather than having several teams compete on a fixed design-build price
  before design work has even begun.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-preconstruction-manager|Preconstruction Manager]] leads the
  progressive pricing and scope development that distinguishes this delivery
  method from a traditional design-build competition, and the [[career-owners-
  representative|Owner's Representative]] manages the owner's side of the
  ongoing negotiation as design and price develop together.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner treats a progressive design-build selection the same way as a
  traditional competitive design-build bid, comparing early-stage proposals on
  price alone, when the method is built around qualifications-based selection
  followed by collaborative price development.
relatedIds:
