id: concept-interior-signage-wayfinding
title: Interior Signage & Wayfinding
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Room numbers, directional signs, and ADA-compliant tactile and Braille signage
  installed throughout a finished building to help occupants and visitors navigate
  it, coordinated against the project's actual room numbering and code-required
  accessible signage locations rather than added as an afterthought once finishes
  are complete.
whyItMatters: >
  ADA sets specific requirements for tactile signage mounting height and location
  at each door, and getting that wrong after fabrication means re-fabricating and
  reinstalling every affected sign in the building rather than making one easy
  fix.
example: >
  An office building's signage package is finalized only after room numbers are
  confirmed against the as-built floor plan, with ADA-compliant tactile signs
  mounted at the latch side of each door at the code-required height.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] often reviews the signage
  plan before fabrication, since these signs stay in service for years. A
  [[career-carpenter|Carpenter]] or specialty installer mounts them once
  fabricated.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A signage package is fabricated from an early floor plan before a late
  room-renumbering change takes effect. The installer mounts an entire building's
  worth of signs bearing the wrong room numbers, and the whole package has to be
  refabricated and reinstalled.
