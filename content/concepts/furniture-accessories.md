---
id: concept-furniture-accessories
title: Furniture Accessories
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Smaller furnishing components, task lighting, monitor arms, cable
  management, and modesty panels, specified alongside a building's main
  furniture package to complete a workspace, usually coordinated by an
  interior designer or furniture dealer rather than the general contractor.
whyItMatters: >
  These items are easy to treat as an afterthought, but cable management and
  power access in particular have to be coordinated with the electrical
  rough-in, since retrofitting power to a furniture system after the floor is
  finished is far more disruptive.
example: >
  An interior designer specifies furniture accessories including under-desk
  cable trays and grommets, coordinating their exact locations with the
  electrician's floor-box layout before the open-office furniture package is
  installed.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] or interior designer
  coordinates accessory specification with the furniture dealer, and the
  [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] tracks accessory delivery against
  the furniture installation schedule.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Furniture accessories like cable trays are ordered separately from the main
  furniture package and arrive weeks after installation day, leaving desks in
  use with cables running loose across the floor in the meantime.
relatedIds:
  - career-facilities-manager
  - career-project-manager
---
