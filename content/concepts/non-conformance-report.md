id: concept-non-conformance-report
title: Non-Conformance Report (NCR)
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A formal, written document that flags completed or in-progress work that doesn't meet
  the contract's drawings, specifications, or quality requirements, creating a
  documented record of the problem and tracking it through to correction.
whyItMatters: >
  Without a formal record, a quality problem can get fixed informally and forgotten,
  with no proof it ever happened or that it was actually corrected. An NCR creates a
  paper trail that protects everyone if the same issue resurfaces, or if a dispute over
  responsibility comes up later.
example: >
  A quality control manager discovers a section of concrete slab poured out of
  tolerance for flatness. They issue an NCR documenting the deviation, the affected
  area, and the required corrective action, and the item stays open in the quality log
  until it's re-inspected and verified fixed.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]] issues and tracks NCRs as
  a core part of the job, using them to hold subcontractors accountable for correcting
  deficient work. A [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] often has to coordinate the
  actual rework a subcontractor performs to close out an NCR, since it usually affects
  the schedule for trades working nearby.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A quality control manager issues an NCR for out-of-spec welding, but nobody follows up
  to confirm the correction actually happened before the work is covered by the next
  trade. The NCR sits open on paper while the real defect gets buried behind drywall,
  discovered only much later during a [[concept-special-inspection|special inspection]]
  or, worse, a structural failure.
