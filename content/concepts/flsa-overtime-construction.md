id: concept-flsa-overtime-construction
title: Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) Overtime
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  The federal wage-and-hour law requiring most construction workers be paid one
  and a half times their regular rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a
  workweek, calculated separately from and in addition to any state
  prevailing-wage obligations on public projects.
whyItMatters: >
  On a public, prevailing-wage project, a worker's FLSA overtime rate is based
  on their actual regular hourly rate, which may be higher than the base
  prevailing wage once required fringe benefits are factored in, so prevailing
  wage compliance and FLSA overtime compliance have to be calculated together,
  not treated as separate problems.
example: >
  A laborer on a prevailing-wage public project works 45 hours in a week. The
  contractor has to pay overtime for the extra five hours at one and a half
  times the worker's actual regular rate, which includes certain
  fringe-benefit components, not simply one and a half times the published
  base prevailing wage rate alone.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-certified-payroll-specialist|Certified Payroll Specialist]]
  calculates FLSA overtime correctly alongside prevailing wage requirements on
  certified payrolls, and the [[career-hr-manager-construction|HR Manager]]
  sets company policy for how overtime is tracked and approved in the field.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor assumes paying one and a half times the base prevailing wage rate
  satisfies FLSA overtime obligations, without recalculating the regular rate
  to include required fringe benefits first, which can shortchange the worker
  and create a compliance violation.
