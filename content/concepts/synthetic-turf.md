---
id: concept-synthetic-turf
title: Synthetic Turf
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "32, Exterior Improvements"
definition: >
  An engineered artificial playing or landscaping surface, infill or
  non-infill systems laid over a compacted aggregate base with a drainage
  layer beneath, used for athletic fields and increasingly for landscaping in
  low-water applications, installed to specific base compaction and drainage
  standards rather than laid like carpet.
whyItMatters: >
  Synthetic turf's long-term performance and drainage depend almost entirely
  on the base preparation underneath it; a poorly compacted or improperly
  sloped base causes standing water and premature wear that no amount of
  quality in the turf itself can make up for.
example: >
  A contractor installing a synthetic athletic field compacts and tests the
  aggregate base to the manufacturer's specified density and grades it for
  positive drainage before the turf and infill go down, since correcting base
  issues after installation means tearing the turf back up.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates base compaction
  testing with the geotechnical requirements before the specialty turf
  subcontractor installs the surface.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A turf field's base is compacted without verifying density against the
  manufacturer's spec, and within a year low spots develop where the base
  settled, creating standing water that the turf alone can't drain away.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
  - career-laborer
