---
id: concept-biophilic-design
title: Biophilic Design
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  Incorporating natural elements, such as plants, natural materials, water
  features, and     views of nature, into a building's design based on
  research showing a measurable effect on     occupant stress, wellbeing, and
  productivity, a design approach the WELL Building Standard     specifically
  scores.
whyItMatters: >
  Biophilic design is backed by research linking exposure to natural elements
  with reduced     occupant stress and improved cognitive performance, which
  is why it earns its own dedicated     credit category in the WELL Building
  Standard rather than being treated as purely aesthetic     or decorative.
example: >
  An office renovation incorporates a living plant wall in the main lobby,
  exposed natural     wood finishes throughout common areas, and reconfigures
  workstation layouts to maximize     views toward an adjacent park, pursuing
  specific WELL Building Standard biophilic design     credits.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-sustainability-leed-consultant|Sustainability/LEED Consultant]]
  incorporates     biophilic design elements as part of a
  [[concept-well-building-standard|WELL Building Standard]] certification
  pursuit, since WELL     scores these elements on their own, separate from a
  project's LEED environmental credits.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project adds a few decorative plants and calls it biophilic design, when
  the WELL     Building Standard's actual biophilic design credits require
  documented, intentional design     elements like real nature views and
  specific natural material use, not incidental     greenery added after the
  fact.
relatedIds:
