id: concept-site-remediation-soil-removal
title: Site Remediation & Soil Removal
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "02, Existing Conditions"
definition: >
  The excavation and off-site disposal, or on-site treatment, of contaminated
  soil identified through an environmental assessment, performed under a
  regulator-approved remediation plan before construction can proceed on a
  contaminated site.
whyItMatters: >
  Contaminated soil can't simply be excavated and hauled away without a
  regulator-approved plan specifying disposal or treatment method, since
  mishandling contaminated soil can create liability that follows the property
  for years.
example: >
  After soil testing confirms petroleum contamination at a former industrial
  site, a contractor follows the state-approved remediation plan's specific
  excavation depth and disposal requirements before any new foundation work
  can begin.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-environmental-remediation-specialist|Environmental Remediation
  Specialist]] manages the remediation plan's execution and regulatory
  sign-off.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Contaminated soil is excavated and hauled to a standard landfill without
  following the regulator-approved disposal method, exposing the project to a
  violation and potential re-excavation if the error is discovered.
relatedIds:
  - career-environmental-remediation-specialist
