title: Mold Assessment & Remediation Protocol
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The inspection and testing process (assessment) and the physical removal or
  treatment process (remediation) for mold contamination following water intrusion,
  generally following IICRC S520, with several states requiring separate,
  independent licenses for each role.
whyItMatters: >
  Several states, including Florida and Texas, license mold assessors and mold
  remediators separately and generally prohibit the same company from doing
  both on the same property, Florida bars it for 12 months after an
  assessment, Texas bars it outright, with only narrow exceptions, to
  prevent the obvious conflict of interest in a company inspecting, then
  selling itself the remediation work.
example: >
  A licensed mold assessor inspects a water-damaged basement, writes a remediation
  protocol specifying containment and removal steps, and a separate, independently
  licensed remediation company performs the actual work and gets it cleared by the
  same assessor afterward.
careerAngle: >
  An
  [[career-environmental-remediation-specialist|Environmental/Remediation Specialist]]
  handles the assessment and clearance testing side of mold work, keeping it
  independent from whichever company actually performs the removal.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A single company both assesses and remediates mold on the same property in
  a state that prohibits it. That arrangement can void the state license,
  invalidate the clearance report, and leave the homeowner unable to sell
  the property later without redoing the work through properly separated
  companies.
