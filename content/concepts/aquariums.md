---
id: concept-aquariums
title: Aquariums
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "13, Special Construction"
definition: >
  Large-scale display aquariums requiring a structural engineer's review of
  the floor load the filled tank's weight imposes, along with dedicated
  life-support systems, filtration and temperature control, distinct from
  standard plumbing.
whyItMatters: >
  A large aquarium's water weight is a significant concentrated structural
  load that most floors aren't designed to carry without reinforcement, and
  that load has to be checked against the actual structure early, since
  retrofitting structural support under a built tank is far more difficult
  than designing for it upfront.
example: >
  A contractor building a large lobby aquarium for a corporate headquarters
  has the structural engineer confirm the floor's load capacity accounts for
  the tank's full water weight before the tank's support structure is
  finalized.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-structural-engineer|Structural Engineer]] checks the floor's load
  capacity for the tank's weight, and a specialty aquarium life-support
  subcontractor installs the filtration and water-quality systems.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A large aquarium is installed without a structural review of the floor's
  load capacity, and a later inspection finds the floor deflecting under the
  tank's weight more than the structure was designed to safely carry.
relatedIds:
  - career-structural-engineer
