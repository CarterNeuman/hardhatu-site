title: Bridging Delivery Method
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A hybrid delivery approach where the owner hires a design firm to develop
  plans to roughly 30 to 60 percent completion, the bridging documents, then
  bids that partial design out to [[concept-design-build|design-build]] teams
  who complete the design and construct the project, giving the owner more
  design control than pure design-build while keeping single-point
  responsibility for the rest.
whyItMatters: >
  The bridging documents' actual completeness at handoff determines how much
  ambiguity the design-build team inherits, an owner who stops the bridging
  design too early is really just moving that ambiguity, and its cost, onto
  whoever wins the design-build bid.
example: >
  An owner's design firm develops bridging documents to about 40 percent
  completion, establishing the building's layout and major systems, then bids
  that package to design-build teams who complete the detailed design and
  construction.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] manages the
  bridging design phase and its handoff, making sure the documents are
  complete enough that the design-build team isn't left guessing at the
  owner's real intent.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Bridging documents are handed off with several ambiguous or unresolved
  details left for "design-build to figure out," and the design-build team
  interprets those gaps differently than the owner actually intended. A dispute
  follows over whether the resulting design was really included in the
  guaranteed price.
