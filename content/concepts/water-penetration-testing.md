id: concept-water-penetration-testing
title: Water Penetration Testing (ASTM E1105)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "08, Openings"
definition: >
  A standardized test, defined by ASTM E1105, that sprays water at a window,
  curtain wall, or storefront mockup while applying a static air pressure
  difference across it, checking whether the assembly leaks water under
  conditions meant to simulate wind-driven rain.
whyItMatters: >
  A building's full glazing system can't be fully re-tested once it's
  installed across an entire facade, which is exactly why a mockup gets tested
  this way before the real installation begins. Passing this test on a mockup
  is often the condition that has to be met before a project is allowed to
  proceed with full-scale installation of that glazing system.
example: >
  Before a project proceeds with installing curtain wall across an entire
  building facade, a full-size mockup panel is built and subjected to ASTM
  E1105 testing, confirming the system doesn't leak under the test's simulated
  wind-driven rain conditions.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-glazier|Glazier]] builds the mockup panel that undergoes ASTM
  E1105 testing, and the result determines whether that exact glazing system
  and installation method is approved to proceed at full scale.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project skips formal mockup water testing to save time and schedule,
  assuming the manufacturer's standard product performance is good enough.
  Water intrusion shows up during the first heavy wind-driven rain after
  installation, a problem that a mockup test would likely have caught before
  it affected the entire building facade.
