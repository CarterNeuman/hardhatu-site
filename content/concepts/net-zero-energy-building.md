---
id: concept-net-zero-energy-building
title: Net Zero Energy Building
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  A building designed and built to produce as much renewable energy, typically through
  on-site solar, as it consumes over the course of a year, an outcome that depends on
  both an aggressively efficient [[concept-building-envelope|building envelope]] and
  mechanical systems, and enough on-site generation capacity to offset what's left.
whyItMatters: >
  Net zero isn't achieved by adding solar panels to an otherwise ordinary building,
  since a building that isn't first designed to use dramatically less energy would need an
  impractically large renewable system to offset its consumption. The envelope and
  systems efficiency work has to happen before renewable generation can realistically
  close the remaining gap.
example: >
  A project team runs [[concept-energy-modeling|energy models]] early in design and finds
  that the building's baseline energy use would require more rooftop solar capacity than
  the roof can physically hold. The team tightens the building envelope and upgrades
  mechanical efficiency until the projected energy use drops low enough for the available
  roof area to realistically offset it.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-sustainability-leed-consultant|Sustainability/LEED Consultant]] coordinates
  the envelope, systems, and renewable generation decisions that together make a net
  zero target achievable, rather than treating any one of them as sufficient alone. An
  [[career-hvac-technician|HVAC Technician]] installs and commissions the
  high-efficiency mechanical systems the target depends on actually performing as
  designed.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project markets itself as "net zero ready" based on the design model's projected
  energy use, without commissioning the mechanical systems to confirm they actually
  perform to that standard once installed. The building's real energy use comes in well
  above the model's projection, and the renewable system sized for the model can't
  actually offset it.
