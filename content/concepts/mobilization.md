---
id: concept-mobilization
title: Mobilization
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The setup work at the very start of a project, such as bringing in temporary
  facilities, fencing the site, setting up equipment laydown areas, and getting crews
  and materials in place, before any actual construction work begins.
whyItMatters: >
  A project that starts building before it's properly mobilized runs into chaos fast: no
  place to store materials, no site safety plan in place, crews with nowhere to park.
  Mobilization is what turns an empty lot into a functioning jobsite.
example: >
  Before framing starts on a new building, the contractor sets up a site trailer, fences
  the perimeter, installs temporary power, and designates where delivery trucks will
  stage materials.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] typically runs mobilization directly,
  since it's one of the first real tests of whether they've thought through how the
  whole site is going to function once work actually starts. A
  [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] cares because a slow or disorganized
  mobilization eats into the schedule before a single wall goes up.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A crew shows up ready to start excavation, but the site was never properly fenced or
  graded for equipment access, and the first two days of the schedule burn away just
  getting the site into a workable state, days that were never built into the schedule
  because mobilization was assumed to be instant.
