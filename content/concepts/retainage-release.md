---
id: concept-retainage-release
title: Retainage Release
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  The process of paying out the [[concept-retainage|retainage]] withheld from
  progress payments throughout a project, typically released partially at
  [[concept-substantial-completion|substantial completion]] and fully at final
  completion, often requiring [[concept-lien-waiver|lien waivers]] from every
  payee first.
whyItMatters: >
  Because full release often waits on every subcontractor finishing and
  submitting paperwork, one slow subcontractor's punch list or paperwork can
  hold up cash that's rightfully owed to every other subcontractor on the
  project.
example: >
  A general contractor releases half of a subcontractor's retainage at
  substantial completion once its work is largely done, holding the remaining
  half until final completion and a signed lien waiver confirm the punch list is
  fully resolved.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] tracks each
  subcontractor's retainage balance and the specific conditions, punch list
  sign-off, lien waivers, that have to be met before each release.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A subcontractor completes its punch list work but the GC won't release
  retainage until every other subcontractor on the project also finishes and
  submits lien waivers, tying one sub's cash flow to the slowest sub on the job.
relatedIds:

  - concept-lien-waiver
