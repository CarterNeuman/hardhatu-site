id: concept-mortar
title: Mortar
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "04, Masonry"
definition: >
  The cement-based paste that bonds masonry units (brick, block, stone) together into
  a wall, filling the joints between units and, once cured, holding the wall together
  as a single structural or veneer assembly.
whyItMatters: >
  Mortar isn't just an adhesive: its strength, workability, and composition are
  specifically formulated, in standardized types like Type N, S, or M, for the
  specific application, and using the wrong mortar type for a given wall's structural
  or exposure requirements can compromise the whole assembly.
example: >
  A mason mixes mortar to match the specified type for a load-bearing masonry wall,
  rather than substituting whatever mortar is on hand, since the wall's structural
  capacity was calculated assuming that specific mortar strength.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mason|Mason]] mixes and applies mortar to consistent joint widths and
  tooling, since inconsistent mortar joints affect both the wall's water resistance and
  its finished appearance. A [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]]
  may require mortar samples or field testing on a structural masonry project to
  confirm the mix meets the specified strength.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A mason crew runs low on the specified mortar type near the end of a job and
  substitutes a different, more available type without getting approval first,
  assuming "mortar is mortar." A subsequent inspection catches the inconsistency, and
  the affected wall sections have to be evaluated and potentially rebuilt to
  confirm they still meet the structural design.
