---
id: concept-distributed-antenna-systems
title: Distributed Antenna Systems (DAS)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "27, Communications"
definition: >
  A network of antennas distributed throughout a building and connected to a
  central signal source, used to boost cellular signal and, in many
  jurisdictions, to provide the in-building coverage fire and police radios
  need to communicate during an emergency.
whyItMatters: >
  Many building codes now require an Emergency Responder Radio Coverage (ERRC)
  system, a type of DAS, as a life-safety requirement, tested and certified
  before occupancy, not as an optional cellular-signal amenity.
example: >
  A contractor building a large concrete parking structure installs an
  ERRC-compliant DAS specifically because the structure's thick concrete would
  otherwise block first-responder radio signals, and the fire marshal requires
  a signal-strength test before issuing the certificate of occupancy.
careerAngle: >
  A specialty low-voltage subcontractor typically installs the DAS, while the
  [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] coordinates the required
  signal-strength certification with the fire marshal.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building's DAS is installed for cellular coverage only, without being
  engineered to the emergency responder radio frequency and signal-strength
  requirements, and it fails the fire marshal's ERRC certification test before
  occupancy can be granted.
relatedIds:
  - career-safety-manager
