---
id: concept-fixed-seating-bleachers
title: Fixed Seating & Bleachers
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Permanently installed seating for gymnasiums, auditoriums, and stadiums,
  including telescoping bleachers that retract against a wall when not in use,
  anchored to the structure and governed by code requirements for row spacing,
  aisle width, and accessible seating placement.
whyItMatters: >
  Accessible seating locations and quantities in fixed seating venues are set
  by code as a percentage of total capacity, distributed across different
  price points or sightlines rather than clustered in one spot, which has to
  be planned into the seating layout from the start.
example: >
  A contractor installing telescoping bleachers in a high school gym verifies
  the accessible seating locations are distributed across the bank rather than
  grouped at one end, matching the code requirement for dispersed accessible
  seating.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates the anchoring and
  structural loads for fixed seating with the structural engineer, and the
  [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] confirms accessible seating
  distribution before occupancy.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A venue's accessible seating gets clustered in one section to simplify
  installation, and the building fails its accessibility inspection because
  code requires those seats dispersed across different sightlines and price
  levels.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
  - career-qaqc-inspector
