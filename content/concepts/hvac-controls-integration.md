---
id: concept-hvac-controls-integration
title: HVAC Controls Integration
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "25, Integrated Automation"
definition: >
  The integration of a building's individual HVAC equipment controllers, air
  handlers, VAV boxes, chillers, into a single building-wide control platform
  that can schedule, monitor, and optimize the whole system together rather
  than each piece of equipment running independently.
whyItMatters: >
  Individual HVAC equipment usually comes with its own built-in controller,
  and without integration into a central system, a facilities team has to
  manage dozens of separate control panels rather than seeing and adjusting
  the whole building's HVAC from one place.
example: >
  A controls contractor integrating a new office building's HVAC system
  programs the central platform to coordinate air handler schedules across
  floors, so unoccupied floors ramp down together rather than each air handler
  running on its own independent schedule.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] designs the controls integration
  architecture, and a specialty controls subcontractor programs and
  commissions the central platform.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Individual air handlers are installed and left on their factory-default
  independent schedules because the central controls integration was descoped
  to save budget, and the building never achieves the coordinated energy
  savings the design intended.
relatedIds:
  - career-mep-engineer
