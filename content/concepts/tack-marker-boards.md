id: concept-tack-marker-boards
title: Tack & Marker Boards
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Wall-mounted cork tack boards and dry-erase marker boards installed in
  classrooms, conference rooms, and offices, specified by size and surface
  type, magnetic versus non-magnetic, to match the room's intended use.
whyItMatters: >
  A marker board's trim and mounting height have to clear door swings, light
  switches, and other wall-mounted items in a small room, a coordination
  detail easy to overlook until the board is delivered and doesn't actually
  fit the wall as planned.
example: >
  A contractor installing marker boards in a new classroom confirms the
  board's width clears the light switch and door swing on the same wall before
  ordering, rather than assuming a standard board size will fit any classroom
  wall.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] confirms wall-mounted item
  locations before finish items like marker boards are ordered.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A marker board is ordered at a standard width without checking the specific
  wall it's going on, and it arrives too wide to clear the adjacent light
  switch, requiring a different board to be reordered.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
