---
id: concept-laboratory-equipment
title: Laboratory Equipment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Fixed lab equipment, fume hoods, biosafety cabinets, emergency
  eyewash/shower stations, and specialized casework, installed as part of the
  building rather than brought in afterward, each tied to dedicated exhaust,
  plumbing, or power that has to be coordinated during construction.
whyItMatters: >
  A fume hood or biosafety cabinet is only as safe as the exhaust system
  behind it; if the ductwork, makeup air, and exhaust fan aren't sized and
  balanced correctly for that specific hood, it won't actually protect the
  people using it.
example: >
  A contractor installing a fume hood in a new chemistry lab coordinates its
  dedicated exhaust duct and fan sizing directly with the mechanical
  engineer's design, rather than tying it into a shared exhaust system sized
  for general ventilation.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] designs the dedicated exhaust and
  makeup air for each piece of lab equipment, and the
  [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] verifies face velocity testing
  before the lab is turned over.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A fume hood gets tied into a shared exhaust system to save ductwork cost,
  and the hood's face velocity test fails once other equipment on the same
  system is running, since the shared exhaust can't deliver what one hood
  alone needs.
relatedIds:
  - career-mep-engineer
  - career-qaqc-inspector
