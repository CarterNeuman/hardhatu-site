---
id: concept-wall-corner-guards
title: Wall & Corner Guards
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Protective strips or corner guards, made of stainless steel, PVC, or rubber,
  mounted on walls and outside corners in high-traffic areas, such as hospital
  corridors, loading docks, and school hallways, to prevent damage from carts,
  gurneys, and equipment impacts, distinct from cosmetic trim since their whole
  purpose is absorbing repeated abuse.
whyItMatters: >
  Specifying the right guard for actual traffic, a hospital gurney corridor needs
  a heavier-duty guard than an office hallway, protects finishes from becoming an
  ongoing repair cost the owner has to keep paying for after occupancy.
example: >
  A hospital corridor gets stainless steel corner guards and continuous bumper
  rails rated for repeated gurney impacts, rather than the lighter PVC guards
  specified for the same building's administrative office wing.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-carpenter|Carpenter]] installs the guards. A
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] ends up dealing with ongoing
  wall damage and repair costs if guards were under-specified for the traffic a
  corridor actually sees.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor value-engineers a hospital's specified stainless corner
  guards down to a lighter PVC option without getting owner sign-off on the
  change. Gurney traffic dents and cracks the PVC guards within the first year,
  and the owner ends up paying out of pocket to replace them with the heavier
  guards originally specified.
relatedIds:
