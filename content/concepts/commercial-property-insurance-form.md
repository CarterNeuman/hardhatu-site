id: concept-commercial-property-insurance-form
title: Commercial Property Insurance Form
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The standardized base policy form, like ISO's CP 00 10 Building and Personal
  Property Coverage Form, that most commercial property policies are built on,
  defining what property is covered, what causes of loss are included, and core
  conditions like the [[concept-coinsurance-clause|coinsurance clause]].
whyItMatters: >
  A shared standard form lets adjusters, brokers, and risk managers compare
  commercial policies from different carriers on roughly equal terms, instead of
  every policy reading like a completely different contract.
example: >
  Two competing insurers each quote a warehouse policy built on the same ISO CP 00 10
  base form, differing mainly in the causes-of-loss form attached to it and the
  coinsurance percentage, rather than in the underlying structure of the policy
  itself.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-builders-risk-underwriter|Builder's Risk Underwriter]] works from these
  standardized base forms daily, attaching endorsements to customize coverage rather
  than drafting a policy from nothing each time.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An adjuster assumes a client's commercial property policy is written on a standard
  ISO base form and applies default coinsurance and valuation assumptions, but the
  carrier actually issued a heavily manuscripted, custom-drafted form with different
  terms, producing a claim analysis that doesn't match what the policy actually says.
