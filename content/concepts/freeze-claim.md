---
id: concept-freeze-claim
title: Freeze Claim (Frozen Pipe Damage)
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  An insurance claim for water damage caused by a pipe that froze and burst, a
  loss type that spikes seasonally and that many policies cover only if the
  policyholder took reasonable steps to prevent freezing, such as maintaining
  heat or insulating pipes in a vacant property.
whyItMatters: >
  Whether the policyholder maintained reasonable precautions is often the
  central dispute on a freeze claim, since insurers frequently investigate
  whether heat was kept on and pipes were adequately protected before paying
  out.
example: >
  A plumbing and restoration contractor repairing a burst pipe in an unheated
  vacation home documents the thermostat's setting and the state of pipe
  insulation for the insurer, since the claim's approval may hinge on whether
  reasonable freeze-prevention steps were in place.
careerAngle: >
  Contractors responding to freeze claims should photograph and document the
  property's heating and insulation conditions at arrival, since that evidence
  often determines whether the insurer accepts or disputes the claim.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Property owners sometimes assume any frozen-pipe burst is automatically
  covered, when most policies require reasonable care was taken to prevent
  freezing, and a clearly neglected, unheated vacant property can be denied on
  that basis.
