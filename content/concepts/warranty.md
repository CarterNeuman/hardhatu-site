id: concept-warranty
title: Warranty
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A contractor's guarantee to repair defects in materials or workmanship for a set period
  after the project is complete, typically one year, though some materials and
  equipment carry longer manufacturer warranties of their own.
whyItMatters: >
  Construction defects don't always show up on day one: a roof leak might not appear
  until the first heavy rain, months later. The warranty period is what protects an
  owner from paying twice for the same work if something the contractor built goes wrong
  early.
example: >
  Eight months after moving in, an owner notices a door frame that's started to sag.
  Because it's within the one-year warranty period, the contractor sends a crew back to
  fix it at no charge, rather than the owner having to pay for the repair themselves.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] hands over warranty documentation as part
  of [[concept-closeout|closeout]] and is often the one fielding the owner's call when a
  warranty issue comes up later. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] on
  the owner's side is the one actually tracking which systems are still under warranty
  years after the contractor is gone.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner discovers a leaking pipe fitting fourteen months after moving in, two months
  past the one-year warranty deadline, and the contractor declines to fix it for free,
  pointing to the expired warranty period. Whether the underlying installation mistake
  actually happened well within the warranty year becomes a dispute nobody can easily
  resolve once the deadline has passed.
