id: concept-cobie
title: COBie
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  Construction Operations Building Information Exchange (COBie), a
  standardized spreadsheet-like data format for handing over a building's
  equipment and asset information, model numbers, warranties, maintenance
  schedules, from the design and construction team to the owner's
  facilities management system, structured so it can be imported directly
  rather than manually re-entered.
whyItMatters: >
  Without a structured format like COBie, an owner's facilities team often
  has to manually extract equipment data from PDF submittals and O&M
  manuals one item at a time to populate their own maintenance software, a
  slow, error-prone process COBie exists specifically to eliminate by
  delivering the same data already structured for direct import.
example: >
  A project's closeout package includes a completed COBie spreadsheet
  listing every major piece of mechanical equipment with its model number,
  warranty period, and maintenance requirements, formatted so the owner's
  facilities team can import it directly into their computerized
  maintenance management system.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-bim-vdc-specialist|BIM/VDC Specialist]] or document control
  specialist compiles COBie data throughout the project so it's ready at
  closeout rather than reconstructed after the fact. A
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] imports and relies on
  the completed COBie data set once the building turns over.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project's contract requires COBie deliverables at closeout, but nobody
  actually populates the data incrementally as equipment gets installed
  throughout construction. The project team scrambles to reconstruct the
  required data from submittals after the fact, and the resulting COBie
  file is incomplete and full of gaps the facilities team discovers only
  once they try to actually import it.
