---
id: concept-final-completion
title: Final Completion
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The point at which every remaining obligation under the contract, including
  the full [[concept-punch-list|punch list]], final cleanup, and all closeout
  documentation, has actually been completed, distinct from and typically
  occurring later than [[concept-substantial-completion|substantial
  completion]].
whyItMatters: >
  Substantial completion starts the clock on things like the owner's use of the
  building and the warranty period, but final completion is what actually
  closes out the contract itself, including release of final retainage, which
  is why a contractor still has real financial exposure in the gap between the
  two milestones.
example: >
  A hotel reaches substantial completion and opens for guests, starting its
  warranty period and letting the owner begin generating revenue from the
  space. The contractor doesn't reach final completion, and release of the
  remaining retainage, until several more weeks later, once the full punch
  list and every closeout document is actually finished and submitted.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] tracks the gap
  between substantial and final completion to make sure closeout obligations
  don't quietly stall out once the owner is already using the building, and
  the [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] pushes to close that gap
  quickly since retainage release depends on it.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor treats substantial completion as the real finish line and lets
  momentum drop on closing out the remaining punch list items, not realizing
  final retainage, often a meaningful sum, stays unpaid until final completion
  is actually reached.
