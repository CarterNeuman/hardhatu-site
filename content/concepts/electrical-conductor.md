id: concept-electrical-conductor
title: Conductor (Electrical)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "26, Electrical"
definition: >
  The technical term for the actual metal wire that carries electrical
  current, as distinct from conduit, which is the protective tube a conductor
  sometimes runs through, and distinct from its everyday meaning as a job
  title in an entirely different industry.
whyItMatters: >
  Electricians use "conductor" precisely, since not every wire in a system
  does the same job, some carry current, some are strictly for grounding, and
  getting the terminology right matters when reading code requirements that
  specify conductor size, count, or material explicitly.
example: >
  An electrician references code to confirm the minimum conductor size
  required for a given circuit's amperage, distinguishing the current-carrying
  conductors from the separate grounding conductor also required in the same
  circuit.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] and a
  [[career-solar-installer|Solar Installer]] both work with conductor sizing
  constantly, since undersizing a conductor for its circuit's load is a basic
  but serious wiring error.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A system is wired with a conductor sized for a lower amperage than the
  circuit actually carries, an error that doesn't show any obvious symptom at
  first. Over time, the undersized conductor overheats under load, creating a
  real fire risk that an inspection is specifically designed to catch before
  it becomes dangerous.
