---
id: concept-honeycombing
title: Honeycombing
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "03, Concrete"
definition: >
  Visible voids and gaps left inside cured concrete, resembling a honeycomb,
  when the mix isn't properly vibrated during placement and the aggregate
  doesn't fully consolidate around the rebar and into the corners of the
  formwork.
whyItMatters: >
  Honeycombing isn't just a cosmetic flaw. The voids it leaves weaken the
  concrete structurally and can expose rebar directly to moisture and air,
  leading to corrosion that weakens the reinforcement from the inside over
  time.
example: >
  After stripping the formwork from a column, a crew finds visible
  honeycombing along one face where the concrete wasn't vibrated thoroughly
  enough during placement, and the area has to be evaluated and repaired
  before the column is considered acceptable.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-concrete-worker|Concrete Worker]] vibrates concrete thoroughly
  during placement specifically to prevent honeycombing, working the vibrator
  methodically rather than rushing through the pour.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A crew under schedule pressure rushes through vibrating a heavily reinforced
  column, leaving sections where the concrete never fully consolidated around
  the rebar. The resulting honeycombing isn't caught until formwork is
  stripped, and the column has to be repaired or, in a severe case, removed
  and repoured.
