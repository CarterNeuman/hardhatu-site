id: concept-duress-panic-alarm-systems
title: Duress & Panic Alarm Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "28, Electronic Safety & Security"
definition: >
  Discreet alarm devices, fixed panic buttons, wearable pendants, or panic
  software on a workstation, that let staff silently summon help during a
  threatening situation, distinct from a general intrusion alarm since they're
  triggered by a person rather than a sensor.
whyItMatters: >
  A duress alarm's value depends entirely on how discreetly it can be
  triggered and how clearly the alert identifies the exact location of the
  person who triggered it, so placement and programming matter as much as the
  device itself.
example: >
  A contractor installing duress buttons at a bank's teller stations locates
  each button where it can be pressed without visible movement, and programs
  the alarm to report the specific teller station rather than just a generic
  branch-wide alarm.
careerAngle: >
  A specialty security subcontractor installs the devices, while the
  [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] defines which staff positions need
  duress coverage.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Duress buttons are installed in a visible location that a teller can't reach
  without tipping off whoever is threatening them, defeating the purpose of a
  discreet alarm.
relatedIds:
