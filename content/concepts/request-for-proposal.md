id: concept-request-for-proposal
title: Request for Proposal (RFP)
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A document an owner issues asking contractors or construction managers to submit a
  detailed proposal, covering approach, team, qualifications, and price, for
  delivering a project, used when the owner wants to weigh more than just the lowest
  number.
whyItMatters: >
  A straight low-bid process picks the cheapest price; an RFP process lets an owner pick
  the best overall fit, weighing experience and approach alongside cost. For complex or
  unusual projects, that difference can matter more than the price gap between
  proposals.
example: >
  A hospital system issues an RFP for a new surgical wing, asking each construction
  manager to submit not just a price, but their proposed project team, similar past
  healthcare projects, and their approach to keeping the existing hospital operational
  during construction.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] often writes the RFP and
  evaluates the responses on the owner's behalf, since choosing the right team matters
  as much as the price. A [[career-preconstruction-manager|Preconstruction Manager]] on
  the responding side builds the proposal, translating the firm's actual qualifications
  into a compelling, specific response.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner issues an RFP for a delivery method meant to weigh qualifications heavily, but
  the internal selection committee ends up picking whichever proposal has the lowest
  price anyway, treating it like a disguised bid process. The firm that's actually best
  suited for a complex project, but priced its risk more honestly, loses out to a
  competitor who underpriced the job and struggles to deliver.
