id: concept-plumb-alignment
title: Plumb
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "05, Metals"
definition: >
  Perfectly true vertical, the reference line gravity itself defines, which a
  steel column, a framed wall, or any other vertical building element has to
  be checked and adjusted against before it's permanently fixed in place.
whyItMatters: >
  An element that's even slightly out of plumb compounds that error as more
  structure gets built on top of it. Checking plumb, traditionally with a
  level or plumb bob and now often with a laser instrument, is a basic
  verification step that happens constantly throughout construction, not just
  once at the start.
example: >
  Before permanently bolting a steel column's base connection, an ironworker
  checks that the column is plumb in both directions using a level, and
  adjusts it with a come-along if it's off, since a column that's out of plumb
  now throws off every connection made to it afterward.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-ironworker|Ironworker]] checks every column for plumb before
  finalizing its connection, since plumb has to be verified and corrected
  before the structure, not after.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A column gets permanently bolted while slightly out of plumb, an error that
  seems too small to matter at the time. By the time several more stories of
  steel have been erected on top of it, that small initial error has
  compounded into a visible and expensive lean that's far harder to correct
  than it would have been at the start.
