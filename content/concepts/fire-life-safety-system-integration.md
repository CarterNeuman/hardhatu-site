id: concept-fire-life-safety-system-integration
title: Fire/Life Safety System Integration
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "25, Integrated Automation"
definition: >
  The integration of a building's fire alarm with other systems it needs to
  control during an emergency, like releasing magnetic door locks, shutting
  down air handlers to prevent smoke spread, and triggering elevator recall,
  coordinated so these systems respond correctly together rather than
  independently.
whyItMatters: >
  A fire alarm that activates without successfully triggering elevator recall
  or HVAC shutdown isn't just incomplete, it can actively work against the
  building's life-safety design, letting elevators keep running to a fire
  floor or letting HVAC keep circulating smoke.
example: >
  A fire/life-safety integrator testing a new high-rise confirms the fire
  alarm's activation successfully triggers elevator recall to the ground floor
  and shuts down the affected floor's air handlers, rather than testing the
  fire alarm panel in isolation.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] designs the integration points
  between systems, and the [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] oversees
  the full-system commissioning test before occupancy.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building's fire alarm and elevator recall are commissioned separately by
  different subcontractors, and a full-system test reveals the elevators don't
  actually recall when the alarm activates, a gap that wasn't caught until the
  systems were tested together.
