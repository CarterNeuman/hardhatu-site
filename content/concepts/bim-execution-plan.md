id: concept-bim-execution-plan
title: BIM Execution Plan (BEP)
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  A project-specific document defining how BIM will actually be used on a
  project, which model elements each party is responsible for, at what
  level of development, file formats, and coordination procedures, agreed to
  by all major design and construction parties before modeling work begins
  rather than assumed informally.
whyItMatters: >
  Without an agreed BEP, different parties often build their models to
  different levels of development or in incompatible formats, discovering
  the mismatch only when trying to coordinate models together, a BEP exists
  specifically to prevent that costly rework by settling those expectations
  in writing before modeling starts.
example: >
  A project's BEP specifies that the structural engineer's model must
  include actual beam sizes at a specific level of development by a
  defined milestone, so the MEP coordination team can reliably run clash
  detection against real structural geometry rather than placeholder
  shapes.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-bim-vdc-specialist|BIM/VDC Specialist]] develops and enforces
  the BEP across the project team. A
  [[career-construction-technology-manager|Construction Technology Manager]]
  often champions adopting a BEP as standard practice across a company's
  projects.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project proceeds without a formally agreed BEP, with each design
  discipline modeling to its own informal standard. When the team finally
  tries to run coordinated clash detection midway through design, several
  disciplines' models turn out to be at incompatible levels of development,
  and weeks are lost reconciling the models to a common standard that should
  have been agreed before modeling began.
