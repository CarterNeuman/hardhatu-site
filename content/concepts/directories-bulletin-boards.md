id: concept-directories-bulletin-boards
title: Directories & Bulletin Boards
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Built-in directory boards, often electronic in larger buildings, and
  bulletin/display boards mounted in lobbies and corridors, requiring
  coordination for power and data if the directory is digital rather than a
  static printed insert.
whyItMatters: >
  A digital directory needs power and a network connection planned into the
  wall during rough-in, a requirement easy to miss since the directory is
  often selected late in the finish-selection process, well after the
  electrical rough-in is complete.
example: >
  A contractor coordinates power and data rough-in for a planned digital lobby
  directory early in construction, even though the specific directory vendor
  and software won't be selected until closer to occupancy.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] flags the directory's power
  and data needs early enough that they're not missed in the electrical
  rough-in.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A digital directory is selected late in the project, and the lobby wall
  where it's meant to go has no power or data rough-in, requiring an exposed
  conduit retrofit into an otherwise finished lobby.
relatedIds:
  - career-project-manager
