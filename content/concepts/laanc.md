---
id: concept-laanc
title: LAANC (Low Altitude Authorization and Notification Capability)
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  The FAA's automated system that gives drone operators near-real-time
  authorization to fly in controlled airspace near airports, replacing a much
  slower manual approval process that used to be required for the same
  airspace.
whyItMatters: >
  A large share of construction sites sit inside controlled airspace near an
  airport, where flying a drone without authorization is both illegal and a
  real safety risk to manned aircraft. LAANC exists specifically to make legal
  authorization fast enough that drone operations near airports are actually
  practical to schedule.
example: >
  A drone operator planning a mapping flight on a site located inside
  controlled airspace near a regional airport requests authorization through
  LAANC, receiving approval to fly up to a specific altitude within minutes
  rather than waiting on a manual approval process.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-drone-uav-specialist|Drone/UAV Specialist]] checks LAANC
  availability and requests authorization before every flight near controlled
  airspace, since flying without it is a federal violation regardless of good
  intentions.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An operator flies a drone near an airport without checking whether the
  airspace requires LAANC authorization first, assuming an open-looking sky
  means it's clear to fly. The flight turns out to be a real violation of
  controlled airspace rules, carrying legal and safety consequences the
  operator wasn't expecting.
