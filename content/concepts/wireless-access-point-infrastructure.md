---
id: concept-wireless-access-point-infrastructure
title: Wireless Access Point (WAP) Infrastructure
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "27, Communications"
definition: >
  The cabling, power, and mounting infrastructure built in to support a
  building's wireless network access points, placed according to a coverage
  design rather than wherever is structurally convenient, and increasingly
  powered over the same cable that carries data (Power over Ethernet).
whyItMatters: >
  Access point locations are driven by a wireless coverage and capacity
  design, often done by a separate IT consultant, so the low-voltage
  contractor has to get that design before rough-in or risk running cable to
  the wrong ceiling locations entirely.
example: >
  A contractor roughs in Power over Ethernet cabling to access point locations
  mapped out in a wireless coverage study, rather than spacing them evenly
  across the ceiling grid without regard to the building's actual wall layout
  and expected device density.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] or low-voltage technician runs the
  cabling to each access point location, coordinated against the IT
  consultant's coverage design.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Wireless access point cabling is roughed in on an even grid pattern without
  a coverage study, and the completed network has dead zones in areas with
  denser wall construction that the even spacing didn't account for.
relatedIds:
  - career-electrician
