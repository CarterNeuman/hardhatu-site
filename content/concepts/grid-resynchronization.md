id: concept-grid-resynchronization
title: Grid Resynchronization
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  The process of safely reconnecting a building's electrical system, after
  it's been running on a microgrid or generator during a utility outage, back
  to the returning utility power, which requires matching the microgrid's
  voltage phase and frequency to the grid's before the connection closes.
whyItMatters: >
  Reconnecting two live power sources that aren't synchronized can cause
  serious equipment damage or a dangerous electrical event, since the two
  sources would effectively be fighting each other the instant they're
  connected. Automated controls handle this matching in a modern system, but
  understanding why the process exists explains why a microgrid can't simply
  flip back to grid power the instant utility service is restored.
example: >
  When utility power is restored after an outage, a building's microgrid
  controller first matches its own voltage phase and frequency precisely to
  the returning grid power before closing the connection, rather than
  reconnecting immediately and risking a damaging mismatch.
careerAngle: >
  A
  [[career-renewable-energy-project-manager|Renewable Energy Project Manager]]
  oversees the controls that manage grid resynchronization as part of a
  microgrid or battery storage system's design.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A poorly configured microgrid controller reconnects to the returning utility
  grid before fully matching phase and frequency. The mismatch creates a surge
  that damages sensitive equipment connected to the system at the moment of
  reconnection.
