id: concept-pre-installation-meeting
title: Pre-Installation Meeting
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A meeting held before a specific trade begins a critical or complex
  installation, such as a roofing membrane, curtain wall, or waterproofing system,
  bringing together the installer, the manufacturer's technical representative, the
  general contractor, and relevant inspectors to review substrate conditions,
  sequencing, and manufacturer requirements before work starts. Distinct from the
  project-wide [[concept-oac-meeting-minutes|OAC meeting]], which covers overall
  project status rather than one trade's installation.
whyItMatters: >
  Manufacturers often make attending and documenting this meeting a condition of
  their own product warranty, so skipping it doesn't just risk a bad installation,
  it can void the warranty coverage the owner is counting on regardless of how well
  the work actually turns out.
example: >
  Before a roof installation begins, the roofing subcontractor, the membrane
  manufacturer's technical rep, and the general contractor's superintendent walk
  the roof deck together, confirming the deck's moisture content is within the
  manufacturer's specified range before any membrane goes down.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates and documents the meeting.
  A [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] verifies the substrate conditions
  discussed actually match what the manufacturer requires before installation
  proceeds.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor skips the required pre-installation meeting for a
  waterproofing system to keep the schedule moving. When a leak develops after
  occupancy, the manufacturer denies the warranty claim, citing the missing
  documented meeting as grounds that its installation requirements were never
  confirmed as met.
