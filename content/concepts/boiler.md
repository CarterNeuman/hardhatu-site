---
id: concept-boiler
title: Boiler
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "23, HVAC"
definition: >
  A pressure vessel that heats water or generates steam by burning fuel or
  using electric resistance, used to provide space heating, domestic hot
  water, or process heat throughout a building, sized and selected, fire-tube
  or water-tube, condensing or non-condensing, based on the building's actual
  heating load and fuel availability.
whyItMatters: >
  A boiler is a pressure vessel operating at genuinely dangerous temperatures
  and pressures, so its installation, venting, and combustion air supply are
  governed by specific code requirements, and a mistake in any of those isn't
  just an efficiency problem, it's a real safety hazard.
example: >
  A building's mechanical engineer specifies a condensing boiler sized to the
  calculated heating load, with venting and combustion air sized specifically
  to the manufacturer's requirements for that unit rather than a generic vent
  size assumed to work for any boiler.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-pipefitter|Pipefitter]] or
  [[career-hvac-technician|HVAC Technician]] installs the boiler and its
  piping. A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] calculates the building's
  heating load and selects the boiler sized to meet it.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor installs a boiler using a smaller vent size than the
  manufacturer's installation instructions specify, assuming any reasonably
  sized vent will work. The undersized vent causes incomplete combustion and a
  buildup of carbon monoxide, caught only when a combustion safety inspection
  during commissioning flags the mismatch before the building is occupied.
