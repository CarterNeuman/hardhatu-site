id: concept-epa-608-certification
title: EPA Section 608 Certification
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  The federal credential, required under the Clean Air Act, that an HVAC
  technician must hold to legally purchase, handle, and work with refrigerant,
  earned by passing an EPA-approved exam covering refrigerant handling and
  recovery.
whyItMatters: >
  Older refrigerants, CFCs and HCFCs, damage the Earth's ozone layer when
  released into the atmosphere, which is why federal law bans venting them and
  requires anyone handling refrigerant to be certified in proper recovery
  procedures. This certification is the direct consequence of that
  environmental law, not a generic trade license.
example: >
  Before a technician can legally service a building's air conditioning
  system, a job that involves handling refrigerant, they have to hold EPA
  Section 608 certification in the correct category for that type of
  equipment.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-hvac-technician|HVAC Technician]] has to hold EPA Section 608
  certification before being allowed to purchase refrigerant or legally
  service equipment containing it.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An uncertified technician vents refrigerant into the atmosphere while
  servicing a unit, rather than properly recovering it, both violating federal
  law and releasing an ozone-depleting substance. The violation carries real
  regulatory penalties if it's discovered.
