id: concept-bms-integration
title: Building Management System (BMS) Integration
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "25, Integrated Automation"
definition: >
  The work of connecting a building's separate specialty systems, fire alarm,
  access control, elevators, lighting control, metering, into a single
  [[concept-building-automation-system|building automation system]] so facilities
  staff can monitor them from one place, rather than switching between each
  manufacturer's own standalone software.
whyItMatters: >
  Each specialty system typically comes from a different manufacturer with its
  own proprietary protocol, and getting them to actually communicate with a
  central BAS, rather than simply sitting next to it, has to be scoped, budgeted,
  and tested as its own piece of work, not assumed to happen automatically once
  every system is installed.
example: >
  On a large office building, the commissioning agent requires a documented
  integration test confirming the BAS can actually read real-time status from the
  fire alarm and metering systems, not just display a static value, before signing
  off on the integration as complete.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-construction-technology-manager|Construction Technology Manager]]
  scopes and oversees the integration work across specialty subcontractors. A
  [[career-commissioning-agent|Commissioning Agent]] verifies the integration
  actually functions before turnover.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor assumes the BAS will automatically integrate with the
  elevator controls once both systems are installed, but the elevator
  manufacturer's proprietary protocol was never scoped into the BAS integration
  contract. The owner discovers at turnover that elevator status simply doesn't
  appear on the BAS dashboard at all.
