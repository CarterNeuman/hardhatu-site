id: concept-residential-appliances
title: Residential Appliances
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  The kitchen and laundry appliances, ranges, refrigerators, dishwashers, washers
  and dryers, installed in multifamily or single-family construction, specified
  either as a builder-grade package included in the base price or as owner and
  buyer upgrade selections, each requiring the rough-in, electrical, gas, water,
  venting, to match the specific unit ultimately selected.
whyItMatters: >
  On a multifamily project with hundreds of units, a late change to the appliance
  package, a different refrigerator depth, or a gas range swapped for electric,
  can mean re-coordinating rough-in across every unit type in the building, not
  just the one unit that changed.
example: >
  A multifamily developer locks in the appliance package and specific model
  numbers before MEP rough-in begins, so the electrician and plumber can rough in
  outlets, gas lines, and dryer venting sized to the exact units rather than a
  generic placeholder.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] coordinates the appliance package
  selection against the construction schedule. An [[career-electrician|Electrician]]
  and [[career-plumber|Plumber]] rough in the specific connections each unit type
  needs.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A developer locks in a compact refrigerator model, and the electrician and
  cabinet installer build each unit's kitchen alcove to its exact depth. A late
  supply-chain substitution swaps in a different manufacturer's unit that runs two
  inches deeper, and the alcove is too shallow for the door to swing clear,
  requiring the built-in cabinetry to be reworked on site.
