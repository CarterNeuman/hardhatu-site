---
id: concept-visitor-management-systems
title: Visitor Management Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "28, Electronic Safety & Security"
definition: >
  Digital check-in systems at a building's front desk that screen, badge, and
  log visitors, replacing a paper sign-in sheet, tied into the building's
  access-control system so a visitor badge can be scoped to specific doors and
  time windows.
whyItMatters: >
  A visitor management system is most useful when it's actually integrated
  with access control rather than running as a separate standalone kiosk,
  since integration is what lets a visitor's badge expire automatically or
  restrict them to only the floor they're visiting.
example: >
  A contractor wiring a corporate lobby's visitor kiosk coordinates its
  network connection with the access-control integrator so visitor badges
  issued at the kiosk can be scoped to a single floor for the day rather than
  granting building-wide access.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-construction-technology-manager|Construction Technology
  Manager]] integrates the visitor system with the broader access-control
  platform during commissioning.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A visitor kiosk is installed and networked independently of the
  access-control system, and visitor badges end up granting building-wide
  access by default because no one configured the intended per-floor
  restriction.
relatedIds:
  - career-construction-technology-manager
