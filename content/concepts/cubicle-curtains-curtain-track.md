id: concept-cubicle-curtains-curtain-track
title: Cubicle Curtains & Curtain Track
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Privacy curtains hung from a ceiling-mounted track, used in hospital bays
  and exam rooms to create a private space around a bed or table without
  building a full partition.
whyItMatters: >
  The curtain track has to be anchored to the structure above the ceiling
  grid, not just the grid itself, since a track that's only screwed into
  lightweight ceiling tile will sag or pull loose under repeated use.
example: >
  A contractor installing cubicle curtain track in a hospital bay anchors the
  track to blocking fastened to the structure above, coordinating the blocking
  location with the ceiling grid installer before the ceiling tile goes in.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates the required
  structural blocking with the ceiling grid schedule, and the
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] plans for periodic curtain
  replacement, since these curtains are routinely laundered or replaced for
  infection control.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Curtain track is anchored directly into the suspended ceiling grid without
  separate structural blocking, and the track begins sagging within months
  under the repeated weight of opening and closing the curtains.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
