id: concept-design-build
title: Design-Build
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A project delivery method where a single entity, the design-builder, holds one
  contract with the owner covering both design and construction, replacing the
  traditional arrangement of separate contracts with an architect and a general
  contractor.
whyItMatters: >
  Combining design and construction under one contract shifts risk and responsibility
  differently than the traditional approach: the owner has a single point of
  accountability for both the design and the build, which can speed up delivery but
  changes how disputes over design errors get resolved compared to
  [[concept-design-bid-build|design-bid-build]].
example: >
  A developer hires a single design-build firm to both design and construct a new
  distribution warehouse, rather than hiring an architect to design it first and then
  bidding the finished design out to separate general contractors.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-preconstruction-manager|Preconstruction Manager]] gets involved much
  earlier on a design-build project, often participating directly in design decisions
  to keep the project constructible and on budget from day one. A
  [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] manages a single integrated team instead
  of coordinating between a separate architect and contractor.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner chooses design-build assuming it will automatically be faster and cheaper,
  without realizing that having one point of accountability also means less
  independent oversight of the design; with no separate architect representing the
  owner's interests, a design-build firm has more room to make design decisions that
  favor its own construction efficiency over what the owner actually wanted.
