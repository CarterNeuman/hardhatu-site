---
id: concept-flagpoles
title: Flagpoles
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Freestanding or building-mounted poles for flying flags, engineered for wind
  load and foundation depth based on their height and the local wind zone,
  rather than simply set in a hole and backfilled.
whyItMatters: >
  A flagpole is a tall, exposed structure that catches significant wind load,
  and an undersized foundation or base sleeve can let it lean or fall, which
  is both a liability risk and a common punch-list item on civic and school
  projects.
example: >
  A contractor installing a 40-foot flagpole at a new school verifies the
  foundation depth and rebar cage against the manufacturer's engineering table
  for the local wind zone, rather than using a generic foundation depth that
  might work for a shorter pole.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates the foundation work
  with the sitework schedule, since the flagpole foundation usually needs to
  go in during early sitework before paving closes in the area.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A flagpole foundation is poured to a generic depth without checking the
  manufacturer's wind-zone table for that specific pole height, and the pole
  develops a visible lean within its first year as the undersized foundation
  shifts.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
