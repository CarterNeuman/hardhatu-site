---
id: concept-exterior-site-furnishings
title: Exterior Site Furnishings & Amenities
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "32, Exterior Improvements"
definition: >
  Fixed outdoor furnishings, benches, bike racks, bollards, and trash
  receptacles, anchored to paving or set in concrete footings as part of
  sitework, specified for durability against weather and, where applicable,
  vehicle impact protection.
whyItMatters: >
  Bollards specifically are sometimes a life-safety element protecting
  pedestrians from vehicle incursion, with required spacing and impact ratings
  set by the site's security or accessibility requirements rather than chosen
  for appearance alone.
example: >
  A contractor installing bollards at a building's pedestrian plaza entrance
  verifies spacing meets both ADA wheelchair clearance and the specified
  vehicle-impact rating, rather than spacing them for visual symmetry alone.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates furnishing
  locations with the final hardscape layout, and the
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] plans for ongoing
  replacement and maintenance after installation.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Bollards are placed for visual symmetry rather than the specified
  impact-rated spacing, and a post-installation security review finds the gap
  between bollards wide enough for a vehicle to pass through, undermining the
  protection they were installed to provide.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
  - career-facilities-manager
