id: concept-net-zero-carbon-building
title: Net Zero Carbon Building
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  A building that eliminates or offsets its total carbon footprint, both the
  embodied     carbon in its construction materials and the operational carbon
  from its energy use over its     lifetime, a broader standard than
  [[concept-net-zero-energy-building|net zero energy]], which     only
  addresses operational energy consumption and generation, not materials.
whyItMatters: >
  A building can achieve net zero energy, generating as much renewable energy
  on-site as it     consumes annually, while still carrying a large
  embodied-carbon footprint from its concrete     and steel that a net zero
  energy certification never accounts for at all.
example: >
  A developer pursuing net zero carbon certification has to account for both
  the building's     projected operational energy use, offset through on-site
  solar, and the embodied carbon in     its structural concrete, pursuing a
  lower-carbon mix design to reduce the embodied-carbon     side of the ledger
  the energy offset alone wouldn't address.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-sustainability-leed-consultant|Sustainability/LEED Consultant]]
  tracks both     halves of a net zero carbon claim separately, since a
  project can hit its energy target while     still falling short on embodied
  carbon if the structural material choices aren't addressed     just as
  deliberately.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project markets itself as net zero carbon after only addressing its
  operational energy     use, when a genuine net zero carbon claim also
  requires accounting for and offsetting the     embodied carbon baked into
  the building's materials before construction even finishes.
