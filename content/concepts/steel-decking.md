id: concept-steel-decking
title: Steel Decking
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "05, Metals"
definition: >
  Corrugated sheet steel panels installed across structural steel beams to form the
  base for a concrete floor or roof; the decking itself often also acts as a working
  platform for other trades before the concrete is poured on top of it.
whyItMatters: >
  Steel decking has to be properly welded or fastened to the structural steel beneath
  it before it can safely support workers, equipment, or a concrete pour: an
  unsecured or improperly attached deck is a serious fall hazard and can't reliably
  support the wet concrete poured on top of it.
example: >
  After structural steel beams are erected on a floor, a crew installs steel decking
  across them, welding it to the beams at the required spacing before electricians,
  plumbers, and other trades are allowed to use it as a working platform.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-ironworker|Ironworker]] installs and welds steel decking into place. A
  [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] confirms decking is properly secured before
  allowing other trades onto a floor, since decking that looks installed can still be
  inadequately fastened underneath.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A decking crew tack-welds panels in fewer locations than specified to move faster,
  and the shortfall isn't caught by inspection before other trades start working on
  the floor. Under the weight of workers and staged material, a panel shifts, creating
  both a fall hazard and a gap that has to be caught and corrected before the concrete
  pour can proceed.
