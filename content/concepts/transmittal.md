---
id: concept-transmittal
title: Transmittal
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A cover document accompanying any set of drawings, submittals, or other
  project documents sent between parties, recording what was sent, to whom,
  when, and why, creating a paper trail independent of the documents
  themselves.
whyItMatters: >
  The document itself proves what was sent, but only the transmittal proves
  when it was sent and to whom, exactly the details a later dispute over
  timing or notice usually turns on.
example: >
  A general contractor sends a revised drawing set to a subcontractor with a
  formal transmittal recording the date, the specific revision number, and
  who received it, rather than an informal email with the files attached.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-document-control-specialist|Document Control Specialist]] issues
  and files every transmittal, maintaining the project's official record of
  what moved between parties and when.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A revised drawing set gets emailed informally without a formal transmittal
  recording what was sent and when. Months later, during a dispute over which
  drawing revision a subcontractor was actually working from, there's no
  transmittal record to settle the question.
