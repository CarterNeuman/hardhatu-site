id: concept-jobsite-iot-sensors
title: Jobsite IoT Sensors
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  Internet-connected sensors deployed around a jobsite to automatically
  collect real-time data, such as concrete curing temperature, equipment
  location and usage hours, or ambient jobsite conditions, feeding that
  data into a dashboard rather than relying on someone manually walking the
  site to check and log it.
whyItMatters: >
  Sensor data is only useful if someone actually monitors and acts on it, a
  concrete curing sensor that flags a temperature outside the acceptable
  range does nothing to prevent a quality problem if the alert goes
  unread, so an IoT deployment has to include a real process for someone to
  respond to what the sensors report.
example: >
  A contractor places wireless concrete curing sensors embedded in a large
  foundation pour, monitoring internal temperature remotely to confirm the
  concrete is curing within the specified range without a crew member
  having to physically check it every few hours around the clock.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-construction-technology-manager|Construction Technology Manager]]
  selects and deploys jobsite IoT systems. A
  [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] uses the sensor data as part of
  verifying the pour actually cured correctly.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project installs concrete curing sensors but nobody is assigned to
  actually monitor the dashboard over a holiday weekend. A cold snap pushes
  the curing temperature outside the acceptable range for several hours,
  and by the time anyone reviews the data days later, the affected
  concrete's strength is already in question, requiring core samples to
  verify it before construction can proceed.
