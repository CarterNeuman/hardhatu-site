---
id: concept-escalator
title: Escalator
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "14, Conveying Equipment"
definition: >
  A powered moving staircase installed between floors, engineered and installed by a
  specialized contractor similarly to an [[concept-elevator|elevator]], requiring a
  structural opening and support conditions built to the manufacturer's precise
  specifications well before the equipment arrives.
whyItMatters: >
  An escalator's structural support and the opening it sits within have to be
  coordinated early in design and construction, since the equipment is heavy,
  requires specific support points, and, like an elevator, is subject to its own
  dedicated safety code and inspection regime separate from general building
  inspections.
example: >
  A shopping center coordinates its structural design around the escalator
  manufacturer's required support points and opening dimensions from early in the
  design phase, rather than trying to fit a standard escalator into an opening sized
  without that coordination.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-engineer|Project Engineer]] coordinates escalator submittals
  and structural coordination early, given the equipment's long lead time and
  specific structural demands. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]]
  manages the escalator's ongoing code-mandated inspection and maintenance
  requirements after the building opens.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project's structural design finalizes the floor opening for an escalator before
  the specific manufacturer and model are selected, assuming escalator dimensions are
  standardized enough not to matter yet. The selected escalator's actual support
  requirements don't quite match the already-built opening, forcing a structural
  modification that would have been avoidable with earlier coordination.
