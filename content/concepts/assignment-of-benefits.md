---
id: concept-assignment-of-benefits
title: Assignment of Benefits (AOB)
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  An agreement in which a property owner transfers their right to collect
  insurance benefits directly to a contractor, letting the contractor bill and
  pursue payment from the insurer directly rather than going through the
  homeowner.
whyItMatters: >
  This arrangement, long used by restoration and roofing contractors to speed up
  getting paid, has drawn major legal reform in several states after a wave of
  abuse, so Florida overhauled its own assignment-of-benefits statute in 2019
  after years of abuse complaints, and several other states have since
  considered or passed similar restrictions.
example: >
  A water-damage restoration company has a homeowner sign an assignment of
  benefits form before starting emergency mitigation work, allowing the
  company to bill the insurer directly for the mitigation costs rather than
  waiting for the homeowner to be reimbursed and pay the contractor.
careerAngle: >
  Contractors using AOB agreements need current knowledge of their state's
  specific requirements, since an AOB that does not meet a state's current
  statutory requirements for notice, cancellation rights, or form content can
  be unenforceable, and several states have added new requirements in recent
  years.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Contractors sometimes treat an AOB as a simple, universally accepted payment
  shortcut, when several states have adopted detailed statutory requirements
  and some insurers now write policies that restrict or prohibit assignment
  altogether.
