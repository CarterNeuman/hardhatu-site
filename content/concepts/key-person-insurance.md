---
id: concept-key-person-insurance
title: Key Person Insurance
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A life or disability insurance policy a construction company buys on an owner,
  estimator, or other critical employee, with the business itself as the
  beneficiary, to cushion the financial impact if that person dies or becomes
  unable to work.
whyItMatters: >
  Many contracting companies are built around the relationships and technical
  knowledge of one or two key people, like a founding estimator or a licensed
  principal, and losing that person suddenly can threaten bonding capacity,
  client relationships, and the ability to complete bid work already on the
  books.
example: >
  A small specialty contractor's sole licensed principal, who personally holds
  the trade license the company operates under, is seriously injured in an
  accident. Key person insurance proceeds help the company cover the cost of
  bringing in a replacement licensed supervisor and stabilize operations while
  it reorganizes.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-business-development-manager|Business Development Manager]] and
  ownership weigh key person coverage as part of succession and continuity
  planning, and the [[career-risk-manager-construction|Risk Manager]] helps
  size the coverage against the real financial exposure a key person's loss
  would create.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A company assumes general business insurance already protects it if a key
  employee is suddenly lost, without realizing that standard commercial
  policies don't replace the lost revenue, client relationships, or bonding
  capacity tied to one irreplaceable person.
