---
id: concept-cat-team-storm-chaser
title: CAT Team / Storm Chaser
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  A catastrophe response crew, either an insurance carrier's own CAT team of
  traveling adjusters or a contracting crew that travels to disaster areas to
  handle a surge of storm-damage repair work, informally called storm chasers
  when referring to roofing and restoration contractors.
whyItMatters: >
  The term carries a mixed reputation in the industry: legitimate CAT response
  is essential after a major disaster overwhelms local capacity, but the
  storm-chaser label is also used for contractors known for aggressive
  door-to-door solicitation and lower-quality work that leaves town once the
  claims are paid.
example: >
  After a major hail event, a roofing company sends a storm-chaser crew into the
  affected region, going door to door offering free inspections and help
  filing claims, a model that brings fast capacity to an overwhelmed local
  market but also draws regulatory scrutiny over solicitation practices.
careerAngle: >
  Contractors doing legitimate CAT response work should expect skepticism from
  both homeowners and local regulators because of the storm-chaser reputation,
  and should lead with licensing, local presence, and references.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Not every out-of-town contractor working a disaster area is a bad actor, and
  not every local contractor is trustworthy; the storm-chaser label is about
  business practices, not simply where the crew is from.
relatedIds:
