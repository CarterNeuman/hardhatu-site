title: Wind Mitigation Inspection / Credit
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  An inspection, common in hurricane-prone states, that documents a home's
  wind-resistant construction features, such as roof shape, roof-to-wall
  connections, and opening protection, using a standardized state form that
  insurers use to apply premium discounts.
whyItMatters: >
  A contractor who installs wind-mitigation features without documenting them on
  the required inspection form leaves the homeowner's potential insurance
  savings on the table, since insurers generally require the completed form
  before applying any credit.
example: >
  After a contractor retrofits a home's roof-to-wall connections with hurricane
  clips and installs impact-rated windows, a licensed inspector completes the
  state's wind mitigation inspection form documenting each feature, which the
  homeowner submits to their insurer for a premium discount.
careerAngle: >
  Contractors doing hurricane-resilience retrofits in states with wind
  mitigation credit programs should tell clients to get a post-work
  inspection, since the insurance savings is often a bigger selling point than
  the retrofit cost itself.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Homeowners sometimes assume having wind-resistant features is enough on its
  own, when insurers typically require the completed, dated inspection form
  itself before applying any premium credit, and some states require the form
  to be refreshed periodically as rules are updated.
