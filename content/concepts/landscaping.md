id: concept-landscaping
title: Landscaping
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "32, Exterior Improvements"
definition: >
  The planting, irrigation, and finish grading work that completes a site's exterior
  appearance, typically one of the last scopes to finish on a project, since it has
  to happen after heavy equipment traffic and utility work near the building are
  already done.
whyItMatters: >
  Landscaping done before other exterior work is finished is landscaping that gets
  damaged and has to be redone, so its position at the very end of the site
  sequence isn't just aesthetic timing; it's a practical response to how much other
  work still needs vehicle and equipment access to the same areas.
example: >
  A project schedules final landscaping and irrigation installation only after
  paving, utility work, and exterior building trades are substantially complete,
  avoiding the cost of installing plantings that would just get driven over or dug up
  again by other trades still finishing nearby work.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-laborer|Construction Laborer]] or specialty landscaping crew installs
  plantings, irrigation, and finish grading to the landscape plan. A
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] inherits ongoing landscape
  maintenance responsibility after turnover, relying on the as-built irrigation plan
  to service the system without guessing at buried line locations.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project installs landscaping and irrigation ahead of a delayed paving scope,
  assuming paving would follow shortly after. When paving is delayed for weeks,
  construction equipment repeatedly crosses the newly planted areas, killing much of
  the landscaping and damaging irrigation lines that then have to be repaired and
  replanted at the project's expense.
