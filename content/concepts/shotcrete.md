---
id: concept-shotcrete
title: Shotcrete
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "03, Concrete"
definition: >
  Concrete or mortar pneumatically sprayed onto a surface at high velocity
  through a hose, placed either as a wet mix, pre-mixed with water, pumped, and
  sprayed, or a dry mix, dry materials sprayed with water added at the nozzle,
  used for pool shells, retaining walls, tunnel linings, and slope stabilization
  where conventional formwork isn't practical.
whyItMatters: >
  Shotcrete's quality depends heavily on the nozzleman's technique, holding the
  correct distance and angle, since spraying from too far away or at the wrong
  angle increases rebound, material that bounces off instead of bonding, and can
  leave hidden voids behind rebar, which is why ACI certifies nozzlemen
  specifically for this skill.
example: >
  A contractor building a swimming pool shell uses wet-mix shotcrete, sprayed by
  an ACI-certified nozzleman working around the pool's rebar cage from multiple
  angles to avoid trapping voids behind the steel.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-concrete-worker|Concrete Worker]] or specialty shotcrete crew
  places it. A [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] checks for voids and
  rebound pockets, particularly behind congested rebar.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An uncertified nozzleman sprays a retaining wall's rebar cage from too great a
  distance. Rebound material builds up behind the closely spaced bars instead of
  bonding solidly, leaving a hidden void that isn't discovered until a core
  sample taken during a later inspection reveals a soft spot.
