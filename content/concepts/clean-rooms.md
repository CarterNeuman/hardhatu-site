id: concept-clean-rooms
title: Clean Rooms
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "13, Special Construction"
definition: >
  Rooms engineered to control airborne particle contamination to a specific
  level, classified under ISO 14644-1 from ISO Class 1 (cleanest) to ISO Class
  9, achieved through HEPA or ULPA filtered air, controlled air changes, and
  sealed, easy-to-clean wall and floor finishes.
whyItMatters: >
  Semiconductor, pharmaceutical, and medical device manufacturing all depend
  on contamination levels low enough that ordinary HVAC and finishes can't
  achieve them, so clean room construction is really a specialized mechanical
  and finishing discipline layered onto standard building construction.
example: >
  A contractor building an ISO Class 7 clean room for a medical device
  manufacturer installs sealed, coved wall-to-floor transitions and verifies
  the mechanical contractor's air change rate meets the particle count
  required for that classification before the room is certified.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] designs the filtration and air change
  rate for the required ISO class, and the [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC
  Inspector]] oversees the particle-count certification testing before the
  room is turned over.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A clean room's wall finish is installed with standard caulked joints instead
  of the required sealed, coved transitions, and the room fails its
  particle-count certification because the joints are shedding fibers the
  filtration can't keep up with.
relatedIds:
  - career-mep-engineer
  - career-qaqc-inspector
