id: concept-cooling-tower
title: Cooling Tower
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "23, HVAC"
definition: >
  A large mechanical unit, typically located on a rooftop or at grade, that
  rejects heat from a building's chilled water system to the outside air by
  evaporating a portion of the water passing through it, the outdoor
  heat-rejection component that works together with a building's
  [[concept-chiller|chiller]] to provide cooling.
whyItMatters: >
  A cooling tower's warm, standing water is a documented environment where
  Legionella bacteria can grow and spread through the tower's mist, which is
  why a written water treatment and maintenance program, following ASHRAE 188
  guidance, is a genuine public health requirement rather than an optional
  maintenance nicety.
example: >
  A hospital's facilities team maintains a documented water treatment program
  for its rooftop cooling towers, including regular biocide treatment and
  water testing specifically to control Legionella risk, following ASHRAE
  guidance for building water systems.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-hvac-technician|HVAC Technician]] maintains the cooling tower's
  mechanical components. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] is
  typically responsible for ensuring the required water treatment program is
  actually followed, not just documented on paper.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building's facilities team lets its cooling tower's water treatment
  program lapse for several months to cut costs, skipping the routine biocide
  treatment and testing. A subsequent water test reveals Legionella bacteria
  at levels well above the acceptable threshold, forcing the tower to be shut
  down and disinfected before it can be safely restarted.
