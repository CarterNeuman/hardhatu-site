---
id: concept-mass-notification-systems
title: Mass Notification Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "28, Electronic Safety & Security"
definition: >
  Systems designed to alert building occupants to a wide range of emergencies,
  not just fire, including severe weather, active threats, and chemical
  releases, often integrated with the fire alarm's voice notification but
  governed by its own code requirements (NFPA 72 Chapter 24).
whyItMatters: >
  A mass notification system has to support pre-scripted and live messaging
  for different emergency types, which means its wiring, speaker coverage, and
  control interface go beyond what a standard fire alarm voice system alone
  provides.
example: >
  A contractor installing a university's mass notification system coordinates
  the system's ability to override the fire alarm's voice announcements for a
  non-fire emergency, like a weather alert, with the fire alarm contractor to
  avoid the two systems conflicting.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] defines the emergency scenarios
  the system needs to cover, and a fire/life-safety subcontractor integrates
  it with the building's fire alarm system.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A mass notification system is installed without coordinating its
  message-priority logic with the fire alarm system, and a test reveals the
  two systems can't agree on which message takes priority during a
  simultaneous fire and weather alert.
relatedIds:
  - career-safety-manager
