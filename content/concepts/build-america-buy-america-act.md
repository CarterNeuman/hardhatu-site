id: concept-build-america-buy-america-act
title: Build America, Buy America Act (BABA)
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A federal law requiring that iron, steel, manufactured products, and
  construction materials used in infrastructure projects receiving federal
  financial assistance, like federally funded water, transit, or broadband
  projects, be produced in the United States, subject to specific waivers.
whyItMatters: >
  BABA is broader than, and legally distinct from, the traditional "Buy
  American" requirements that apply to direct federal construction contracts
  under the Federal Acquisition Regulation; BABA instead reaches
  infrastructure projects built by state and local governments, utilities, and
  other recipients of federal grant funding.
example: >
  A city uses a federal infrastructure grant to fund a water-treatment plant
  upgrade. Because the project receives federal financial assistance, the
  construction materials and manufactured products used have to meet BABA's
  domestic-production requirements, unless the city successfully applies for a
  waiver for a specific component that isn't reasonably available
  domestically.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-procurement-manager|Procurement Manager]] verifies that materials
  and manufactured products sourced for a federally assisted infrastructure
  project meet BABA requirements, and the [[career-construction-
  attorney|Construction Attorney]] advises on pursuing a waiver when a
  required material genuinely isn't available from domestic sources.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor on a federally assisted infrastructure project assumes ordinary
  Buy American rules for direct federal contracts apply, without realizing
  BABA's separate and in some ways more specific domestic-content requirements
  govern the project instead.
