---
id: concept-site-visit-report
title: Site Visit Report
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A written record an architect or owner's representative prepares after
  periodically visiting the job site, documenting observed progress and any
  apparent issues, distinct from a contractor's own
  [[concept-daily-report|daily report]] since it reflects an independent,
  less-frequent outside observation rather than the contractor's own daily
  account.
whyItMatters: >
  An architect's site visit report carries particular weight because it's an
  independent observation, not the contractor's own account of its own work,
  and it's often what the architect relies on when certifying that a pay
  application's claimed percentage of completion actually matches what's
  visible in the field.
example: >
  An architect's monthly site visit report notes that framing appears roughly on
  pace with the contractor's claimed progress, but flags that a specific
  area's rough-in doesn't look as far along as the pay application's
  percentage suggests. That discrepancy becomes part of the basis for the
  architect holding back certification of that line item until it's resolved.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] or project
  architect prepares the site visit report during periodic observation visits,
  and the [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] watches
  for any discrepancy it flags against the contractor's own pay application
  claims.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor assumes its own daily reports and pay application claims are the
  only record of progress that matters, without accounting for the architect's
  independent site visit report potentially contradicting that account and
  affecting whether a pay application actually gets certified.
