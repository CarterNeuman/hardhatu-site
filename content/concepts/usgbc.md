---
id: concept-usgbc
title: U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC)
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  The nonprofit organization that created and administers LEED certification,
  setting the rating system's standards and overseeing the certification
  process projects go through to earn a LEED rating.
whyItMatters: >
  LEED doesn't exist as a government regulation, it's a voluntary
  certification program run by this specific nonprofit, which matters for
  understanding how LEED's standards get set, updated, and enforced, through
  the organization behind it rather than through a building code.
example: >
  A project team pursuing LEED certification submits documentation through the
  USGBC's certification process, which independently reviews the project's
  actual performance against the rating system's requirements before awarding
  a certification level.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-sustainability-leed-consultant|Sustainability/LEED Consultant]]
  manages a project's certification process directly with the USGBC, tracking
  documentation requirements throughout design and construction.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project markets itself as LEED certified before actually completing the
  USGBC's formal certification process and documentation review. The claim
  turns out to be premature once the certification is still pending, creating
  a credibility problem if anyone checks the actual status.
