---
id: concept-access-flooring
title: Access Flooring (Raised Flooring)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  A raised floor system of removable panels set on an adjustable pedestal grid
  above the structural slab, creating a plenum below for power, data cabling,
  and sometimes underfloor air distribution that can be reconfigured without
  cutting into the slab.
whyItMatters: >
  Access flooring is common in data centers and trading floors specifically
  because it lets cabling and cooling be reconfigured as equipment changes,
  without the cost and disruption of core-drilling the actual structural slab
  every time.
example: >
  A contractor installing access flooring in a new data center sets the
  pedestal grid height to accommodate both the underfloor cable routing and
  the air distribution the mechanical engineer designed for cooling the server
  racks above.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] designs the underfloor air
  distribution and cabling layout, and the [[career-electrician|Electrician]]
  routes power through the plenum once the panel grid is set.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Access flooring pedestals are set at a uniform height without accounting for
  a structural slab that isn't perfectly level, leaving some panels rocking
  slightly underfoot once the floor is fully installed.
relatedIds:
