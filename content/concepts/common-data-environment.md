title: Common Data Environment (CDE)
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  A single, shared online platform where all of a project's models,
  drawings, and documents are stored, organized by status, such as
  work-in-progress, shared, or published, so every project party accesses
  the same current information rather than working from files emailed
  around or stored in separate, disconnected systems.
whyItMatters: >
  A CDE's status workflow exists specifically to prevent someone from
  mistakenly working off an unapproved, in-progress file as though it were
  final, a mistake that's genuinely easy to make when files circulate by
  email with no clear indication of which version is actually current and
  approved.
example: >
  A project's CDE keeps a structural model in its work-in-progress area
  until it's formally reviewed and approved, only then moving it to the
  shared area other disciplines are permitted to coordinate against, so
  nobody accidentally builds on an unapproved version.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-document-control-specialist|Document Control Specialist]]
  manages the CDE's organization and status workflow. A
  [[career-bim-vdc-specialist|BIM/VDC Specialist]] ensures models are
  uploaded to the correct status area as they're developed.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A subcontractor bypasses the project's CDE and emails an outdated version
  of a coordination drawing directly to a colleague, who uses it to
  fabricate ductwork without realizing a newer, already-approved version
  existed in the CDE with several routing changes. The fabricated ductwork
  doesn't match the actual current design and has to be reworked.
