id: concept-cybersecurity-construction
title: Cybersecurity in Construction
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  The practices and technology protecting a construction company's project
  data, from BIM models and financial systems to jobsite IoT devices and
  connected equipment, against unauthorized access, a growing concern as
  more of a project's information and physical systems become networked and
  internet-connected rather than kept on paper or standalone computers.
whyItMatters: >
  Construction companies increasingly handle sensitive data, project
  financials, building security system designs, client information, that
  makes them a real target, and the industry's historically lower
  cybersecurity maturity compared to other sectors makes it an attractive
  target precisely because it's often less defended.
example: >
  A general contractor requires two-factor authentication and restricted
  access permissions on its cloud-based project management software,
  treating project financial and design data with the same security
  discipline as any other sensitive corporate system.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-construction-technology-manager|Construction Technology Manager]]
  or a dedicated IT role manages a construction company's
  cybersecurity practices across its office and jobsite systems.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A subcontractor's project management account is compromised through a
  phishing email, giving an attacker access to project schedules and,
  through a connected system, the building's own security system design
  during construction. The breach isn't discovered until fraudulent payment
  requests referencing real project details start reaching the general
  contractor's accounting department, well after the initial compromise.
