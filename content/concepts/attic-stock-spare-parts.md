id: concept-attic-stock-spare-parts
title: Attic Stock / Spare Parts
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  Extra material or equipment parts, like leftover flooring, paint, or
  replacement filters, turned over to the owner at project closeout
  specifically to make future repairs and maintenance easier by matching the
  exact product originally installed.
whyItMatters: >
  A product discontinued or changed by the manufacturer a few years after
  installation can leave an owner unable to find an exact match for a repair;
  attic stock solves that by setting aside enough of the original material at
  the time of installation, before it becomes unavailable.
example: >
  A hospital renovation project turns over several boxes of matching floor tile
  and a gallon of each wall paint color used on the project as attic stock at
  closeout, so facilities staff can patch a damaged section of floor or touch
  up a wall years later using the exact original product instead of a close
  but visibly different substitute.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] receives and inventories
  attic stock at turnover, and the [[career-contracts- administrator|Contracts
  Administrator]] confirms the contract's attic stock requirements are
  actually met before closeout is considered complete.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project closes out without confirming attic stock requirements were actually
  delivered, and the owner discovers years later, when a repair is needed,
  that no matching spare material was ever set aside and the original product
  is no longer available to match.
relatedIds:
