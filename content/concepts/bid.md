---
id: concept-bid
title: Bid
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A contractor's formal, priced proposal to perform the work described in a set of plans
  and specifications, the document an owner uses to compare contractors and choose who
  gets the project.
whyItMatters: >
  It's the moment a design on paper turns into an actual number and a commitment. A bid
  that's rushed or missing scope can lock a contractor into a price that doesn't cover
  the real cost of the work.
example: >
  Three general contractors each submit a sealed bid for a new warehouse project. The
  owner opens all three on the same day and compares not just the price, but each
  contractor's proposed schedule and qualifications.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-estimator|Estimator]] builds the bid from the ground up, pricing every
  trade, material, and piece of equipment the job will need. A
  [[career-senior-estimator|Senior Estimator]] typically reviews the final number before
  it goes out, since a bid mistake can't be undone once it's submitted and accepted.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An estimator misses an entire scope item, say, site utility work, while assembling a
  bid under a tight deadline. The bid comes in low and wins the project, but the
  contractor then has to either eat the cost of the missed scope or fight an uphill
  battle to get it added later as a change order, since the omission was their own
  mistake.
