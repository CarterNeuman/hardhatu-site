---
id: concept-window-treatments
title: Window Treatments
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Blinds, shades, and drapery systems specified and installed as part of a
  building's furnishings package, either manually operated or motorized and tied
  into a [[concept-building-automation-system|building automation system]],
  coordinated against window sizes and, for motorized units, low-voltage wiring
  that has to be roughed in before walls close.
whyItMatters: >
  Motorized shades need power and control wiring run to each window head before
  drywall closes the wall, an easy detail to miss if window treatments aren't
  finalized until late in the design process.
example: >
  An office building specifies motorized roller shades tied into the building
  automation system, and the electrician roughs in low-voltage wiring to each
  window head before the ceiling and wall finishes go in.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] roughs in the low-voltage wiring motorized
  units need. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] operates and
  maintains the system after occupancy.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Window treatments aren't finalized until after walls are already closed in, and
  the owner later selects motorized shades that need control wiring nobody roughed
  in. The fix requires surface-mounted conduit that wasn't part of the original
  design intent.
