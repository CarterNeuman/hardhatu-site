---
id: concept-interior-plantscaping
title: Interior Plantscaping
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Built-in planters and living plant installations integrated into a
  building's interior design, requiring coordination for drainage, irrigation
  access, and sometimes supplemental lighting that has to be planned during
  construction rather than added afterward.
whyItMatters: >
  A built-in planter without a drain tied into the building's plumbing becomes
  a maintenance problem the moment it's watered, since standing water in an
  undrained planter damages finishes and can promote mold.
example: >
  A contractor building a lobby's built-in planter boxes coordinates a floor
  drain connection for each one with the plumber, rather than leaving the
  planters as sealed boxes that will trap excess irrigation water.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] plans ongoing plant
  maintenance, while the [[career-plumber|Plumber]] roughs in drainage for any
  built-in planter during construction.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Built-in lobby planters are installed without floor drains, and within
  months the undrained boxes develop standing water and mold, requiring the
  planters to be opened up and retrofitted with drainage after the fact.
relatedIds:
  - career-facilities-manager
  - career-plumber
---
