id: concept-living-building-challenge
title: Living Building Challenge
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  A green building certification, administered by the International Living
  Future     Institute, organized around seven performance areas called Petals
  (Place, Water, Energy,     Health + Happiness, Materials, Equity, and
  Beauty), requiring all applicable Imperatives     within each Petal to be
  met, and based on verified actual performance after a full year of
  operation rather than on modeled or anticipated performance the way most
  other green     certifications allow.
whyItMatters: >
  Most green building certifications, including LEED, award points based on
  design     intent and energy modeling before a building even opens; Living
  Building Challenge     certification is only awarded after a full year of
  actual, measured operational     performance confirms the building really
  achieves what it was designed to, a materially     higher bar.
example: >
  A project pursuing Living Building Challenge certification completes
  construction and     operates for a full year, monitoring actual energy and
  water performance, before submitting     for certification, since a design
  that only models well on paper doesn't qualify until the     real-world
  performance data confirms it.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-sustainability-leed-consultant|Sustainability/LEED Consultant]]
  advising a     Living Building Challenge pursuit has to plan for that
  mandatory post-occupancy verification     period from the start, since it
  changes the project's certification timeline compared to a     LEED project
  that can certify at or shortly after construction completion.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A team assumes Living Building Challenge certification works the same way as
  LEED, where     a strong, well-modeled design is enough to certify at
  project completion, when Living     Building Challenge specifically requires
  a full year of verified actual performance data     before certification can
  be awarded at all.
