id: concept-compaction-equipment
title: Compaction Equipment (Rollers)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "31, Earthwork"
definition: >
  Heavy rollers or plate compactors that press loose soil or fill into dense,
  stable layers after it's placed, a required step before anything can be
  safely built on top of that fill.
whyItMatters: >
  Loose fill settles unevenly over time if it isn't compacted first, which can
  crack a slab or foundation poured on top of it months or years later.
  Compaction equipment is what turns loose material into a stable base that a
  structure can actually be trusted to sit on.
example: >
  After fill is placed in shallow layers, called lifts, a crew runs a roller
  over each lift to compact it before placing the next layer, rather than
  filling the whole area at once and compacting it only at the top.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-equipment-operator|Equipment Operator]] runs compaction equipment
  in careful, measured passes over each lift, since compacting too few passes
  or too thick a lift leaves the lower material under-compacted even if the
  surface looks fine.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A crew places fill in one deep lift instead of several shallow ones to save
  time, then compacts only the top. A soil compaction test taken later shows
  the lower material never actually reached the required density, and the fill
  has to be removed and redone in proper lifts.
