id: concept-drying-log-documentation
title: Drying Log / Moisture Readings Documentation
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The day-by-day moisture-reading record a restoration crew keeps for every affected
  material and room throughout a drying job, per IICRC S500, the paper trail proving
  a structure was actually returned to a dry standard before it gets closed back up.
whyItMatters: >
  Without a drying log, there's no way to prove how many days of equipment rental
  and labor a job actually required, which is exactly the kind of line item an
  insurer disputes first when a bill looks high.
example: >
  A five-day drying job logs moisture readings for the subfloor, drywall, and framing
  in each affected room every day, showing the numbers trending down toward a dry
  standard until the final reading confirms it, before equipment is pulled and the
  job is closed.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-insurance-restoration-estimator|Insurance Restoration Estimator]]
  submits the drying log alongside the invoice to justify billed equipment days to
  the insurer.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A restoration company submits a final invoice for a multi-day drying job without a
  supporting drying log showing daily moisture readings. The insurer's adjuster
  disputes the number of drying days billed, since there's no documentation proving
  equipment was actually needed that long, and cuts the payment down to a guessed,
  shorter timeline.
