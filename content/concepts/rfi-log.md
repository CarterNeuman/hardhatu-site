---
id: concept-rfi-log
title: RFI Log
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A tracking document listing every [[concept-rfi|RFI]] submitted on a project,
  its date, who it was sent to, its due date, and whether and when it was
  answered, the working tool behind the broader RFI process.
whyItMatters: >
  An unanswered RFI sitting past its due date is often the quiet cause of a
  field delay nobody notices until it's already too late, so the log's real
  value is surfacing overdue RFIs before the work waiting on an answer
  actually stalls out.
example: >
  A project engineer reviews the RFI log every Monday and finds three RFIs from
  the mechanical subcontractor sitting unanswered past their due dates.
  Following up with the design team that day gets answers back before the
  mechanical crew actually reaches the point where they need that information
  to keep working.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-project-engineer|Project Engineer]] maintains the RFI log and
  chases overdue responses, and the [[career-document-control-specialist|
  Document Control Specialist]] keeps the log itself current and accessible to
  the whole project team.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project tracks RFIs informally through email threads rather than a proper
  log, and nobody notices a critical RFI sitting unanswered for weeks until
  the subcontractor waiting on that answer finally stops work and files a
  delay claim.
