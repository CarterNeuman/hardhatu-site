id: concept-debris-removal-coverage
title: Debris Removal Coverage
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  A property insurance provision that pays for clearing and disposing of damaged
  materials, wreckage, and debris after a covered loss, typically capped at a
  percentage of the overall claim or a specific dollar sublimit separate from
  the repair coverage itself.
whyItMatters: >
  Contractors scoping a large loss should check this sublimit early, since a
  major catastrophe loss can generate debris removal costs that exceed the
  coverage's cap, leaving the property owner to fund the difference.
example: >
  After a storm collapses a detached garage, the demolition and debris hauling
  costs come in close to the policy's debris removal sublimit, leaving a
  restoration contractor to flag for the homeowner that disposal costs beyond
  that cap may not be reimbursed.
careerAngle: >
  Estimators on total-loss or heavy-debris jobs should calculate anticipated
  debris removal costs against the policy's specific sublimit early, rather
  than assuming it's folded into the general dwelling coverage limit.
whatGoesWrong: >
  People sometimes assume debris removal is automatically and fully covered as
  part of any repair claim, when most policies cap this cost separately and a
  large-loss cleanup can exceed that cap.
