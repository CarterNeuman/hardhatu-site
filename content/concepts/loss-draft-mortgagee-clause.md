id: concept-loss-draft-mortgagee-clause
title: Loss Draft / Mortgagee Clause
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  An insurance claim check written jointly to the homeowner and their mortgage
  lender, issued because the mortgagee clause in the property policy names the
  lender as a loss payee with its own stake in the repair.
whyItMatters: >
  Contractors who don't know this process get blindsided: the big check arrives,
  but the lender's name is on it too, and the lender often won't release the
  full amount until it sees proof the repair work is actually happening.
example: >
  A roofing contractor finishing a $40,000 claim-funded reroof has to submit an
  inspection report and sometimes photos to the mortgage company's loss-draft
  department before the final disbursement clears, even though the homeowner
  already endorsed the check.
careerAngle: >
  Contractors doing insurance restoration work need to budget for the lender's
  inspection and paperwork cycle, not just the insurer's, or cash flow on the
  job stalls.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Crews sometimes finish the job expecting payment within days of submitting the
  final invoice, not realizing a mortgage company's loss-draft department can
  take weeks to release held-back funds, and don't warn the homeowner this
  delay is normal.
