---
id: concept-water-leak-detection-automation
title: Water Leak Detection Automation
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "25, Integrated Automation"
definition: >
  Sensors placed near water heaters, under sinks, and at other leak-prone
  locations, tied into an automated system that can alert facilities staff
  and, in more advanced installations, automatically shut off the water supply
  when a leak is detected.
whyItMatters: >
  A leak detector that only sounds a local alarm is far less useful than one
  that's networked to alert staff remotely, since a leak in an unoccupied area
  can otherwise go undetected for days before anyone notices the local alarm.
example: >
  A contractor installing leak detection in a multifamily building's
  mechanical closets ties each sensor into the building's central monitoring
  system so a leak triggers a remote alert to the facilities team rather than
  relying on someone walking past to hear a local alarm.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] defines which locations
  need coverage, and the [[career-plumber|Plumber]] or electrician installs
  the sensors and automatic shutoff valves.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Leak detectors are installed with only a local audible alarm in an
  unoccupied mechanical room, and a leak runs for a full weekend before anyone
  discovers it, causing far more water damage than a networked alert would
  have allowed.
relatedIds:
  - career-facilities-manager
  - career-plumber
---
