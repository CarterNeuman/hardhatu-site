id: concept-general-requirements
title: General Requirements (CSI Division 01)
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  The section of the project specifications, CSI MasterFormat Division 01,
  covering administrative and procedural requirements that apply across the entire
  project rather than to one specific trade: submittal procedures, quality control
  requirements, temporary facilities, closeout procedures, and similar cross-cutting
  items.
whyItMatters: >
  Every trade-specific specification section assumes Division 01's procedures are
  already in place, such as how submittals get formatted and routed or what closeout
  documentation looks like, so it's effectively the instruction manual for how the
  whole project's paperwork and procedures are supposed to work.
example: >
  Division 01 of a project's specifications spells out exactly how every submittal
  must be formatted and transmitted, a requirement that then applies uniformly across
  the electrical, plumbing, and every other trade-specific specification section.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-engineer|Project Engineer]] refers to Division 01 constantly,
  since it governs the actual mechanics of submittals, RFIs, and closeout that make
  up much of the job day to day. A
  [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]] follows its quality
  control and testing procedures as the baseline framework for the project's entire
  QC plan.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project engineer processes submittals using a generic format from a previous
  project instead of checking this project's own Division 01 requirements. The
  architect rejects a batch of submittals purely for improper formatting, creating a
  delay that has nothing to do with the actual technical content of what was
  submitted.
