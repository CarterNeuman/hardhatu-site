id: concept-xactimate
title: Xactimate
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The estimating software the property insurance industry has effectively
  standardized on for writing a [[concept-scope-of-loss|scope of loss]], a shared
  price list and line-item structure that lets adjusters, public adjusters, and
  contractors all produce estimates that can actually be compared against each
  other, even though no regulation requires using it specifically.
whyItMatters: >
  Because nearly every party in a claim uses the same software and the same
  underlying price list, a dispute over an estimate usually comes down to specific
  line items or quantities rather than two completely incompatible formats: that
  shared structure is what makes negotiation between competing estimates possible at
  all.
example: >
  An insurance adjuster and a public adjuster each write their own estimate for the
  same water-damaged room using Xactimate, and because both estimates use the same
  line-item codes and regional pricing, the two sides can identify and negotiate
  their specific disagreements rather than reconciling entirely different formats.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-insurance-adjuster|Insurance Adjuster]] and an
  [[career-insurance-restoration-estimator|Insurance Restoration Estimator]] both
  rely on Xactimate proficiency as close to a practical job requirement, even though
  no state license mandates the specific software.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor unfamiliar with Xactimate submits a repair estimate in a homemade
  spreadsheet format instead. The insurer's adjuster can't easily map the
  contractor's line items to Xactimate's standard categories, and the mismatch
  slows down negotiation considerably compared to a claim where both sides start
  from the same estimating structure.
