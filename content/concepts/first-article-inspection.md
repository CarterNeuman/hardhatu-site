id: concept-first-article-inspection
title: First Article Inspection
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  An inspection of the very first unit built or installed of a repetitive item,
  such as a typical hotel guest room or a precast panel, verifying it meets
  spec before the contractor proceeds to fabricate or install the remaining
  units, catching a systemic error once instead of after it's already been
  repeated dozens or hundreds of times. Distinct from a
  [[concept-mock-up|mock-up]], which is often a standalone sample built
  specifically for approval and torn down afterward, a first article
  inspection checks the actual first unit in the real production run itself.
whyItMatters: >
  Catching a mistake in unit one of two hundred is far cheaper and faster to fix
  than catching the same mistake in unit one hundred fifty, once rework has to
  chase down every unit built in between.
example: >
  On a hotel renovation, the owner's representative and general contractor inspect
  the first fully finished guest room prototype in detail before crews start work
  on the remaining one hundred ninety-nine rooms.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] performs and documents the
  inspection. A [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] signs off
  before the contractor is authorized to proceed to the remaining units.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor proceeds to build out an entire floor of rooms using the same tile
  layout approved in the first article, but a subtle grout color mismatch isn't
  caught until the owner walks room forty. The rework now spans dozens of finished
  rooms instead of the one unit the inspection was supposed to catch it in.
