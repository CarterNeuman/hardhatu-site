id: concept-fire-pump
title: Fire Pump
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "21, Fire Suppression"
definition: >
  A dedicated pump that boosts water pressure and flow to a building's fire
  sprinkler and standpipe systems when the incoming municipal water supply
  alone can't provide the pressure or volume the fire protection design
  requires, typically powered by an electric motor or a diesel engine as a
  backup against a power outage.
whyItMatters: >
  A fire pump's reliability is tested specifically through a "churn" test,
  running the pump against a closed valve at no flow, as part of both its
  initial acceptance test and ongoing annual testing, since a pump has to
  actually perform on demand during a real fire, not just sit installed and
  assumed to work.
example: >
  A high-rise building's diesel fire pump undergoes its required annual churn
  test, run against a closed valve to confirm the pump starts and operates
  correctly without a real flow demand, followed by a flow test at its rated
  capacity.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-pipefitter|Pipefitter]] or specialty fire protection contractor
  installs the fire pump and its piping. A
  [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] or fire protection engineer
  coordinates the required acceptance and annual testing.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building's fire pump passes its initial acceptance test, but the annual
  testing required afterward is skipped for several years as the
  responsibility falls through the cracks between the owner and the
  maintenance contractor. The building's property insurer flags the lapsed
  test history during a routine loss-control survey and threatens to cancel
  coverage, forcing the owner to bring the pump current on testing before the
  policy renews.
