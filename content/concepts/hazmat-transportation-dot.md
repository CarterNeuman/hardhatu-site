id: concept-hazmat-transportation-dot
title: Hazardous Materials Transportation (DOT HAZMAT)
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  Department of Transportation rules governing how regulated hazardous
  materials, like fuel, certain chemicals, or contaminated soil above a
  reportable threshold, must be packaged, labeled, documented with a shipping
  paper, and transported by a properly trained driver.
whyItMatters: >
  A contractor moving hazardous material off a jobsite, even just fuel or a
  batch of contaminated soil, can be a hazmat shipper under these rules
  whether or not the company thinks of itself as being in the hazmat business.
example: >
  A remediation contractor shipping drums of contaminated soil off a jobsite
  properly labels and placards the load, completes the required hazmat
  shipping paper, and confirms the hauling company's driver holds current
  hazmat transportation training before the truck leaves the site.
careerAngle: >
  Project managers coordinating hazardous material removal should confirm the
  hauler's hazmat certifications and paperwork before the shipment leaves,
  since the shipper, not just the hauler, can share liability for a
  transportation violation.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Contractors sometimes assume that hiring a hauling company transfers all
  hazmat transportation responsibility away from them, when the shipper
  generating and offering the material for transport carries its own distinct
  set of packaging, labeling, and documentation obligations under these rules.
