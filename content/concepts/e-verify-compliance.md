id: concept-e-verify-compliance
title: E-Verify Compliance
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  The federal electronic system contractors use to confirm that newly hired
  employees are legally authorized to work in the United States, mandatory for
  most federal construction contractors and increasingly required by
  individual states.
whyItMatters: >
  Federal contractors covered by the FAR E-Verify clause must enroll within 30
  days of contract award and run E-Verify on new hires working on the covered
  contract, and the requirement flows down to covered subcontracts over a set
  dollar threshold, so prime contractors have to confirm their subcontractors
  are enrolled too.
example: >
  A general contractor wins a federal building contract that includes the FAR
  E-Verify clause. The company enrolls in the program within the required
  30-day window, and its subcontract agreements for work above the covered
  threshold require those subcontractors to enroll and participate as well.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-hr-manager-construction|HR Manager]] manages E-Verify enrollment
  and new-hire verification, and the [[career-contracts-
  administrator|Contracts Administrator]] confirms E-Verify flow-down language
  is included in covered subcontracts before they're executed.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor assumes E-Verify only applies to its own direct hires and
  overlooks that the requirement often has to flow down contractually to
  subcontractors performing covered work above the federal dollar threshold.
