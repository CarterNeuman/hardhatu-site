id: concept-retainage-bond
title: Retainage Bond
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A surety bond a contractor can post in place of cash retainage, in
  jurisdictions that allow it, letting the contractor receive the withheld
  retainage amount in cash up front while the bond itself stands behind the
  owner's right to recover that amount if the work is never properly finished.
  It's an alternative path to freeing up cash tied to
  [[concept-retainage|retainage]], distinct from the normal timeline for
  [[concept-retainage-release|retainage release]] at substantial and final
  completion.
whyItMatters: >
  It improves a contractor's cash flow by freeing up retainage dollars that
  would otherwise sit unavailable until the very end of the project, but it
  shifts the owner's protection from actual withheld cash to a bond claim,
  which some owners are less comfortable with and which not every state
  statute or contract actually permits.
example: >
  A contractor nearing the end of a long project has a large retainage balance
  tied up in unreleased cash. Instead of waiting for final completion, the
  contractor posts a retainage bond under the state statute that allows it,
  receiving the retained funds immediately while the bond stands behind the
  owner's protection if a defect surfaces later.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-surety-bond-underwriter|Surety Bond Underwriter]] evaluates and
  prices a contractor's request for a retainage bond, and the
  [[career-construction-accountant|Construction Accountant]] weighs the
  improved cash flow against the bond's premium cost before recommending it.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor assumes retainage bonds are available on every project, without
  checking that the specific state or contract actually permits substituting a
  bond for cash retainage, and finds the option simply isn't on the table for
  that particular job.
