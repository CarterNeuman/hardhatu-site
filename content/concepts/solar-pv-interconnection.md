id: concept-solar-pv-interconnection
title: Solar PV Interconnection
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "26, Electrical"
definition: >
  The process and equipment required to connect a building's solar
  photovoltaic system to the local electric utility's grid, governed by a
  formal interconnection agreement with the utility and requiring specific
  equipment, such as an inverter with anti-islanding protection, that
  automatically disconnects the solar system from the grid during a utility
  outage to protect utility workers.
whyItMatters: >
  A solar system that keeps feeding power into the grid during a utility
  outage, rather than automatically disconnecting, creates a genuine hazard
  for utility line workers who reasonably expect a de-energized line during an
  outage, which is exactly why anti-islanding protection is a code and
  utility requirement, not an optional feature.
example: >
  A commercial building's solar installer submits an interconnection
  application to the local utility before construction begins, since utility
  review and approval of the interconnection agreement can take longer than
  the solar installation itself.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-solar-installer|Solar PV Installer]] installs the system and its
  interconnection equipment. A
  [[career-renewable-energy-project-manager|Renewable Energy Project Manager]]
  manages the utility interconnection application and approval process,
  which often has to start well before
  construction.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A solar installer completes a system's physical installation without
  submitting the utility interconnection application until construction is
  essentially finished. The utility's review and approval process takes
  several additional months, and the system sits fully built but unable to
  legally operate while the owner waits on the interconnection agreement.
