id: concept-warranty-log
title: Warranty Log
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A tracking document listing every warranty on a project, who issued it, what
  it covers, its start date, and when it expires, since different materials
  and equipment carry different warranty periods that don't all start or end
  on the same date.
whyItMatters: >
  A general one-year contractor [[concept-warranty|warranty]] and a twenty-year
  manufacturer roof warranty don't run on the same clock, and an owner who
  doesn't track each warranty's actual start and end date can easily miss a
  window to file a legitimate claim before it quietly expires.
example: >
  A facilities manager inherits a warranty log at closeout listing a one-year
  general contractor warranty, a ten-year HVAC equipment warranty, and a
  twenty-year roof membrane warranty, each with its own start date. Years
  later, a roof leak surfaces well after the general contractor's one-year
  warranty has expired, but the log confirms the roof membrane's longer
  manufacturer warranty is still active and the claim can still be filed.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] maintains the warranty
  log after turnover and checks it before filing any repair claim, and the
  [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] compiles the
  log's initial entries from closeout documentation before turnover.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner assumes every warranty on a project expires at the same time as the
  general one-year contractor warranty, and misses filing a legitimate claim
  under a longer manufacturer warranty simply because no warranty log exists
  to show that specific coverage was actually still active.
