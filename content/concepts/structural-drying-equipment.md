id: concept-structural-drying-equipment
title: Structural Drying Equipment (Air Movers, LGR Dehumidifiers)
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The core equipment package used in water-damage drying: air movers that create
  airflow across wet surfaces to speed evaporation, and low-grain refrigerant
  (LGR) dehumidifiers that pull the resulting moisture out of the air, sized
  and placed according to the affected area's square footage and the IICRC's
  drying standards.
whyItMatters: >
  Insurance adjusters and claim reviewers often check equipment counts and
  run-time logs against IICRC-based placement guidelines, so under- or
  over-equipping a drying job directly affects whether the restoration invoice
  gets paid in full.
example: >
  A restoration company dries a 500-square-foot flooded room using a calculated
  number of air movers positioned to create airflow across every wet surface,
  plus LGR dehumidifiers sized to the room's moisture load, logging daily
  readings to document the drying progress.
careerAngle: >
  Drying technicians need to know industry placement formulas, since an
  insurer's claim reviewer may challenge equipment billing that looks out of
  proportion to the documented affected area.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Crews sometimes add extra equipment beyond what's documented as necessary to
  speed billing, which invites exactly the kind of invoice scrutiny from
  adjusters that can delay or reduce payment on the whole claim.
