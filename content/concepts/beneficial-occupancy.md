---
id: concept-beneficial-occupancy
title: Beneficial Occupancy
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  An arrangement allowing an owner to occupy and use part or all of a
  facility before the project reaches full
  [[concept-substantial-completion|substantial completion]], common on
  phased projects, requiring clear agreement on what obligations, insurance,
  maintenance, warranty start dates, shift to the owner once occupancy
  begins.
whyItMatters: >
  Moving in early feels like a simple convenience, but it quietly starts the
  clock on obligations, insurance responsibility, maintenance duties, warranty
  periods, that a documented beneficial occupancy agreement is what actually
  pins down, rather than leaving them to assumption.
example: >
  An owner moves into the completed east wing of a phased office building
  under a signed beneficial occupancy agreement specifying that its warranty
  period for that wing begins on the move-in date, not on the project's
  eventual full completion.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] and an
  [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] negotiate the
  beneficial occupancy agreement's specific terms before any early move-in
  happens.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner moves into a completed wing of a phased project under an informal
  verbal understanding rather than a documented beneficial occupancy
  agreement. A dispute later arises over whether the warranty clock started
  at that early move-in or at the project's eventual full substantial
  completion.
