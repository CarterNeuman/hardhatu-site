---
id: concept-oac-meeting-minutes
title: OAC (Owner-Architect-Contractor) Meeting Minutes
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The formal written record of a regular Owner-Architect-Contractor
  coordination meeting, capturing decisions made, action items assigned, and
  open issues discussed, serving as an official project record that can later
  resolve disputes over what was actually agreed to.
whyItMatters: >
  A verbal decision made in a meeting only becomes part of the project's
  actual record once it's written into the minutes, everyone's memory of a
  conversation tends to drift, but the minutes don't.
example: >
  An owner verbally approves a minor scope adjustment during a weekly OAC
  meeting, and the meeting minutes capture that approval as a specific,
  dated action item rather than leaving it as an unrecorded verbal
  understanding.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] typically prepares and
  distributes OAC meeting minutes, making sure decisions made in the room
  actually make it into the project's written record.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A verbal decision made during an OAC meeting never makes it into the formal
  meeting minutes. Months later, when a dispute arises over whether the owner
  actually approved a change, there's no written record supporting either
  side's memory of the conversation.
