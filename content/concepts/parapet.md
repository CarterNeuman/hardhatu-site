---
id: concept-parapet
title: Parapet
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "07, Thermal and Moisture Protection"
definition: >
  The short wall that extends above a roof's edge, capping where the roofing
  membrane and the building's exterior wall meet, and that has to be properly
  flashed and waterproofed since it's one of the most common places a roof
  actually leaks.
whyItMatters: >
  A parapet creates a transition between two different waterproofing systems,
  roof membrane and wall cladding, meeting at a vulnerable corner, which is
  exactly why parapet flashing details get so much attention in roofing
  design. A detail that looks minor on a drawing is often where a real roof
  leak ends up starting.
example: >
  A roofing crew installs flashing that wraps the parapet wall's cap and ties
  the roof membrane up and over it, creating a continuous waterproof
  transition at the corner where the roof and the parapet wall meet.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-roofer|Roofer]] pays particular attention to parapet flashing
  details, since this transition is one of the most common points of failure
  on an otherwise well-installed roof.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A roofing crew installs the main roof membrane correctly but rushes the
  parapet flashing detail where it should wrap properly up and over the wall.
  A gap at that transition isn't noticed until water finds its way through
  during the first significant rain after installation.
