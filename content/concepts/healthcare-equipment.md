---
id: concept-healthcare-equipment
title: Healthcare Equipment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Fixed medical equipment built into a healthcare facility, imaging rooms
  (MRI, CT), surgical lighting and booms, and nurse call integration points,
  each with structural, shielding, power, and clearance requirements specific
  to the equipment manufacturer, coordinated well before the equipment itself
  arrives on site.
whyItMatters: >
  An MRI suite in particular needs radiofrequency shielding and a structural
  floor rated for the magnet's weight, decided by the equipment vendor's
  requirements rather than general building code, so equipment selection has
  to happen early enough to inform the structural and shielding design.
example: >
  A contractor building an MRI suite coordinates the room's RF shielding and
  reinforced floor framing directly against the specific MRI model the
  hospital has already selected, since shielding requirements vary by
  manufacturer and magnet strength.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] coordinates the specialized power,
  cooling, and shielding each piece of equipment requires, and the
  [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] verifies the room meets the
  vendor's commissioning checklist before equipment installation.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An MRI suite's structural and shielding design is finalized before the
  hospital locks in its equipment vendor, and the eventually-selected magnet's
  actual requirements don't match what was built, forcing costly rework to the
  shielding.
relatedIds:
  - career-mep-engineer
  - career-qaqc-inspector
