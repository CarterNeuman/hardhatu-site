id: concept-reality-capture
title: Reality Capture (Laser Scanning & Point Clouds)
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  Using laser scanners or photogrammetry to capture a highly accurate, three-dimensional
  "point cloud" of existing physical conditions, such as a site, a structure, or an
  already-built space, that can be compared directly against design drawings or a
  [[concept-bim|BIM]] model.
whyItMatters: >
  Existing conditions on a renovation project rarely match the original drawings exactly,
  after years of undocumented changes. Reality capture replaces guesswork about what's
  actually there with a precise, measurable record, which matters most on renovation and
  retrofit work where the design has to fit around what already exists.
example: >
  Before designing a building renovation, a survey team laser-scans the existing
  structure and produces a point cloud that reveals the actual floor-to-floor heights are
  two inches different from the original drawings, a discrepancy the design team
  accounts for before finalizing new ceiling and mechanical layouts.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-surveyor|Surveyor]] increasingly uses laser scanning alongside traditional
  survey equipment to capture existing conditions with a level of detail conventional
  methods can't match. A [[career-drone-uav-specialist|Drone/UAV Specialist]] captures
  the same kind of point cloud data from the air for a site's exterior and topography,
  using [[concept-drone-mapping|photogrammetry]] instead of ground-based scanning.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A renovation project designs against the original drawings without verifying existing
  conditions with a reality capture scan, assuming decades-old drawings are still
  accurate. Field crews discover a significant discrepancy once demolition begins,
  forcing a design change mid-construction that a pre-design scan would have caught.
