---
id: concept-bid-alternates
title: Bid Alternates
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  Optional scope items priced separately from a bid's base price, letting an owner
  add or remove specific work, such as upgraded flooring, an additional floor, or
  alternate HVAC equipment, after seeing what each alternate actually costs,
  without having to rebid the whole project.
whyItMatters: >
  Alternates give an owner facing budget uncertainty real control over final scope
  after bids come in, letting them add or drop specific items based on actual
  pricing rather than committing to everything, or nothing, before knowing the
  numbers.
example: >
  A school district's bid package includes a base price for a standard roof plus a
  separately priced alternate for a more durable membrane. Once bids come in
  within budget, the district accepts the alternate on top of the base bid.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-estimator|Estimator]] prices each alternate separately from the base
  bid. An [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] decides which
  alternates to accept once real bid numbers are in hand.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An estimator prices an alternate assuming it shares mobilization costs with the
  base bid scope. The owner doesn't accept the alternate until several weeks after
  award, by which point the relevant subcontractor has already demobilized, and
  the actual cost of executing the alternate now runs well above the number
  carried in the bid.
