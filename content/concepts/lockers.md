---
id: concept-lockers
title: Lockers
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Individual storage compartments, metal, plastic laminate, or solid plastic,
  installed in groups (banks) in locker rooms, schools, and employee break
  areas, specified by gauge, ventilation style, and base/trim condition to
  match the room's use.
whyItMatters: >
  Locker banks have to be anchored and leveled correctly on uneven slab, and
  their ventilation style (louvered versus solid doors) matters for odor
  control in athletic and industrial settings, details easy to overlook until
  the finished room doesn't perform as intended.
example: >
  A contractor installing a locker bank in a high school athletic facility
  specifies louvered-door lockers rather than solid-door, since the
  ventilation keeps gear from developing odor between uses.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] or
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] confirms the locker count
  and configuration match the room's actual occupancy before the order is
  placed, since lockers are a long-lead item that's expensive to resize after
  delivery.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A locker order is placed from an early floor plan, and a later room-layout
  change leaves several lockers blocked by a relocated door swing, discovered
  only once the bank is already delivered and partially installed.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
  - career-facilities-manager
