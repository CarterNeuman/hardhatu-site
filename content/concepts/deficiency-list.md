---
id: concept-deficiency-list
title: Deficiency List
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A running list of incomplete, non-conforming, or substandard work identified
  by quality control staff during construction itself, distinct from the
  [[concept-punch-list|punch list]] compiled specifically near the end of the
  project for the owner's final walkthrough.
whyItMatters: >
  Catching and tracking a deficiency while the relevant trade is still actively
  on site, rather than waiting until a final punch walk months later, means it
  can usually be fixed immediately and cheaply; the same issue left until
  closeout often requires remobilizing a trade that's already moved on to
  another project.
example: >
  A QA/QC inspector notices improperly spaced anchor bolts during a mid-project
  walk and adds the issue to the deficiency list immediately, getting it
  corrected the same week while the steel erector is still actively on site,
  rather than letting it sit undiscovered until the final punch list
  walkthrough months later when remobilizing that crew would cost far more.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] maintains the deficiency list
  throughout construction, flagging issues as they're found, and the
  [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]] tracks deficiency
  trends across trades to catch a systemic quality problem early.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project relies entirely on the final punch list to catch quality issues,
  without an ongoing deficiency list during construction itself, and discovers
  problems only after the responsible trade has already demobilized and left
  the site, making the same fix far more expensive to arrange.
relatedIds:
