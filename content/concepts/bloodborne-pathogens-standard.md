id: concept-bloodborne-pathogens-standard
title: Bloodborne Pathogens Standard
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  OSHA's standard requiring employers to protect workers reasonably anticipated
  to contact blood or other potentially infectious materials on the job,
  covering exposure control plans, protective equipment, and post-exposure
  response procedures.
whyItMatters: >
  Construction workers rarely think of their job as having bloodborne pathogen
  exposure risk, but first aid responders, workers handling injury scenes, and
  crews on jobs involving biohazard or trauma cleanup all fall under this
  standard's protections and requirements.
example: >
  A company designates specific employees as its first-aid responders, trains
  them under the bloodborne pathogens standard, and stocks first-aid kits with
  gloves and other protective barriers so a responder treating an injured
  coworker isn't directly exposed to blood.
careerAngle: >
  Safety managers should identify which employees on a jobsite are reasonably
  expected to provide first aid and make sure only those specific employees
  get the standard's required training and protective equipment.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Employers sometimes assume this standard only applies to healthcare settings,
  when any construction company with designated first-aid responders, or crews
  doing biohazard-adjacent work like trauma scene cleanup, falls squarely
  under its requirements.
