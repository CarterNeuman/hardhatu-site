id: concept-final-payment-release-of-claims
title: Final Payment & Release of Claims
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A document signed at the close of a contract in which a contractor or
  subcontractor accepts final payment and releases the other party from any
  further claims related to the work, except for claims specifically carved
  out and preserved.
whyItMatters: >
  Signing a final release without carving out a known pending claim, like an
  unresolved change order or a disputed delay claim, can permanently waive
  that claim even if it was never actually paid, which makes reading the
  release language as carefully as the payment amount itself.
example: >
  A subcontractor has a change order still pending approval when asked to sign a
  final release in exchange for the last payment on the contract. The
  subcontractor adds specific carve-out language preserving the right to
  pursue that change order, rather than signing a release that would otherwise
  waive it along with everything else.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] reviews final
  release language against the project's outstanding change orders and claims
  before advising anyone to sign, and the [[career-construction-
  attorney|Construction Attorney]] drafts carve-out language to preserve
  claims that shouldn't be released.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A party signs a final release just to get the last check moving, without
  checking whether a pending claim or change order is still open, and
  unknowingly waives it permanently in the process.
relatedIds:
