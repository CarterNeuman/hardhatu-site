id: concept-request-for-qualifications
title: Request for Qualifications (RFQ)
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A document an owner issues asking contractors to submit their experience, past
  projects, safety record, and team qualifications, without pricing yet, typically
  used to narrow a large pool of interested firms down to a shortlist before asking for
  proposals or bids.
whyItMatters: >
  Reviewing detailed proposals or bids from every interested contractor on a large
  project wastes everyone's time if half of them were never realistically qualified to
  begin with. An RFQ filters the field first, so the more detailed, expensive next step
  only goes to firms genuinely capable of the work.
example: >
  A city issues an RFQ for a new courthouse, asking interested firms to submit their
  experience with similar government buildings and their safety record. Out of twenty
  firms that respond, five are shortlisted to receive the full request for proposal.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] designs the qualification
  criteria and reviews the responses to build the shortlist. A
  [[career-preconstruction-manager|Preconstruction Manager]] assembles the firm's
  qualifications package, which often becomes the foundation for a more detailed
  [[concept-request-for-proposal|RFP]] response later if the firm makes the shortlist.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A firm submits a generic, boilerplate qualifications package to an RFQ without
  tailoring it to the specific project type. A city building a courthouse is looking
  specifically for government and security-sensitive project experience, and the
  generic submission, despite genuinely qualified past work, doesn't make that case
  clearly enough to survive the shortlist cut.
