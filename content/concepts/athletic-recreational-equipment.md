id: concept-athletic-recreational-equipment
title: Athletic & Recreational Equipment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Fixed sports and recreation equipment, gymnasium backstops and basketball
  hoops, scoreboards, climbing walls, and playground structures, installed
  with structural anchoring and, for play equipment, fall-zone surfacing
  specified to safety standards rather than aesthetic placement alone.
whyItMatters: >
  Playground equipment in particular is governed by fall-height and surfacing
  standards (like those from the Consumer Product Safety Commission) that
  dictate how much impact-absorbing surfacing has to extend around each piece,
  and skipping that review is a common liability exposure.
example: >
  A contractor installing a new playground structure calculates the required
  fall-zone radius and engineered wood fiber depth based on the structure's
  platform height, rather than using a fixed surfacing depth for every piece
  of equipment regardless of height.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates equipment anchoring
  with the sitework and landscaping schedule, and the
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] plans for the surfacing's
  ongoing maintenance once installed.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Playground equipment is installed with a uniform surfacing depth that
  doesn't account for the fall height of the tallest piece, and a safety audit
  after opening flags the shortfall, requiring the surfacing to be excavated
  and redone.
relatedIds:
