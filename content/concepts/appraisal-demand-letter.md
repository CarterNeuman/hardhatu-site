id: concept-appraisal-demand-letter
title: Appraisal Demand Letter
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The formal written notice a policyholder (or insurer) sends invoking the
  policy's appraisal clause, triggering the contractual process of each side
  naming an appraiser to resolve a disputed claim amount.
whyItMatters: >
  Sending this letter is a specific procedural trigger, not just a request to
  negotiate further, and once sent it starts a clock on deadlines for naming
  appraisers under most policy language.
example: >
  After months of disagreement over a hail-damage estimate, a homeowner's
  attorney sends the insurer a formal appraisal demand letter invoking the
  policy's appraisal clause, which requires the insurer to name its own
  appraiser within the policy's specified timeframe.
careerAngle: >
  Contractors advising clients on a stuck claim should know that invoking
  appraisal requires this specific written demand, not just a verbal request,
  and that it shifts the dispute into a separate, more formal process.
whatGoesWrong: >
  People sometimes assume simply disagreeing with an offer repeatedly is the
  same as invoking appraisal, when the formal process typically only begins
  once this specific written demand is actually sent.
