id: concept-vr-design-review
title: Virtual Reality Design Review
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  Using a fully immersive VR headset to walk through a BIM model at true scale
  before     construction begins, letting an owner or design team experience
  spatial relationships,     ceiling heights, and sightlines directly,
  distinct from     [[concept-augmented-reality-jobsite|augmented reality on
  the jobsite]], which overlays     digital information onto the real,
  already-built physical environment rather than     simulating a space that
  doesn't exist yet.
whyItMatters: >
  A 2D floor plan or even a 3D screen rendering doesn't reliably convey what a
  space will     actually feel like at true human scale; an owner reviewing a
  hospital room layout in VR can     catch that a planned equipment location
  blocks the bed's turning radius in a way the same     model viewed on a flat
  screen never made apparent.
example: >
  A hospital's design team walks the facility's clinical staff through a VR
  model of a     proposed patient room before construction, and a nurse flags
  that the planned equipment     placement would block the bed's turning
  radius during an emergency, a problem caught in VR     that the 2D floor
  plan review had missed entirely.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-bim-vdc-specialist|BIM/VDC Specialist]] prepares BIM models for
  VR walkthroughs     during design review meetings, giving an owner or end
  user a true-scale sense of a space     well before construction locks in
  decisions that would be far more expensive to change     later.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A team conducts a VR design review but doesn't actually capture and act on
  the     feedback gathered during the walkthrough, treating it as a
  presentation rather than a design     input, which wastes the session's real
  value: catching spatial problems before they're built.
