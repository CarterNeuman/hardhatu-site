---
id: concept-additional-insured-endorsement
title: Additional Insured Endorsement
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A policy endorsement adding another party, typically the owner or general
  contractor, as an additional insured on a subcontractor's liability policy,
  extending that policy's coverage to protect the additional insured for
  liability arising from the subcontractor's work.
whyItMatters: >
  A [[concept-certificate-of-insurance|certificate of insurance]] showing
  additional insured status isn't proof the endorsement was actually issued,
  the certificate is just a snapshot someone filled out, the endorsement itself
  is the actual policy language that has to be verified separately.
example: >
  A general contractor's contract requires every subcontractor to name it as an
  additional insured, and the risk manager verifies the actual policy
  endorsement, not just the certificate of insurance, before allowing that
  subcontractor to mobilize on site.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-risk-manager-construction|Risk Manager]] verifies additional
  insured endorsements are actually issued, not just claimed on a certificate,
  while a [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] tracks
  which subcontracts require it.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A GC's contract requires every subcontractor to name it as an additional
  insured, but the contracts administrator never actually verifies the
  endorsement was issued, just files the certificate of insurance without
  checking further. A claim arises, and the GC discovers it was never actually
  added as an additional insured, leaving it without the coverage the contract
  was supposed to guarantee.
