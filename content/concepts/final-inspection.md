id: concept-final-inspection
title: Final Inspection
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The building department's last inspection confirming a completed project
  meets code before issuing a
  [[concept-certificate-of-occupancy|certificate of occupancy]], distinct
  from the [[concept-punch-list|punch list]] walk, which addresses the
  owner's and architect's own contractual completeness
  standard rather than code compliance.
whyItMatters: >
  Passing the owner's punch list walk says nothing about whether the
  building department will pass its own, separate final inspection, the two
  are checking for genuinely different things, and clearing one doesn't
  guarantee clearing the other.
example: >
  A completed office renovation passes its punch list walk with the owner
  and architect, then separately passes the building department's final
  inspection confirming code compliance, two distinct approvals that both
  had to happen before occupancy.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] verifies code items are
  actually in place before the building department's final inspection,
  catching gaps the owner's own punch list might never have checked for.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project passes its owner-side punch list walk with the architect but
  fails the building department's final inspection over a code item the
  punch list never checked, since the two inspections are looking for
  genuinely different things. The certificate of occupancy the punch list
  alone can't produce is delayed as a result.
