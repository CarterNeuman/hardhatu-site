---
id: concept-pre-construction-survey
title: Pre-Construction Condition Survey
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A documented survey, photos, video, and written notes, of the existing
  condition of a jobsite and neighboring properties before construction
  begins, establishing a baseline record to resolve later disputes over
  whether construction activity actually caused new damage.
whyItMatters: >
  Without a documented baseline, "did the project cause that crack" becomes a
  question of competing memories rather than evidence, exactly the kind of
  dispute a pre-construction survey exists to prevent before it can even
  start.
example: >
  Before starting deep excavation near a historic neighboring building, a
  contractor documents every existing crack, settlement sign, and visible
  defect on that building with dated photos, creating a record to compare
  against once construction is underway.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] typically coordinates the survey
  before mobilization, while a
  [[career-risk-manager-construction|Risk Manager]] relies on it later if a
  neighboring property claim arises.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor skips a pre-construction survey of an adjacent building before
  starting deep excavation nearby. When the neighbor later claims new cracks
  appeared, there's no baseline documentation to prove whether the damage
  predates the project or was actually caused by it.
