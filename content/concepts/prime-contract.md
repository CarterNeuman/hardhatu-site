---
id: concept-prime-contract
title: Prime Contract
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  The direct contract between the owner and the general contractor (or design-builder,
  or CM at risk), the main agreement everything else on a project, including every
  subcontract, ultimately flows from and has to be consistent with.
whyItMatters: >
  Every subcontract is supposed to "flow down" the relevant terms of the prime
  contract, so a subcontractor is bound by many of the same obligations the GC agreed
  to with the owner. Understanding what's actually in the prime contract is often the
  starting point for resolving a dispute anywhere else on the project.
example: >
  A prime contract between an owner and general contractor includes a strict notice
  requirement for any delay claim. That same notice requirement gets written into
  every subcontract too, so a subcontractor's late notice of their own delay can
  jeopardize the GC's ability to pass that claim up to the owner.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] makes sure every
  subcontract properly flows down the prime contract's key terms rather than leaving
  gaps. A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] refers back to the prime contract
  constantly when a dispute or change comes up, since it's the ultimate source of what
  the GC actually agreed to.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor signs subcontracts that don't flow down a strict notice
  requirement from the prime contract. A subcontractor's delay isn't reported in time
  under the prime contract's own deadline, and the GC loses the ability to pass a
  legitimate delay claim up to the owner, a gap created entirely by an incomplete
  flow-down of the prime contract's terms.
