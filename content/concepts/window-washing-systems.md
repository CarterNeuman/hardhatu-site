---
id: concept-window-washing-systems
title: Window Washing Systems (Building Maintenance Units)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Permanently installed equipment, roof-mounted davit arms, tie-back anchors,
  or full building maintenance units (BMUs) that travel on a roof-level track,
  used to access and clean a building's exterior facade safely after
  construction, required by code on most mid-rise and high-rise buildings.
whyItMatters: >
  A building's facade access plan has to be designed in along with the
  structure, since retrofitting anchor points or a BMU track onto a finished
  building's roof and facade is far more expensive than designing them in from
  the start.
example: >
  A structural engineer sizes roof anchor points for a mid-rise office
  building's window-washing system during design, so the rigging loads are
  accounted for in the roof structure rather than requiring reinforcement
  after the building is complete.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-structural-engineer|Structural Engineer]] designs the anchor
  points or BMU track loads into the roof structure, and the
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] schedules the periodic
  inspections most jurisdictions require for the system.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building is designed without a facade access plan, and the owner discovers
  after occupancy that there's no safe, code-compliant way to clean the upper
  floors' windows without expensive after-the-fact anchor installation.
relatedIds:
  - career-structural-engineer
  - career-facilities-manager
---
