---
id: concept-detention-equipment
title: Detention Equipment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Security-rated equipment for correctional and detention facilities, cell
  doors and hardware, security glazing, and detention furniture, built to
  resist tampering and impact well beyond standard commercial-grade products.
whyItMatters: >
  Detention equipment specifications come from the facility's security
  classification rather than general building code, and substituting standard
  commercial hardware for detention-rated equipment, even temporarily during
  construction, can create a real security gap.
example: >
  A contractor building a county detention facility installs security-rated
  cell door hardware specified to the facility's security classification,
  rather than substituting standard commercial door hardware even as a
  temporary measure during phased construction.
careerAngle: >
  A specialty detention equipment subcontractor typically self-installs this
  equipment, while the [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates
  the structural openings and sequencing around the secure installation.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Standard commercial door hardware is temporarily installed in a detention
  facility during a construction phase to save time, and the gap is discovered
  during a security audit before the facility's final detention-rated hardware
  goes in.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
---
