---
id: concept-excavator
title: Excavator
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "31, Earthwork"
definition: >
  A piece of heavy equipment with a rotating cab and a hinged digging arm
  ending in a bucket, used for precise digging, trenching, and loading
  material, as distinct from equipment designed for pushing or grading.
whyItMatters: >
  An excavator's hinged arm lets an operator dig with real precision in a
  tight, specific spot, which is exactly why it's the machine used for
  trenching around utilities or digging a foundation, rather than a machine
  like a bulldozer that's built to move large volumes of material across a
  wide area.
example: >
  A crew digging a trench for a new sewer line uses an excavator rather than a
  bulldozer, since the excavator's operator can dig a precise, narrow trench
  to an exact depth without disturbing the ground on either side.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-equipment-operator|Equipment Operator]] trains specifically on
  excavators to dig accurately around buried utilities and existing
  structures, where precision matters more than raw volume.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An operator digs a trench with an excavator without first confirming where
  buried utility lines actually run, relying on an old site map instead of a
  current utility locate. The bucket strikes a live line that wasn't where the
  map showed it.
