id: concept-certified-restorer-designation
title: Certified Restorer (CR) Designation
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The IICRC's advanced-level individual credential, sitting well above entry-level
  technician certifications like WRT (Water Damage Restoration Technician) and
  requiring years of documented experience plus a rigorous exam, generally
  considered the top individual credential in the restoration industry.
whyItMatters: >
  A lot of restoration marketing leans on "IICRC certified" without specifying which
  credential, and most technicians hold only an entry-level cert achievable in a few
  days of coursework, genuinely different from the years of experience a CR
  designation represents.
example: >
  A restoration company's senior project manager holds the CR designation after
  years of documented fieldwork and passing IICRC's advanced exam, while most of the
  crew working under them hold only the entry-level WRT certification.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-restoration-project-manager|Restoration Project Manager]] with several
  years of field experience is a natural candidate to pursue the CR designation as a
  career-advancing credential.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A restoration company advertises its technicians as "IICRC certified" without
  distinguishing that most hold only an entry-level WRT certification rather than the
  CR designation, misleading a policyholder who's comparing contractors on
  credentials that aren't actually equivalent.
