id: concept-systems-furniture
title: Systems Furniture
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Modular office furniture, workstations, and panel systems, along with their
  integrated power and data infrastructure, that ties directly into a building's
  raised floor or overhead power and data distribution, specified and purchased
  separately from the base building construction and coordinated for installation
  timing against the base building's electrical rough-in.
whyItMatters: >
  The furniture's power and data whips have to plug into a distribution system, a
  raised floor, a poke-through, or an overhead grid, that the electrician has to
  rough in to match the furniture layout, and a layout finalized after that
  rough-in is complete means relocating outlets rather than simply plugging in.
example: >
  An office tenant finalizes its systems furniture layout and works with the
  electrician to place floor poke-throughs at each workstation cluster before the
  raised access floor panels go in over them.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] roughs in the power and data distribution
  the furniture plugs into. A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]]
  coordinates the furniture vendor's install schedule against the general
  contractor's.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A tenant reuses furniture inventory from a previous office at a higher
  workstation density than the electrician's rough-in was designed for. Several
  poke-through circuits end up carrying more workstations than they're rated for,
  and the tenant discovers the overload only when breakers start tripping during
  the first full week of occupancy.
