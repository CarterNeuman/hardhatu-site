title: Video Surveillance (CCTV)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "28, Electronic Safety and Security"
definition: >
  Cameras and recording infrastructure installed to monitor and record
  activity around a building, ranging from a handful of cameras on a small
  commercial building to a comprehensive system with hundreds of cameras and
  dedicated video management software on a large campus, specified based on
  coverage needs, resolution requirements, and how long recorded footage has
  to be retained.
whyItMatters: >
  Camera placement and lens selection have to be planned around what the
  footage will actually need to prove, a wide-angle camera covering a whole
  parking lot might never capture a legible license plate or face, which only
  becomes a real problem after an incident, when the footage exists but turns
  out to be useless for identification.
example: >
  A retail building's security consultant specifies a dedicated, narrow-angle
  camera at each entrance capable of capturing a legible facial image,
  separate from wider-coverage cameras monitoring the general parking lot,
  since the two cameras serve genuinely different identification purposes.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] or specialty security integrator
  installs cameras and their cabling. A
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] manages the system's video
  retention and access after occupancy.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building's security system is installed with wide-angle cameras covering
  every area equally, without a dedicated close-in camera at the entrances.
  After a theft, the recorded footage shows the incident happening but the
  suspect's face is never resolvable at that camera's coverage width, leaving
  the business unable to identify who was responsible.
