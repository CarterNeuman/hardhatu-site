id: concept-decorative-art-glass-metal
title: Decorative Art Glass & Metal
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "12, Furnishings"
definition: >
  Custom decorative elements, stained or etched glass panels, ornamental metal
  screens and railings, and sculptural features, fabricated off-site to a
  design and installed as a finish element, distinct from structural glazing
  or structural metalwork.
whyItMatters: >
  Because these pieces are custom-fabricated and often one of a kind, lead
  times run long and field verification of the actual opening dimensions has
  to happen before fabrication, since a custom glass or metal piece that
  doesn't fit can't simply be swapped for a stock replacement.
example: >
  A glazier field-verifies the exact opening dimensions for a custom
  stained-glass installation after the surrounding framing is complete, rather
  than relying on the architect's drawn dimensions, since the fabricator needs
  exact measurements before cutting irreplaceable glass.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-glazier|Glazier]] or [[career-ironworker|Ironworker]] installs
  the finished piece, while the [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]]
  builds the long fabrication lead time into the overall schedule.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A custom decorative metal screen is fabricated from architectural drawings
  without field verification, and the finished piece arrives slightly too
  large for the as-built opening, forcing an expensive refabrication that
  wasn't in the schedule.
relatedIds:
  - career-glazier
  - career-ironworker
  - career-project-manager
