id: concept-builders-risk-exclusions
title: Builder's Risk Exclusions
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  The specific categories of loss a builder's risk policy does not cover, even
  though the policy broadly protects a structure under construction, such as
  faulty workmanship, design error, normal wear, or loss from the contractor
  simply walking off the job. These exclusions apply on top of whatever
  [[concept-builders-risk-insurance|builder's risk insurance]] the project
  carries in the first place.
whyItMatters: >
  Builder's risk is often sold as a broad, all-risk form of coverage, which
  makes it easy to assume everything is covered, but the exclusions determine
  exactly where that protection actually ends, and those gaps are usually
  filled by other, more specific policies.
example: >
  A builder's risk claim for water damage from a burst pipe gets denied because
  the policy's faulty workmanship exclusion applies once the insurer
  determines the pipe was improperly installed. The contractor's commercial
  general liability policy, not the builder's risk policy, ends up being the
  relevant coverage for that loss instead.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-builders-risk-underwriter|Builder's Risk Underwriter]] prices and
  structures the policy around which exclusions apply to a given project, and
  the [[career-insurance-adjuster|Insurance Adjuster]] determines whether a
  specific loss falls inside an exclusion when a claim comes in.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor assumes builder's risk covers every loss on the job site, and is
  caught off guard when a claim for a defect or workmanship issue is denied
  under an exclusion the policy always contained.
