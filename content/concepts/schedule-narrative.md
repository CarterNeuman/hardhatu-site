---
id: concept-schedule-narrative
title: Schedule Narrative
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A written explanation accompanying a schedule update, describing in plain
  language what changed since the last update, why, and what it means for the
  project's finish date, rather than leaving the reader to interpret a dense
  schedule file on their own.
whyItMatters: >
  A schedule update full of shifted dates and new logic ties doesn't explain
  itself; the narrative is what tells an owner or executive who isn't going to
  open the actual schedule file what actually happened this month and whether
  it matters, in language that doesn't require scheduling software to
  understand.
example: >
  A monthly schedule update shows dozens of shifted activity dates. The
  accompanying schedule narrative explains in plain language that the shifts
  stem from one delayed equipment delivery, that the delay has been absorbed
  by available float without affecting the finish date, and that no owner
  action is currently needed.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-scheduler|Scheduler]] writes the schedule narrative accompanying
  each update, and the [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] relies on it
  to brief the owner on schedule status without requiring everyone in the room
  to read the schedule file itself.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A schedule update is distributed without any accompanying narrative, and an
  owner reviewing dozens of shifted dates has no way to tell which changes
  actually matter and which are routine, creating unnecessary alarm or, worse,
  missing a real problem buried in the noise.
relatedIds:
