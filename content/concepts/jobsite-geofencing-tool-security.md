id: concept-jobsite-geofencing-tool-security
title: Jobsite Geofencing & Tool Theft Prevention
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  GPS or Bluetooth-tag-based virtual perimeters set around a jobsite or piece
  of equipment     that trigger an alert the moment a tracked tool, machine,
  or vehicle crosses the boundary     without authorization, aimed
  specifically at theft prevention and unauthorized movement     rather than
  general asset-location tracking.
whyItMatters: >
  Tool and equipment theft is a significant, recurring cost on construction
  sites, and a     geofence turns what used to be a next-morning discovery
  that something is missing into a     real-time alert the moment it actually
  leaves the site, often while there's still time to     intervene.
example: >
  A contractor sets a geofence around a laydown yard storing tagged power
  tools; when a     tagged tool crosses the boundary after hours, the system
  immediately texts the site     superintendent, who reviews the gate's
  security camera footage in time to identify the     vehicle involved.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] or site superintendent sets up
  geofence     boundaries around high-value laydown areas and equipment,
  distinct from the     broader [[concept-key-asset-tracking-systems|key and
  asset tracking]] used for     facility-level access control.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A company tags its tools for geofencing but never actually sets an active
  boundary alert,     assuming the tags alone provide protection; a tag with
  no configured geofence just sits there     collecting location history
  nobody is actively monitoring.
