id: concept-structural-welding
title: Structural Welding
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "05, Metals"
definition: >
  Welding performed on structural steel connections that are critical to a building's
  structural integrity, distinguished from general welding by strict qualification
  requirements for both the welder and the specific welding procedure, since a
  defective structural weld can be a life-safety failure hidden inside a finished
  connection.
whyItMatters: >
  Structural welds are typically required to be performed by welders certified to a
  specific code (commonly the American Welding Society's structural welding code) and
  are subject to non-destructive testing, ultrasonic or radiographic inspection,
  precisely because a visual inspection alone can't reveal internal weld defects.
example: >
  A steel erection project requires all structural welders to hold current AWS D1.1
  certification for the specific welding process and position being performed, and
  critical welds are tested with ultrasonic inspection before the connection is
  considered complete.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-welder|Welder]] performing structural connections has to hold and
  maintain the specific certifications the project requires, distinct from general
  fabrication welding. A [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]]
  coordinates the non-destructive testing of critical structural welds as a required
  [[concept-special-inspection|special inspection]].
whatGoesWrong: >
  A welder without current certification for the specific welding position performs a
  critical structural connection under schedule pressure, and the lapse isn't caught
  until a later document review. The weld has to be independently tested to confirm it
  actually meets code before it can be accepted, since the welder's own qualification,
  not just the weld's visual appearance, is part of what code compliance requires.
