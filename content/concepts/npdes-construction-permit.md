---
id: concept-npdes-construction-permit
title: NPDES Construction General Permit
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  A Clean Water Act permit required for any construction activity disturbing one
  acre or more of land, or a smaller site that's part of a larger common plan of
  development that will ultimately disturb an acre or more, requiring a stormwater
  pollution prevention plan and erosion/sediment controls in place before any
  ground disturbance begins.
whyItMatters: >
  The "common plan of development" rule means several small, individually
  under-an-acre lots inside one larger development can still require permit
  coverage together, even though no single lot would trigger it on its own.
example: >
  A developer building six half-acre residential lots inside a ten-acre
  subdivision needs NPDES permit coverage for the whole development, even though
  each individual lot disturbs less than an acre by itself.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-civil-engineer|Civil Engineer]] designs the erosion and sediment
  control plan the permit requires before a single acre of ground gets disturbed.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A general contractor starts clearing and grading a three-acre site before the
  NPDES permit and its required stormwater controls are actually in place. A state
  inspector cites the project for an unpermitted discharge, and work stops until
  the paperwork and controls catch up to where the grading already is.
