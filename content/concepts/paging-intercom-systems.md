id: concept-paging-intercom-systems
title: Paging & Intercom Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "27, Communications"
definition: >
  Building-wide speaker and intercom systems used for announcements, door
  intercoms, and emergency paging, wired separately from a building's regular
  sound system and often tied into the fire alarm system for life-safety
  announcements.
whyItMatters: >
  When a paging system doubles as part of the life-safety notification
  strategy, it has to meet the same code-driven intelligibility and coverage
  requirements as a standalone fire alarm voice system, which changes how
  speakers are spaced and how the system is tested.
example: >
  A contractor installing a school's paging system coordinates speaker
  placement and amplifier zoning with the fire alarm contractor, since the
  same speakers carry both daily announcements and emergency instructions.
careerAngle: >
  A low-voltage technician under the [[career-electrician|Electrician]]'s
  supervision installs the wiring and speakers, and the
  [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] confirms the system meets
  emergency-notification coverage requirements.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A paging system is installed and zoned for daily announcement use only, and
  the fire alarm contractor later finds the speaker coverage doesn't meet the
  intelligibility requirement for emergency voice announcements, forcing
  additional speakers to be added.
relatedIds:
  - career-electrician
  - career-safety-manager
