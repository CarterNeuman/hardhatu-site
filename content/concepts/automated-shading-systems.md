---
id: concept-automated-shading-systems
title: Automated Shading Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "25, Integrated Automation"
definition: >
  Motorized window shades or blinds tied into a building automation system,
  adjusting automatically based on sun position, time of day, or interior
  temperature rather than requiring manual operation at every window.
whyItMatters: >
  Automated shading only delivers its energy and comfort benefits if its
  control logic actually reflects the building's orientation and glare
  patterns, so a generic schedule applied without that analysis often leaves
  shades closed when they should be open or vice versa.
example: >
  A controls contractor programs a building's automated shading to track sun
  angle specifically for each facade's orientation, rather than applying one
  shading schedule uniformly to every window regardless of which direction it
  faces.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] coordinates the shading control logic
  with the building's energy model, and the [[career-electrician|Electrician]]
  wires the motorized shade motors.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Automated shades are programmed with a single schedule for the whole
  building, and the west-facing offices get no relief from afternoon glare
  because the schedule was tuned to the south-facing side instead.
relatedIds:
  - career-mep-engineer
  - career-electrician
---
