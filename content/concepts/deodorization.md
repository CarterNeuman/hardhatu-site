---
id: concept-deodorization
title: Deodorization (Odor Removal)
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The restoration step that targets lingering odors, commonly from smoke,
  sewage, or mold, using methods like thermal fogging, ozone treatment, or
  hydroxyl generators, applied after the source of the odor has been
  physically removed or remediated.
whyItMatters: >
  Odor that isn't fully eliminated is one of the most common reasons a
  restoration customer disputes a completed job, since smell is something the
  customer notices immediately and attributes to incomplete work even when the
  structural repair itself was done correctly.
example: >
  After removing smoke-damaged materials and cleaning all affected surfaces, a
  fire-restoration crew runs thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators through
  the space for several days to neutralize residual smoke odor before the
  homeowner moves back in.
careerAngle: >
  Restoration technicians need to treat deodorization as its own documented
  step, since skipping it or doing it before the odor source is fully
  addressed often means the smell returns and the customer calls the job
  incomplete.
whatGoesWrong: >
  People sometimes think masking an odor with a scented product counts as
  deodorization, when the goal of professional odor removal is to neutralize
  the odor-causing molecules, not cover the smell temporarily.
