---
id: concept-novation
title: Novation
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A three-way agreement that substitutes a new party for one of the original
  parties to a contract, releasing the original party and making the new party
  fully responsible for the remaining obligations.
whyItMatters: >
  It differs from a simple assignment because all three parties, including the
  one not being replaced, must consent, and the original party is completely
  released rather than staying on the hook if the replacement fails to
  perform.
example: >
  A subcontractor's company is acquired mid-project, and the owner, general
  contractor, and both the old and new corporate entities sign a novation
  agreement so the new entity takes over the subcontract and the old entity is
  released from further liability.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-construction-attorney|Construction Attorney]] drafts the novation
  agreement to make sure all three parties' consent is documented, and the
  [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] tracks which
  contracts have been novated so the project's contract files reflect who is
  actually bound going forward.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A party assumes a simple assignment of the contract is enough to fully release
  the original entity, and is surprised to learn that without a true novation,
  the original party can still be held liable if the new party defaults.
