id: concept-gis
title: GIS (Geographic Information System)
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  A system for capturing, storing, and analyzing data that's tied to a
  specific location, layering information like utility lines, property
  boundaries, or flood zones onto a map so relationships between different
  datasets become visible and usable.
whyItMatters: >
  Drone mapping and surveying both produce location-based data, but a GIS is
  what actually makes that data useful for decision-making, by layering it
  together with other spatial information rather than leaving each dataset as
  an isolated map.
example: >
  A GIS specialist layers a site's drone-captured topography data together
  with existing utility maps and a FEMA flood zone map, giving the project
  team a single view that shows how the site's actual terrain relates to
  buried infrastructure and flood risk at once.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-gis-specialist|GIS Specialist]] builds and maintains the layered
  spatial datasets a project relies on, distinct from a surveyor, who focuses
  on collecting the field data a GIS specialist then organizes and analyzes.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project treats drone-captured mapping data as a standalone product rather
  than feeding it into a proper GIS alongside other spatial datasets. Without
  that layering, a real conflict, like new construction planned directly over
  an existing utility line, isn't caught until it's discovered in the field.
