---
id: concept-multiple-prime-contracting
title: Multiple Prime Contracting
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A delivery approach where the owner contracts directly with several prime
  contractors, separate general, mechanical, and electrical primes, for
  example, instead of one general contractor holding all the subcontracts,
  requiring the owner or a construction manager to coordinate directly between
  primes who have no contractual authority over each other.
whyItMatters: >
  Without a single general contractor responsible for the whole schedule, the
  owner (or whoever it hires to coordinate) inherits the job of resolving
  conflicts between primes that a traditional GC arrangement would normally
  absorb.
example: >
  A public university contracts directly with separate general, mechanical,
  and electrical primes for a new building, relying on its own construction
  manager to coordinate sequencing between the three rather than one general
  contractor managing all of it.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] takes on the
  coordination role multiple prime contracting requires, since no single prime
  has contractual authority to resolve conflicts between the others.
whatGoesWrong: >
  On a highway interchange project, the earthwork prime falls behind
  schedule, delaying the site access the paving prime needs to start its own
  work. Neither prime has any contractual obligation to the other, only to
  the owner, so the paving prime has no direct way to hold the earthwork
  prime accountable for the delay it's now absorbing.
