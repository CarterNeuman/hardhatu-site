id: concept-hud-section-3-workforce-requirement
title: Section 3 (HUD Workforce Requirement)
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development requirement, triggered once
  HUD funding to a housing or public construction project passes a set dollar
  threshold, that a defined share of labor hours go to low-income workers or
  Section 3 businesses.
whyItMatters: >
  Current HUD rules set the benchmark for Section 3 labor hours as a percentage
  of total project labor hours and apply once total HUD financial assistance
  to a project exceeds a set dollar threshold, with a lower threshold for
  projects funded through HUD's lead hazard control and healthy homes programs
  specifically.
example: >
  A public housing authority renovation funded well above HUD's Section 3
  funding threshold has to track and report what share of labor hours on the
  project were performed by Section 3-eligible workers, and document
  good-faith efforts to hire from that population if the benchmark isn't being
  met.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-hr-manager-construction|HR Manager]] tracks Section 3 labor hour
  reporting and hiring efforts, and the [[career-certified-payroll-
  specialist|Certified Payroll Specialist]] incorporates Section 3 reporting
  into the certified payroll and compliance documentation required on the
  project.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor assumes Section 3 only matters once a project is clearly massive,
  and fails to realize the triggering HUD funding threshold is lower than
  expected, leaving a mid-sized rehab project unexpectedly out of compliance.
