---
id: concept-best-value-procurement
title: Best Value Procurement
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A public or private procurement method that awards a contract based on a
  combination of price and non-price factors, like qualifications, technical
  approach, and past performance, rather than automatically awarding to the
  lowest bidder.
whyItMatters: >
  It gives an owner room to pass over the cheapest bid in favor of a proposal
  judged more likely to deliver a successful project, but it also requires the
  owner to publish clear evaluation criteria in advance and apply them
  consistently, since an unsuccessful low bidder can challenge an award that
  looks arbitrary.
example: >
  A transit agency structures a procurement so that price counts for only part
  of the evaluation score, with technical approach and past project
  performance making up the rest. A bidder with a higher price but a stronger
  technical proposal and track record wins the award over the lowest-priced
  bidder.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-procurement-manager|Procurement Manager]] designs the evaluation
  criteria and scoring weights for a best-value procurement, and the
  [[career-estimator|Estimator]] prepares pricing that accounts for how it
  will be weighted against the non-price factors in the evaluation.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A losing bidder assumes the lowest price should have automatically won,
  without realizing a properly structured best-value procurement is designed
  to weigh other factors alongside price from the outset.
relatedIds:
