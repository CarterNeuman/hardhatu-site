---
id: concept-vault-security-equipment
title: Vault & Security Equipment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Purpose-built security enclosures, bank vaults, safe rooms, and evidence
  storage rooms, with reinforced walls, specialized doors, and ventilation
  requirements that go well beyond standard room construction, often requiring
  a specialty subcontractor rather than standard framing and door hardware.
whyItMatters: >
  A vault's wall and door ratings are certified to resist specific attack
  methods (drilling, torching, explosives) for a defined time, and
  substituting standard construction for even part of the enclosure can void
  that rating entirely.
example: >
  A contractor building a bank vault room follows the vault manufacturer's
  specified wall assembly exactly, including the embedded reinforcement,
  rather than substituting standard concrete block even where it looks
  visually similar.
careerAngle: >
  A specialty vault subcontractor typically self-installs the certified
  assembly, while the [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates
  structural support and ventilation penetrations around it.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A vault's ventilation penetration is cut by a trade unaware of the
  enclosure's security rating, and the vault manufacturer later confirms the
  penetration voids the UL burglary rating the owner paid for.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
