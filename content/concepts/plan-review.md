id: concept-plan-review
title: Plan Review
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  The local building department's examination of submitted construction documents
  against applicable [[concept-building-code|building code]] before a
  [[concept-building-permit|building permit]] is issued, the point where a design gets
  independently checked for code compliance before construction ever starts.
whyItMatters: >
  Catching a code compliance problem during plan review, on paper, is far cheaper and
  faster than catching the same problem after it's built: plan review exists as a
  gatekeeping step specifically to push that discovery as early as possible in the
  project timeline.
example: >
  A plan review identifies that a proposed stairwell doesn't meet the required egress
  width for the building's occupancy classification, sending the design back for revision
  weeks before construction would otherwise have started on that area.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-preconstruction-manager|Preconstruction Manager]] tracks plan review
  timelines as a real schedule risk, since a project can't legally start construction
  until the permit clears review. A [[career-project-engineer|Project Engineer]] often
  helps assemble and respond to plan review comments, coordinating between the design
  team and the building department.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project team submits for plan review without first checking the design against
  common local plan review comments from past projects. Preventable review comments
  come back that a quick internal check beforehand would have caught, extending the
  permit timeline the project's schedule was counting on.
