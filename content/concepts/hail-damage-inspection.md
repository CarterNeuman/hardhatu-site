id: concept-hail-damage-inspection
title: Hail Damage Inspection
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  A specialized roof and exterior inspection, often performed with a test
  square, chalk testing, or granule-loss assessment, to document hail impacts
  and distinguish genuine storm damage from pre-existing wear, cosmetic
  marking, or normal aging.
whyItMatters: >
  This inspection is often the single most contested piece of evidence in a hail
  claim, since insurers frequently dispute whether documented impacts
  represent functional damage requiring replacement or purely cosmetic marks
  that don't affect the roof's performance.
example: >
  A roofing contractor performs a hail damage inspection using a marked test
  square on several roof slopes, photographing and counting hail hits per
  square to document impact density and granule displacement that supports a
  full-slope replacement rather than a spot repair.
careerAngle: >
  Roofing contractors doing insurance work should document hail inspections
  thoroughly with photos and measured test squares, since the quality of this
  documentation often determines whether an insurer approves a full roof
  replacement or disputes the claim down to a partial repair.
whatGoesWrong: >
  People sometimes assume any visible mark on a roof after a hailstorm proves
  insurance-covered damage, when inspectors distinguish between functional
  damage that affects performance and cosmetic marking that many policies,
  particularly on metal roofing, explicitly exclude from coverage.
