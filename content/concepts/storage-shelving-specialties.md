id: concept-storage-shelving-specialties
title: Storage & Shelving Specialties
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Fixed or mobile shelving systems, wire, solid steel, or high-density mobile
  (compact) shelving on tracks, specified for storage rooms, libraries, and
  archives based on the load per shelf and the floor's structural capacity to
  support it.
whyItMatters: >
  High-density mobile shelving in particular is heavy enough, fully loaded, to
  require a structural engineer's review of the floor it sits on; installing
  it without that check risks overloading a floor that wasn't designed for
  that concentrated weight.
example: >
  A contractor installing compact mobile shelving in a records room confirms
  with the structural engineer that the floor's live-load rating accounts for
  the shelving's fully-loaded weight before the tracks are anchored.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-structural-engineer|Structural Engineer]] checks the floor's load
  capacity for high-density shelving, and the
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] plans the room's future
  load against what was actually engineered.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A records room is filled with compact shelving loaded well past the original
  floor-load assumptions used in design, and a later structural inspection
  flags visible deflection that requires costly reinforcement to correct.
relatedIds:
  - career-structural-engineer
  - career-facilities-manager
