---
id: concept-saunas-steam-rooms
title: Saunas & Steam Rooms
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "13, Special Construction"
definition: >
  Enclosed rooms built to withstand sustained high heat and humidity, saunas
  using dry heat and steam rooms using saturated humid heat, each requiring
  specific waterproofing, ventilation, and material choices that standard room
  construction doesn't address.
whyItMatters: >
  A steam room in particular needs a continuous vapor barrier and sloped floor
  drainage, since standard wall assemblies will trap moisture inside the wall
  cavity and develop mold or rot if built without steam-room-specific
  waterproofing details.
example: >
  A contractor building a spa's steam room installs a continuous vapor barrier
  behind the tile and slopes the floor to a drain, rather than using the
  standard bathroom waterproofing details that wouldn't handle the steam
  room's sustained humidity.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-plumber|Plumber]] or tile specialist installs the drainage and
  waterproofing to the manufacturer's steam-room-specific requirements.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A steam room is built with standard bathroom waterproofing instead of a
  continuous vapor barrier, and moisture trapped behind the tile leads to mold
  growth inside the wall within the first year of use.
relatedIds:
  - career-plumber
