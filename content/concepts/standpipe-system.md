---
id: concept-standpipe-system
title: Standpipe System
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "21, Fire Suppression"
definition: >
  A network of vertical piping installed within a building, most commonly in
  stairwells of taller buildings, that firefighters connect hoses to at each floor,
  providing water for manual firefighting without having to run hose lines up
  multiple flights of stairs from a fire truck.
whyItMatters: >
  A standpipe system is specifically for firefighter use, distinct from an automatic
  [[concept-fire-sprinkler-system|fire sprinkler system]], and code requirements for
  when a standpipe is required, typically based on building height, are separate
  from sprinkler requirements, so a building can need one, the other, or both
  depending on its height and occupancy.
example: >
  A 10-story office building's stairwells each include a standpipe with a fire
  department connection at every floor, allowing firefighters to connect hoses
  directly at whatever floor a fire is on rather than running hose lines up from
  ground level.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] confirms standpipe connections remain
  accessible and unobstructed throughout construction, since a standpipe blocked by
  staged materials could delay a real emergency response. A
  [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]] witnesses the required
  pressure testing of the standpipe system before it's accepted for use.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A construction crew stages material in front of a standpipe connection in an
  active stairwell for convenience, not treating it as a life-safety obstruction
  since the building isn't yet occupied. A fire inspector cites the obstruction
  during a routine construction-phase inspection, since standpipe access has to be
  maintained even during construction on a phased or occupied renovation project.
