id: concept-closeout
title: Closeout
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  The final phase of a project, finishing the punch list, handing over warranties and
  as-built drawings, training the owner's staff on new equipment, and collecting the last
  payment, that turns a physically finished building into one the owner can actually
  take over and run.
whyItMatters: >
  A building that's physically complete isn't actually done until closeout wraps up: an
  owner without warranty documents or as-built drawings inherits a building nobody can
  maintain properly, and a contractor who skips closeout steps often can't collect their
  final payment.
example: >
  Before final payment, a contractor hands the owner a binder of equipment manuals and
  warranties, a set of as-built drawings showing what was actually built (not just what
  was originally designed), and walks the facilities staff through operating the new HVAC
  system.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] assembles the closeout package and chases
  the last signatures. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] on the owner's
  side is often the one actually using everything handed over: the warranties and
  as-built drawings become their reference for years after the contractor is gone.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor rushes off to start the next project without finishing the closeout
  binder, and six months later the owner discovers a rooftop unit needs warranty service,
  but nobody can find the paperwork proving it's still under warranty, leaving the
  owner to pay for a repair that should have been free.
