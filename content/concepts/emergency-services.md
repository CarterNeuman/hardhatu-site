---
id: concept-emergency-services
title: Emergency Services
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  The immediate, often after-hours response work performed right after a loss,
  water extraction, board-up, roof tarping, temporary power, billed and typically
  approved somewhat separately from the full reconstruction scope because of how
  time-sensitive it is.
whyItMatters: >
  Emergency services exist specifically to satisfy a policy's
  [[concept-mitigation|mitigation]] requirement, so insurers generally approve this
  work quickly, sometimes before a full inspection even happens, since delaying
  approval would work directly against the insurer's own interest in limiting
  further damage.
example: >
  Within hours of a pipe bursting overnight, an emergency services crew extracts
  standing water and sets up industrial drying equipment, work that gets approved
  and billed before the full scope of loss for reconstruction is even written.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-restoration-project-manager|Restoration Project Manager]] dispatches
  emergency crews immediately after a loss is reported, often before a full
  inspection is scheduled. An [[career-insurance-adjuster|Insurance Adjuster]]
  approves reasonable emergency services costs quickly, since delay itself increases
  the total damage the policy will end up covering.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A restoration company delays dispatching an emergency crew over a weekend,
  treating the call as non-urgent. By the time water extraction finally begins on
  Monday, the standing water has caused additional damage to flooring and
  drywall that a same-day response would have prevented.
