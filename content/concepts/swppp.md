---
id: concept-swppp
title: SWPPP (Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan)
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A written plan required under a project's
  [[concept-npdes-construction-permit|NPDES Construction General Permit]],
  detailing the specific erosion and sediment
  control measures, such as silt fencing, inlet protection, and stabilized
  construction entrances, a site will use to keep sediment-laden stormwater from
  leaving the site, along with the inspection and maintenance schedule for those
  controls throughout construction.
whyItMatters: >
  The permit sets the legal requirement, but the SWPPP is what actually gets
  inspected and enforced on the ground, so a permit on file without a SWPPP being
  followed in the field doesn't protect a site from a violation.
example: >
  A site superintendent's SWPPP names the specific best management practices
  installed around the site perimeter and requires a documented inspection of
  every control after any rain event producing a quarter inch (0.25 inches) or
  more of precipitation.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-safety-manager|Safety Manager]] or superintendent maintains the
  SWPPP and its inspection log day to day, work that ties closely to what a
  [[career-loss-control-engineer|Loss Control / Risk Engineer]] reviews when
  assessing a site's environmental risk.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A crew installs silt fencing per the SWPPP at the start of the job, then skips
  the required post-rain inspections for months as the schedule gets busy.
  Sediment escapes off site into a storm drain, and a state inspector issues a
  violation because the inspection logs the permit requires simply don't exist.
