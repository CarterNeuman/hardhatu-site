id: concept-fire-blocking
title: Fire-Blocking
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "06, Wood, Plastics, and Composites"
definition: >
  Wood or other approved blocking installed inside concealed stud and joist
  cavities during framing, specifically to stop fire and smoke from spreading
  quickly through the hidden, connected void spaces inside a wall or floor
  system.
whyItMatters: >
  This is a different job from [[concept-firestopping|Firestopping]], which
  seals a penetration, like a pipe or cable passing through an
  already-finished, fire-rated wall or floor. Fire-blocking happens earlier,
  during framing itself, closing off the hidden cavities a fire could
  otherwise travel through undetected before anyone even sees smoke.
example: >
  A framing crew installs fire-blocking at the top and bottom of stud cavities
  in a stairwell wall, and at other code-required locations, specifically to
  prevent fire from racing upward through the concealed space behind the
  finished wall surface.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-carpenter|Carpenter]] installs fire-blocking as part of the
  framing scope, and it's one of the specific items an inspector checks for
  before framing can be covered with drywall.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A framing crew skips fire-blocking in a few concealed cavities to save time,
  assuming the drywall covering will be inspected and nothing will be checked
  behind it. A framing inspection catches the gap before the walls close in,
  requiring the crew to open the wall back up and correct it.
