---
id: concept-nurse-call-systems
title: Nurse Call Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "27, Communications"
definition: >
  Dedicated call-and-response systems in healthcare facilities letting
  patients alert staff and letting staff locate and prioritize calls, wired
  and zoned to the facility's specific unit layout and acuity level, distinct
  from a general paging or intercom system.
whyItMatters: >
  Nurse call wiring and device placement have to match the exact bed layout
  and staff workflow of each unit, and healthcare facility codes set specific
  requirements for response-time visibility and code-blue/emergency call
  escalation that general building codes don't address.
example: >
  A contractor installing nurse call in a new hospital wing coordinates device
  placement and station wiring with the unit's final bed layout and the
  hospital's code-blue escalation protocol, rather than a generic room-by-room
  installation.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] coordinates the nurse call system
  design with the hospital's clinical workflow requirements, and the
  [[career-electrician|Electrician]] installs the wiring and devices.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Nurse call devices are installed based on an early floor plan, and a later
  bed-layout change leaves call buttons positioned on the wrong side of
  several beds, requiring rework after the unit is otherwise complete.
relatedIds:
  - career-mep-engineer
  - career-electrician
---
