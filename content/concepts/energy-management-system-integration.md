id: concept-energy-management-system-integration
title: Energy Management System (EMS) Integration
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "25, Integrated Automation"
definition: >
  The integration of a building's metering, HVAC, and lighting controls into a
  single platform that tracks real-time energy use and can automatically shed
  or shift load, distinct from a general building automation system in that
  its primary purpose is energy cost and consumption management.
whyItMatters: >
  An EMS is only as useful as the metering granularity feeding it, since a
  system that only sees whole-building energy use can't actually identify
  which specific system is driving a spike in consumption the way sub-metered
  data can.
example: >
  A controls contractor installing an EMS in a new office building specifies
  sub-metering at the floor level rather than a single whole-building meter,
  so the facilities team can later identify which floor's HVAC schedule is
  driving an unexpected energy spike.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] designs the metering architecture the
  EMS depends on, and the [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]]
  uses the resulting data to manage ongoing energy costs.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An EMS is installed with only whole-building metering to save on sub-meter
  costs, and the facilities team can't pinpoint which system is responsible
  for a sustained energy use increase, undermining the entire point of having
  the EMS.
