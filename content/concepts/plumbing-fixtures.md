id: concept-plumbing-fixtures
title: Plumbing Fixtures
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "22, Plumbing"
definition: >
  The visible plumbing components a building's occupants actually use, such as sinks,
  toilets, faucets, and showers, installed near the end of construction once
  [[concept-rough-in-plumbing|rough-in plumbing]], walls, and flooring are complete,
  and selected for both function and code compliance (water efficiency ratings,
  accessible-height requirements).
whyItMatters: >
  Accessible fixture requirements, such as mounting height, clearances, and operable
  controls, are specific, code-mandated dimensions, not general guidelines, and a fixture
  installed at the wrong height or without adequate clearance can fail an
  accessibility inspection even if it functions perfectly.
example: >
  A restroom designed for accessibility installs a toilet at the specific height
  code requires for an accessible stall, with grab bars and clearances positioned to
  the code's exact dimensions rather than a standard residential layout.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-plumber|Plumber]] installs fixtures to the manufacturer's connection
  requirements and the project's accessibility and plumbing code requirements. A
  [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] deals with fixture maintenance,
  repair, and eventual replacement throughout the building's life, relying on
  records of exactly what was installed.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A plumber installs a standard-height toilet in a restroom stall designated as an
  accessible unit, missing that the accessibility code required a different mounting
  height for that specific stall. The error isn't caught until a final accessibility
  inspection, requiring the fixture to be removed and reinstalled at the correct
  height before occupancy can be approved.
