id: concept-well-building-standard
title: WELL Building Standard
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  A building certification standard, administered by the International
  WELL Building Institute, that certifies a building's design and operation
  for occupant health and wellness, covering categories like air and water
  quality, lighting, acoustics, and mental health, distinct from
  [[concept-leed|LEED]]'s primarily environmental focus.
whyItMatters: >
  WELL certification requires ongoing performance verification, actual
  measured air quality and water testing after occupancy, not just design
  intent, which means a WELL project has to keep meeting its targets in
  operation, not just on paper at the point of design.
example: >
  An office tenant targeting WELL certification specifies enhanced air
  filtration and commits to periodic post-occupancy air quality testing to
  confirm the space continues meeting WELL's air quality thresholds, not
  just at initial commissioning.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-sustainability-leed-consultant|Sustainability/LEED Consultant]]
  manages the WELL certification process alongside or instead of LEED,
  depending on the owner's actual priorities.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project pursuing WELL certification completes construction and initial
  commissioning but never schedules the required post-occupancy air and
  water quality testing the certification depends on. The certification
  stalls indefinitely, incomplete, since WELL's performance verification
  can't be skipped the way a purely design-based certification might allow.
