id: concept-dispute-review-board
title: Dispute Review Board (DRB)
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A standing panel of neutral, technically qualified people appointed at the
  start of a large project to review disputes as they arise throughout
  construction and issue non-binding (or sometimes binding) recommendations,
  rather than waiting until the project ends to resolve everything at once.
whyItMatters: >
  Because the board members stay familiar with the project from the beginning,
  they can address a dispute while memories and documentation are still fresh,
  which tends to produce faster, more informed outcomes than litigation or
  arbitration launched only after the project has long since finished.
example: >
  A large tunnel project convenes a three-person dispute review board at the
  start of construction, made up of engineers experienced in underground work.
  When a dispute arises over a differing site condition mid-project, the board
  reviews the issue promptly and issues a recommendation while the relevant
  field records are still current, rather than the dispute waiting to be
  litigated years later.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-scheduling-delay-consultant|Scheduling Delay Consultant]] often
  presents technical delay analysis to a dispute review board, and the
  [[career-construction-attorney|Construction Attorney]] represents a party's
  position before the board when a dispute is submitted for review.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project owner sets up a dispute review board but then routes every minor
  disagreement to it immediately, overwhelming the board with issues the
  project team could have resolved informally, defeating the board's purpose
  of reserving its attention for disputes the team genuinely can't settle on
  its own.
