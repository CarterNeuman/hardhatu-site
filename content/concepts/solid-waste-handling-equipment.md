---
id: concept-solid-waste-handling-equipment
title: Solid Waste Handling Equipment
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Built-in equipment for managing a building's trash and recycling,
  compactors, balers, and chutes from upper floors to a ground-level
  collection room, sized to the building's expected waste volume and
  coordinated with the loading dock and collection schedule.
whyItMatters: >
  An undersized compactor or a trash chute that doesn't match the building's
  actual occupant load creates an ongoing operational problem long after
  construction is done, since it's expensive to retrofit once walls and floor
  penetrations are finished.
example: >
  A contractor installing a trash chute in a high-rise residential building
  sizes the chute diameter and the ground-floor compactor capacity to the
  building's unit count and local hauler's pickup frequency, rather than to a
  generic standard.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]] weighs in during design
  on expected waste volume, and the [[career-operations-manager|Operations
  Manager]] confirms the hauler's collection schedule matches the compactor's
  capacity before occupancy.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A building's trash compactor is sized using a generic per-unit waste
  estimate that undercounts the building's actual occupancy, and residents
  start reporting overflowing chutes within the first few months of operation.
relatedIds:
  - career-facilities-manager
  - career-operations-manager
---
