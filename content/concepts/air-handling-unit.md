id: concept-air-handling-unit
title: Air Handling Unit (AHU)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "23, HVAC"
definition: >
  A larger, typically indoor mechanical unit that mixes outside and return air, filters
  and conditions it using chilled or hot water coils, and pushes it out through
  ductwork to the building, the workhorse of a central-plant HVAC system, distinct
  from a rooftop unit's smaller, fully self-contained packaged design.
whyItMatters: >
  An AHU's filtration and coil performance directly determine a building's indoor air
  quality, not just its temperature, so specifying and maintaining the wrong filter
  rating or letting coils go unmaintained can create a comfort and health issue long
  before anyone notices a temperature problem.
example: >
  A large office building's mechanical room houses an AHU that draws chilled water
  from the building's chiller plant, conditions a mix of outside and return air, and
  distributes it through ductwork to VAV boxes serving each floor's zones.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-hvac-technician|HVAC Technician]] commissions and balances the AHU,
  verifying it delivers its designed airflow and temperature to each zone it serves. A
  [[career-quality-control-manager|Quality Control Manager]] confirms the unit's
  startup and balancing report matches the design airflow before accepting the system
  as complete.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An AHU is started up and put into service before its air balancing report is
  actually reviewed against the design airflow numbers, under pressure to get cooling
  running for other trades finishing nearby work. Several zones end up under-ventilated
  for weeks before the balancing discrepancy is caught during commissioning, well after
  the unit was assumed to be performing correctly.
