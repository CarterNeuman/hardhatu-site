id: concept-toilet-bath-accessories
title: Toilet & Bath Accessories
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Grab bars, soap and towel dispensers, mirrors, and similar fixtures mounted
  in restrooms, with mounting height and grab bar placement governed by ADA
  accessibility requirements in any restroom serving the public.
whyItMatters: >
  ADA sets exact mounting-height ranges and grab bar locations relative to the
  toilet and wall, and getting these wrong is one of the most common
  accessibility violations caught in building inspections, since it's easy to
  mount fixtures at a normal-feeling height that doesn't actually comply.
example: >
  A contractor installing grab bars in an accessible restroom stall measures
  mounting height and placement directly against the ADA standard's dimensions
  rather than against the adjacent fixture's apparent centerline, since those
  two references don't always agree.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-plumber|Plumber]] or finish carpenter installs the accessories,
  and the [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] checks mounting heights
  against the ADA checklist before the restroom is signed off.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Grab bars are mounted at a height that looks right by eye but falls outside
  the ADA-required range, and the error isn't caught until a post-occupancy
  accessibility audit forces a retrofit in an occupied building.
relatedIds:
  - career-plumber
  - career-qaqc-inspector
---
