---
id: concept-outdoor-shade-structures
title: Outdoor Shade Structures (Pergolas & Shade Sails)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "32, Exterior Improvements"
definition: >
  Freestanding or attached structures, pergolas, shade sails, and similar
  canopies, providing partial sun protection for outdoor gathering spaces,
  engineered for wind and snow load like any other structure despite often
  being treated as a landscaping feature.
whyItMatters: >
  Shade sails in particular are under constant tension and wind loading, and
  anchor points designed as if the structure were a simple decorative feature
  rather than a loaded structure are a common cause of failure in high wind.
example: >
  A contractor installing a shade sail over an outdoor dining area has the
  structural engineer size the anchor points for the sail's actual wind load
  rather than using a generic landscaping anchor detail.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-superintendent|Superintendent]] coordinates the shade
  structure's foundation work with the sitework schedule and the structural
  engineer's anchor design.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A shade sail's anchor points are installed using a generic
  decorative-structure detail rather than a wind-load calculation specific to
  the sail's size, and the sail tears loose from one anchor during the first
  significant windstorm.
relatedIds:
  - career-superintendent
