---
id: concept-shared-savings-clause
title: Shared Savings Clause
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A contract provision splitting the difference between a project's
  [[concept-guaranteed-maximum-price|guaranteed maximum price]] and its actual
  final cost, when the project comes in under the GMP, between the owner and the
  contractor, giving the contractor a real financial incentive to control costs
  rather than just protecting its own fee.
whyItMatters: >
  Without this clause, a contractor that saves the owner real money on a GMP
  project gets nothing extra for it, the owner keeps the entire savings, which
  removes much of the financial incentive a GMP contract is supposed to create in
  the first place.
example: >
  A CM at Risk contract includes a fifty-fifty shared savings clause, so when
  the finished project comes in under its GMP, the contractor and owner split
  the difference evenly instead of the owner keeping it all.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-preconstruction-manager|Preconstruction Manager]] negotiates the
  shared savings clause's split ratio during contract formation, while a
  [[career-cost-engineer|Cost Engineer]] tracks the actual savings the clause
  ends up applying to.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor delivers real cost savings on a GMP project, but the contract's
  shared savings clause was never actually negotiated or included. The owner
  keeps the entire savings, and the contractor has no contractual claim to any
  share of the value it created.
