---
id: concept-wheelchair-lifts
title: Wheelchair Lifts (Accessibility)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "14, Conveying Equipment"
definition: >
  Platform or incline lifts installed to provide accessible vertical
  circulation over a small change in elevation, a stage, a raised entry, or a
  short flight of stairs, where installing a full elevator or a ramp isn't
  practical, sized and regulated to meet ADA accessibility requirements.
whyItMatters: >
  A wheelchair lift is often the only accessible route to a specific space, so
  its reliability matters more than a typical convenience feature, a lift
  that's frequently out of service can functionally exclude someone from
  reaching a space at all, not just inconvenience them.
example: >
  A historic theater installs a wheelchair lift to provide accessible access
  to its raised stage, since the building's existing structure and limited
  space made a code-compliant ramp impractical to add.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-elevator-technician|Elevator & Escalator Technician]] installs
  and maintains wheelchair lifts. A
  [[career-historic-preservation-specialist|Historic Preservation Specialist]]
  coordinates accessibility solutions like wheelchair lifts on historic
  buildings where a full accessible renovation
  isn't feasible.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A facility installs a wheelchair lift as its only accessible route to a
  mezzanine level but doesn't budget for a maintenance contract, and the lift
  sits out of service for weeks after a minor breakdown. The mezzanine becomes
  effectively inaccessible during that time, a real ADA compliance problem, not
  just an inconvenience.
