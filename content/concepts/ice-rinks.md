---
id: concept-ice-rinks
title: Ice Rinks
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "13, Special Construction"
definition: >
  Refrigerated concrete slabs with embedded piping that circulates a chilled
  brine or glycol solution to freeze and maintain an ice surface, requiring
  specialized refrigeration equipment and a structural slab designed around
  the embedded piping grid.
whyItMatters: >
  An ice rink's refrigeration piping has to be embedded in the slab during the
  pour with exact spacing for even freezing, which means the refrigeration
  contractor's layout has to be finalized and coordinated before concrete work
  begins, not after.
example: >
  A contractor pouring a rink slab coordinates the refrigeration contractor's
  embedded piping grid and leveling tolerances with the concrete subcontractor
  before the pour, since the piping can't be added or adjusted once the slab
  is cured.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] designs the refrigeration system and
  piping layout, and the [[career-concrete-worker|Concrete Worker]] places and
  finishes the slab around the embedded grid to the tight flatness tolerance
  ice surfaces require.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A rink slab is poured slightly out of level, and once refrigerated, the
  uneven surface creates thin spots in the ice that crack under skater
  traffic, requiring the surface to be built back up before the rink can open.
relatedIds:
  - career-mep-engineer
  - career-concrete-worker
---
