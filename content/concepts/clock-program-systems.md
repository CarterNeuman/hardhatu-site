id: concept-clock-program-systems
title: Clock & Program Systems
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "27, Communications"
definition: >
  Centrally synchronized clock systems, most common in schools, tying wall
  clocks throughout a building to a master controller that also triggers
  scheduled bell or tone signals for class periods.
whyItMatters: >
  A building-wide synchronized clock and bell system has to be wired to every
  room from a central head-end, which is far easier to coordinate during
  initial construction than retrofitting into finished ceilings and walls
  later.
example: >
  A contractor building a new school runs low-voltage clock wiring to every
  classroom from the main electrical room during rough-in, coordinating the
  master clock controller's location with the school's final technology closet
  layout.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] runs the low-voltage wiring during
  rough-in, and the [[career-construction-technology-manager|Construction
  Technology Manager]] configures the master controller and bell schedule
  before occupancy.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Clock wiring is run to classrooms without confirming the head-end
  controller's final location, and the technology integrator discovers after
  walls are closed that the wiring run is too long for the system's voltage
  drop limits.
relatedIds:
  - career-electrician
  - career-construction-technology-manager
