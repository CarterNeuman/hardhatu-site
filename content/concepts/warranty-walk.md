id: concept-warranty-walk
title: Warranty Walk (11-Month Walkthrough)
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A walkthrough of the completed project roughly eleven months after substantial
  completion, conducted jointly by the owner and contractor to catalog any defects
  while they still fall within the standard one-year [[concept-warranty|warranty]]
  period, timed deliberately to leave enough runway to get everything fixed before
  that window closes.
whyItMatters: >
  The timing is the whole point: scheduling the walk at month eleven rather than
  waiting for the owner to call leaves roughly a month to get warranty items fixed
  before the deadline, instead of discovering problems only after coverage has
  already expired.
example: >
  A project manager contacts the owner around month ten to schedule the warranty
  walk, and the owner's facilities team spends the weeks before it compiling a
  list of every issue noticed since move-in, from a sticking door to an
  intermittent HVAC noise.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] schedules and conducts the walk on
  the contractor's side. A [[career-facilities-manager|Facilities Manager]]
  compiles the owner's list of issues noticed during the first year of occupancy.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor doesn't proactively schedule the warranty walk, and the owner,
  unaware the one-year clock is running, doesn't think to compile its issues list
  until month thirteen. By then the warranty period has closed, and the contractor
  declines to cover repairs it might otherwise have made for free.
