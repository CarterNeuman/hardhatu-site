id: concept-postal-specialties
title: Postal Specialties (Mail Centers)
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Built-in mail receptacles and sorting units, cluster mailboxes for
  multifamily buildings, individual mail slots, or full mail-sorting rooms for
  office buildings, sized and located according to USPS access and placement
  standards where the building receives centralized mail delivery.
whyItMatters: >
  USPS has specific approval requirements for cluster mailbox units in new
  multifamily construction, including accessible mounting height and a
  dedicated, approved location, and a project can't get its occupancy
  certificate without that sign-off in many jurisdictions.
example: >
  A contractor building a new apartment complex submits the cluster mailbox
  unit's location and specifications to the local postmaster for approval well
  before move-in, since a late or rejected submission can hold up residents'
  ability to receive mail.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] tracks the USPS approval as a
  required submittal on the project schedule, separate from the building
  permit process.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A mailbox unit's location is finalized without USPS approval, and the
  postmaster rejects the site plan after installation because it doesn't meet
  accessible-route requirements, forcing a relocation after the surrounding
  sidewalk is already poured.
relatedIds:
  - career-project-manager
