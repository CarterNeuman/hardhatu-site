---
id: concept-unit-kitchens
title: Unit Kitchens
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "11, Equipment"
definition: >
  Compact, factory-assembled kitchen units combining a cooktop, small
  refrigerator, and sink into one pre-built module, used in micro-units,
  dorms, and senior living facilities where space doesn't allow for a
  conventional kitchen layout.
whyItMatters: >
  A unit kitchen's plumbing and electrical connections are standardized by the
  manufacturer, which makes coordinating the rough-in locations exactly to the
  unit's specifications far more important than for a conventional kitchen
  built up piece by piece on site.
example: >
  A contractor building a micro-unit apartment building roughs in plumbing and
  electrical connections to the exact locations specified by the unit kitchen
  manufacturer, rather than estimating typical kitchen connection points as if
  building a conventional kitchen.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-plumber|Plumber]] and [[career-electrician|Electrician]]
  coordinate rough-in locations directly against the manufacturer's unit
  kitchen specifications.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Plumbing and electrical rough-in for a unit kitchen is placed using typical
  kitchen dimensions rather than the specific manufacturer's connection
  points, and the pre-built unit doesn't line up with the rough-in when it
  arrives.
relatedIds:
  - career-plumber
  - career-electrician
---
