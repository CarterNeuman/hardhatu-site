---
id: concept-char-depth-testing
title: Char Depth Testing (Fire Damage)
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  A measurement taken during fire-damage assessment of how deeply structural
  wood members were burned, used to judge whether a charred beam or joist
  retains enough sound material to keep its structural capacity or needs to be
  replaced.
whyItMatters: >
  This directly decides repair scope and cost on any fire claim involving
  structural framing, since a char depth measurement can be the difference
  between a surface cleaning and a full structural replacement.
example: >
  A structural engineer measures char depth on a fire-damaged roof truss, finds
  the remaining sound wood is well within the required structural
  cross-section, and recommends surface treatment and sealing rather than
  replacing the truss.
careerAngle: >
  Restoration estimators working structural fire claims should bring in a
  structural engineer for char depth assessment on load-bearing members rather
  than estimating remaining capacity by eye.
whatGoesWrong: >
  People sometimes assume any visibly charred wood must be replaced, when a char
  depth assessment can show the member retains adequate structural capacity
  after surface charring is addressed.
