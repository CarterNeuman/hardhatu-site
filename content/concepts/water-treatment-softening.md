id: concept-water-treatment-softening
title: Water Treatment & Softening
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "22, Plumbing"
definition: >
  Equipment installed in a building's domestic water system to treat water
  quality issues at the source, water softeners to remove hardness-causing
  minerals, filtration to remove sediment, or chemical treatment systems,
  selected based on the specific water quality problem the building's incoming
  water supply actually has.
whyItMatters: >
  Specifying water treatment equipment without first testing the actual
  incoming water quality is a common, expensive mistake, since a softener
  sized for moderate hardness won't perform adequately against genuinely hard
  water, and treatment equipment specified for a problem the water doesn't
  actually have is simply wasted cost.
example: >
  A hotel's mechanical engineer specifies a water softener sized to the local
  water utility's published hardness data and the building's actual peak
  domestic water demand, rather than a generic unit sized for an average
  commercial building.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-plumber|Plumber]] installs the treatment equipment and connects
  it into the building's domestic water piping. A
  [[career-mep-engineer|MEP Engineer]] specifies the system based on
  documented water quality testing.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A mechanical engineer specifies a standard water softener without reviewing
  the specific site's actual water hardness data. Once occupied, the
  building's water remains noticeably hard, scaling water heaters and fixtures
  faster than expected, and the owner has to pay for a properly sized
  replacement system.
