id: concept-desk-vs-field-adjuster
title: Desk Adjuster vs. Field Adjuster
tier: free
category: Insurance & Restoration
definition: >
  Two distinct claims-handling roles. A desk adjuster works a claim remotely, by
  phone, video, and submitted photos, while a field adjuster physically inspects the
  property in person, with the insurer deciding which type a given claim gets based
  on its complexity, value, and how many other claims are competing for field
  resources.
whyItMatters: >
  During a major catastrophe event, insurers route far more claims to desk
  adjusters than usual simply because there aren't enough field adjusters available,
  which means the depth of the initial inspection a policyholder gets can depend as
  much on claim volume that week as on the damage itself.
example: >
  A small, straightforward wind claim with clear photos of a few missing shingles
  gets handled entirely by a desk adjuster, while a complex claim involving
  suspected structural damage gets assigned to a field adjuster who inspects the
  property in person.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-insurance-adjuster|Insurance Adjuster]] may work either role, or
  shift between them, field adjusters are especially in demand during catastrophe
  events when claim volume spikes far beyond a normal week.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A claim involving hidden damage, water intrusion under a slab, or a compromised
  attic, gets assigned to a desk adjuster based on the photos alone, and the
  resulting estimate misses damage a physical inspection would have caught. The
  claim has to be reopened and reassigned to a field adjuster once the
  under-scoping surfaces during repairs.
