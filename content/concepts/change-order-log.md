---
id: concept-change-order-log
title: Change Order Log
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A tracking document listing every [[concept-change-order|change order]] on a
  project, whether pending, approved, or rejected, its value, and its
  cumulative effect on the contract's overall price and schedule.
whyItMatters: >
  A single change order is easy to track in isolation, but a project's
  cumulative change order total, visible only in a maintained log, is what
  actually reveals whether a project's scope has drifted significantly from
  what was originally bid, a trend an owner or GC needs to see building before
  it becomes a budget surprise at closeout.
example: >
  A project manager reviewing the change order log notices the cumulative
  approved change order total has quietly reached nearly ten percent of the
  original contract value. Flagging that trend to the owner well before
  closeout, rather than it surfacing as a surprise in the final accounting,
  gives both sides time to manage the budget impact proactively.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] maintains the
  change order log and its running totals, and the [[career-project-
  manager|Project Manager]] reviews cumulative change order trends against the
  contingency budget throughout the project.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project tracks each change order individually as it's approved, without a
  maintained log showing the cumulative running total, and doesn't realize how
  significantly the original contract value has grown until the final
  accounting at closeout reveals it all at once.
