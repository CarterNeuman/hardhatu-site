---
id: concept-notice-to-cure
title: Notice to Cure / Cure Period
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A formal notice one contract party sends the other identifying a specific
  default and giving a set period, the cure period, to fix it before the
  notifying party can pursue further remedies like
  [[concept-termination-for-default|termination for default]].
whyItMatters: >
  Skipping this step, even when the default is real and obvious, can turn a
  clean termination for default into a legally vulnerable one, courts generally
  expect the defaulting party to have actually been given the chance the
  contract promised them.
example: >
  An owner notices a subcontractor repeatedly missing scheduled milestones and
  sends a formal notice to cure identifying the specific defaults and giving ten
  days to correct course, before any termination is on the table.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-contracts-administrator|Contracts Administrator]] issues and tracks
  notices to cure, making sure the cure period the contract requires is actually
  honored before any termination proceeds.
whatGoesWrong: >
  An owner issues a notice to cure but sets the cure period at just two days for
  a defect that would reasonably take a couple of weeks to actually fix. The
  subcontractor challenges the notice as unreasonably short, and the dispute
  over whether the cure period itself was fair delays the underlying default
  issue from ever getting resolved.
