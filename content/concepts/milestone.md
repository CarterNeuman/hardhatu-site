---
id: concept-milestone
title: Milestone
tier: free
category: Project Process & Lifecycle
definition: >
  A specific, significant point in a project schedule, like
  [[concept-substantial-completion|substantial completion]] of a phase, or a major
  system going live, used as a checkpoint for tracking progress, often tied to a
  contract payment or a deadline with real consequences.
whyItMatters: >
  A schedule with hundreds of individual tasks is hard for anyone outside the scheduling
  team to follow. Milestones translate that detail into a handful of dates that owners,
  lenders, and project leadership can actually track progress against.
example: >
  A hotel renovation contract sets milestones for "Phase 1 floors complete," "Phase 2
  floors complete," and "final substantial completion," each triggering a specific
  payment to the contractor and, on the last one, the start of the warranty period.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-scheduler|Scheduler]] builds milestones into the schedule as key tracking
  points, often tied directly to contract terms. A
  [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] reports progress against milestones to the
  owner regularly, since they're usually the dates the owner actually cares about, more
  than the hundreds of individual tasks underneath them.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project manager reports that the project is "on track" based on overall
  percent-complete, without flagging that a specific contractual milestone, a partial
  occupancy date the owner needs for move-in planning, is actually at risk. The
  milestone is missed with no advance warning, damaging trust even though the project as
  a whole wasn't far behind.
