id: concept-underground-storage-tank-removal
title: Underground Storage Tank (UST) Removal
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "02, Existing Conditions"
definition: >
  The excavation and removal of a buried fuel or chemical storage tank,
  regulated under EPA rules (40 CFR Part 280) that require at least 30 days'
  advance notice to the regulating agency before permanent closure, along with
  soil testing to check for leaks.
whyItMatters: >
  Older USTs can corrode and leak contaminants into soil and groundwater;
  removal, rather than abandoning a tank in place, is the only way to inspect
  it directly and confirm whether contamination needs to be addressed before
  the site can be redeveloped.
example: >
  Before redeveloping a former gas station site, a contractor excavates and
  removes three old steel USTs, and the required soil sample at the excavation
  reveals contamination that triggers a remediation plan before construction
  can proceed.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-environmental-remediation-specialist|Environmental Remediation
  Specialist]] manages the removal, required notifications, and any
  contamination follow-up, while a [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]]
  builds the regulatory timeline into the project schedule.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A contractor begins excavating a UST without submitting the required 30-day
  closure notice, and the regulating agency halts the work, delaying the
  entire site redevelopment while the paperwork catches up.
