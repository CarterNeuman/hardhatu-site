---
id: concept-fire-rated-access-doors-panels
title: Fire-Rated Access Doors & Panels
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "10, Specialties"
definition: >
  Access doors and panels installed in fire-rated walls or ceilings to reach
  concealed equipment, rated to match the fire resistance of the surrounding
  assembly so the access point doesn't create a weak point in the fire
  separation.
whyItMatters: >
  An access panel installed in a fire-rated wall without a matching fire
  rating defeats the purpose of the rated assembly entirely, and this is a
  commonly missed detail since access panels are often an afterthought added
  after the wall's rating has already been inspected.
example: >
  A contractor installing an access panel in a fire-rated corridor wall to
  reach a damper specifies a panel with a matching fire rating, rather than a
  standard non-rated panel that would compromise the wall's rating.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-qaqc-inspector|QA/QC Inspector]] verifies access panel fire
  ratings match their surrounding assembly during inspection.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A standard non-rated access panel is installed in a fire-rated wall to reach
  mechanical equipment, and the fire marshal flags the mismatch during
  inspection, requiring the panel to be replaced after the wall finish is
  already complete.
relatedIds:
  - career-qaqc-inspector
