---
id: concept-owner
title: Owner
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  The person or organization that owns the property and ultimately pays for and
  receives the finished project, the party every other role on a project (architect,
  general contractor, owner's representative) is ultimately working to serve or
  protect.
whyItMatters: >
  Contracts, decisions, and disputes across a project all trace back to what the owner
  actually wants and is willing to pay for. Understanding a project means
  understanding who the owner actually is and what they're trying to accomplish, not
  just the contractor's or architect's side of the story.
example: >
  A hospital system acts as the owner on a new surgical wing, signing a
  [[concept-prime-contract|prime contract]] with a general contractor and hiring an
  architect and an owner's representative to protect its own interests throughout
  design and construction.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-owners-representative|Owner's Representative]] is hired specifically to
  represent the owner's interests full-time, since most owners aren't in the
  construction business themselves. A [[career-project-manager|Project Manager]] on
  the contractor's side has to balance the contractor's own interests against staying
  responsive to what the owner actually needs.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project team treats "the owner" as a single, simple decision-maker, when in
  reality the actual owner is a committee, a board, or a property management company
  with multiple stakeholders who don't always agree. A decision the project team
  thought was final gets reversed weeks later once it works its way through the
  owner's own internal approval chain.
