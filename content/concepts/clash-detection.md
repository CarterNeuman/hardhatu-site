id: concept-clash-detection
title: Clash Detection
tier: free
category: Technology, Data & Sustainability
definition: >
  An automated check that scans a coordinated [[concept-bim|BIM]] model for elements from
  different trades occupying the same physical space, such as a duct crossing through a
  structural beam or a sprinkler line clashing with a light fixture, flagging conflicts
  for resolution before construction.
whyItMatters: >
  A conflict between two trades' work is far cheaper to fix as a flagged item in a model
  than as a real problem discovered in the field, where resolving it can mean cutting
  into or relocating already-installed work. Clash detection exists specifically to move
  that discovery as early as possible.
example: >
  A weekly clash detection run flags a plumbing line routed through a structural beam's
  reinforcement zone. The mechanical and structural teams resolve it by rerouting the
  line in the model, weeks before either trade is actually on site to install it.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-bim-vdc-specialist|BIM/VDC Specialist]] runs clash detection reports
  regularly throughout design and routes each conflict to whichever trade needs to
  resolve it. A [[career-project-engineer|Project Engineer]] often files the
  [[concept-rfi|RFI]] that results when a clash can't be resolved without a design
  decision from the architect or engineer.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A project skips a clash detection pass late in design to save schedule time, assuming
  the trades have coordinated closely enough on their own. An unresolved conflict between
  ductwork and a structural member isn't caught until the ductwork is already fabricated
  to the clashing dimensions, forcing an expensive re-fabrication that an earlier pass
  would have avoided entirely.
