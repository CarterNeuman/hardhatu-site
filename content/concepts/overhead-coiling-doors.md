id: concept-overhead-coiling-doors
title: Overhead Coiling Doors
tier: free
category: Materials & Systems
csiDivision: "08, Openings"
definition: >
  Doors made of interlocking horizontal slats that coil up into a drum above
  the opening, used for loading dock openings, warehouse bays, and service
  openings, available in manually operated, motorized, and fire-rated
  versions, the fire-rated versions required to close automatically on a fire
  alarm signal to maintain a fire-rated wall's separation.
whyItMatters: >
  A fire-rated overhead coiling door has to be tested regularly to confirm it
  actually closes automatically when triggered, since a door that's been
  chained open or has a malfunctioning drop-out link for convenience defeats
  the entire purpose of the fire-rated wall it sits in.
example: >
  A warehouse's fire-rated overhead coiling door between two fire-separated
  areas is tested during commissioning to confirm it releases and closes fully
  when the fire alarm system signals it, not just when someone manually
  triggers the release mechanism.
careerAngle: >
  An [[career-electrician|Electrician]] or specialty door installer wires the
  fire-rated door's automatic release mechanism to the fire alarm system. A
  [[career-commissioning-agent|Commissioning Agent]] verifies it actually
  closes on signal before turnover.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Warehouse staff chain a fire-rated overhead coiling door partially open
  during regular operations to speed up forklift traffic, defeating its
  automatic closing function. A fire inspector discovers the chain during a
  routine inspection, and the facility has to correct the life-safety
  violation before it passes.
