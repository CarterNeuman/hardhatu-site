id: concept-dot-drug-alcohol-testing-program
title: DOT Drug & Alcohol Testing Program
tier: free
category: Safety & Regulatory Compliance
definition: >
  A federally mandated testing program for any employee who operates a
  commercial motor vehicle requiring a CDL, covering pre-employment, random,
  post-accident, and reasonable-suspicion testing, with violations tracked in
  the FMCSA's Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse, a database employers must check
  before hiring a CDL driver and query annually for current drivers.
whyItMatters: >
  A contractor who runs its own dump trucks or other CDL-required equipment is a
  motor carrier under this program whether it thinks of itself that way or
  not, and skipping the Clearinghouse query on a new CDL hire can mean
  unknowingly putting a driver with an unresolved violation back on the road.
example: >
  A contractor hiring a new dump truck driver queries the FMCSA Clearinghouse
  before putting the driver behind the wheel, discovering the applicant has an
  unresolved failed drug test from a previous employer that bars the driver
  from operating a commercial vehicle until resolved.
careerAngle: >
  Contractors with any CDL-required equipment in their fleet need a compliant
  drug and alcohol testing program and a habit of querying the Clearinghouse,
  since skipping either one creates real liability exposure if that driver is
  later involved in an accident.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Smaller contractors sometimes assume DOT drug testing rules are only for big
  trucking companies, when any employer operating CDL-required vehicles, even
  just a few dump trucks, falls under the same federal testing and
  Clearinghouse requirements.
