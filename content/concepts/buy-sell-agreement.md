---
id: concept-buy-sell-agreement
title: Buy-Sell Agreement
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  A contract among the owners of a construction company that spells out, in
  advance, what happens to an owner's share of the business if that owner
  dies, becomes disabled, retires, or wants to sell, including who can buy
  that share and how its value will be determined.
whyItMatters: >
  Without one, the sudden death or departure of a co-owner can leave the
  remaining owners negotiating company control and valuation with an estranged
  former partner, a divorced spouse, or an owner's estate at the worst
  possible time, rather than following terms already agreed to in advance.
example: >
  Two co-owners of a mid-sized contracting company have a buy-sell agreement
  funded by life insurance on each owner. When one owner dies unexpectedly,
  the life insurance proceeds fund the company's purchase of that owner's
  shares from the estate at a valuation method the agreement already
  specified, avoiding a contested negotiation during an already difficult
  time.
careerAngle: >
  The [[career-construction-attorney|Construction Attorney]] drafts the buy-sell
  agreement's valuation and triggering-event terms, and the
  [[career-construction-accountant|Construction Accountant]] helps determine
  the business valuation method the agreement will rely on when a triggering
  event actually occurs.
whatGoesWrong: >
  Co-owners put off drafting a buy-sell agreement because the business
  relationship feels stable, and then face an unplanned ownership transition
  with no agreed valuation method or funding mechanism in place when a
  triggering event actually happens.
