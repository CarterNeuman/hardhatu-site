id: concept-surety-bond-claim-process
title: Surety Bond Claim Process
tier: free
category: Contracts & Business
definition: >
  The formal sequence a bond claimant follows to notify a surety of a
  contractor's default, submit proof of the claim, and seek payment or
  completion under a performance or payment bond, separate from the
  underwriting process that set the contractor's
  [[concept-surety-bonding-capacity|bonding capacity]] in the first place.
whyItMatters: >
  A surety doesn't pay out automatically just because a contractor failed; the
  claimant has to give proper written notice within the bond's required time
  limits and provide documentation supporting the claim, and the surety then
  investigates before deciding whether and how to respond.
example: >
  A subcontractor goes unpaid when the general contractor's project runs into
  financial trouble. The subcontractor sends a formal notice of claim to the
  GC's payment bond surety within the bond's notice deadline, attaching unpaid
  invoices and proof the work was performed, triggering the surety's
  investigation.
careerAngle: >
  A [[career-surety-claims-manager|Surety Claims Manager]], not the
  underwriter who originally priced the bond, investigates incoming claims to
  decide whether the bond principal actually defaulted and runs the
  cost-to-complete analysis that follows; the
  [[career-surety-bond-underwriter|Surety Bond Underwriter]] is consulted on
  the account's history but has moved on to pricing other risks by the time a
  claim comes in. The
  [[career-construction-claims-consultant|Construction Claims Consultant]]
  helps claimants assemble the documentation a surety needs to process a
  claim.
whatGoesWrong: >
  A claimant assumes they can submit a bond claim whenever convenient, and
  misses the bond's specific notice deadline, which can bar an otherwise valid
  claim entirely regardless of how clearly the contractor defaulted.
