title: Fieldwire
tier: premium
category: "Field management"
whatItIs: >
  A jobsite-focused plan and task management app, owned by Hilti, built
  around keeping field crews and the office working off the same live set
  of plans, tasks, and punch lists.
whatItDoes: >
  Fieldwire keeps a plan set, the tasks tied to it, and the punch list
  that closes a project out all in one mobile-friendly view. A task or
  punch item gets pinned to its exact location on the drawing rather than
  described in a separate list, so a crew opening the app on site sees
  exactly where a problem is instead of just reading about it. It's
  positioned as a lighter, more affordable alternative to a full platform
  like Procore or Autodesk Build, aimed at contractors who mainly need
  field task and plan coordination rather than the deeper budget and
  document control modules those bigger platforms include.
keyFeatures:
  - Plan viewing and markup with every task, photo, and punch item pinned
    to its exact location on the drawing
  - Task assignment and tracking, so a foreman can see who owns what and
    by when across the whole crew
  - Punch list creation and closeout tracking tied directly to a specific
    spot on the plan
  - A free tier for small teams, with paid tiers adding RFI tracking,
    submittals, and deeper reporting
commonUses:
  - A foreman assigns a punch list item to a specific subcontractor
    directly on the floor plan, and the item stays open until that sub
    marks it complete and attaches a photo
  - A superintendent tracks dozens of open tasks across several trades on
    one plan view instead of juggling separate paper lists per trade
tutorialVideos: []
pricingModel: >
  Tiered, per user per month, with a free tier limited by project and
  sheet count and three paid tiers that add RFI tracking, submittals, and
  deeper reporting as you move up.
