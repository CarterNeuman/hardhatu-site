whatItIs: >
  Workforce and equipment scheduling software built for contractors
  juggling crews and machines across several active jobsites at once,
  common in heavy civil and infrastructure work.
whatItDoes: >
  Assignar centers on the dispatch board: a scheduler drags and drops
  crew members and pieces of equipment onto specific jobs for the day
  or week, seeing at a glance who and what is already booked elsewhere
  before double-booking a crew or a piece of equipment two projects
  need at once. Digital timesheets and equipment usage logs tie back to
  the same schedule, and compliance data, certifications, licenses,
  equipment inspections, flags automatically if someone or something
  gets assigned to a job they're not actually qualified or certified
  for.
keyFeatures:
  - A drag-and-drop dispatch board for scheduling crews and equipment
    across multiple active jobsites
  - Digital timesheets and equipment usage logs tied to the day's
    schedule
  - Certification and compliance tracking that flags a worker or
    machine scheduled onto a job they're not qualified for
  - Real-time visibility into who and what equipment is already
    committed elsewhere before a new assignment is made
commonUses:
  - A scheduler assigns three excavator crews across two active sites
    for the coming week without double-booking a single operator
  - A general superintendent gets flagged that a worker scheduled for
    a confined-space task doesn't have the required certification on
    file
