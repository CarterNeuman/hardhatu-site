category: "Project management"
whatItIs: >
  A construction management platform built specifically for home
  builders, remodelers, and specialty contractors, covering a project
  from the initial sales lead through the final invoice.
whatItDoes: >
  Buildertrend is aimed at a different scale of business than a platform
  like Autodesk Build or Procore: a residential builder or remodeler
  juggling many smaller jobs at once, not one large commercial project. A dedicated client portal lets a homeowner see the schedule,
  approve selections, and message the builder directly, and change orders
  route through the same portal so a homeowner's approval on added scope
  is on record rather than a verbal agreement in a hallway. Scheduling
  and job costing tools are sized for residential and remodeling work,
  and a built-in QuickBooks integration carries the numbers into the
  company's actual books.
keyFeatures:
  - A dedicated client portal where a homeowner can see the schedule,
    approve selections, and message the builder directly
  - Scheduling and job costing tools sized for residential and remodeling
    projects rather than large commercial programs
  - Change order and proposal tools that keep a homeowner's approval on
    record when project scope changes
  - Built-in integration with QuickBooks for the accounting side of the
    business
commonUses:
  - A remodeling contractor sends a homeowner a change order for an added
    feature through the client portal and gets a recorded approval before
    the extra work starts
  - A residential builder tracks several subcontractors' schedule
    commitments across many active homes from one dashboard
