whatItIs: >
  A subcontractor pay application platform that moves a monthly draw
  request, and all the compliance documents attached to it, online
  instead of through paper or email.
whatItDoes: >
  GCPay centers on the monthly pay application cycle between a general
  contractor and its subcontractors: a sub submits a draw request with
  its schedule of values, retainage, and change orders already
  calculated, and the required lien waivers and compliance documents
  attach to that same submission. A GC's project accountant reviews and
  approves each sub's application in the same system, and the approved
  numbers sync directly into the GC's accounting software (commonly
  Sage 300 CRE or Viewpoint Vista) instead of being re-keyed by hand.
keyFeatures:
  - Online pay application submission with schedule of values,
    retainage, and change orders calculated automatically
  - Required compliance documents (lien waivers, insurance
    certificates) attached to the same pay application
  - Direct integration with construction accounting/ERP systems, so
    approved draws sync instead of requiring manual re-entry
  - An approval workflow that gives a GC's project accountant one place
    to review every subcontractor's monthly draw
commonUses:
  - A subcontractor submits its monthly draw with lien waivers already
    attached, instead of emailing a PDF pay app separately from the
    waiver documents
  - A GC's project accountant approves a batch of subcontractor pay
    applications and has the numbers sync straight into Viewpoint Vista
