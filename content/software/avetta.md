whatItIs: >
  A contractor prequalification network that lets owners and general
  contractors in regulated industries vet a contractor's safety record
  and insurance before ever hiring them.
whatItDoes: >
  Avetta centers on the prequalification file: a contractor submits
  its safety statistics (incident rates, OSHA logs), insurance
  certificates, and compliance documents once into the platform, and
  any hiring client using Avetta can review that same verified file
  instead of each owner collecting and checking the same documents
  separately. Avetta's own team audits and scores a contractor's safety
  program, so a hiring client gets a standardized risk rating rather
  than just a stack of unverified paperwork, and expiring insurance or
  certifications trigger automatic renewal reminders.
keyFeatures:
  - Centralized contractor prequalification files (safety statistics,
    insurance certificates, compliance documents) shared across
    multiple hiring clients
  - Third-party audit and safety scoring of a contractor's program
    rather than self-reported numbers alone
  - Automatic renewal reminders for expiring insurance certificates
    and safety certifications
  - A standardized risk rating hiring clients use to compare
    contractors before awarding work
commonUses:
  - A subcontractor submits its OSHA incident rates and insurance
    certificates once, so every Avetta-using client it bids to can
    review the same verified file
  - A safety manager gets an automatic reminder before a required
    insurance certificate expires, instead of a client finding out
    after the fact

pricingModel: >
