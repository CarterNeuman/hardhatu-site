title: "Autodesk Build"
tier: premium
category: "Project management"
whatItIs: >
  Autodesk's cloud-based construction management platform, tying a
  project's drawings, RFIs, submittals, daily field reports, and budget
  into one shared record.
whatItDoes: >
  Build centers on a single project record that a superintendent in the
  field, a project manager in the office, and an owner checking progress
  remotely can all see at their own permission level. Drawings and
  documents live in one version-controlled place, RFIs and submittals get
  tracked with due dates and tied to the specific sheet or model location
  they concern, and daily field reports, safety forms, and photos flow in
  from a mobile app that keeps working even without a signal on site,
  syncing once a connection is available again.
keyFeatures:
  - Drawing and document management with markup tools and version
    comparison built in
  - RFI, submittal, and change order tracking linked to a specific sheet
    or model location
  - Daily field reports, safety and quality forms, and photo logs from a
    mobile app that works offline on site
  - Budget and cost tracking tied to the project's change orders and
    committed costs
commonUses:
  - A superintendent files a daily report from a tablet on the jobsite,
    including safety checks and photos, with the report syncing once a
    signal is available
  - A project manager tracks an RFI from submission through the
    architect's response, with the whole exchange attached to the exact
    sheet it concerns
