category: MEP design & estimating
whatItIs: >
  Paired mechanical estimating and design software for piping and
  ductwork, FastPIPE for plumbing and process piping, FastDUCT for
  HVAC ductwork, used by MEP estimators to take off and price mechanical
  scope.
whatItDoes: >
  FastPIPE and FastDUCT work from a digital plan set the same way a
  general takeoff tool does, but with mechanical-specific material and
  labor databases built in: an MEP estimator traces pipe or duct runs
  on screen and the software immediately prices fittings, valves,
  insulation, and labor hours against those databases rather than an
  estimator pricing each component by hand. Finished takeoffs export
  into a bid or, on the design side, into fabrication-ready drawings
  that a mechanical or sheet metal shop can build directly from.
keyFeatures:
  - Digital takeoff for piping and ductwork directly off a plan set
  - Built-in mechanical material and labor cost databases for pricing
    fittings, valves, insulation, and labor hours
  - Separate modules for piping (FastPIPE) and ductwork (FastDUCT) that
    share the same takeoff workflow
  - Export to fabrication-ready drawings for a mechanical or sheet
    metal shop
commonUses:
  - An MEP estimator takes off a building's ductwork runs and gets an
    automatic material and labor price using the software's built-in
    cost database
  - A mechanical contractor's estimating team prices a plumbing bid's
    piping scope using FastPIPE's fitting and valve database instead of
    pricing each component by hand
