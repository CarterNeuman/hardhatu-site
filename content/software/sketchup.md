whatItIs: >
  Trimble's 3D modeling software, known for being far easier to pick up
  than a full BIM platform like Revit, which is why it shows up
  everywhere from architecture firms to a contractor's own design-build
  sketches.
whatItDoes: >
  SketchUp trades some of Revit's deep parametric building intelligence
  for a much gentler learning curve, letting someone without formal CAD
  training build a real 3D model quickly. Its companion tool, LayOut,
  turns that 3D model into 2D construction documents, and higher tiers
  add point cloud and scan-to-3D workflows for modeling existing
  conditions. It can import and export Revit models and standard formats
  like IFC and DWG, so it fits into a project's workflow rather than
  replacing the rest of it.
keyFeatures:
  - An approachable 3D modeling interface that doesn't require the
    steeper learning curve of a full BIM platform
  - LayOut, a companion tool for turning a 3D model into 2D construction
    documents
  - Point cloud and scan-to-3D workflows on its higher tiers for
    modeling existing conditions
  - Import and export compatibility with Revit models and
    industry-standard formats like IFC and DWG
commonUses:
  - A design-build contractor sketches a remodel concept in SketchUp to
    show a homeowner before committing to full construction drawings
  - A field team imports a point cloud scan of an existing building into
    SketchUp to model conditions that were never fully documented on
    paper
