category: Equipment & fleet management
whatItIs: >
  Connected-equipment and fleet telematics software, tracking the
  location, usage, and condition of trucks and heavy equipment through
  GPS sensors installed on each machine.
whatItDoes: >
  Samsara centers on the sensor data a GPS tracker reports from each
  piece of equipment or vehicle: location, hours of use, fuel level,
  and diagnostic codes that flag a mechanical problem before it becomes
  a breakdown in the field. An equipment or fleet manager sees a whole
  fleet's locations and status on one dashboard, sets up automatic
  maintenance alerts based on actual engine hours rather than a
  calendar guess, and can geofence a jobsite to get an alert if a piece
  of equipment leaves it unexpectedly.
keyFeatures:
  - Real-time GPS location and status tracking across an entire
    equipment and vehicle fleet
  - Engine diagnostics and maintenance alerts triggered by actual usage
    (engine hours, fuel level) rather than a fixed calendar schedule
  - Geofencing that flags when a piece of equipment leaves a defined
    jobsite boundary
  - Dashcam and driver-safety monitoring options alongside the core
    equipment tracking
commonUses:
  - An equipment manager gets an automatic alert that an excavator is
    due for scheduled maintenance based on its actual logged engine
    hours
  - A fleet manager gets notified the moment a piece of equipment
    leaves a jobsite's geofenced boundary overnight
