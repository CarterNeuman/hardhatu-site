whatItIs: >
  A mobile-first inspection and checklist platform that construction
  safety teams use to run digital jobsite inspections, log hazards, and
  track corrective actions, known as SafetyCulture and its iAuditor app
  before an August 2026 rebrand to Mitti.
whatItDoes: >
  Mitti replaces a paper clipboard inspection with a digital checklist
  that a safety manager builds once and reuses across every site or crew.
  A worker running an inspection in the field can attach a photo directly
  to the specific item it concerns, and a flagged hazard gets tracked as
  an open corrective action until someone actually closes it out, rather
  than sitting on a completed form that never gets revisited. The app
  keeps working without a signal on a remote jobsite and syncs everything
  once a connection is available again.
keyFeatures:
  - Customizable digital checklists and inspection templates, built once
    and reused across every site or crew
  - A mobile app that works offline in the field and syncs once a
    connection is available
  - Photo and note capture attached directly to a specific checklist
    item, not just a separate report
  - Corrective action tracking that follows a flagged hazard until it's
    actually closed out
commonUses:
  - A safety manager builds a digital jobsite hazard inspection checklist
    once and has every superintendent on the company's projects run the
    same inspection every week
  - A worker photographs a damaged piece of scaffolding directly inside
    the inspection app, and the flagged item stays open and assigned
    until someone confirms it's fixed
