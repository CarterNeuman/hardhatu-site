whatItIs: >
  Tool and equipment inventory tracking software (ToolWatch was its
  standalone name before a 2024 rebrand folded it into the AlignOps
  construction operations platform), built so a contractor knows
  which tool is checked out to which worker or jobsite, instead of
  tools simply disappearing from the yard.
whatItDoes: >
  ToolWatch centers on the checkout: a worker scans or logs a tool as
  it leaves the yard for a specific jobsite, and that tool stays
  attached to that worker and job in the system until it's checked back
  in. An equipment manager can see a whole company's tool and small
  equipment inventory at once, where everything currently is, what's
  overdue for return, and what's due for maintenance or
  recertification, instead of relying on a paper sign-out sheet or
  simply not knowing where a tool went.
keyFeatures:
  - Tool and small equipment checkout tracking tied to a specific
    worker and jobsite
  - Company-wide inventory visibility showing where every tracked tool
    currently is
  - Maintenance and recertification scheduling for tools and equipment
    that require it
  - Overdue-return alerts to cut down on lost or never-returned tools
commonUses:
  - A worker scans a power tool out to a specific jobsite, so it's
    clear who has it if it doesn't come back
  - An equipment manager runs a company-wide report on which tools
    are overdue for return across every active project
