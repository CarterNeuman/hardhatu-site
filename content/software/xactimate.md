whatItIs: >
  The estimating software the property insurance industry has largely
  standardized on for pricing repair work after a claim, made by
  Verisk's Xactware division.
whatItDoes: >
  Xactimate lets an adjuster, restoration contractor, or public adjuster
  sketch a damaged structure's layout, measure the areas affected, and
  price the repair using a shared, regionally localized line-item price
  list. Because nearly every party to a claim references the same
  underlying price list, a dispute over an estimate usually comes down to
  specific line items rather than two completely incompatible formats. A
  field visit can start an estimate on a tablet or phone, with the same
  file finished later back at a desk, so the office and field sides of
  the work stay in one record instead of two.
keyFeatures:
  - 2D and 3D sketching tools for drawing a damaged structure's layout
    from field measurements
  - A standardized, regionally priced line-item database that every party
    to a claim can reference against
  - Mobile capture in the field with the estimate finished later on
    desktop, so a site visit doesn't require carrying a laptop
  - Photo documentation attached directly to specific line items in the
    finished estimate
commonUses:
  - An insurance adjuster sketches a water-damaged room's dimensions on a
    tablet during an inspection and finishes pricing the repair later
    from the same file
  - A restoration contractor and an adjuster compare two independently
    written estimates for the same loss line item by line item to
    negotiate their specific disagreements
