whatItIs: >
  A safety and compliance platform built specifically for construction,
  covering worker orientations, permits, and inspections in one system
  rather than several disconnected tools.
whatItDoes: >
  HammerTech centers on getting a worker's orientation and safety
  assessment done digitally, often before they ever set foot on site,
  then keeps permits, safety data sheets, and their expiration dates in
  one tracked place instead of a filing cabinet. Safety inspections and
  audits log against a project's own compliance requirements, and
  incident reports tie back to the same worker and site records the rest
  of the platform already has on file.
keyFeatures:
  - Digital worker orientations and inductions that can be completed
    before someone ever steps on site
  - Centralized permit tracking, including hot work permits, plus
    safety data sheet storage with expiration alerts
  - Safety inspections and audits logged against a project's own
    compliance requirements
  - Incident reporting tied to the same worker and site records the rest
    of the platform already tracks
commonUses:
  - A new worker completes a digital site orientation and safety
    assessment from home before their first day on a jobsite
  - A safety manager gets an automatic alert before a subcontractor's
    insurance certificate or safety data sheet expires, instead of
    discovering it lapsed after the fact
