title: AutoSPRINK

category: MEP design & estimating
whatItIs: >
  Fire sprinkler system design software, built to lay out piping,
  run hydraulic calculations, and check a sprinkler design against NFPA
  code requirements before it's ever installed.
whatItDoes: >
  AutoSPRINK centers on the sprinkler layout: a designer places piping,
  heads, and risers on a 3D model of the building, and the software
  runs the hydraulic calculations (flow and pressure through every pipe
  and fitting) that prove the system will actually deliver the water
  a fire requires under NFPA 13. Material lists and fabrication
  drawings generate directly from that same model, and clash detection
  against other MEP trades' models catches a sprinkler pipe routed
  through a duct or beam before it becomes a field conflict.
keyFeatures:
  - 3D sprinkler system layout and piping design
  - Automated hydraulic calculations proving a design meets NFPA flow
    and pressure requirements
  - Material lists and fabrication drawings generated directly from
    the design model
  - Clash detection against other MEP trades' models
commonUses:
  - A fire protection designer runs a hydraulic calculation to confirm
    a sprinkler system will deliver required flow at the building's
    highest, most remote head
  - A sprinkler fitter uses a fabrication drawing generated from the
    design model to prefabricate pipe sections before installation
