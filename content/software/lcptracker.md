whatItIs: >
  A certified payroll and prevailing-wage compliance platform built for
  contractors working on public, government-funded construction
  projects.
whatItDoes: >
  LCPtracker centers on the weekly certified payroll report every
  contractor and subcontractor on a prevailing-wage public project has
  to submit: workers' hours, classifications, and wage rates get
  checked against the project's published prevailing wage determination
  before the report ever reaches the awarding agency, flagging
  underpayments or misclassifications before they become a compliance
  finding. Electronic certified payroll reports (including the federal
  WH-347 form many public projects require) submit directly through the
  platform instead of being filled out and mailed by hand.
keyFeatures:
  - Weekly certified payroll report generation, including the federal
    WH-347 form format many public agencies require
  - Automatic wage-rate and classification checks against a project's
    published prevailing wage determination
  - Compliance flags for underpayment or misclassification before a
    report reaches the awarding agency
  - Electronic submission and recordkeeping across every public project
    a contractor works on at once
