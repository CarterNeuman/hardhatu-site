title: Matterport

category: "Reality capture"
whatItIs: >
  A 3D scanning platform that turns a physical space into a
  photorealistic, walkable digital model, used in construction mainly
  for documenting existing conditions and finished work.
whatItDoes: >
  Matterport's camera scans a real space and stitches the result into a
  photorealistic digital twin someone can walk through remotely, closer
  to a real visit than a set of photos. In construction, that's most
  useful for documenting existing conditions before a renovation or
  finished work before it gets covered by later trades, and the
  underlying point cloud data can feed into a BIM model for existing
  conditions modeling. The camera itself is a real upfront cost, which
  is worth weighing against how often a company would actually use it.
keyFeatures:
  - 3D scanning that produces a photorealistic, walkable digital twin of
    a physical space
  - As-built documentation of a finished space before it gets covered by
    later work or handed over to the owner
  - Point cloud data that can feed into a BIM model for existing
    conditions modeling
  - Hardware and software sold together, with the camera itself a
    significant upfront cost
commonUses:
  - A BIM/VDC Specialist scans a building's existing conditions before a
    renovation so the design team is working from an accurate model
    instead of outdated drawings
  - A facilities manager scans a finished space at turnover so a future
    maintenance question can be answered by walking the digital model
    instead of visiting in person
