whatItIs: >
  Oracle's enterprise scheduling software, the standard critical path
  method tool for large and complex construction programs.
whatItDoes: >
  P6 handles scheduling at a scale general-purpose tools aren't built
  for, tracking a single massive project or dozens of projects running at
  once inside one program. Its critical path method engine calculates
  which activities directly control a project's finish date and how much
  float every other activity has before its own slip would push that
  finish date too. Its resource optimization tools let a scheduler model
  whether a crew or a piece of equipment is overcommitted across multiple
  projects before that conflict actually shows up on the jobsite.
keyFeatures:
  - Critical path method scheduling across large, multi-project programs,
    not just a single job
  - Resource optimization tools for planning crew and equipment capacity
    across overlapping projects
  - What-if scenario modeling to test how a delay in one activity would
    ripple through the rest of the schedule
  - Role-based, multi-user access so schedulers, project controls staff,
    and executives all work from the same live schedule
commonUses:
  - A scheduler on a multi-year infrastructure program models several
    what-if scenarios for a delayed material delivery before recommending
    a recovery plan
  - A project controls team tracks resource loading across a dozen active
    projects at once to catch a crew being double-booked before it
    actually happens
