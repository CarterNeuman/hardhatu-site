whatItIs: >
  A mobile app for running digital safety inspections and audits, so a
  jobsite walk-through turns into a saved, photo-backed checklist
  instead of a paper form that gets filed and forgotten.
whatItDoes: >
  SafetyCulture centers on the inspection checklist: a safety manager
  or coordinator builds (or pulls from a template library) a checklist
  for a specific inspection, walks the site checking items off on a
  phone or tablet, and attaches a photo directly to any item that fails.
  A failed item can trigger a corrective action assigned to a specific
  person with a due date, and every past inspection stays searchable,
  so a pattern of recurring issues on a site shows up instead of
  getting buried in a filing cabinet of paper forms.
keyFeatures:
  - Digital inspection checklists, built from scratch or pulled from a
    template library, completed from a phone or tablet
  - Photo documentation attached directly to any checklist item,
    especially failed items
  - Corrective action assignment with a due date and an owner for any
    item that fails an inspection
  - Searchable inspection history across a project or a whole company
commonUses:
  - A safety coordinator walks a jobsite with a digital fall-protection
    checklist and photographs a missing guardrail on the spot
  - A safety manager searches past inspections to see whether the same
    housekeeping issue keeps recurring on one particular site
