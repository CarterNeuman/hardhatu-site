whatItIs: >
  Construction-focused accounting and payroll software aimed at small to
  midsize contractors, sitting between a general tool like QuickBooks
  and a full enterprise system like Sage 300 CRE or Viewpoint Vista.
whatItDoes: >
  FOUNDATION tracks dollars, hours, and quantities down to the
  individual project, and runs payroll across multiple jobs, localities,
  and pay rates in a single pass rather than as separate manual runs.
  Its certified payroll reporting is built for prevailing wage compliance
  on government contracts, and mobile time tracking with GPS confirms a
  worker actually clocked in from the jobsite they're billing to, not
  just somewhere convenient.
keyFeatures:
  - Job costing that tracks dollars, hours, and quantities down to the
    individual project
  - Payroll across multiple jobs, localities, and pay rates in one run
  - Certified payroll reporting built for prevailing wage compliance on
    government contracts
  - Mobile time tracking with GPS confirming where a worker actually
    clocked in
commonUses:
  - A payroll administrator runs one payroll covering workers on several
    different jobs, each with its own prevailing wage rate, in a single
    pass
  - A field supervisor has crew members clock in from their phones, with
    GPS confirming they're actually on the jobsite they're billing to
