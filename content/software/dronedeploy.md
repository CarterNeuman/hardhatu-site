category: Reality capture
whatItIs: >
  Drone-based mapping and progress-tracking software, built for
  flying a jobsite or earthwork site on a schedule and turning the
  footage into measurable maps and volumes, rather than just photos.
whatItDoes: >
  DroneDeploy plans and automates a drone's flight path over a site,
  then stitches the resulting images into an orthomosaic map and a 3D
  model a superintendent or surveyor can measure directly, distances,
  areas, and earthwork volumes, without ever walking the site with a
  tape measure. Flying the same flight path on a regular schedule turns
  those maps into a visual timeline of how a site has changed, letting
  a project team compare this week's progress photo against last
  week's or against the original design surface.
keyFeatures:
  - Automated drone flight planning that repeats the same flight path
    on a schedule for consistent progress comparisons
  - Orthomosaic mapping and 3D model generation from drone imagery
  - Volumetric measurement for stockpiles and earthwork cut/fill
  - Side-by-side progress comparison between flights over time
commonUses:
  - A surveyor measures a stockpile's volume from a drone flight
    instead of a manual ground survey
  - A general superintendent compares this month's aerial map of the
    site against last month's to show progress at an owner meeting
