title: Raken
tier: premium
category: Field management
whatItIs: >
  A mobile app for daily field reports, jobsite photos, and crew time
  tracking, built so a foreman or superintendent can log a day's work
  from a phone rather than filling out paper forms at the end of a
  shift.
whatItDoes: >
  Raken centers on the daily report: a superintendent logs weather,
  manpower, work completed, and safety notes from a phone or tablet,
  attaches jobsite photos directly to that day's entry, and the report
  generates automatically as a shareable PDF instead of being typed up
  separately later. Crew time tracking runs alongside the same daily
  log, so hours worked tie back to the same day's report rather than
  living in a separate timecard system, and completed reports roll up
  across a whole company's projects for an office team to review
  without chasing down paper forms.
keyFeatures:
  - Daily field reports (weather, manpower, work completed, safety
    notes) logged from a phone and auto-generated as a shareable PDF
  - Jobsite photo documentation attached directly to the day's report
  - Crew time tracking and digital timecards tied to the same daily log
  - Company-wide reporting that rolls up every project's daily reports
    for the office to review
commonUses:
  - A superintendent closes out the day by logging manpower, weather,
    and a few jobsite photos from their phone before leaving the site
  - An owner's rep reviews a week of daily reports and photos remotely
    instead of requesting a written weekly progress summary
