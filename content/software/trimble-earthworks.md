title: Trimble Earthworks
tier: premium
category: Surveying & machine control
whatItIs: >
  In-cab GPS machine control software that guides a dozer's, excavator's,
  or grader's blade or bucket to a 3D design surface in real time,
  instead of an operator working off grade stakes.
whatItDoes: >
  Trimble Earthworks runs on a display mounted in the equipment cab: a
  3D design surface, built from a civil engineer's or surveyor's design
  file, shows on screen as a target grade, and GPS positioning on the
  machine tells the operator in real time how far the blade or bucket
  is from that target. On equipment with full automatics, the system
  can control the blade or bucket directly rather than just displaying
  guidance, cutting and filling to the design surface without an
  operator manually chasing a number on a screen.
keyFeatures:
  - Real-time 3D grade guidance in the equipment cab, referenced to a
    GPS-positioned design surface
  - Automatic blade/bucket control on equipped machines, cutting and
    filling to a design surface without manual adjustment
  - Design file import directly from civil engineering software like
    Civil 3D
  - Cut/fill and progress reporting that tracks earthwork completed
    against the design
commonUses:
  - A dozer operator grades a pad to final subgrade using in-cab GPS
    guidance instead of checking grade stakes by hand
  - A heavy civil estimator compares earthwork actually moved against
    the design surface to see how a job is tracking against the bid
