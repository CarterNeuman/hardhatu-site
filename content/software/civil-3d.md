title: Civil 3D
tier: premium
category: Design & BIM
whatItIs: >
  Autodesk's civil engineering design software, built for site design,
  grading, roadways, and underground utilities, rather than the
  general-purpose drafting AutoCAD is built for.
whatItDoes: >
  Civil 3D works from a dynamic model instead of static lines: a
  surveyor's point data builds an existing-ground surface, a civil
  engineer lays out roadway corridors and grading plans against that
  surface, and the model recalculates cut-and-fill volumes and contours
  automatically every time a design changes rather than requiring a
  manual redraw. Pipe networks for storm and sanitary utilities carry
  their own slope and invert-elevation data, and the finished design
  exports to the GPS coordinates a machine-control system or a
  surveyor's stakeout equipment uses in the field.
keyFeatures:
  - Dynamic surface modeling from survey point data, with automatic
    cut-and-fill volume and contour updates when the design changes
  - Roadway corridor and grading design tools for site and civil
    projects
  - Pipe network design for storm and sanitary utilities, with slope
    and invert elevation data built in
  - Data export to the GPS coordinates used by machine control systems
    and surveying equipment in the field
commonUses:
  - A civil engineer models a parking lot's grading plan and gets an
    automatic cut-and-fill volume estimate before construction starts
  - A surveyor exports a finished site design's point data directly to
    a GPS machine control system running on a dozer or excavator
