whatItIs: >
  Autodesk's cloud-based estimating platform, built to centralize a
  company's takeoffs, cost databases, and bid proposals in one place.
whatItDoes: >
  ProEst combines digital takeoff and cost estimating in one connected
  platform, drawing on reusable cost databases and templates so a bid
  doesn't start from a blank sheet every time. Its dashboard reporting
  compares an estimate's original numbers against a project's actual
  costs once work is underway, and integrations with accounting
  platforms like QuickBooks carry a winning bid's numbers straight into
  the company's books.
keyFeatures:
  - Digital takeoff and cost estimating in one connected platform rather
    than separate tools
  - Reusable cost databases and templates so a bid doesn't start from a
    blank sheet every time
  - Job costing and dashboard reporting comparing an estimate against a
    project's actual costs later
  - Integration with accounting platforms like QuickBooks so a winning
    bid's numbers flow into the company's books
commonUses:
  - An estimator builds a bid from a reusable cost database instead of
    re-pricing every material and labor line from scratch
  - A preconstruction manager compares a job's original estimate against
    its actual costs once the project is underway, to see where the bid
    was accurate and where it wasn't
