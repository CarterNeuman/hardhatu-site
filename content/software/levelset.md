category: Payment & lien management
whatItIs: >
  A payment-compliance platform that tracks the paperwork construction
  companies need to protect their right to get paid, preliminary
  notices, lien waivers, and mechanics liens, across every project they
  work on.
whatItDoes: >
  Levelset centers on keeping a company's payment rights intact on
  every job: it tracks notice and lien deadlines by state (since the
  rules differ everywhere), generates and sends preliminary notices,
  and produces conditional and unconditional lien waivers that match
  what a particular project or GC requires. A credit or risk manager
  can see, across every active project, exactly which payment documents
  are outstanding before a invoice or draw goes out, instead of
  tracking deadlines state by state in a spreadsheet.
keyFeatures:
  - State-specific preliminary notice and mechanics lien deadline
    tracking across every active project
  - Lien waiver generation (conditional and unconditional, progress and
    final) matching project-specific requirements
  - A payment-status dashboard showing which notices, waivers, or
    releases are still outstanding company-wide
  - Mechanics lien filing when a payment dispute actually requires one
commonUses:
  - A credit manager checks a dashboard before month-end to see which
    projects still owe an outstanding lien waiver before a draw releases
  - A subcontractor sends a preliminary notice on a new job the same
    week work starts, to preserve lien rights in case payment problems
    come up later
