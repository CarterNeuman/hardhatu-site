title: BuildingConnected
tier: premium
category: Bid management
whatItIs: >
  A bid management platform, built by Autodesk, where a general
  contractor invites subcontractors to bid, collects and compares those
  bids, and keeps a database of vetted subs to invite again next time.
whatItDoes: >
  BuildingConnected centers on the invitation-to-award cycle: a
  preconstruction team builds a bid package, invites subcontractors from
  a searchable network or their own private directory, and tracks who
  has viewed, downloaded, and responded to the invite. Incoming bids
  land in a single bid-leveling view so an estimator can compare scope,
  price, and exclusions side by side instead of juggling separate
  emails and spreadsheets, and a subcontractor's insurance, licensing,
  and past-performance data travels with their profile for prequalification.
keyFeatures:
  - Subcontractor invitation and bid tracking, including read receipts
    on who has opened and responded to a bid package
  - Bid leveling that lines up multiple subs' pricing, scope, and
    exclusions in one comparison view
  - A searchable network of subcontractors plus a private, firm-specific
    directory of preferred subs
  - Subcontractor prequalification data (insurance, licensing, past
    performance) attached to each sub's profile
commonUses:
  - An estimator sends a bid invitation to a dozen electrical subs at
    once and tracks which ones have actually opened the drawings
  - A preconstruction team levels five incoming masonry bids side by
    side to spot which one left out a scope item the others included
