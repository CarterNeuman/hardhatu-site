whatItIs: >
  A digital takeoff tool from ConstructConnect, built for measuring a
  plan set on screen and feeding those quantities into an estimate.
whatItDoes: >
  On-Screen Takeoff lets an estimator measure lengths, areas, and counts
  directly from a digital plan set using reusable templates and layers,
  instead of starting a new takeoff from a blank sheet on every project.
  Its AI-assisted auto-count feature can identify and count repeated
  items, like fixtures or outlets, across a whole plan automatically.
  On-Screen Takeoff itself focuses on the measurement side; turning that
  measured quantity into a fully priced proposal happens in its
  companion Quick Bid software, which it pairs closely with.
keyFeatures:
  - On-screen measurement of lengths, areas, and counts directly from a
    digital plan set
  - Customizable takeoff templates and layers reused across similar
    projects
  - AI-assisted automatic counting for repeated items like fixtures or
    outlets
  - Pairs with Quick Bid, ConstructConnect's companion estimating
    software, to turn a finished takeoff into a priced proposal
commonUses:
  - An estimator measures every door and window on a digital plan set
    once and reuses the same takeoff template on the next similar
    project
  - A preconstruction team lets the software auto-count repeated
    fixtures across a large floor plan instead of clicking each one
    individually
