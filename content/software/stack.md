title: STACK
tier: premium
category: Estimating
whatItIs: >
  A cloud-based takeoff and estimating tool, where a contractor measures
  a digital plan set to build a bid instead of measuring paper drawings
  by hand.
whatItDoes: >
  STACK lets an estimator upload a digital plan set and measure it
  directly on screen, calibrated to the drawing's real scale, then turns
  that completed takeoff into a priced estimate using built-in labor and
  material cost data. Markups and takeoffs stay attached to the exact
  plan sheet they were measured from, so a reviewer can see both the
  number and where it came from in the same place, and a separate field
  side of the platform carries the same job forward once it's actually
  won.
keyFeatures:
  - Digital takeoff tools for measuring length, area, and count directly
    off an uploaded plan set
  - Cost estimating that turns a completed takeoff into a priced bid
    using built-in labor and material data
  - Markup and document sharing so a takeoff and its underlying plan
    sheet stay attached to each other
  - Field-side project tracking that connects a winning estimate to the
    job once it's actually under construction
commonUses:
  - An estimator measures every wall and door on a digital floor plan to
    build a material list for a bid instead of walking a printed set with
    a scale ruler
  - A preconstruction team compares a takeoff-based estimate against the
    actual buyout numbers once subcontractor bids come in, to see where
    the original estimate was off
